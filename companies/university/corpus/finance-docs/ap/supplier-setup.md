# Setting up a supplier

**Owner:** Accounts payable  
**Last edited by:** Caleb Salazar (Accounts Payable Assistant)

---

## When a new supplier is needed

New supplier requests come through Procurement. A request is not considered complete until Procurement have confirmed the goods or service have gone through the appropriate buying route (see `finance-docs/procurement/buying.md`).

Once Procurement have raised the request, Accounts payable will set the supplier up in the finance system.

---

## Checks before setup

- [ ] Confirm the supplier is not already on the finance system under a slightly different name (check by registration number as well as name).
- [ ] Confirm the supplier's legal entity name matches the bank account name exactly.
- [ ] UK suppliers: obtain the company registration number and VAT registration number. Verify these on the public registers.
- [ ] Non-UK suppliers: obtain the local registration or tax identifier where one exists. The finance system holds a list of acceptable identifiers by country.
- [ ] Check the supplier against the finance system's flagged list. If the supplier appears, do not proceed — pass the request to Diego Achebe (Accounts Payable Manager).
- [ ] Check whether the supplier is already set up on the old purchasing system that Procurement still use for some legacy contracts. If so, the supplier record will need to be migrated as part of setup.

---

## Bank details

- [ ] Obtain bank details on the supplier's letterhead or via the secure supplier portal.
- [ ] Confirm the bank account details by call-back. The call-back must be to a number we already hold for the supplier — not a number provided in the same email or letter as the bank details.
- [ ] Record the call-back on the supplier record, including who did the call-back and which number was used.
- [ ] If we do not already hold a number for the supplier, ask Procurement to obtain one through their existing contact channels. Do not use a number from an unsolicited email.

---

## Who can create or change suppliers

Only Accounts payable staff with the finance system's supplier maintenance role can create or change supplier records.

- **Create new supplier:** Lucia Ferreira (Accounts Payable Supervisor) or Diego Achebe (Accounts Payable Manager).
- **Change bank details on an existing supplier:** Lucia Ferreira or Diego Achebe.
- **Change supplier name or address (non-bank):** Esther Rao (Accounts Payable Officer) can do this where the change has come through with supporting evidence.
- **Merge duplicates:** Diego Achebe only.
- **Mark a supplier inactive:** Lucia Ferreira or Diego Achebe.

Changes are logged in the finance system's audit trail. If a supplier asks us to change bank details by email alone, refer them to the secure supplier portal. If the email looks unexpected, contact the supplier on the number we hold before making any change — see the bank details section above.

---

## After setup

- [ ] Send the supplier the finance system's confirmation email (this goes automatically when the record is saved).
- [ ] Add the supplier to the relevant purchasing and payment groups so they appear in the right drop-downs.
- [ ] Tell Procurement when the supplier is live so they can complete their part of the request.
- [ ] If the supplier is in Malaysia, also let Siew Lan Chong (Finance Manager, Malaysia Campus) know so the local finance team can set up the payment details on their side.
