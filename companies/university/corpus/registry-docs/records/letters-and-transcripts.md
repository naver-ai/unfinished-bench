# Letters, transcripts and certificates

**Owner:** Student Records & Data  
**Last edited by:** Luis Ferreira (Student Records Manager (Operations))

## How do students order a status letter or council tax exemption certificate?

Through the student portal. They go to **My documents** and pick the letter type. Most letters generate immediately, including council tax exemption certificates. Anything that needs a signature or a bespoke wording goes to the Student Records & Data team via the casework queue.

## What status letters are self-service?

- Confirmation of enrolment (standard wording, for the current academic year)
- Council tax exemption certificate (current year only)
- Bank letter (confirms enrolment for student accounts, includes the university bank sort code)
- Proof of student status for the current term

## What needs to come through the team?

- Letters addressed to a third party (sponsor, embassy, local authority)
- Letters confirming a change of name or personal details
- Letters for previous academic years (the portal only holds the current one)
- Any letter with custom wording — these go to the Academic Registry for approval if it touches on academic standing

## How long does a non-automated letter take?

Normally two working days from the request landing in the queue. If the wording is unusual or it needs a signature from the Deputy Academic Registrar, allow five working days. We do not do same-day letters except for the automated portal ones.

## Can students get a transcript from the portal?

Unofficial transcripts are self-service in the portal — these show modules and marks but no degree classification. Official transcripts (with the university crest and the Academic Registrar's signature) go through the team. Official transcripts for current students are free; alumni pay a fee via the online store.

## How long do official transcripts take?

Usually three working days. Around exam board periods (end of each term) it can stretch to ten working days because the Examinations & Assessment team is validating marks at the same time. We tell students to order early if they have a visa or professional body deadline.

## What about degree certificates?

Certificates are produced by the Examinations & Assessment team after the relevant exam board confirms the award. The certificate itself is printed and dispatched by an external supplier, but the data comes from the student record system. Student Records & Data checks the certificate run against the exam board outcomes before it goes to the supplier.

## When do certificates arrive?

Certificates for July exam boards are dispatched in the autumn; November and March exam boards follow about six to eight weeks after the board. The supplier sends them by tracked post to the address on the student record. We do not send certificates to a third-party address unless the student has updated their correspondence address in the portal and told us separately.

## What if a certificate is lost or damaged?

Students can request a replacement certificate through the portal. The replacement shows the same wording with a note that it is a replacement issued on a specified date. Replacements cost £35 and take ten working days. If the original was damaged in transit, we waive the fee — the student should send a photo through the casework system.

## Can we issue a digital copy of a certificate?

We can produce a PDF copy of the certificate wording, but it is not a legal replacement and it does not carry the signature in a verifiable form. It is mainly used for employers who just need to see the award title and date. The official route for verification is the graduate verification service — contact the Examinations & Assessment team for that.

## Who handles enquiries about certificates that haven't arrived?

The Examinations & Assessment team owns certificate dispatch. If a student comes to us, we check the dispatch date in the portal and, if it is past the expected delivery window, we log a query with Examinations & Assessment. We do not promise a date beyond what the tracking shows.

## Anything else to watch?

- Status letters only confirm what is on the student record — if the record has a pending change (name, module, course), fix that first, otherwise the letter goes out wrong.
- Council tax certificates only cover the current academic year. Students need a new one each year.
- We do not issue letters to the Kuala Lumpur campus from here — the Malaysia campus has its own student services team for local letters, and they use the same portal but a different letter template set.
