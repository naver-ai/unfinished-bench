# Lease administration

**Owner:** Campus Property & Leasing (Malaysia)  
**Last edited by:** Marcus Lindahl, Campus Property Manager, Malaysia

This note covers the lease events diary, service charge budgets and year-end reconciliation, bank guarantees and security deposits, and keeping the tenancy register current after each lease event.

## Lease events diary

The lease events diary is maintained in the lease administration system (the tenancy module). Each tenancy record carries the key dates that drive our work:

| Event | Trigger | Action | Lead |
|-------|---------|--------|------|
| Rent review | Date in the lease terms | Confirm the review mechanism, obtain updated market evidence, issue the notice within the contractual window | Kenneth Tan |
| Lease renewal | Expiry date minus the notice period stated in the lease | Write to the tenant with renewal terms, chase the signed copy, update the register | Aoife Brennan |
| Break option | Break date minus the notice period | Check whether the tenant has served notice, confirm the break is exercisable, update the register | Aoife Brennan |
| Rent payment change | Rent review or renewal completed | Raise the change in the lease administration system so the finance system picks up the new amount from the next billing period | Sienna Okafor |

The diary is reviewed at the weekly Campus Property & Leasing (Malaysia) team meeting. Any event in the next two months is flagged for action. If a rent review or renewal is still outstanding after the contractual date, tell Suresh Pillai so he can raise it with the tenant.

## Service charge budgets and year-end reconciliation

Service charge budgets are prepared by Campus Property & Leasing (Malaysia) with the estates finance team in Aldercombe. The budget for each calendar year is issued to tenants with the January invoice, and the supporting schedule shows the property costs included.

At year end, the actual costs are reconciled against the budget. The reconciliation is produced from the estates helpdesk and the finance system, and is checked by Kenneth Tan before it goes to the tenant. Any balancing charge or credit is applied to the next service charge invoice. Where a lease does not allow a balancing charge, the difference is carried forward to the following year's budget.

## Bank guarantees and security deposits

| Type | Held by | Recorded in | Release |
|------|---------|-------------|---------|
| Bank guarantee | Issuing bank | Lease administration system tenancy record and the finance system | On lease expiry once the final service charge reconciliation is agreed |
| Security deposit | Aldercombe Estates Services Ltd | Lease administration system tenancy record and the finance system | On lease expiry, less any agreed deductions |

Deposits are lodged with the Aldercombe Estates Services Ltd bank account. Nurul Hassan maintains the deposits log and matches each deposit to the tenancy record. When a lease ends, the deposit is released only after the tenant has confirmed the return of keys and the final reconciliation is agreed. Any deduction is raised as a charge on the tenancy and the tenant is sent the breakdown.

## Tenancy register

The tenancy register is the lease administration system tenancy list. It shows the tenant, the property, the lease start and end dates, the rent, the review and break dates, and the current deposit or guarantee. The register is the source for the lease events diary and for the monthly tenancy summary sent to Suresh Pillai.

After each lease event, the register is updated as follows:

| Event | Update |
|-------|--------|
| New lease signed | Create the tenancy record, attach the signed lease, set the rent and dates, record the deposit or guarantee |
| Rent review completed | Update the rent, confirm the review date has passed |
| Renewal completed | Extend the end date, update the rent and any new terms |
| Break exercised | Set the end date to the break date |
| Lease ended | Mark the tenancy closed, confirm the deposit or guarantee is released or being processed |

Aoife Brennan is responsible for the register being current. If a lease event has happened but the register has not been updated, escalate to Marcus Lindahl. The monthly report is pulled from the lease administration system by Sienna Okafor and circulated to the team ahead of the monthly property meeting.
