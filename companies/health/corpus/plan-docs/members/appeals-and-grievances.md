# Appeals and grievances

**Owner:** Member services  
**Last edited by:** Dara Hartmann, Appeals Coordinator

## Purpose

This document defines how members and providers may appeal an adverse benefit determination (a denial, reduction, or termination of coverage) and how grievances are handled. It applies to all Kellsbrook Health Plan lines of business unless a section says otherwise.

## Scope

Appeals and grievances for Kellsbrook Health Plan members. This includes employer-group plans, individual plans, and {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}}. It does not cover provider disputes about claims payment or network status — those go through Provider services per the provider manual.

## Definitions

1. **Adverse benefit determination** — a denial, reduction, or termination of, or a failure to provide or make payment (in whole or in part) for, a benefit. Includes denials based on medical necessity, experimental/investigational status, and plan exclusions or limitations.

2. **Appeal** — a request for review of an adverse benefit determination.

3. **Grievance** — any complaint or dispute expressing dissatisfaction with the administration of the plan, other than an adverse benefit determination. Examples: wait times, staff conduct, facility cleanliness, failure to return calls.

4. **Expedited appeal** — an appeal that is processed on an accelerated timeline because waiting for a standard review could seriously jeopardize the member's life, health, or ability to regain maximum function, or in the case of a member receiving urgent care, subject the member to severe pain that cannot be adequately managed without the care or treatment being requested.

## Filing an appeal

1.1 Members (or their authorized representative) may file an appeal orally or in writing. Oral appeals must be confirmed in writing within the timeframes in 1.2 if the member has not already submitted written confirmation.

1.2 Appeals must be filed within 180 calendar days of the date of the adverse benefit determination notice. For {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}}, the filing window is 60 calendar days from the date of the notice.

1.3 Appeals should be submitted to Member services by phone, through the member portal, or by mail to the appeals address on the adverse benefit determination notice. Appeals received by any Kellsbrook Health employee or department must be forwarded to Member services the same working day they are received.

1.4 Member services representatives (including Ava Kowalski, Chidi Costa, and others on the team) log all appeals in the claims adjudication system and assign an appeal reference number. The member receives written acknowledgment within 5 calendar days of receipt.

## Standard appeals

2.1 Standard appeals are reviewed within 30 calendar days of receipt. For {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}}, standard appeals are reviewed within 30 calendar days for pre-service requests and within 60 calendar days for post-service requests.

2.2 The Appeals Coordinator (currently Dara Hartmann) assigns the appeal to a clinical reviewer or an administrative reviewer per section 3.

2.3 The member and, where applicable, the treating provider are given a reasonable opportunity to present evidence and testimony. The member may request copies of all documents, records, and other information relevant to the appeal.

2.4 The member is notified of the decision in writing within the timeframes in 2.1. The notice includes the rationale for the decision and, if adverse, the member's right to external review and how to request it.

## Who reviews what

3.1 **Administrative appeals** — Appeals based on plan interpretation, eligibility, timely filing, or coverage rules are reviewed by the Member services Manager (Connor Dahl) or a designee on the Member services team. These include denials based on plan exclusions, benefit limits, or coordination of benefits.

3.2 **Clinical appeals** — Appeals involving medical necessity, experimental/investigational status, or level-of-care decisions are reviewed by a clinical reviewer. Clinical reviewers are licensed physicians or other appropriate health care professionals with clinical expertise in the field in question. They are not the same individual who made the original adverse benefit determination, and they are not subordinate to that individual.

3.3 The clinical reviewer may consult with the treating provider or request additional clinical information. If additional information is needed, the member is notified and the review timeline is extended by up to 14 calendar days provided the extension is in the member's interest.

## Expedited appeals

4.1 Expedited appeals may be requested by the member or the treating provider. Requests may be made orally or in writing.

4.2 The Appeals Coordinator or a Member services Team Lead (Sofia Meyer) determines whether the request meets the definition in section 1. If approved, the appeal is reviewed and decided within 72 hours of receipt. The member and treating provider are notified of the decision orally, followed by written notice within 48 hours of the oral notice.

4.3 If an expedited appeal is denied, the appeal proceeds as a standard appeal and the member is notified of the expedited review decision and the standard timeline.

4.4 For {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}}, expedited appeals follow the same 72-hour timeline. The plan must also accept oral requests for expedited appeals and may require the member to submit written confirmation within the timeframes specified in the notice.

## External review

5.1 If a standard or expedited appeal is denied, the member may request external review by an independent review organization. The plan must grant the request if the member meets the external review criteria in the applicable state or federal law.

5.2 For fully insured employer-group and individual plans, external review is available when the appeal involves medical necessity or experimental/investigational treatment and the member meets the applicable state external review criteria. The external review request must be filed within 4 months of the adverse appeal decision.

5.3 For {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}}, external review is available through the {{PUBLIC_HEALTH_INSURANCE_1}} independent review entity. The request must be filed within 60 calendar days of the adverse appeal decision. The independent review entity decides the appeal within timeframes set by {{PUBLIC_HEALTH_INSURANCE_1}}.

## Grievances

6.1 Grievances may be filed orally or in writing through Member services, the member portal, or the patient experience team. Grievances are logged in the claims adjudication system with a grievance reference number.

6.2 Grievances are acknowledged within 5 calendar days of receipt. A resolution is issued within 30 calendar days of receipt. If more time is needed, the member is notified in writing with a revised resolution date.

6.3 Grievances involving quality of care or patient safety are referred to the Quality & patient safety team (Joy Balogun's team) for review. Grievances involving provider conduct are referred to Provider services.

6.4 Grievance resolution does not affect the member's right to file an appeal for an adverse benefit determination, nor does filing a grievance extend any appeal deadline.

## {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}} differences

7.1 {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}} appeals follow the {{PUBLIC_HEALTH_INSURANCE_1}} appeals process. The plan's internal appeals process in sections 1–4 applies as the first level of appeal (reconsideration). The timeframes in section 2 apply.

7.2 The plan must ensure that the clinical reviewer for a {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}} appeal has not previously been involved in the original determination and is not subordinate to the individual who made it.

7.3 {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}} members have the right to file a grievance about the quality of care or the plan's administration. Grievances are handled per section 6.

7.4 If a {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}} member requests an expedited appeal and the plan denies the request, the plan must automatically forward the request to the independent review entity for a determination. The member does not need to file a separate request.

7.5 The plan must continue benefits for a {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}} member who is receiving ongoing coverage for a service that is being reduced or terminated, if the member requests continuation of benefits before the reduction or termination takes effect, until the appeal is decided. The member may be required to pay for the continued services if the appeal is ultimately denied.

## Recordkeeping

8.1 The Member services team maintains a complete record of each appeal and grievance in the claims adjudication system, including all correspondence, evidence considered, reviewer notes, and the final decision.

8.2 Records are retained per the retention schedule in the records office. Access to appeal and grievance records is limited to Member services, the Appeals Coordinator, and compliance staff.
