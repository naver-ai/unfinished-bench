# Getting access to systems

**Owner:** Identity & access  
**Last edited by:** Sara Nunez, Identity & Access Manager  
**Applies to:** All Kessendorf employees, contractors, and agency staff

---

## Requesting access

1. Open the **service desk portal** and choose **Access request** from the request catalogue.
2. Select the system(s) you need. The form shows the current owner for each system:
   - **ERP**, **CPQ tool** — Business applications (ERP & finance systems)
   - **MES** — Factory IT
   - **PLM**, **QMS** — Engineering & Quality
   - **CMMS**, **RMA portal** — Plant operations
   - **HR system**, **time-recording terminals**, **expenses app** — HR
   - **Supplier portal** — Purchasing
   - Anything not listed — ask in **#it-help**
3. Add your line manager as **approver 1**. The form will route the request to the system owner for **approver 2** automatically — do not chase them separately.
4. Submit. You will get a ticket number in the portal and by email. The ticket stays with you until the access is granted.

## What happens next

- Identity & access processes requests in the order they arrive, usually within two working days. Urgent requests (same-day) go through **Sara Nunez** or **Chloe Schmidt** directly.
- You will receive an email when the access is live. For most systems that is immediate; for the **ERP** and **HR system** it takes effect from the next working day.
- If your manager or the system owner rejects the request, the ticket is returned to you with their comment. Reply on the ticket if you need to discuss it.

## Temporary access for contractors

- Contractors get access through the same form, with **contract end date** filled in on the request.
- Access expires automatically on that date. If the contract is extended, the requester submits a new request referencing the old ticket number.
- Contractors do **not** get access to the **consolidation tool**, the **HR system** or the **contract register**.

## Shared shop-floor terminals

- Terminals in the plants (Reutlingen, Telford, Greenville) use a shared login. No individual accounts are created for these.
- The shared credentials are held by the plant supervisor on shift. Do not save them to personal devices.
- If a terminal shows an account that looks unfamiliar, report it through **#it-help** — do not log in.

## Moving teams

- When you move teams, your line manager or the new team lead raises a **mover request** in the service desk portal.
- Access to systems you no longer need is removed at the end of the month following the move. You keep access to systems that remain relevant — the system owner decides.
- Your **ERP** and **MES** roles are reviewed as part of the move. The new team lead confirms which roles you need; anything not confirmed is removed.

## If it goes wrong

- Access not working after the confirmation email: check the ticket first, then contact **#it-help** with the ticket number.
- Wrong access granted (too much or too little): reply on the ticket or tell your manager. Identity & access will correct it the same day.
- You think someone has access they should not have — report it via the **service desk portal** (category *Security*) or directly to **Sara Nunez**. Do not mention it in open channels.
