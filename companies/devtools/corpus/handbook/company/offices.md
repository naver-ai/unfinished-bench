# Offices

*Owner: Workplace & facilities · Last edited by Olivier Nunez*

## Lisbon HQ

**Which floors do we have?**
We occupy floors 2–6 of the building on Avenida da Liberdade. Floor 2 is mostly meeting rooms and the reception area, floors 3–5 are team areas, and floor 6 holds the kitchen, the quiet room, and the roof terrace access.

**How does badge access work?**
Your badge opens the external door, the lift lobby, and the floors you're assigned to. If you need access to a floor you don't normally work on (for example, the finance folks visiting engineering), ask in #it-help and the IT & workplace tech team will sort it. Badges are tied to your HR record, so when you leave or change roles the access updates automatically.

**Where do I store my bike?**
There's a bike room in the basement. You need to badge in, and the door closes behind you. There are racks and a pump. If the room is full, there's a second rack on floor 2 near the lifts — ask Olivier Nunez for the combination to that one.

**Is there lunch nearby?**
Plenty. Most people go out — there's a decent set of options within a five-minute walk. The kitchen has fridges and microwaves if you bring your own. The coffee machine on floor 4 is the least busy one, by the way.

**Where do visitors sign in?**
At reception on floor 2. The receptionist will call the person they're visiting, and the visitor gets a badge that only opens the ground floor and floor 2. If you're expecting a guest, tell them to arrive at the main entrance and mention your name.

## Portland office

**How big is the Portland office?**
It's much smaller — one floor in a shared building in the Pearl District. We share the building with a couple of other companies, so the main entrance is public. Our suite is on the third floor.

**Is there parking?**
The building has an underground garage, but spaces are limited and not guaranteed. There's a waiting list — talk to Kasia Laurent to get on it. Street parking is metered, and there's a pay lot two blocks over. If you drive in, factor in time to find a spot.

**Do visitors sign in?**
They should check in at the building's front desk, then come up to our floor. Our own badge readers are on the suite doors. There's no separate reception area — visitors wait in the small lounge near the entrance until their host comes out.

## Desks and rooms

**Can I book a desk?**
Both offices have a mix of assigned and unassigned desks. The unassigned ones are first-come, first-served. For the Lisbon HQ, the desk map is pinned in #lisbon-office. In Portland, just take a free desk — there's no booking system for those.

**How do I book a meeting room?**
Room bookings go through the calendar system. Each room is named after a Portuguese railway station in Lisbon and a Pacific Northwest peak in Portland. If a room is double-booked, the earlier booking wins — ping the other party and sort it out. For the small rooms, there's a 25-minute buffer between bookings so people can clear out.

**What if I need a quiet space?**
Lisbon has the quiet room on floor 6 — no calls, no meetings, just heads-down work. There's a similar room in Portland. Book them like any other room, but the booking note should say "quiet room" so people know not to schedule calls there.

## Working from the other office

**I'm visiting the other office. What do I need to know?**
If you're from Lisbon visiting Portland, or vice versa, you don't need to do anything special. Badge access is handled by the Workplace team before you arrive — just mention it in #it-help or to Hannah Delgado and you'll be set. Visitors from Lisbon should bring their Gantline laptop; the dock standards are the same in both offices. If you need a monitor or a second screen, grab one from the spare equipment shelf — there's one in each office.

**Is there a desk for me?**
There's a small visitor desk area in each office, but if you're staying for more than a day or two, let the Workplace team know and they'll find you a proper spot. In Portland, the visitor area is near the window; in Lisbon, it's on floor 3.

**Can I use the other office's address for deliveries?**
Sure — the reception desk accepts personal packages in both offices. Just put your name and the office address, and you'll get a ping when it's arrived.
