## Moving between buildings

**Reutlingen:** Buildings A and B are connected by a covered walkway. Building C is across the yard — allow five minutes. The test lab and calibration lab are access-controlled; if you need to escort someone there, contact the lab team through the lab calendar.

**Telford:** Units 1 and 2 are connected. Unit 3 is a short walk across the car park. The distribution centre (Unit 2) has its own goods-in entrance on the north side; use the pedestrian entrance on the south side unless you're with a delivery.

**Greenville:** The plant and service workshop are one building; the office block is separate. The walk between them takes about three minutes.

## Entry and access

- All three sites require a staff badge for entry outside normal working hours. If you've forgotten your badge, report to reception.
- Visitors must be signed in at reception and collect a visitor pass. The host is responsible for the visitor while on site.
- Deliveries go to the goods-in area at each site, not to reception. See `ops-docs/warehouse/goods-in.md` for the goods-in process.

## Site-specific notes

- **Reutlingen:** The calibration lab is in Building C and has its own booking calendar. The test lab is also in Building C — see `ops-docs/test-lab/lab-overview.md` for what's in there.
- **Telford:** The drives line runs in Unit 1; the distribution centre in Unit 2 handles UK and export outbound. See `ops-docs/plants/telford-drives-line.md` for line details.
- **Greenville:** The Americas service centre shares the workshop with the plant. Service technicians check in at the workshop office at the start of each shift.

For maintenance issues at any site, submit a request via the CMMS or the service desk portal. For anything urgent outside working hours, call the site's on-call number (listed on the intranet under Facilities).
