# Working time and shifts

**Owner:** People & HR · **Last edited by:** Claudia Rehm

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do I find the working-time works agreement?**

The works agreement is published on the intranet under People & HR → Policies → Working time. The works council page also links to it. If you have questions after reading it, contact your HR Business Partner.

**How does flexitime work for office staff?**

Office staff at Reutlingen (and office-based roles at Telford and Greenville) record their working time at the time-recording terminals on arrival and departure. You build up a personal time account. Core hours apply on working days; outside core hours you may flex your start and end times provided your duties allow it. The time account has a maximum balance – when you reach it, your manager will ask you to take time off to bring it down.

**What about breaks and rest periods?**

Breaks are not recorded automatically; you enter them at the terminal when you take them. The legal minimum rest periods apply to everyone. If you work a shift that crosses a meal time, the break is agreed with your team lead and entered in the same way.

**How do the plant shifts work?**

Shift patterns are posted on the shift boards in each plant; the times are not listed here because they change with the production calendar.

- **Reutlingen main plant:** early and late shifts, with a night shift on the lines that run continuously.
- **Telford drives plant:** early and late shifts on the drives assembly line; the distribution centre operates on its own two-shift pattern.
- **Greenville plant:** three-shift rotation covering early, late and night. The rotation schedule is on the shift board and in the MES shift calendar.

Shift premiums and any shift-related allowances are set out in the works agreement for each site. If you are moving to a shift pattern for the first time, your manager will arrange for you to shadow the outgoing shift for a few days.

**Can I swap shifts with a colleague?**

Yes, with your team lead's approval. Both shifts must remain fully staffed for the work scheduled. Record the swap in the time-recording terminal or tell the shift lead so the attendance list is correct. Swaps across sites are not possible.

**What counts as overtime?**

Overtime is time worked beyond your contractual weekly hours that your manager has asked you to work in advance. Unplanned extra time at the end of a shift is not automatically overtime – discuss it with your manager if it happens regularly.

**How is overtime compensated?**

Overtime is credited to your time account as time off in lieu, unless the works agreement for your site allows payment. Payment for overtime is the exception and needs approval from the plant manager or, for Reutlingen office staff, from the department head. Time off in lieu should be taken within the following month where the workload allows.

**What happens to my time account at the end of the financial year?**

The year-end balance is reconciled against the works agreement limits. Balances above the maximum are normally paid out or, with your agreement, carried over if there is a documented peak in workload. Your HR Business Partner can tell you the current carry-over rules – they have changed a couple of times.

**I work from home sometimes – how do I record that?**

Home-working days are recorded in the same way: clock in and out at the terminal when you start and finish, or use the time-recording web page if you are not on site. The web page is listed under the time-recording section on the intranet. If you cannot reach the terminal or the web page, note your times and give them to your manager so they can enter them for you.

**Who do I talk to if my time account looks wrong?**

Your manager can correct entries on request. For anything that cannot be corrected at that level, raise it with your HR Business Partner. Time-recording data is kept for the period required by the works agreement.

**What about shift workers – do they have flexitime?**

No. Shift workers are on fixed shift patterns and record their attendance on the shift sign-in sheet or the MES terminal, depending on the plant. Their working time is the shift as posted.

**Where can I find the shift calendar for the next quarter?**

The shift calendar is maintained by the planning team and published on the intranet under Planning → Shift calendars. The Greenville rotation is also shown on the MES shift board. If you need a printed copy, your team lead can print one from the same page.
