# Patient complaints and compliments

**Owner:** Patient experience  
**Last edited by:** Hannah Muller (Patient Advocate)  
**Applies to:** All Kellsbrook Health sites including the Cork medical centre

## How complaints arrive

Complaints come in through several routes. All of them get logged the same way once they reach us.

1. **Phone** – callers reach the Patient Experience line or are transferred from the main hospital switchboard, clinic reception, or the Health Plan member services line. If you take the call, capture the patient’s name, MRN or member ID if they have one, and a short summary of the concern.
2. **Patient portal** – patients submit through the portal’s "Contact us" form under the Patient Experience category. These land in the Patient Experience shared mailbox automatically.
3. **Letters** – mailed to any Kellsbrook address. Mailroom forwards envelopes marked "Patient Experience" or "Complaint" to our office unopened.
4. **In person** – a patient or family member raises a concern at a clinic, hospital desk, or the Cork medical centre reception. Staff should offer to call Patient Experience with the patient present, or take the patient's contact details and let us call them back.

## Logging

Every complaint is logged in the safety event reporting system using the **Patient Experience** category, not the clinical safety category. If the complaint also involves a clinical concern, log it once and note the clinical aspect in the description – do not submit two separate entries.

Required fields:

- Date and time received
- Patient name and MRN (or member ID for Health Plan complaints)
- Site where the concern happened
- Route (phone, portal, letter, in person)
- Summary of the concern
- Staff member(s) involved, if named
- Whether the patient is asking for anything specific (apology, explanation, financial adjustment, etc.)

Assign the complaint to the **site patient experience lead** – South Bend, Valparaiso, Kalamazoo, or the Cork medical centre manager for Cork complaints. Health Plan complaints are assigned to the member services supervisor instead.

Set the follow-up date for **five working days** from receipt. The system sends a reminder if it is still open.

## Response targets

- **Acknowledgement** – within **two working days** of receipt. A phone call is fine if the patient is happy to talk; otherwise send a letter or portal message.
- **Full response** – within **ten working days** of receipt. If we cannot resolve it in that window, contact the patient, explain why, and give a date for the next update.
- **Resolution** – most complaints close within **fifteen working days**. Anything open past thirty working days is reviewed at the monthly Patient Experience team meeting.

## When a complaint becomes a formal grievance

If the patient is not satisfied with our response and asks to escalate, or if the complaint involves:

- alleged harm to a patient
- discrimination or harassment
- a breach of privacy or confidentiality
- a regulatory matter (e.g. a {{HEALTH_QUALITY_REGULATOR|code}} or {{MEDICAL_REGULATOR}} concern at Cork, or a state complaint in Indiana or Michigan)

…move it to **formal grievance** status in the system. This changes the workflow:

- The site patient experience lead reviews the file and response to date.
- The Director of Patient Experience (Eleni Lee) is notified automatically.
- The grievance gets a separate reference number and a **twenty working day** response target.
- Grievances are reported quarterly to the Quality & Patient Safety committee.

Do not attempt to resolve a formal grievance informally once it has been logged. Stop routine correspondence and let the grievance owner handle it.

## Passing on compliments

Compliments are logged as a separate type in the same system. They do not get a response target and do not go through the complaint workflow.

- When a compliment names a specific staff member, forward it to their manager and to the staff member themselves.
- If the compliment is about a team or a whole clinic, send it to the site manager to share.
- Monthly, the Patient Experience team posts an anonymised round-up of compliments to **#patient-experience** so people can see when their colleagues have been recognised.
- Staff are allowed to include compliments in their annual review discussion; managers can view the compliment log for their team.

## If it goes wrong

- **Missed acknowledgement target** – the system flags it. Send the acknowledgement the same day and note the delay in the record. Do not change the original received date.
- **Patient calls back because they never heard from us** – apologise, check the record for what was promised, and make sure the next step happens same day.
- **Complaint logged to the wrong site** – reassign it in the system; the original assignee gets a notification. Do not delete the entry.
- **Patient asks for a copy of everything we hold about them** – that is a release of information request, not a complaint. Point them to the ROI process and keep the complaint record open.
- **Staff member named in a complaint disputes the account** – do not edit the complaint text. Add a separate note with their response and keep both versions.
