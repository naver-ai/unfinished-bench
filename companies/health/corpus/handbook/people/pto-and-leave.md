# PTO and leave

**Owner:** Human resources  
**Last edited by:** Kofi Klein (Benefits Specialist)  
**Applies to:** All Kellsbrook Health staff, with separate provisions for Cork-based staff in section 8.

## Purpose and scope

This policy covers paid time off (PTO), sick time, protected leave, bereavement and jury duty for Kellsbrook Health employees. It applies to all staff regardless of site, except where section 8 explicitly calls out different rules for the Cork medical centre and the European trials office. The timekeeping system is the system of record for all leave requests and balances.

---

## 1. PTO accrual

1.1 PTO accrues on a pay-period basis and is calculated from your hire date. Accrual rates are based on your length of service and your scheduled FTE.

1.2 Full-time staff (1.0 FTE) accrue at the following rates per pay period:

| Length of service | Hours per pay period | Annual equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 years | 6.16 | 160 hours (20 days) |
| 5–9 years | 7.39 | 192 hours (24 days) |
| 10+ years | 8.62 | 224 hours (28 days) |

1.3 Staff scheduled at less than 1.0 FTE accrue PTO proportionally. For example, a 0.6 FTE employee with 0–4 years of service accrues 3.70 hours per pay period. The timekeeping system calculates this automatically; you do not need to track it yourself.

1.4 Length of service is measured from your most recent hire date. If you leave Kellsbrook and are rehired within twelve months, prior service counts toward your accrual tier. Otherwise, service restarts.

1.5 PTO balances carry over from one fiscal year to the next up to a maximum of 320 hours. Any balance above 320 hours at the end of the fiscal year (end of June) is forfeited. The timekeeping system flags accounts approaching the cap in the pay period before the fiscal year closes, and managers are expected to encourage staff to schedule time off.

1.6 New hires may use PTO after 90 days of employment, except for staff transferring from another Kellsbrook entity (e.g., from Kellsbrook Health Plan to the hospital), who may use PTO immediately.

## 2. Requesting PTO

2.1 Request PTO in the timekeeping system at least two weeks before the requested start date, unless your manager has approved a shorter lead time. For requests longer than five consecutive working days, submit at least four weeks in advance.

2.2 Your manager approves or denies the request in the timekeeping system. If a request is denied, the reason must be recorded in the system. Denials are typically due to staffing coverage, scheduled go-lives, or more than the allowed number of staff off on the same day.

2.3 The following departments have blackout periods during which PTO requires director approval: Revenue cycle during the first week of each month, the claims operations teams during the last three working days of each month, and laboratory services during accreditation survey windows. These blackout periods are posted in the respective channels in chat.

2.4 If you are unable to report to work due to illness, follow section 3. Do not submit those days as PTO.

## 3. Sick time

3.1 Full-time staff receive 10 sick days per fiscal year. Part-time staff receive sick time prorated to their FTE. Sick time does not accrue; it is available at the start of each fiscal year and does not carry over.

3.2 Use sick time for your own illness, medical appointments that cannot be scheduled outside working hours, or to care for a dependent who is ill. Sick time is not a substitute for PTO and may not be used for vacation, personal errands, or travel.

3.3 Report sick time in the timekeeping system no later than the start of your shift. If you are too unwell to log in, have a colleague or your manager enter it on your behalf. For absences longer than three consecutive working days, you may be asked to provide documentation from your clinician.

3.4 Managers may ask for a clinician's note for any pattern of sick time use that suggests the time is being used for purposes other than those in 3.2. This is handled case by case and is not automatic.

## 4. FMLA and other protected leave

4.1 The Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) provides up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave in a rolling 12-month period for qualifying reasons, including your own serious health condition, the birth or placement of a child, or caring for a family member with a serious health condition. Kellsbrook Health also recognises state-level leave protections for Indiana and Michigan staff; where state law provides greater coverage, state law applies.

4.2 To request FMLA, open a case in the HR system at least 30 days before the leave is expected to start, or as soon as practicable if the need is unforeseen. HR Operations (Mikael Aoki's team) will send you the certification paperwork within five working days.

4.3 While on FMLA, you may use accrued PTO and sick time to keep receiving pay. The timekeeping system will show the leave as FMLA with the applicable paid time attached.

4.4 Short-term disability is separate from FMLA and is administered through the benefits team. See the benefits overview in the handbook for how short-term disability interacts with FMLA.

## 5. Bereavement

5.1 Staff receive up to five consecutive working days of paid bereavement leave for the death of a spouse or domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or the corresponding in-laws. Up to three working days for the death of an aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, or close friend.

5.2 Bereavement leave is paid at your regular base rate and does not reduce PTO or sick balances. If you need additional time beyond what is listed, use PTO or request unpaid leave through the HR system.

5.3 For staff in Cork, bereavement is covered under the compassionate leave provisions of Irish employment law; see section 8.

## 6. Jury duty

6.1 Staff summoned for jury duty receive paid time off for the days they are required to serve. Notify your manager as soon as you receive the summons and submit the summons notice in the timekeeping system with your jury duty request.

6.2 You are not required to use PTO for jury duty. If the court releases you before the end of a working day, you are expected to return to work for the remainder of your shift.

6.3 If you are excused from service, return to work on the day you are excused unless your manager has approved otherwise.

## 7. Reporting and tracking

7.1 All leave balances and requests are visible in the timekeeping system. Managers and HR have access to team-level views; individual balances are visible to you and to HR staff with a legitimate need.

7.2 Payroll uses the timekeeping system's approved leave records to calculate pay. It is your responsibility to ensure your requests are approved before the pay period closes. Unapproved absences are treated as unexcused and may be subject to the attendance policy in the code of conduct.

7.3 Questions about accrual balances or the PTO carry-over cap go to the HR Operations team. Questions about how leave interacts with benefits (e.g., FMLA and short-term disability) go to the benefits team.

## 8. Cork staff

8.1 Staff employed by the Cork entity are not covered by the PTO accrual tables in section 1. Instead, annual leave accrues under the Organisation of Working Time Act at a rate of 8% of hours worked, subject to a minimum of four weeks per leave year. The leave year runs from the date of hire and annually thereafter.

8.2 Cork staff must take at least two of their four annual leave weeks in the leave year. The remaining leave may be carried over into the next leave year if agreed with your manager, but no more than one week may be carried over.

8.3 Public holidays in the Republic of Ireland are paid days off for Cork staff. The schedule is published in the Cork office channel at the start of each calendar year. If a public holiday falls on a day you would normally work, you receive the day off. If you are required to work on a public holiday, you receive a day off in lieu within the following month.

8.4 Sick leave for Cork staff follows the company sick time provisions in section 3, except that the entitlement is 10 days per leave year and is calculated on a pro-rata basis for part-time staff. The timekeeping system handles this automatically for Cork-based employees.

8.5 Protected leave for Cork staff (maternity, paternity, parental, carer's leave) is governed by Irish law. Contact HR Operations in Cork for the applicable forms and timelines.

8.6 Bereavement for Cork staff follows the compassionate leave provisions in Irish employment law. These are administered by HR Operations in Cork; the standard five-day provision in section 5 does not apply.
