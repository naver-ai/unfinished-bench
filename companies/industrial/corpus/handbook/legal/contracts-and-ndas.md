# Contracts and NDAs

**Owner:** Legal  
**Last edited by:** Hugo Martins (Senior Counsel)

---

## Do I need to use the standard NDA?

Yes, unless Legal has agreed otherwise. The standard NDA is the only one we sign with customers, suppliers, and partners. It covers mutual confidentiality, two-year survival, and the usual exclusions. If the other side insists on their own paper, send it to Legal before you sign anything. We see a lot of US-style NDAs that have warranty and indemnity language in them — those are not acceptable.

## Where do I find the standard NDA?

On the intranet under Legal > Contracts. There is one version for Germany (English and German side by side), one for the UK, and one for the US. They are substantively the same but reference the correct governing law and dispute resolution for each entity. If you work with a customer in another country, ask Legal — we will tell you which version to use.

## How do I request a customer or supplier contract?

Use the contract request form on the intranet (Legal > Contract request). The form asks for the counterparty name, the entity (Kessendorf GmbH, Kessendorf UK Ltd, or Kessendorf Inc.), the product or service, the value if known, and the date you need it. Attach any commercial terms you have already agreed with the other side.

The form goes to the contract mailbox and Delphine triages it. She will assign it to the right person. For standard terms, we normally turn these around within a few working days. If you need something faster, put that in the form and flag it to Delphine directly — but please do not wait until the day before the customer expects a signed copy.

## Which templates exist?

For customers, the starting point is our standard terms of sale (see `legal-docs/contracts/customer-terms.md`). For larger or more complex deals, we have a separate framework agreement template. For suppliers, there is a purchase terms template and an NDA. Service agreements (for example, for subcontracted calibration work or maintenance services) have their own template.

If the deal does not fit a template, Legal will draft from scratch. That takes longer, so please give us as much notice as possible.

## Where do signed contracts go?

Everything signed goes into the contract register. The register is the system of record for all contracts, amendments, and NDAs. If you sign something and it is not in the register, it does not exist as far as Finance or the auditors are concerned.

For NDAs, we also keep a copy in the Legal share under `NDAs`. The register entry includes the counterparty, the signatories, the effective date, and the expiration or renewal date. Legal gets a task to chase renewals.

## Can I sign on behalf of Kessendorf?

Only if you have signing authority. The commercial sign-off matrix is on the intranet under Legal > Signing authority. For customers, that is normally the sales director for the region. For suppliers, it is Procurement. If you are not sure, ask Legal before you sign anything. A contract signed without authority is still binding on the company in most jurisdictions — that is a problem we would rather avoid.

## What about amendments and renewals?

Amendments go through the same process as new contracts: request form, Legal review, contract register. Renewals are handled by the register reminders. If a contract auto-renews, that is usually stated in the terms — make sure you know whether your contract has a notice period.

## Do I need Legal review for every NDA?

For the standard NDA with no changes, you do not need a review. Sign it, send a copy to the other side, and upload the signed copy to the register. If the other side signs first, just make sure we sign it and it is dated correctly.

If the other side has made any changes — even a changed address or a different governing law — send it to Legal. Do not try to fix it yourself.
