# Who does what

This page gives a quick map of the teams at Gantline and what each one owns. For names, reporting lines and team rosters, check the HR system rather than this page — people move around more often than we manage to update this.

## Departments

| Department | Owns | Main channel |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | Everything we build and run — see the next table | #engineering |
| Product management & Design | Roadmap, specs, user research, the design system | #engineering |
| Sales | New business, expansions, deal desk (with finance) | #sales |
| Customer success | Enterprise onboarding, renewals, CS operations | #customer-success |
| Developer support | The forum, email and the enterprise priority queue | #support |
| Marketing | Growth & lifecycle, content, developer relations, communications | #growth, #content, #devrel |
| Finance | Accounting & payroll, billing operations, month-end close | #finance |
| People | People operations, talent acquisition, workplace & facilities | #people-ops |
| Legal | Contracts, customer paper, open source questions | #contracts-help |

## Engineering

| Group | Teams | Main channel |
|---|---|---|
| Developer productivity | Release engineering & CI, Build infrastructure, Quality engineering | #dev-productivity |
| Infrastructure | Site reliability, Lab & hypervisors, Network engineering, IT & workplace tech | #sre |
| Product engineering | Hosted workspaces, Hosted runners, CLI & SDKs, Public API, Search & indexing, Editor, Mobile apps, Billing engineering | #engineering |
| Data platform | The data warehouse and the pipelines that feed it | #engineering |
| Security | Product security, Identity & access | #security |

Most product teams also have their own channel (#cli, #editor, #mobile, #search, #api-platform, #identity); the build and lab folks are in #buildfarm and #lab-infra. Quality engineering still shows up as "QA" in a lot of older pages — same team.

A few things that span teams: the feature flag service and the metrics dashboards are run by Site reliability, and the data warehouse by Data platform, with finance and growth as its heaviest users. If you are not sure who to ask, start in the department channel and someone will point you the right way.

For anything about a specific person's role, their manager, or who sits on which team, use the HR system — the org chart there is the source of truth. This page is only a rough guide.
