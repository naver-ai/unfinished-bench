# Submitting a new study to the IRB

**Owner:** Research ethics & IRB office  
**Last edited by:** Piotr Zielinski (Deputy Ethics Liaison)  
**Applies to:** All Kellsbrook Research Institute staff, investigators, and study teams preparing a new submission

---

## Purpose

This document walks through preparing and submitting a new study to the Kellsbrook Research Ethics & IRB office through the eIRB system. It covers the three review levels, document requirements, turnaround expectations, reliance agreements, and the board's meeting cycle.

If you are new to the eIRB system, start with the eIRB training module in the learning management system before you begin a submission.

---

## Before you start

1. Confirm whether your study needs IRB review at all. Activities that are not research (quality improvement, operational audits, public health practice) do not go through the eIRB. If you are unsure, email the Research ethics & IRB office inbox and Clara or Ingrid will help you route it.
2. Make sure all key personnel listed on the submission have current GCP training on file. See `research-docs/training/gcp-training.md` for the refresher schedule.
3. If the study involves the Cork medical centre, check with the European trials office early — they have separate document requirements for sites in Ireland.

---

## Creating the submission in the eIRB system

4. Log in to the eIRB system with your Kellsbrook credentials. The system is reachable from the research institute network or via the VPN client.
5. From the dashboard, select **New Submission** and choose **Initial Review – New Study**.
6. Enter the study's full title, short title, and the principal investigator's name. The PI must have a profile in the system already. If they do not, ask the PI to complete their profile before you continue.
7. Attach the protocol version and date. The filename should follow the convention: `protocol_v1.0_YYYYMMDD.docx`. The version number and date on the file must match what you enter in the protocol metadata fields.
8. Complete the application form section by section. The form saves as you go; you can leave and return at any time. Pay attention to the following fields:
   - **Funding source** — select the internal cost centre for institute-funded work, or enter the sponsor name and award number for external grants. The pre-award team tracks this separately; see `research-docs/grants/pre-award.md`.
   - **Sites** — list every site where research procedures will happen. For Kalamazoo, South Bend, Valparaiso, or Cork, select the site from the dropdown. For external sites, add them manually.
   - **Study team** — add all personnel who will interact with participants or handle identifiable data. The system checks training records automatically and flags anyone missing GCP.
9. Upload the required documents in the **Documents** tab. See the list below for what goes in.
10. Once the application form is complete and all documents are uploaded, run the **Completeness Check**. The system will list any missing fields or attachments.
11. Submit the package by clicking **Submit to IRB Office**. You will get a confirmation email with the submission reference number (e.g., KH-IRB-0143). Keep this number for all correspondence.

---

## Documents to include

The document checklist varies by review level. The eIRB system shows the required list when you select the review level, but the common items are:

- Protocol (with version number and date)
- Investigator's brochure (for drug or device studies)
- Consent form(s) — one per participant group, with version numbers
- HIPAA authorization form (if applicable)
- Recruitment materials (flyers, emails, scripts)
- Questionnaires or survey instruments
- Data collection forms (case report forms for interventional studies)
- Site-specific documents for external sites (local context, translations, local lab certifications)
- For studies at Cork: a copy of the GP practice's letter of access for the study team

Upload documents as PDF unless the sponsor requires otherwise. The system accepts most common formats, but PDF is the safest for the eIRB archive.

## Review levels

### Exempt review (administrative)

12. Studies that qualify for exempt review (minimal risk, no identifiable data, certain categories of educational or survey research) are reviewed by the IRB office staff. Piotr or Nico will issue the exemption determination.
13. Exempt determinations are usually issued within **3–5 working days** of a complete submission.
14. Note: exempt does not mean no submission — you still need the eIRB record and a determination letter.

### Expedited review

15. Expedited review is for studies that are no more than minimal risk and fit one of the expedited categories listed in the federal regulations. The eIRB system will prompt you to select the category.
16. Expedited studies are reviewed by one or two board members designated by Mateo. You may receive questions from the reviewer; respond through the eIRB system's **Correspondence** tab.
17. Turnaround for expedited review is typically **10–15 working days** from the date the office confirms the submission is complete.

### Full board review

18. Studies that are more than minimal risk, involve vulnerable populations, or do not qualify for expedited review go to the full board.
19. Full board submissions are assigned to the next available monthly meeting. See the meeting cycle section below.
20. You will receive the board's decision (approve, approve with modifications, or defer) through the eIRB system, usually within **2 working days after the meeting**.
21. If the board requests modifications, you will have a specified number of days to respond (typically 30). The revised package goes back to the full board or to the expedited reviewer depending on the nature of the changes.

---

## The board's monthly meeting cycle

22. The full board meets **once per month** on the second working day of the month, except when that falls near a US public holiday, in which case the meeting moves to the next working day.
23. The meeting alternates between the Kalamazoo campus conference room and a videoconference link for members at the South Bend and Valparaiso campuses. Cork members attend by videoconference during their normal working day.
24. Submissions must be complete in the eIRB system **one week before the meeting** to be placed on the agenda. Anything submitted after that cutoff rolls to the next month's meeting.
25. The meeting agenda and study packages are distributed to board members **three working days before the meeting**. You cannot add materials after that point.
26. If you need a study reviewed sooner than the next monthly meeting allows, contact Mateo directly. He can consider a special expedited review for certain studies, but this is not guaranteed and should not be planned around.

## Reliance agreements

27. Kellsbrook has reliance agreements with several external IRBs. Under these agreements, Kellsbrook may rely on an external IRB's review for multi-site studies, or an external institution may rely on Kellsbrook's IRB.
28. To request a reliance arrangement, select **Reliance / External IRB** as the review type when creating your submission in the eIRB system.
29. The office will confirm whether the external institution has an active agreement on file. Ingrid maintains the reliance agreement registry — check with her before you assume a specific institution is covered.
30. For reliance submissions, the external IRB's approval letter and the full approval package must be uploaded to the eIRB record. Kellsbrook still requires a local submission for institutional purposes even when the ethics review is done elsewhere.
31. Turnaround for reliance submissions depends on the external IRB's schedule. The office does not control their timeline, so plan accordingly.
32. For studies where Kellsbrook is the reviewing IRB for external sites, the external site's local context documents must be included in the submission. Piotr handles the communication with external site contacts.

## If it goes wrong

- **Submission is returned as incomplete.** The office will send a list of what is missing through the eIRB system. Fix the items and resubmit. The completeness review restarts, so do not submit until you have everything.
- **You cannot find a document template.** Check the eIRB system's **Forms Library** first. If it is not there, email the IRB office — do not reuse a template from another institution without checking.
- **The system errors out on upload.** Try a different browser or clear your cache. If it persists, file a ticket with IT and mention the eIRB system in the description. The submission is not lost; the system saves draft versions.
- **You realize you submitted the wrong protocol version.** Withdraw the submission using the **Withdraw** action and submit again with the correct version. Do not try to overwrite the attachment after submission — the audit trail does not like it.
- **A study team member's training has lapsed.** The system will flag this at submission. The person can complete the refresher through the learning management system; the office can place the submission on hold briefly while they finish.
- **You need to change the PI after submission.** Contact Nico in the IRB office. PI changes after submission require a formal amendment and may go back to the board depending on the review level.
- **The board defers your study.** Read the deferral letter carefully. It will state what the board needs clarified. You may respond in writing or request to attend the next meeting briefly to answer questions. Clara coordinates these requests.
