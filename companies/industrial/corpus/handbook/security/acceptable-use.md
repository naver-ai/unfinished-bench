# Acceptable use

**Owner:** Security (IT & product)
**Last edited by:** Laszlo Moreau

## Purpose

This policy sets out the acceptable use of Kessendorf systems, accounts and data. It applies to all employees, contractors and temporary staff at all Kessendorf sites (Reutlingen, Telford, Greenville) and to anyone else granted access to Kessendorf systems.

## Scope

Covers company-provided devices, personal devices used for work, company accounts, network access, data handling, removable media and generative tools.

## Policy

1. **General use**
   1.1 Company systems and accounts are provided for business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted where it does not interfere with your work, consume disproportionate resources, or risk company data.
   1.2 Accounts are issued to named individuals. Do not share passwords, badges or tokens. Account holders are responsible for all activity under their credentials.
   1.3 Report suspected compromise of any company account immediately via the service desk portal or per the incident reporting guidance.

2. **Monitoring and privacy**
   2.1 Kessendorf operates monitoring on company systems in line with the works council agreement. This includes network traffic, system logs, email and file access records.
   2.2 Employees are reminded of this monitoring when they log on and annually in the security awareness module.
   2.3 Do not use company systems with an expectation of privacy beyond what the works council agreement provides. Personal files may be stored only in the designated personal drive space.

3. **Devices and access**
   3.1 Company laptops and phones are configured by IT. Do not disable or bypass security controls, including disk encryption, screen lock or the mobile device management profile.
   3.2 Connect only to approved networks. Use the VPN when working from any network other than a Kessendorf site.
   3.3 Request access to systems through the access request process in the handbook. Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed periodically by system owners.

4. **Removable media**
   4.1 On the Reutlingen, Telford and Greenville shop floors, USB sticks and other removable media are not permitted on production or test equipment. Use the plant or lab file shares for moving data.
   4.2 Encrypted USB sticks may be used in office areas. Unencrypted sticks must not be used for company data.
   4.3 If you find unlabelled removable media on the shop floor, hand it to the shift lead or the test lab team. Do not plug it in.

5. **Data handling**
   5.1 Handle customer drawings, BOMs and firmware according to their classification. Export-controlled items follow the shipping and classification guidance in legal-docs.
   5.2 Do not copy company data to personal devices or personal cloud services.
   5.3 Company data must stay on company systems unless a specific process says otherwise (for example, sending drawings to a supplier through the supplier portal).

6. **Generative tools**
   6.1 Only generative tools approved by Security may be used for work. The current list is on the intranet under Security / Approved tools.
   6.2 Do not paste drawings, BOMs, source code, customer names or any data from the PLM, ERP or MES into a generative tool.
   6.3 Output from approved tools must be reviewed before it is used in any deliverable. The tool's output is not authoritative for engineering or calibration decisions.

7. **Software and internet**
   7.1 Install only software approved by IT. If you need a tool that is not listed, raise a service desk request.
   7.2 Do not download or run peer-to-peer file sharing software on company devices.
   7.3 Company devices may be used for personal browsing within reason, but not for anything illegal or that would embarrass Kessendorf if seen on the network logs.

8. **Non-compliance**
   8.1 Breaches of this policy may result in restricted access, disciplinary action or referral to the works council and HR as appropriate.
   8.2 If you are unsure whether an action is permitted, ask the Security team before you do it.
