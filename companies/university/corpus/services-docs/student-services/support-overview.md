# Student Services at a glance

Student Services brings together the teams that support students through casework, wellbeing, disability, money advice, accommodation and residence life. We work across the Hollins Hill and City campuses, and alongside colleagues at Aldercombe University Malaysia where their local services mirror ours.

## Teams and what they cover

| Team | What they cover | Where they sit |
|---|---|---|
| Student Casework | Complex or cross-cutting cases, complaints signposting, fitness to study panels, support for students in crisis | Hollins Hill main building |
| Wellbeing & Disability Support | Mental health and wellbeing appointments, disability advisors, exam access arrangements, study support plans | Wellbeing Centre, City campus; satellite rooms on Hollins Hill |
| Student Money | Budgeting advice, hardship fund applications, help with fee queries, benefit and funding questions | Student Hub, City campus |
| Accommodation Office | Halls allocations, private sector housing advice, contract checks, the accommodation booking system | Student Hub, City campus |
| Residence Life | Wardens and resident assistants at our halls, community events, out-of-hours support for residents | Each hall on Hollins Hill and City campus |

The team leads meet weekly to go through anything that needs more than one service. If you are not sure who a student should speak to, send the referral through the student casework system and the duty coordinator will route it.

## Referring a student

Staff can refer a student to any of the teams above using the student casework system. The system is the single place for referrals, case notes and outcomes, and it is linked to the student record system so the student's details come through automatically.

- Use the referral form for wellbeing, disability, money or accommodation requests.
- Use the urgent contact route for immediate risk concerns, and call the duty coordinator on the internal number if it cannot wait.
- Students can self-refer through the Student Hub, and their request lands in the same queue.

The Student Services file store holds supporting documents that should not go into the casework system, such as medical evidence or correspondence with third parties. Case notes and decisions stay in the casework system.

## The student casework system

The casework system is the record of all ongoing support cases. Every case has a named caseworker, and the duty coordinator reviews open cases each working day. Cases are closed when the student's issue is resolved or the student has disengaged and the caseworker has made reasonable attempts to re-engage them.

If you need access to the casework system, request it through the IT service desk portal with your manager's approval. Read access is granted to Student Services staff and to colleagues in Student Records and the Academic Registry who deal with extenuating circumstances. Write access is restricted to Student Services teams.
