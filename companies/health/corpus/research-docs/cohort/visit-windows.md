# Cohort visit windows and scheduling

**Owner:** Cohort studies  
**Last edited by:** Rafael Lund, Cohort Studies Program Manager  
**Applies to:** Metabolic cohort waves at South Bend, Valparaiso, Kalamazoo, and Cork sites

## Visit schedule

The metabolic cohort runs in waves. Each participant is assigned to a wave at enrolment and keeps that wave for all follow-up visits. Baseline visits are scheduled at enrolment; follow-up visits are scheduled from the previous visit date, not from enrolment.

| Visit | Window | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Baseline | Day 0 | Must occur within 14 days of consent. |
| Follow-up 1 | Day 180 ± 30 | |
| Follow-up 2 | Day 360 ± 45 | |
| Follow-up 3 | Day 540 ± 45 | |
| Follow-up 4 | Day 720 ± 60 | |
| Follow-up 5 | Day 900 ± 60 | Final scheduled visit for the current protocol. |

## Booking clinic rooms

- South Bend and Kalamazoo: book exam rooms through the scheduling and registration system. Request the "metabolic cohort" room type so the room is set up with the study scales and the phlebotomy kit.
- Valparaiso: cohort visits run in the health plan office conference rooms on the second floor. Book through the same system; flag the booking with "cohort" in the subject line so facilities knows to stage the equipment.
- Cork: book through the medical centre's own scheduler. The GP-led centre has two rooms that can take a cohort visit; the front desk team knows which ones. If neither is free, talk to the practice administrator before moving the visit to a room that is not on the list.

## Missed visits

A visit counts as missed if the participant does not attend and the visit is not rescheduled within the visit window. If the participant cancels or no-shows, the study coordinator makes two attempts to reschedule by phone or through the patient app. If both attempts are unsuccessful, the visit is marked missed in the CTMS and the participant is notified through the patient portal.

Participants who miss two consecutive scheduled visits are moved to the "follow-up by phone" track. They remain in the cohort but are not booked for further in-person visits unless they ask to come back. The phone track uses the same visit windows; the calls are logged in the CTMS and the data go into the same study record.

## Equipment and lab work

- Fasting blood draws are done at the visit. The lab information system has a specific order set for cohort draws; use it rather than building a new order each time.
- If a draw is missed because the phlebotomist is not available, the visit is not automatically missed. Reschedule the draw within 5 working days and note it in the CTMS.
- Cork sends its samples to the Kalamazoo lab via the usual courier. Use the research specimen label pack so the samples are routed to the research institute's lab rather than the clinical lab.

## Old notes

The original wave 1 schedule used a 90-day window for all follow-up visits. That was tightened when the protocol change went through; the table above reflects the current windows. Wave 1 participants who are still active were re-mapped to the current windows at their last visit, so do not go back and adjust old visit dates.

For questions about the visit schedule, contact the cohort studies team in #research-institute. For room bookings outside normal clinic hours, talk to the site coordinator before booking — the Valparaiso conference rooms and the Cork rooms both need advance notice for after-hours access.
