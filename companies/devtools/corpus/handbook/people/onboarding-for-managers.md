# Onboarding checklist (managers)

Owner: People operations · Last edited by Pavel Novacek

This checklist is for managers who are onboarding a new team member. Work through it with the People Partner for your team. The new hire also gets the employee onboarding guide; this page covers the manager-specific actions.

## Before day one

- [ ] Confirm the start date in the HR system. If the date moves, tell the HRIS team so the record is correct before the first payroll run.
- [ ] Order the laptop through the laptop portal at least five working days before the start date. Standard image, no admin rights. If the role needs something else ({{DESKTOP_OS_3}}, extra RAM), note it in the request.
- [ ] Check that the new hire's access request covers the systems their team uses. The access form in the handbook (`handbook/it/getting-access.md`) lists the standard sets. Add anything else the role needs (for example a specific runner pool or a service account) in the comments.
- [ ] Arrange a buddy from the team, ideally someone who started within the last year. Introduce them by email before day one so the new hire has a point of contact.
- [ ] Ask the buddy to book a coffee chat for the first afternoon.
- [ ] If the new hire is in the other office, set up a video intro with your counterpart there. Don't leave it to the new hire to find people.

## Day one

- [ ] Be at the office (or on video) for the first hour. Walk them through the team's work board and current priorities.
- [ ] Make sure they can get into the building and have a desk. If they're in Lisbon, point them at the office map on the intranet.
- [ ] Check that the HR system record is complete: bank details, tax forms, emergency contact. If anything is missing, flag it to People operations that day.
- [ ] Introduce them to the team in #general and in the team channel. Include their role and what they'll be working on first.
- [ ] Ask them to set up their local environment using the gnt CLI bootstrap command. If it fails, escalate to #it-help rather than letting them rabbit-hole on setup.

## First two weeks

- [ ] Book a 30-minute session to walk through Gantline Cloud from a user's perspective: create a workspace, push a branch, run a build, open a PR. Do it together, not as a demo.
- [ ] Arrange a half-day shadowing session with support. The new hire sits with a support engineer and watches how tickets come in and get triaged. This is about product feel, not process. Ask the support manager to pick a shift with a mix of ticket types.
- [ ] Make sure the first PR lands by the end of the first week, even if it's small (docs, a label, a comment in code). Assign it yourself, and review it the same day it's opened.
- [ ] Introduce them to someone in the other office (Portland or Lisbon) who does similar work. The point is to start building the cross-office network early.
- [ ] Check in at the end of week one and week two. Ask what's confusing, not just whether things are fine. Write down what they say; it's often a sign of a docs or process gap.
- [ ] By the end of week two, confirm they can run the team's usual workflow end to end: checkout, build, test, commit, push, open a PR, get it reviewed and merged.
- [ ] If they're in a customer-facing role, add them to the relevant customer calls by week two, even as a listener. Don't wait for them to feel "ready".

## After onboarding

- [ ] Close out the checklist in the HR system once the two-week mark passes. People operations uses the completion date to trigger the first check-in survey.
- [ ] Tell the buddy when the onboarding period is over so they can wrap up.
- [ ] If anything in this checklist was unclear or missing, file a ticket to People operations. We keep this page current, but it only works if people tell us what's wrong.
