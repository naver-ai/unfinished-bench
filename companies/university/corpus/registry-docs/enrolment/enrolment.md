# Enrolment

**Owner:** Admissions, Enrolment & Student Hub  
**Last edited by:** Marcus Bell (Student Hub Coordinator)

---

## Online enrolment

- All new and returning students complete enrolment online through the student record system before the start of the academic year.
- Enrolment opens in phases: new students first, then returning students. The dates are published on the Student Hub pages and in the VLE.
- Students must complete all steps in one session where possible: personal details, module selections (where applicable), fee declaration, and photo upload.
- The photo must meet the ID photo rules (plain background, no filters, facing forward). The system rejects photos that are too dark or too small.
- Once enrolment is submitted, the record moves to "enrolled" status and the student card is produced.
- Students who do not enrol by the first teaching day are marked as non-enrolled. The Student Hub team follows up with them through their university email.

## ID photos and student cards

- Cards are printed on the Hollins Hill campus and sent to City campus, Kuala Lumpur, and Kestrel Vale Academy as needed.
- Kuala Lumpur campus cards are printed locally. Hafiz Rahim's team handles those — send any KL card queries through them.
- Replacement cards cost £15 and are ordered through the Student Hub counter or the student record system. Lost or stolen cards should be reported so the old card can be deactivated.
- Staff cards are a separate process — see the IT service desk portal for those.

## Right-to-study checks

- International students must have their passport and visa (or share code) checked before enrolment can be completed.
- Checks are done by Admissions, Enrolment & Student Hub staff. For students at the Kuala Lumpur campus, the Malaysia campus team does the checks and records the outcome in the student record system.
- If a student cannot provide the required documents at enrolment, they are placed on a deferred enrolment status. They have a limited window to provide the documents — the team contacts them by email and phone.
- Students on deferred status cannot attend classes or access the VLE until the check is cleared.
- Where a visa decision is pending, the team works with the student to confirm whether they can enrol remotely or need to wait. Remote enrolment is only possible where the visa route allows it.

## Late enrolment

- Students who miss the main enrolment window can still enrol up to the end of the second week of teaching, but they must contact the Student Hub first.
- Late enrolment requires a short form explaining the delay. Hamza Nakamura's team reviews these and approves or declines within one working day.
- Students enrolling late may miss the first round of card printing — they can collect a temporary card from the Student Hub or from the Malaysia campus office.
- After the second week of teaching, late enrolment is only possible with approval from Miriam Novak's team.

## Enrolment helpdesk

- During welcome week, the Student Hub runs an enrolment helpdesk on both Hollins Hill and City campuses. The hours are published in the welcome week schedule.
- The helpdesk handles photo rejections, card collection, and any enrolment errors that students can't resolve online.
- For Kuala Lumpur campus, the Malaysia campus team runs a similar helpdesk using the same guidance.
- Staff with an enrolment question can ask the Hub team in #registry during helpdesk hours. Staff outside the Student Hub who get enrolment questions from students should point them to the Hub rather than trying to resolve issues directly.
- Any systemic issues (for example, a form not loading, or a payment step failing) should be logged with the IT service desk portal, and a note put in #student-systems so the team knows it's being looked at.
