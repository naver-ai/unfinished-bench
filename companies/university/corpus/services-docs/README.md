# services-docs

Runbooks and operational notes for professional services teams. Owned by IT Services; last edited by Priti Desai.

## What lives here

Folders by team, for teams that don't keep their own repository:

- `student-services/` — Student Services casework, Student Money, complaints, support plans
- `it/` — IT service desk, infrastructure on-call, change management, backups
- `student-systems/` — student record system integrations, the Student Services file store
- `development/` — Development & Alumni Relations gift processing and appeals
- `kestrel-vale/` — Kestrel Vale Academy office routines and whole-school messaging

## Conventions

- Pages name their owning team at the top where they can; if one doesn't, ask in that team's channel.
- Anything that touches finance or registry processes links to `finance-docs/` or `registry-docs/` rather than duplicating them.
- Change requests go through the IT service desk portal; estates-related changes go through the estates helpdesk.

## Notes

- The Kuala Lumpur campus property team keeps their own docs under `estates-docs/commercial/`; anything tenancy-related comes through them.
- The academy's school information system is covered in `kestrel-vale/`.
- If a runbook is missing, check the chat archives in `#it-services` or `#student-systems` before raising a ticket.
