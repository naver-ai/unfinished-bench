# Payroll calendar

**Owner:** Payroll & payables  
**Last edited by:** Nico Holm (Payroll & payables)

This page covers the monthly payroll runs for Ireland and Lithuania, the semi-monthly US run, and the cut-offs that feed them. If you are changing salary, allowances, or one-off payments, the cut-offs below are what matter.

## Run schedule

| Payroll | Pay period | Cut-off for changes | Pay date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ireland | 1st to last day of the month | 2 working days before run day | Last working day of the month |
| Lithuania | 1st to last day of the month | 2 working days before run day | 10th working day of the following month |
| US (first run) | 1st to 15th | 3 working days before run day | 20th of the month |
| US (second run) | 16th to last day | 3 working days before run day | 5th of the following month |

All cut-offs are at mid-afternoon Irish time. Changes submitted after the cut-off will go into the following run; the payroll team cannot accept late entries once the run has been prepared in the payroll system.

## What runs through payroll

- **Base salary and allowances** — set up by Payroll & payables on the HR system. Managers submit changes via the HR system; they must be approved by the time of the cut-off.
- **Expenses** — approved expenses in the expenses tool are pulled into the payroll run on the cut-off day. Any expense still pending approval at that point waits for the next run.
- **Bonuses and one-off payments** — these are entered directly by Payroll & payables. The requesting manager should send the approved amount to the payroll team at least 2 working days before the cut-off so it can be checked and loaded.

## Payslips

Payslips are available in the HR system on pay day. Ireland and Lithuania payslips are published in the morning; US payslips are published by end of day.

If you spot an error on a payslip, raise it in the HR system and let Payroll & payables know in chat. Corrections are processed in the next run unless the error is on the employer side — in that case the team will agree a correction date with you.

## Notes

- The US run uses the same HR system as Ireland and Lithuania; pay frequency is set per entity, so no manual re-keying is needed between the runs.
- Bank files for the Ireland and Lithuania runs are generated from the payroll system and sent to the treasury workstation by Payroll & payables. The treasury team releases them on pay day.
- If a pay date falls on a non-working day, the payment is made on the preceding working day for Ireland and Lithuania, and on the following working day for the US.
- New joiners and leavers: make sure the HR system record is complete before the cut-off. Leaver final payments follow the same cut-off; a leaver whose record is completed after the cut-off will be paid in the following run.
