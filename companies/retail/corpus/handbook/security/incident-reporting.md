# Reporting a security incident

Owner: Security engineering · Last edited by Kasia Vogel

Anything that looks like an intrusion, account compromise, data exposure, or malware on a Mossgrove device or system counts — including at stores or with wholesale partners. If you're unsure, report it anyway.

**To report:**

1. Go to the `#security` channel in chat and post what you saw, or
2. Raise a ticket via the helpdesk with the category `Security incident`, or
3. If you can't reach either, call the IT service desk and ask for the Security engineer on call.

Include: what happened, when you noticed, which system or device, and anything you clicked or ran. Screenshots help. Don't wait for more detail — early reports are easier to handle.

**What happens next:**

4. Security engineering triages the report. You'll get an acknowledgement in the same channel or ticket within one working day.
5. A responder may ask you for logs, files, or to leave a device untouched. Cooperate and don't modify anything further.
6. If it involves customer data, the Data engineering team and Legal get pulled in as needed. The incident process in `eng-docs/oncall/incident-process.md` applies for wider outages.

**Do not investigate yourself.** Don't run scans, don't poke at files, don't try to contain it. Doing so can destroy evidence and slow us down. If you've already done something, just tell us — it happens.

**If it goes wrong:**

- You can't access chat or the helpdesk? Call the service desk (phone number on the intranet home page).
- You're at a store and the POS looks compromised? Stop trading on that till, note the time, and call the service desk — the stores team has a separate escalation path.
- You shared something sensitive by mistake? Report it the same way; the quicker we know, the more options we have.
- If you're not sure it was an incident, say so. False alarms are fine.
