# VAT notes for non-finance staff

**Owner:** Financial accounting & reporting  
**Last edited by:** Carlos Ibarra

These notes cover the common VAT questions from faculties and professional services. They are not a full treatment — if your situation is not covered here, ask the tax accountant in Financial accounting & reporting before committing to anything.

## Education and research

Our core teaching and research activity is generally outside the scope of VAT. This includes tuition fees, most research grants, and the associated supplies that are integral to delivering them. Do not assume this extends to everything done in a faculty — see below.

## Business research contracts

Contracts that are not funded by a grant-making body but are bought by a commercial client (including most work for industry) can be taxable. The finance team will confirm the treatment when a contract is set up in the research grants system, so if you are costing a bid and are unsure, check with Research Finance before adding VAT to the costing tool.

## Catering

Catering supplied on both campuses is generally standard-rated, whether it is a staff lunch, a conference buffet, or a hospitality order for visitors. This includes Kestrel Vale Academy events where catering is charged to a third party. If catering is included as part of a wider package, the whole package can be treated differently — ask before pricing it.

## Conferences and lettings

Conference income from external delegates is standard-rated unless it is part of a qualifying education supply. Lettings of our rooms and halls are usually exempt, but this depends on the length of the let and whether services (such as cleaning) are included. The lettings team and Residential Lettings know the standard lines; if a lettings arrangement is at all unusual, flag it to Financial accounting & reporting.

## When to ask

Ask the tax accountant before you:

- sign a contract with a commercial client that includes research or consultancy
- commit to a conference package price for an external organiser
- set up a new type of letting or room hire arrangement
- get asked for a VAT invoice by a supplier or customer

## Loose ends

- The old expenses tool used to show a VAT flag that staff sometimes treated as authoritative; the current expenses tool does not, and the tax team has not yet written guidance on how to treat VAT on personal expense claims.
- The research grants system has a VAT indicator on awards; there are a few older awards where the indicator was never set, and Research Finance is aware.
- Guidance on catering for internal events is still in the staff handbook under event planning, but that section is due to be rewritten and the link from the Finance pages may not work.
