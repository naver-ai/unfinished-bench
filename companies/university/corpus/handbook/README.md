# Aldercombe staff handbook

Owner: People & Culture. Last edited by Morag Dunlop.

## What's in this repo

- University-wide policies and how-tos for staff: writing and brand, information security, data protection, FOI, IT access, expenses, travel, leave, annual review (PDR), new-starter guidance for managers, safeguarding, fire and first aid, and a few pages about how the university is structured (governance, who does what, the academic year).
- Each page names the team that owns it. If a page has no named owner, treat People & Culture as the owner.

## Proposing a change

- Submit a merge request against the page you want to change.
- For most pages, the owning team reviews and merges. For anything in `handbook/people/`, `handbook/safeguarding/`, or the top-level `handbook/university/` pages, send the merge request to People & Culture (Olga or Freya usually pick these up).
- If you are not sure who owns a page, ask in #people-and-culture or #general.

## Where the old intranet pages went

- The wiki was switched off at the start of this financial year. Anything that was still current was migrated into this repo; the rest was archived. If you cannot find something here, check the archive share or ask in #it-help.
