## Cork

The Cork office runs the GP-led medical centre (open to employer-group members and the public), the European trials office for the Research Institute, and part of IT operations (the records platform team, early-shift service desk coverage and some infrastructure). Their day-to-day questions go through #cork-office; the medical centre follows the same care-docs as the US clinics, and the trials office follows the same research-docs as Kalamazoo.

One note: the care-docs and plan-docs repos are the place to look for how a specific process actually runs. This page only tells you which team to ask. If you are new, the onboarding checklist in the handbook has a fuller walkthrough of the first few working days.
