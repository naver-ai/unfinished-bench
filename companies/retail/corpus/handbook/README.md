# Mossgrove handbook

Owner: People team. Last edited by Tomas Nakamura.

This repo holds the internal handbook for Mossgrove staff — policies, how-to guides, and reference material for day-to-day work across HQ, Dublin, Columbus, Wakefield, and the stores.

Covers:

- Company overview and org structure (`company/`)
- People policies: leave, expenses, performance, onboarding (`people/`)
- IT, security, and data handling (`it/`, `security/`)
- Legal and records retention (`legal/`)
- Comms guidance — press, social, customer-facing writing (`comms/`)

Proposing a change:

- Open a PR against this repo and tag the People team for review.
- If the change is specific to one area (e.g. expenses, security), tag the relevant doc owner listed in the file header.
- Small fixes (typos, broken links) can go straight in — no need to wait for a review cycle.

Older wiki pages:

- The legacy wiki was archived and the content migrated here over the past couple of quarters. If you cannot find a page that used to live on the wiki, check the `archive/` branch or ask in #general.
- Anything still on the wiki is considered stale — do not link to it from here.

For access to this repo, see `handbook/it/access-requests.md`.
