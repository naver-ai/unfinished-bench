# Talking to the media and posting on social

**Owner:** Marketing & communications  
**Last edited by:** Monica Feldkamp (Vice President, Marketing & Communications)

**What if a reporter calls me directly?**
Politely say you're not able to speak for Kellsbrook and take their contact details. Pass it to Marketing & communications right away — don't answer questions about patients, staffing, or operations, even "off the record."

**What if a reporter or camera crew turns up at a campus?**
Tell the front desk or reception to call Marketing & communications. Don't escort them around or let them film in public areas. If they're on a patient care floor, ask them to wait in the main lobby until Marketing & comms arrives.

**Can I post about Kellsbrook on my personal social accounts?**
Yes, but keep it general — no patient information, no internal system details, and no photos taken inside clinical areas or offices. If you're unsure whether something is okay to share, ask Marketing & comms first.

**Can I take photos of my team or my workspace?**
Photos of people need their consent. Photos of workspaces are fine if no screens with patient data are visible and no paper records are in frame. Don't take photos of patients or visitors at any of our sites.

**What about posting about my work in a patient-facing role?**
Don't mention specific patients, visits, or outcomes. Even without names, details like "I just helped a family in the ED" can identify someone. Keep posts about your role general.

**Who handles social media for Kellsbrook's official accounts?**
Marketing & communications. If you see something on an official account that looks wrong, flag it to Monica or Diego immediately. Don't reply or correct it yourself.

**What if I'm asked to comment on a Kellsbrook research study?**
Direct to Marketing & communications. The Research Institute has its own review process for study announcements, and Arjun coordinates those with the trials office in Cork.

**What if I see a colleague quoted in the news about Kellsbrook?**
Let Marketing & comms know. Only designated spokespeople (usually Monica or Douglas) are authorized to speak to the media on behalf of Kellsbrook.
