# Expenses

**Owner:** Accounting & payroll  
**Last edited by:** Gaurav Jensen  

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets out what Gantline reimburses, how to claim, and when payment lands. It applies to all employees in Lisbon and Portland, and to contractors who have been issued a company card. Claims must be submitted in the expenses tool within 60 days of the expense being incurred.

## Policy

1. **What can be claimed**
   1.1. Travel and accommodation booked for Gantline business, including flights, trains, hotels, and ground transport. Travel booked through our travel desk is settled directly; anything booked out-of-pocket is reimbursed.
   1.2. Conference and event tickets when attendance is approved by your line manager. Member discounts should be used where available.
   1.3. Home office kit up to €400 (or USD equivalent) per calendar year. This covers chairs, desks, monitors, and peripherals. It does not cover consumables like paper or ink.
   1.4. Meals while travelling on Gantline business, up to the per-diem rates published in the travel policy (see `handbook/people/travel.md`). Alcohol is not reimbursed.
   1.5. Business phone calls and data overages on personal devices while travelling, up to €50 per trip.

2. **Receipts**
   2.1. Receipts are required for every expense over €25 (or USD equivalent). For amounts below that, a line-item description in the expenses tool is sufficient.
   2.2. Upload the receipt to the expense line in the expenses tool. Paper receipts can be photographed; keep the original until the claim is paid.
   2.3. Hotel and flight receipts must show the breakdown of charges. A credit card slip alone is not a receipt.

3. **Approval**
   3.1. Your line manager approves claims in the expenses tool. Approval is required before the claim is submitted to Accounting & payroll.
   3.2. If your line manager is on leave, the approval defaults to their manager. The expenses tool shows who the current approver is.
   3.3. Claims over €2,000 are additionally reviewed by the Finance team (Miguel Antunes or Greta Richter) before payment.

4. **Company card vs out-of-pocket**
   4.1. Company cards are available from Accounting & payroll for employees who travel more than twice a quarter. Request one from Greta Richter.
   4.2. Company card charges are reconciled monthly in the expenses tool. You still need to attach receipts and get manager approval, but the reimbursement step is skipped.
   4.3. Out-of-pocket expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll run after approval. Payroll runs on the last working day of the month; claims approved after the payroll cut-off wait until the following run.
   4.4. Cash advances are not issued. If a trip requires a large outlay, use the company card or ask Accounting & payroll to prepay the hotel directly.

5. **Currency conversion**
   5.1. Expenses incurred in a foreign currency are converted at the rate published by the {{CENTRAL_BANK_2}} on the day the expense was incurred. The expenses tool applies this automatically.
   5.2. If the expenses tool does not have the rate, use the rate published on the {{CENTRAL_BANK_2|code}} site on that day and note it in the claim notes.
   5.3. Lisbon employees are reimbursed in euros; Portland employees in USD. The expenses tool converts at the same {{CENTRAL_BANK_2|code}} rate for the payroll run.
