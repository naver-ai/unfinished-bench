# Kessendorf handbook

Owner: People & HR · Last edited by Claudia Rehm

## What's in this repo

- **Company** — who we are, who does what, our sites (Reutlingen HQ, Telford drives plant, Greenville plant)
- **People** — onboarding, leave, working time, business travel, works council
- **IT & security** — access requests, devices, acceptable use, reporting incidents
- **Site safety** — basics for office staff
- **Legal** — contracts, NDAs, data protection, trade compliance, records

Each page names an owner in its header. For anything missing, ask in #general or open a ticket on the service desk portal.

## Proposing a change

- Merge requests go to the page owner listed in the header.
- If the owner is unclear, send it to People & HR and we'll route it.
- Small fixes (typos, dead links) don't need a ticket; anything structural does.

## Works agreements

The German versions of the works agreements (Betriebsvereinbarungen) live on the intranet under People & HR → Works council. The handbook only carries the short practical summaries.
