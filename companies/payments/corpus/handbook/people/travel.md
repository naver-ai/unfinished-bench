# Business travel

Needs a tidy-up after the Dublin office reorg — ping #dublin-office if something here looks wrong.

**Booking**
- All travel through the travel desk (request in #it-help). No self-booking except rail within Ireland.
- Travel desk handles flights, hotels, car hire. They have the Kilbarra corporate card details — do not put personal cards on bookings unless you're on a US trip (see below).
- Flights: Dublin–Vilnius direct on the morning {{AIRLINE_1}} and the evening return. Book those; anything else means a connection and a longer day.
- Vilnius office is a 10-minute walk from the airport — don't book a hotel near the airport for meetings there, stay in the centre.

**Hotels**
- Dublin: the usual two near the office — the {{HOTEL_4}} and the {{HOTEL_5}}. Both are walkable; the {{HOTEL_4}} has better breakfast.
- Vilnius: the {{HOTEL_1}} or the {{HOTEL_2}}. The {{HOTEL_2|short}} is closer to the office.
- Austin: the {{HOTEL_3}} is the preferred property. Downtown is fine if that's booked — just keep it south of the river.

**US trips**
- ESTA is required for Irish and Lithuanian passport holders. Check it's valid before you book — the travel desk will ask.
- US office is at 3rd and Congress. The sponsor bank is in the same building — sign in at the lobby desk and say you're visiting Kilbarra.
- Austin airport is a 20-minute drive in normal traffic; the office will arrange a car if you're seeing merchants.

**Insurance**
- The travel insurance card lives in the secrets manager under `corporate/travel-insurance`. Your phone wallet copy works everywhere the card does.
- Covers medical, cancellation, and lost luggage. It does not cover rental car excess — take the travel desk's car hire add-on if you're driving outside the city.

**Expenses**
- Claim through the expenses tool within 10 working days of returning. Attach the itinerary from the travel desk; the receipt OCR usually picks up the rest.
- Per-diem rates are in the expenses handbook page. Vilnius is cheaper than Dublin — don't claim Dublin rates for a Vilnius trip.
