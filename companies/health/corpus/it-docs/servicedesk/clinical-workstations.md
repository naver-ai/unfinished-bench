# Clinical workstations and badge tap

**Owner:** IT service desk  
**Last edited by:** Valentina Kato

## What is a clinical workstation?

A clinical workstation is any PC on wheels, wall mount, or fixed desk unit used for direct patient care activities. This includes the carts in the ED bays, the wall units in exam rooms, the nurse station desktops, and the workstations on wheels (WOWs) used for bedside documentation.

## How does badge tap sign-in work?

Badge tap uses the same proximity badge you use for door access. Tap the badge against the reader on the monitor or the side of the cart. The workstation signs you into the EHR.

## Why did I get someone else's session?

That is session roaming. If the previous person did not properly sign out, and you are in the same care area, the workstation may bring up their session. Tap your badge again and choose "Start new session." If that does not work, restart the workstation through the Start menu.

## My badge reader is not reading my badge

Check that the reader light is on. If it is off, check the USB connection at the back of the monitor or the side of the cart. If the light is on but nothing happens when you tap, try wiping the badge on your sleeve and tapping again. If it still fails, call the service desk and have the reader replaced. Do not swap readers between workstations yourself — the readers are tied to the workstation name in the interface engine.

## The cart says "no network" but I can get on the guest Wi-Fi

That means the cart's network drop is not connected. The WOWs in the South Bend ED and the Kalamazoo infusion bays use the wired drops at the bay entrances. Check the cable at the back of the cart and at the wall. If the cable looks fine, reboot the cart. If the problem persists, put a ticket in and we will roll a replacement cart out.

## A label printer is printing blank labels

Open the lid and check that the label roll has not run out. If there are labels, run the printer self-test (hold the feed button until two beeps). If the self-test prints blank, the ribbon is done — swap the ribbon from the spare cart drawer. If the self-test prints but EHR labels are still blank, it is a print driver issue. Try printing to another label printer on the same floor.

## When should I swap a cart?

Swap the cart if:
- The battery does not hold a charge through a full shift
- The screen flickers or has dead pixels
- The cart cannot keep a network connection after a reboot
- The badge reader has been replaced twice in a month
- The wheels are damaged and the cart cannot be moved safely

Do not swap a cart for a slow login or a stuck session. Those are software issues — reboot first, then call the service desk if it does not clear.

## What do I do with a cart that needs swapping?

Call the service desk and give them the cart asset tag (starts with WS-, located on the back of the monitor). They will arrange a swap. Put the old cart in the equipment room on your floor with the charger plugged in. The desktop support team will run diagnostics before it goes back into rotation.

## I am in the Cork medical centre and my badge does not work

Cork badges are issued locally. Tap your badge at the front desk reader first — that activates it for the clinical workstations. If it still does not work, the Cork IT team (part of the IT operations group) handles badge issues locally. Put a ticket in and select the Cork office location.

## Do I need a badge to sign into a workstation?

Yes, badge tap is required on all clinical workstations across the health system. This replaced the old password login for clinical areas. If you are at a workstation that still asks for a password, call the service desk — that workstation has not been updated. The only exception is the workstations in the research institute's European trials office, which are on a separate domain.

## What about the workstations at the Valparaiso health plan offices?

Those are not clinical workstations. They use normal password sign-in. Badge tap is only for patient care areas.

## My badge works for doors but not for the workstation

The door readers and the workstation readers use different registries. Call the service desk and we will sync your badge to the workstation registry. This happens sometimes after a badge reissue.

## Can I sign in with my phone instead of a badge?

No. The patient app on your personal phone is only for patient communication features. Workstation sign-in is badge tap only.
