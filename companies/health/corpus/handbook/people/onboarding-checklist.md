# Onboarding checklist (managers)

Owner: Human resources | Last edited by Kofi Klein (Benefits Specialist)

---

## Before day one

- [ ] Confirm start date and location with the hiring manager; verify which campus or office the new hire reports to (South Bend, Valparaiso, Kalamazoo, or Cork).
- [ ] Submit the new hire request in the HR system at least three working days before the start date so payroll, benefits, and the timekeeping system are ready.
- [ ] Request the badge through the same HR system entry; badge pickup location depends on campus (South Bend and Valparaiso at security, Kalamazoo at the reception desk, Cork at the medical centre front office).
- [ ] If the new hire needs parking, include that in the badge request. Valparaiso and Kalamazoo assign lots by building; South Bend uses the garage levels listed on the badge.
- [ ] Work with the hiring manager to complete the system access request form in tickets. Indicate which systems the role needs: EHR, patient portal, provider portal, member portal, claims adjudication system, scheduling and registration, lab information system, pharmacy system, or others as applicable.
- [ ] Send the access request to the relevant access coordinator. For clinical roles, include the clinical workstations badge-tap profile; for research roles, include the CTMS and EDC access request.
- [ ] Schedule the required compliance courses in the learning management system. Core courses: HIPAA basics, code of conduct, data classification, and phishing awareness. Clinical staff also get EHR privacy and security; research staff get GCP and human subjects training.
- [ ] Assign a buddy from the same team or a closely related team. The buddy should have been in the role for at least six months and should know who to introduce on the first day.
- [ ] Tell the hiring manager to block time on their calendar for the first-day welcome and the end-of-week check-in.

## First day

- [ ] New hire picks up badge and parking assignment before reporting to their desk.
- [ ] Verify the new hire can sign in to the VPN client and the company chat; if not, submit a ticket to #it-help.
- [ ] Walk through the handbook sections most relevant to the role: acceptable use, remote and hybrid work, and PTO and leave.
- [ ] Complete the first compliance course in the learning management system (HIPAA basics for most roles; GCP for research roles).
- [ ] Introduce the new hire to their buddy and schedule a lunch or coffee break together.
- [ ] Confirm the new hire is added to the correct chat channels for their team (for example, #claims-ops for claims roles, #research-institute for research roles, #cork-office for Cork staff).
- [ ] Verify the new hire can reach the patient portal or member portal if their role needs it; the access coordinator will confirm when the account is live.

## First two weeks

- [ ] Ensure all assigned compliance courses are completed. HR runs a report from the learning management system at the end of the second week; any incomplete courses are flagged to the manager.
- [ ] Schedule unit or department orientation with the team lead. This covers local workflows, shift handoff for clinical roles, and any site-specific procedures (for example, the Cork medical centre's own practice system and clinic sessions).
- [ ] Confirm the new hire has completed the relevant system-specific training: EHR training for clinical staff, claims adjudication basics for claims staff, CTMS and EDC for research coordinators.
- [ ] Ask the buddy to check in at least twice during the second week and to introduce the new hire to key contacts in other teams (provider services, member services, revenue cycle, data management).
- [ ] Review the onboarding progress in the HR system and close out any open tasks on the checklist.
- [ ] Hold the end-of-second-week check-in with the hiring manager to confirm the new hire is settled and to address any access or training gaps.

## Notes

- Cork-based new hires at the medical centre follow the same checklist; their system access requests go through the Cork IT operations team, and the buddy is assigned by the clinical lead.
- Research Institute new hires have an additional orientation with the research data management team covering study IDs and pseudonymisation.
- If a system access request is not fulfilled within the first two working days, the manager should follow up through tickets rather than asking the new hire to wait.
