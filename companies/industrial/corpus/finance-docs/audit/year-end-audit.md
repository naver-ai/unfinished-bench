## Stock counts

The auditors attend the year-end stock count at all three plants. The Reutlingen count covers finished motion controllers and torque transducers plus raw materials; Telford covers drives and components in the distribution centre; Greenville covers US-built direct-drive units.

Count attendance is arranged through the plant controllers. The auditors will want to see the count instructions ahead of the count itself — send those to the audit team via the portal, not by email.

## Lead schedules

The lead schedules for each entity live in the shared finance folder under `Finance / Audit / Year-end`. The file naming convention is `YYMMDD_leadschedule_[entity].xlsx`. Moses Adeyemi maintains the master copy and publishes updates to the folder after each monthly close. If you cannot find a schedule, ask Moses — do not recreate it from scratch.

## Auditor requests

All requests from the audit firm arrive through the firm's client portal (the firm rebranded the portal a while back; old bookmarks redirect but do not rely on them). Requests land with the entity lead and are copied to the Finance (UK) inbox. The portal assigns each request a reference — quote that reference in any email follow-up.

Responses should be uploaded to the portal rather than emailed. If a request needs input from another team (e.g. the test lab for calibration certificates or the warehouse for goods-in records), raise it with that team and upload their response once you have it.

For questions on what the auditors can see in the MES or the calibration database, contact the relevant plant controller first. The auditors have read-only access to the ERP and the calibration database for the duration of the audit; access is revoked when the final visit closes.
