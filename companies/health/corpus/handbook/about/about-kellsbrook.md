# About Kellsbrook Health

Kellsbrook Health System, Inc. traces its start to a community hospital in South Bend that served the surrounding neighborhoods for decades before the system took its current shape. In stages, the hospital merged with regional hospitals in Valparaiso and Kalamazoo, which brought together the three inpatient campuses that still anchor the system today. Around the same period, the organization launched Kellsbrook Health Plan to offer employer group coverage, individual plans, and later {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}} products across northern Indiana and southwest Michigan. The Kellsbrook Research Institute in Kalamazoo grew out of the hospital's existing clinical research activity and now operates as a separate arm of the system with its own trials office. The Cork medical centre opened more recently, initially to serve employer-group members at US companies' Irish sites, and has since opened to the general public.

Today, Kellsbrook Health runs three hospitals: the flagship hospital and system headquarters in South Bend, the Valparaiso hospital (which also houses the health plan's main offices), and the Kalamazoo hospital. Across the three campuses there are just over 600 licensed beds in total, with the South Bend campus the largest. The system's ambulatory network includes roughly 40 primary care, urgent care, and specialty clinic locations spread across the two states. The clinics range from small single-provider rural practices to larger multi-specialty sites, and they all share the same EHR, scheduling, and registration platforms. The patient portal and patient app sit on top of the same record, so patients see consistent information whether they are seen in South Bend or at a satellite clinic.

Kellsbrook Health Plan covers about 150,000 members across employer groups, individual plans, and {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}}. The plan's claims examiners and the claims payments desk work out of the Valparaiso office, as do the utilization management, member services and provider services teams; the special investigations unit sits in South Bend. The plan contracts with Kellsbrook's own providers as well as a broader network of independent hospitals and physician groups in the region, and credentialing runs through the system's credentialing system. Provider EFT and remittance are handled by the provider services group.

The Kellsbrook Research Institute in Kalamazoo runs a portfolio of industry-sponsored and investigator-initiated studies across therapeutic areas. The institute's European trials office in Cork supports a growing number of multinational studies. The institute works with the eIRB system, the CTMS, and EDC for study oversight, and the clinical trials office in Kalamazoo coordinates site activation and monitoring across the US and European sites. The institute has roughly 40 active studies at any given time, with a mix of interventional and observational work.

The Cork medical centre is a GP-led practice that operates as part of Kellsbrook Health's clinic network. It serves local patients as well as employer-group members from US companies with Irish sites. The centre keeps its records in an Irish GP practice system rather than the US EHR; a care summary is shared with the US teams when an employer-group member is treated on both sides of the Atlantic. A portion of IT operations is also based in Cork, including the records platform team and early-shift support for the US systems.

The system employs about 9,000 staff across all sites, with the largest concentrations at the South Bend campus and the Valparaiso offices. The Kalamazoo campus includes both the hospital and the research institute staff. The Cork office is the smallest site by headcount. The Executive team is based primarily in South Bend, with the Chief Financial Officer and health plan leadership spending significant time in Valparaiso. The Research Institute has its own leadership structure; its Chief Research Officer sits on the Executive team.
