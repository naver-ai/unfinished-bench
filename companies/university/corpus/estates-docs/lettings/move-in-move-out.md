# Move-in and move-out

**Owner:** Residential Lettings  
**Last edited by:** Marcus Hale (Property Administrator, Residential Lettings)

## Before move-in

- [ ] Confirm the room is ready via the estates helpdesk at least one working day before the tenancy start. Chase through the helpdesk if the room has not been marked clean.
- [ ] Raise any maintenance flags on the flat through the estates helpdesk before the resident arrives; do not leave them for the check-in.
- [ ] Prepare the check-in inventory and photos for the room. Photos should cover walls, flooring, windows, furniture, and any existing damage. Store them in the lettings folder on the shared drive.
- [ ] Check the fob is programmed for the correct building and floor before the resident collects it. Harbour Gate fobs are programmed by Estates & Facilities — request at least two working days ahead.
- [ ] For the visiting academic flats on Hollins Hill, confirm which entrance key set applies. The two townhouses share a street door; make sure the resident gets the right key.
- [ ] Deposit: register the deposit with the government-approved scheme within the statutory window. The deposit amount is in the tenancy agreement; if it does not match what the resident has paid, do not proceed — tell the Finance team via the lettings inbox.
- [ ] Email the resident the move-in checklist (from the lettings overview) along with their check-in time. Harbour Gate residents come to the front desk; visiting academics collect keys from the Hollins Hill campus services team.

## Check-in

- [ ] Resident signs the check-in inventory at the front desk (Harbour Gate) or with campus services (visiting academic flats). If they have not signed by the end of the first working day, email them once to ask them to confirm the inventory in the resident portal.
- [ ] Issue the key and fob. Record the fob number against the tenancy in the lettings system.
- [ ] If the resident reports any damage or marks at check-in that are not on the inventory, add them to the inventory and note the date. Do not edit the original photos — keep the new ones alongside.
- [ ] If the resident is not able to check in on the day (travel, illness), hold the keys at the front desk and agree a collection time. Marcus or the duty Lettings Officer must approve any key collection by someone other than the resident.

## During the tenancy

- [ ] Residents can report maintenance through the estates helpdesk. Do not log maintenance on their behalf unless they have asked the lettings office to do so.
- [ ] If a resident changes rooms mid-tenancy, treat it as a move-out and a move-in: full inventory and photos for both rooms, new deposit registration if the deposit moves with them, and a new check-in signature.
- [ ] If a resident loses a fob, log it with Estates & Facilities and charge the replacement fee per the tenancy agreement. Record the charge in the lettings system.

## Move-out

- [ ] Send the move-out reminder to the resident at least two working weeks before the tenancy end. Include the date, the room inspection date, and how to book the inspection.
- [ ] The end-of-tenancy inspection is done by Marcus or Yusuf. Use the check-in inventory and photos as the baseline. Take fresh photos of anything that is damaged, missing, or unusually worn.
- [ ] Agree the inspection findings with the resident before they hand back the keys where possible. If they are not present, email the findings within one working day and give them a set period to respond (the lettings overview has the standard wording).
- [ ] Keys and fobs: collect at the front desk (Harbour Gate) or campus services (visiting academic flats). Confirm the fob is returned and deactivate it the same day if possible, or the next working day at the latest.
- [ ] If keys are posted back, do not mark the tenancy as ended until they arrive and are checked. Note the postage date on the file.

## Deposit return

- [ ] Raise the deposit return request through the lettings system once the inspection is agreed. Include the agreed deductions, if any, and attach the inspection photos.
- [ ] Deductions should only cover damage beyond fair wear and tear, missing items from the inventory, or cleaning where the room was not left to the standard in the tenancy agreement.
- [ ] The Income team in Finance handles the repayment side; send the request to the lettings inbox addressed to them and copy Marcus. Do not send deposit refund requests directly to the scheme.
- [ ] If the resident disputes the deductions, do not release the disputed amount. Follow the scheme's dispute process and let the resident know in writing.
- [ ] For joint tenancies, the deposit return is split per the tenancy agreement. If the residents cannot agree a split, hold the return until they confirm in writing or the scheme directs otherwise.
- [ ] Keep the move-out photos and the signed inspection form on file for at least one full tenancy cycle after the deposit is returned.

## Notes

- The lettings system holds the tenancy, deposit and inspection records. The room booking system only shows availability for the visiting academic flats — update it when a stay is booked or ends; the two do not sync.
- For furnished rooms, the inventory lists every item in the room. If an item is removed or replaced during the tenancy (e.g. a broken desk), update the inventory and note the replacement — the resident should not be charged for it at move-out.
- Estates & Facilities handles any repairs needed between tenancies; raise them through the helpdesk as soon as the room is vacated, not after the next resident moves in.
