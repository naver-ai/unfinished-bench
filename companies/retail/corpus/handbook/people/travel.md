# Business travel

> Needs a tidy-up after the Dublin desk moved — ping #dublin-office if something here looks wrong.

**Booking**
- All travel via the travel desk in the expenses tool. Raise the request at least 5 working days ahead; urgent requests go to #finance-ops and they'll do their best.
- Rail is the default for any journey under 3 hours door-to-door. Short-haul flights need sign-off from the team lead.
- Low-emission vehicle hire is preferred for store visits outside rail networks. Wholesale visits to garden centres in the south-west often work better with a hire car from Bristol or Exeter.

**Hotel caps (per night, including taxes)**
- Leeds, Dublin, Columbus: £130 / €150 / $160
- London: £185
- Elsewhere in UK/IE: £110 / €125
- US cities other than Columbus: $180
- If the only options are above cap, book anyway and add a note — the cap is a guide, not a hard limit.

**Dublin–Leeds shuttle**
- The office runs a shuttle on Mondays and Thursdays. Book through the travel desk, not chat.
- Seats are limited — the tech hub usually fills them, so book at least a week ahead.
- Shuttle days sometimes shift around public holidays; check the calendar.
