# Trade fairs

**Owner:** Marketing & product management  
**Last edited by:** Sonja Haugland (Product Manager, TX series)

Trade fair season planning starts from the date of the show, counted backwards. Use the table below for the main milestones. For the two big automation fairs each year (spring and autumn), the planning window is about nine months. Smaller regional shows can run on a compressed version of the same timeline.

| Months before show | Action |
|---|---|
| 9 | Confirm which fairs we attend for the year. Get budget sign-off from the Executive board. |
| 8 | Book stand space. Reserve demo rigs and travel slots for the booth team. |
| 7 | Submit stand design to the organiser. Order booth furniture, lighting, and any special power or compressed-air connections. |
| 6 | Confirm demo rig list with Engineering. Start the firmware/configuration work for any new demos. |
| 5 | Order giveaways, brochures, and any new roll-up banners. Check stock in Reutlingen first. |
| 4 | Book hotel block for the booth crew. Publish the booth rota draft in the intranet. |
| 3 | Finalise demo rig shipping. Arrange freight to the show hall (via the Telford or Greenville DC if they supply the rig). |
| 2 | Booth rota freeze. Send lead-capture devices to the show. Test the CRM import from the capture tool. |
| 1 | Ship remaining materials. Confirm stand build and rig commissioning times with the organiser. |
| 0 | Show week. Booth crew follows the rota. |
| +1 | Return rigs. Check for damage and log any maintenance work via the CMMS. |
| +2 | Lead follow-up wave 1 (all leads). |
| +4 | Lead follow-up wave 2 (qualified leads only). Close out in the CRM. |

## Stand booking

Stand bookings go through Procurement using the supplier portal. The PO reference goes on the booking confirmation and on any freight paperwork. For the German shows (Hannover, Munich), the Reutlingen office manages the booking. For UK or US shows, the local site coordinates with the same procurement process.

Stand size depends on what we show: TX series torque transducers and laser encoders get a demo table; MC controllers and DU drives get a machine frame with a live motion axis. If we bring a full test rig, book a stand with a back wall and a dedicated power drop.

## Demo rigs

Demo rigs come from the test lab in Reutlingen or the drives line in Telford. The test lab team prepares the rigs. Each rig gets a label with the show name and a return date. Rigs that go to US shows travel via the Greenville plant and the Americas service team handles the return.

The lab calendar holds the build and test slots for fair rigs. Book rig time early — the test lab runs a tight schedule around the big shows.

## Booth rota

The rota lives in the intranet under the event page. Each shift is half a day. Aim for at least two product managers on the stand at any time, plus one engineer for the technical demos. Sales staff from the CSO team join for customer meetings — those go in the rota as "meeting slots" and are not for walk-up visitors.

Marketing & product management owns the rota. Changes after the freeze go through the team lead for the affected day.

## Lead capture

Leads are captured with the handheld scanners at the stand. At the end of each day, the scanners are synced to the CRM. The sync runs after the show hall closes; imported leads are visible by the time the booth crew starts the next day.

Each lead gets a tag for the show and a source field for the demo they were interested in. If someone specifically asked about the TX series torque transducers, set the product interest field to TX. Otherwise leave it blank for the follow-up team to fill.

## Follow-up

Wave 1 goes out within two working days after the show: a thank-you email with the relevant product page link and the contact for their region. Wave 2 goes to leads with an explicit request or a budget conversation: send the technical datasheet and offer a call.

All follow-up happens through the CRM. Do not send fair leads from a personal mailbox — the CRM tracks the sequence and the sales team needs the history.
