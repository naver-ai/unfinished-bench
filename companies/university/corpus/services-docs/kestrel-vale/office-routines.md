# Office routines

Owner: Kestrel Vale Academy office · Last edited by Sian Pryce (Extended-Day Coordinator)

## Morning registers

- Open the school day in the school information system before the bell; mark the register for each class as the class teacher calls it.
- Use the attendance codes exactly as set out in the school's attendance policy — do not use a generic late code for a child who is absent.
- If a child is not in when the register closes and no reason has been given, call the first contact on the pupil record. Log the call in the school information system.
- Forward any attendance queries from the local authority to the attendance lead in the office; do not reply directly.

## Late arrivals

- Children arriving after the register closes sign in at the office. Take them to their classroom only after the register has been marked.
- Record the arrival time in the school information system using the late code, and add a note if the parent gave a reason.
- If the same child is late more than twice in a fortnight, flag it to the class teacher and copy the attendance lead.

## Visitor sign-in and badges

- All visitors sign in on the tablet at the office entrance. Check they have a reason to be on site and that their host is expecting them.
- Issue a visitor badge. Badges are colour-coded: green for general visitors, orange for contractors, red for unescorted visitors who have had a safeguarding check.
- For contractors, take a copy of their photo ID and confirm they have read the contractor safety note before they go on site.
- Escort any visitor without a red badge while they are in the building.
- Collect badges at the end of the visit and sign the visitor out. Any badge not returned by the end of the school day should be chased before the office closes.

## Medicines in school

- Only accept medicines that are in their original packaging with the pharmacy label showing the child's name and the dose.
- Complete a medicine consent form with the parent or guardian before the first dose. If the form is not signed, do not give the medicine.
- Store medicines in the locked cabinet in the office. Inhalers and emergency medicines go in the labelled box in each classroom, except for residential trips where they are held by the trip lead.
- Two members of staff check each dose against the consent form. Record it in the medicine log with the time and the names of both staff.

## First aid log

- Every incident, however minor, goes in the first aid log in the office — bump to the head, graze, nosebleed, anything.
- For anything more than a graze or minor bump, call the parent or guardian the same day and note the call in the log.
- Check the first aid kits at the start of each week. Replace anything used and note the expiry date on the replacement.
- Incident forms for anything requiring a trip to A&E go to the Head Teacher's PA; do not file them only in the first aid log.

## School lunches

- Confirm the lunch count with the kitchen straight after registration. Use the lunch register in the school information system, not a paper list.
- Add any change to the kitchen by morning break. After that, the kitchen cannot take new orders.
- Children who have packed lunches are marked on the lunch register as such; the kitchen does not need their names.
- If a child is entitled to free school meals, the office checks this at enrolment and it carries over each year. Parents do not need to reapply while the child is at the academy.

## Extended-day club bookings

- Bookings for breakfast club and after-school club are made through the extended-day booking form. The office closes bookings for breakfast club at the end of the previous school day, and for after-school club at lunchtime on the day.
- Sian Pryce manages the waiting list. If a session is full, add the child to the waiting list rather than overbooking.
- Changes or cancellations after the booking closes go to Sian directly. Do not amend the booking record yourself.
- At the end of each month, Sian reconciles the bookings against the attendance sheet and sends the invoice list to the finance team.
