# About Kilbarra

**Owner:** Executive team  
**Last edited by:** Orla Hennessy (Chief Executive Officer)

## Who we are

Kilbarra Payments DAC is an e-money institution authorised in Ireland, headquartered in Dublin with around 900 employees across three offices. We provide payment processing and e-money services for online merchants — card acquiring, merchant accounts with e-money balances, payouts, payment links and invoicing, and platform accounts for software partners who embed payments into their own products.

## Where we came from

Kilbarra started in Dublin as a card gateway for Irish online shops — the original product was a simple redirect page and a payment confirmation webhook, and most of our early merchants were Irish retailers who needed a way to take cards online without building their own acquiring integration. A few years later we obtained our e-money licence and expanded from pure card acquiring into merchant accounts and balances, which let merchants hold funds with us rather than waiting for each transaction to settle separately.

As the merchant base grew beyond Ireland, we opened the Vilnius operations centre to run support, merchant operations and financial crime work closer to the hours our merchants operate. Vilnius now hosts most of our merchant support team, the transaction monitoring function, and a large part of merchant operations. After launching in the US through a sponsor bank programme, we opened the Austin office to support US merchants and the sponsor bank relationship.

## What we do

Our core product is online acquiring: merchants use our APIs or the merchant dashboard to accept card payments on their websites or apps, and we handle authorisation, settlement and the associated reporting. Beyond acquiring, merchants can hold e-money balances with us and use those balances to make payouts to their own suppliers or sub-merchants. Payment links and invoicing let merchants take payments without building an integration — a merchant can generate a link from the dashboard and share it with a customer, or send an invoice that the customer pays online.

For software partners, we offer platform accounts. A platform account lets a software company create and manage merchants under its own brand, using our APIs for onboarding, payments and payouts while we handle the underlying acquiring and e-money infrastructure. Partners integrate with us through the same merchant API plus the partner data feeds, and their merchants are onboarded through the KYB onboarding tool with risk ratings set in line with the rest of our book.

## Who our merchants are

Our merchants are mostly online businesses — e-commerce retailers, subscription services, digital goods sellers, and marketplaces. They range from small Irish shops that have been with us since the gateway days to larger European and US businesses processing significant volumes. We also serve software platforms that embed payments for their own customers; those platform merchants are typically smaller but come in higher numbers.

## Where we are

- **Dublin (HQ)** — executive team, engineering leadership, product, finance, compliance, risk, and most of the platform engineering teams.
- **Vilnius (operations centre)** — merchant support, merchant operations, financial crime operations, and parts of the data and engineering org.
- **Austin, TX (US office)** — US merchant success and implementation, plus the sponsor bank relationship.

## How we work

Most engineering teams sit in Dublin or Vilnius, with the data platform and warehouse sync team split across both. The US office is primarily commercial and operational — merchant success, implementation and the US sponsor bank programme. The Dublin and Vilnius offices run the day-to-day payments platform, the core ledger, the treasury workstation and the reconciliation tool, with on-call rotations covering the payments bus, the data lake pipelines and the platform itself.
