# Student Money

**Owner:** Student Money & Funding  
**Last edited by:** Pablo Dimitrov (Student Money Manager)

## Common questions

**How do I apply for the hardship fund?**
Students apply through the student casework system. Applications go to the Student Money & Funding team, who review them alongside any evidence the student has uploaded. We aim to make a decision within five working days, but during peak times (just before and after each term starts) it can take a little longer.

**What can the hardship fund be used for?**
It's for immediate, essential living costs — rent arrears, essential bills, food, course costs like books or equipment, and travel to placement. We don't pay tuition fees from the hardship fund, and we don't cover non-essential spending. If a student has already had a hardship award in the same academic year, they can still apply again, but they'll need to show their circumstances have changed or that the previous award wasn't enough.

**Are hardship fund payments a loan or a grant?**
They're a grant — students don't pay them back. But if we find out a student gave false information to get an award, we'll ask for the money back and refer the case to Student Services.

**Who is eligible for the bursaries and scholarships you administer?**
It depends on the specific fund. Most of our bursaries are based on household income and are paid automatically to students who have given consent for us to check their {{STUDENT_LOAN_BODY}} data. Some scholarships are awarded by the faculties or by Development & Alumni Relations, and we only make the payment once the awarding team tells us to. The full list of what we administer is on the Student Money pages of the student portal.

**When are bursary payments made?**
In three instalments, usually in the first week of each term. If a student hasn't received theirs by the end of the second week of term, they should raise a case through the student casework system and we'll trace it.

**Can a student get an emergency loan?**
Yes, if they're a current student and they have no other way to cover an essential cost while they wait for their maintenance loan or other funding. The maximum is £300, and it's normally repaid from their next {{STUDENT_LOAN_BODY}} payment. If that payment doesn't arrive, we'll agree a repayment plan. Students can't get another emergency loan until the previous one is cleared.

**What if a student's maintenance loan is late?**
We see this most often at the start of term. First, check whether the student has completed their enrolment — a maintenance loan won't be released until enrolment is confirmed in the student record system. If enrolment is fine, the student should contact the {{STUDENT_LOAN_BODY}} directly, but we can also raise this on their behalf through the finance system. If the delay is causing genuine hardship, an emergency loan or a short-term advance from the hardship fund may be appropriate.

**What if a tuition fee loan is late reaching the Bursary?**
The same principle applies — check enrolment first. If enrolment is confirmed and the loan still hasn't arrived, the Student Fees & Accounts team (the Bursary) can chase the {{STUDENT_LOAN_BODY}}. The student won't be penalised for a late fee loan as long as they can show they've applied for it and the delay isn't their fault.

**Do we help with accommodation deposits?**
Not directly, but a student facing difficulty with a deposit for private accommodation can apply to the hardship fund. For university-owned halls, the Residences team has their own arrangements, so send the student to them first.

**Can we refer a student to the Student Money team?**
Yes, please. Use the student casework system and select "Student Money & Funding" as the team. It helps if you include context about the student's situation and any previous contact they've had with your team.

**What about students on the Malaysia campus?**
Student Money & Funding is based on the Hollins Hill campus and covers all UK-based students. For students at Aldercombe University Malaysia, hardship support is administered locally by the Malaysia campus management team. If a UK-based student is studying at the Malaysia campus for a period, their funding arrangements stay with us here, but anything urgent is best handled by the campus team in the first instance.
