# Expenses

**Owner:** Accounting, AP and payroll  
**Last edited by:** Bo Weber, Payroll Administrator

## Purpose

This document sets out what Brakwater staff can claim, how to submit claims, and when reimbursement lands in your pay. We aim to make sure expenses are submitted once, in the right currency, with the right receipts, so the payroll team can process them without chasing anyone.

## Scope

All permanent and fixed-term employees in Rotterdam and Denver. Contractors and interns should follow the equivalent clauses in their engagement letter or ask Accounting, AP and payroll.

## What can be claimed

1. **Business travel and accommodation** — trains, flights, hotels and parking for travel that is necessary for your role and approved by your manager beforehand. Book through the travel desk where possible; if you book directly, claim the cost and attach the invoice.

1.1. **Ground transport** — taxis, ride-hailing and public transport fares when public transport is impractical or when you are carrying equipment. Attach the receipt for any trip over €25 or $25.

1.2. **Meals** — the cost of meals while travelling on Brakwater business, up to the per diem amounts in clause 3.

1.3. **Field visits to sensor sites** — mileage for using your own vehicle to visit a sensor site in the Netherlands or the US, at the rate published in the expenses tool. Keep a simple log with the date, the site name, and the odometer reading or route distance.

1.4. **Work-related training and books** — only if agreed with your manager in advance.

1.5. **Home internet and phone** — for staff who work from home regularly, a fixed monthly allowance as set in your contract. Do not claim individual bills on top of this.

## Receipts

2. **Receipts are required** for every claim line over €25 or $25. For amounts below that, a single monthly summary in the expenses tool is enough, but keep the receipts yourself in case we ask.

2.1. **Receipts must show** the supplier, the amount, the currency and the date. A screenshot of an online order confirmation is acceptable if it shows all four.

2.2. **Lost receipts** — if you lose a receipt, add a note in the expenses tool explaining what happened. The finance team may ask for a bank statement line as evidence.

## Per diems

3. **Per diem rates** are set by Accounting, AP and payroll and are published in the expenses tool. The rate covers meals and incidental costs for full days away from your home office.

3.1. **Half-day per diems** apply when you are away for part of a working day; the expenses tool calculates this from the departure and return times you enter.

3.2. **Per diems are not paid** for days when Brakwater provides meals, for example at an all-hands or a customer event with catering.

## Mileage

4. **Mileage for field visits** is claimed at the rate in the expenses tool. The rate is reviewed at the start of each financial year.

4.1. **Only the direct route** between your starting point and the sensor site is claimable. If you make a detour for personal reasons, subtract that distance from your claim.

4.2. **Car-sharing** — if two staff travel together to the same site, only the driver claims mileage.

## The expenses tool

5. **All claims go through the expenses tool.** Paper claims or emailed spreadsheets are returned unreviewed.

5.1. **Submit within 30 days** of the expense being incurred. Claims older than 90 days are not payable without a written explanation that your manager approves.

5.2. **Manager approval** is required for every claim. The approval must happen before the payroll cut-off for that cycle.

5.3. **The expenses tool links to the HR system** for your home currency, cost centre and manager. If any of those details look wrong, raise it first — changing them after submission slows the whole batch down.

## Reimbursement

6. **Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run.** The payroll calendar and the cut-off for each run are in the payroll calendar; if you submit after the cut-off, your claim moves to the following run.

6.1. **Same-currency payment** — Rotterdam staff are paid in EUR, Denver staff in USD. If you submit a claim in the other currency, the expenses tool converts it at the rate published in the tool for that month.

6.2. **Advance payments** — for large travel bookings, you can request an advance through the expenses tool. Advances are deducted from your next reimbursement.

## Exceptions

7. **Anything not listed above** needs a written note in the expenses tool plus manager approval. When in doubt, ask Accounting, AP and payroll before you spend the money.
