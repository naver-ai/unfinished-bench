# VAT and sales tax notes

Owner: FP&A. Last edited by Delphine Marchetti.

## Context

We've had a few merchant queries about VAT on fees and US sales tax on our subscription products. These notes summarise the agreed position with the tax adviser.

## VAT on fees

- Card acquiring and payment processing fees are treated as exempt payment services under Irish VAT rules. No VAT is charged on these fees.
- Fees for software features — including the dashboard, reporting exports, and platform API access — are taxable at the Irish standard rate.
- Where a merchant invoice mixes exempt and taxable fees, the taxable portion is itemised separately on the invoice. The merchant dashboard displays the same split.

## EU merchants

- B2B merchants in other EU member states apply the reverse charge for the taxable software portion. Their VAT number must be on file and validated before we issue an invoice without Irish VAT.
- If the VAT number is not validated, we charge Irish VAT. The merchant can reclaim it through their local refund process.

## US sales tax

- For US merchants, subscription fees for platform accounts and payment links are subject to sales tax in states where we have nexus. This is currently limited to Texas (Austin office) and a small number of other states where we have registered.
- Card transaction fees are not subject to sales tax; they are payment processing services.
- The billing system applies tax based on the merchant's registered business address. The tax adviser reviews the state list each quarter.

## Questions

- All VAT and sales tax questions go through FP&A to the tax adviser. Do not give tax advice to merchants directly; refer them to merchant-success or the helpdesk, who will route to us.
