# Occupational health service

**Owner:** Occupational health  
**Last edited by:** Declan Moss (OH Administrator)

---

**What does the OH team cover?**

We provide occupational health support for Telford and Greenville. That includes pre-placement checks for new starters, noise and hand-arm vibration surveillance for shop-floor roles, forklift driver medicals, eyesight tests for screen users, and the annual flu jab programme. We also do work-related sickness referrals and advise managers on adjustments.

**Who can be referred?**

Anyone employed by Kessendorf UK Ltd or Kessendorf Inc. We don't take self-referrals for clinical assessments — if you have a health concern, speak to your GP or use the employee assistance line. Managers can refer a team member for a work-related health matter, or for a fitness-for-role check after a period of absence.

**How do I refer someone?**

Raise a referral with the OH referral form on the intranet (HR → Occupational health). If you can't find it, ask Declan or Saoirse and they'll point you to the right form. The form asks for the employee's name, job title, site, and a brief description of the concern. You don't need to include medical details — the OH team will ask for those directly if needed.

**What happens after I submit a referral?**

The OH administrator (Declan) books the appointment. For Telford, appointments are held in the OH room on the ground floor of the drives plant, usually within a few working days. For Greenville, appointments are with the occupational health clinic the plant uses locally; Saoirse arranges them. The employee gets a letter or email with the appointment time and what to expect.

**Does the employee have to attend?**

Yes, if the referral is part of a statutory health surveillance programme. For other referrals, attendance is strongly encouraged but not mandatory. If the employee declines, let us know — we'll record that and advise you on next steps.

**What should I tell the employee beforehand?**

Tell them the appointment is confidential, that they won't be asked to share anything with you, and that they can bring a colleague or union rep if they want. Let them know the appointment typically takes 20–30 minutes. Don't speculate about outcomes or suggest what the OH nurse might say.

**How do I get the outcome?**

We send a fit-for-role statement to the referring manager once the assessment is complete. This is a simple recommendation — fit for role, fit with adjustments, or not fit for the current role. It won't include clinical detail. If you need more context, arrange a phone call with the OH nurse.

**Pre-placement checks — when do I book these?**

For shop-floor roles at Telford and Greenville, the pre-placement check should be booked before the start date. The HR onboarding checklist in the handbook shows the process. The check covers general fitness, noise and HAV baseline where the role involves those exposures. For office roles, we usually only need a questionnaire unless the role has specific requirements.

**Forklift driver medicals — who needs these?**

Anyone who drives a forklift or other powered industrial truck as part of their role, including occasional drivers. The medical is required before a new driver starts and then at intervals set by the OH nurse. If a driver has a health condition that could affect safe operation, the supervisor should refer them sooner.

**Eyesight tests for screen users — how do I arrange one?**

The test is available to anyone who uses a display screen equipment workstation as a significant part of their job. Employees can ask their manager, or the manager can refer them. The test itself is done at a local optician's — we'll give the employee a voucher to take with them. The voucher covers a basic sight test; if the optician recommends glasses specifically for screen work, the employee claims that through the expenses app.

**Flu jabs — when do these happen?**

The flu jab programme runs in the autumn. Telford sessions are held on site, Greenville sessions are arranged with the local clinic. Details go out through the intranet and on the notice boards. There's no charge to employees.

**I think someone has a hearing problem but they haven't said anything — can I refer them?**

If the role is in a noise-exposed area and they're due their statutory surveillance, refer them through the normal route. If it's not their scheduled check, speak to Declan first — he'll advise whether an early assessment is appropriate.

**What about hand-arm vibration?**

Regular HAV surveillance applies to anyone using vibrating tools — grinders, impact wrenches, chipping hammers — on a regular basis. The assessment includes a questionnaire and a physical check of the hands and fingers. Supervisors should make sure anyone in those roles has had their baseline check within the first few weeks of starting.

**Where can I find the health surveillance schedules?**

The OH team sends each area's schedule to its supervisors; the procedure is in `ops-docs/occupational-health/health-surveillance.md`. The HR (UK) team can also point you to the right place. If you can't find what you need, email Declan or Saoirse directly.
