# Passwords, badges and sign-in

Owner: Information security · Last edited by Isaac Wagner

## Password manager

- Every staff account password is generated and stored in the password manager.
- If you need access to a shared system account, request it through tickets; the vault entry is assigned to your name.
- Passwords are rotated automatically. You do not need to memorise them.

## MFA prompts

- Approve MFA prompts only when you are actively signing in or unlocking a session.
- If you get a prompt you did not start, deny it, then report it via the security channel in chat or tickets. Include the time and the device shown if it is displayed.
- Repeated unsolicited prompts on the same day get routed to Security. Kai or Samir will pick it up.

## Badge tap sign-in — clinical workstations

- Clinical workstations at South Bend, Valparaiso, Kalamazoo and Cork use badge tap sign-in. Hold your badge to the reader until the green light, then enter your PIN.
- Badge tap replaces the long password entry on those machines. The underlying account is the same one managed in the password manager.
- If your badge does not work, contact the service desk; if it is an after-hours issue, the on-call network engineer can look at the reader logs.
- Do not leave a workstation signed in when you step away. Tap out or lock the screen.
- Lost badges: report in tickets immediately. Security deactivates the badge and issues a replacement.

## Sharing logins

- Do not share your login, your badge, or your MFA session with anyone on a unit, even temporarily.
- If a colleague cannot sign in, they need their own access. Escalate through the service desk or their manager.
- Clinical staff who regularly float between sites should have one badge that works across campuses; request that through tickets rather than borrowing one.
