# Pre-award: preparing a grant submission

*Needs updating after the move — ping #research-institute if something here looks wrong.*

Owner: Kellsbrook Research Institute. Last edited by Leonard Brandvold.

## Internal deadlines

- Start the submission record in the CTMS as soon as you know the sponsor deadline. The CTMS calculates the internal dates from the sponsor deadline you enter.
- **WD-15 (working days before sponsor deadline):** budget draft must be in the shared institute drive. The research finance team reviews the budget template — use the current template from the institute share, not an old copy you have saved locally.
- **WD-12:** budget comments back to the PI. Address them and return the revised budget.
- **WD-10:** letters of support must be drafted and sent to the signatory. The institute office keeps a list of the usual signatories for each sponsor type. For Health Plan-related projects, route letters through Colleen Haines's office.
- **WD-8:** letters of support due back. Chase signatories on WD-7 if anything is missing.
- **WD-6:** full package (budget, narrative, letters, biosketches, other support) assembled in the CTMS. The research administrator for your group reviews it.
- **WD-4:** routing for institutional sign-off opens. This is the point of no return for changes to the budget or the facilities and administrative rate.
- **WD-2:** institutional sign-off complete. The final package is locked in the CTMS and the sponsor portal copy is prepared.
- **WD-1:** submission check. The research administrator confirms the sponsor portal has the same files as the CTMS and that the budget totals match.

## Budget template

- Use the institute budget template with the green tab. Do not reformat into a sponsor-required layout until after the internal review — the reviewers need to see the standard line items.
- The facilities and administrative rate for the institute is 52% of modified total direct costs. The rate for the Cork office is different — check the rate sheet in the same folder as the template. The rate sheet is refreshed each quarter.
- Salary caps, if any, are applied at the line-item level. The template has a column for the cap basis; leave it blank if the sponsor has no cap.
- Equipment over $5,000 goes on its own line with a quote attached. The template has a notes column for the quote reference.
- Include the Cork trials office overheads under "Other institution" — the template has a row for it, and it is often missed.
- Travel: use the current per-diem figures from the expenses tool, not the older travel policy numbers.

## Letters of support

- Letters must be on letterhead and signed with a wet or digital signature. Digital signatures from the paging system account do not count.
- The institute office has a folder of approved letter templates by sponsor type. If the sponsor requires a specific format, attach their format alongside the institute template so the signatory sees both.
- For letters from the research institute's European trials office, route them through the Cork office so they can confirm the letterhead version is current.
- Letters from clinical departments need the department chair's signature. If the chair is unavailable, the institute office can advise on the alternate signatory list.

## Routing for institutional sign-off

- Routing starts in the CTMS. The research administrator for your group initiates it and the system sends the routing to the signatories in order.
- Signatories are: the PI, the institute research administrator, the Chief Research Officer's office, and the finance team. The order matters — do not ask signatories to skip ahead.
- The Chief Research Officer's office reviews all submissions with a budget over $250,000 or any involving the Health Plan. Smaller submissions go through the institute research administrator alone.
- If a signatory requests changes, the routing resets to the beginning. Plan for one reset round in your internal dates; two resets means the WD-4 date will slip.
- The final sign-off letter is generated from the CTMS and stored in the study record. The sponsor portal copy should use the generated letter, not a scanned version of a printed one.
