# Works council and employee forum

The Betriebsrat at Reutlingen represents all employees at the German site and is the body we consult on the matters German labour law requires. The current committee has eleven members, elected to serve a four-year term. Their work is supported by the People & HR team, primarily through Luca Petrov, who acts as the main point of contact for the committee's administrative needs. You can reach the Betriebsrat by writing to betriebsrat@kessendorf.com or by dropping a note in the postbox next to the main entrance on the ground floor of building A. The committee publishes its own notices on the board opposite the canteen and in the intranet under **People & HR → Works council**.

The employee forum at Telford is a voluntary consultative body, not a statutory one. It meets once a month with the site director and a representative from HR (UK) (currently Eero Ahmadi) to discuss working conditions, shift patterns, catering, car parking and similar site matters. The forum has eight members drawn from across the UK plant, including the drives line and the distribution centre. Contact them via the forum's shared mailbox, telford-forum@kessendorf.com, or by speaking to any forum member — their names are listed on the intranet under **Our sites → Telford → Employee forum**.

We consult the Betriebsrat on changes that affect working time and shift patterns, the introduction of monitoring tools, moves between buildings or teams, and any restructuring that touches the Reutlingen workforce. This includes changes to the shift rota published in the working-time system, new badge readers or time-recording terminals, and the rollout of new software that tracks activity. When a change falls into one of these categories, the responsible manager should involve People & HR early — ideally before any announcement — so that we can plan the consultation properly. The committee has the right to see relevant documents and to discuss the impact with the affected teams before we proceed.

Agreements reached with the Betriebsrat are published as works agreements (Betriebsvereinbarungen) in the intranet under **People & HR → Works council → Works agreements**. These cover topics such as mobile working, the use of the company chat outside core hours, the employee suggestion scheme and the framework for variable pay in the German entity. If a works agreement affects your day-to-day work, your team lead should point you to it during the relevant change communication.

For the Telford forum, the scope is broader but informal. The forum is consulted on changes to shift handover arrangements, new equipment on the drives line, parking and site access, and the annual shutdown plan. The forum does not have a formal right of veto, but the site leadership team treats its views as material — if the forum raises a concern about a planned change, we expect the change owner to respond in writing and, where possible, to adjust the plan.

Both bodies are covered by the same confidentiality rules that apply to all internal consultations. Information shared with them in the course of a consultation is not for general circulation until the change is announced.

If you are not sure whether a change needs to go to the Betriebsrat or the forum, ask People & HR before you communicate it. The early conversation saves a longer delay later. For Reutlingen, Luca Petrov is the first point of contact; for Telford, Eero Ahmadi.
