# Support coverage across offices

Owner: Customer support · Last edited by Wanjiru Kiplagat

Support operates from two desks: Rotterdam covers European business hours and Denver covers the Americas. The shared inbox rota rotates weekly, with two specialists on the inbox at any time — one per desk during overlap, and a single owner during the other desk's off hours.

| Desk | Coverage window | Primary focus | Handover |
|------|----------------|---------------|----------|
| Rotterdam | European business hours, roughly mid-morning to late afternoon CET | Water boards, ports, agri co-ops in EMEA | Pass open items to Denver before end of shift |
| Denver | US business hours, Mountain Time | US-based ports, insurers, agri customers | Hand back to Rotterdam at end of US day |

Handover happens via the handover note in the helpdesk, plus a short update in #support. Any case that is not resolved by end of shift is summarised with next steps and assigned to the incoming desk. Cases that need a specific specialist (e.g. technical escalation to Laszlo Cruz) stay with that person but are still noted for visibility.

Public holidays are observed locally. The Rotterdam desk is closed on Dutch public holidays; the Denver desk closes on US federal holidays. On those days the other desk covers the shared inbox for urgent items. The rota in the helpdesk calendar shows who is covering each holiday. If both desks are closed on the same day (rare), the Support Operations Manager arranges cover for urgent issues.

The shared inbox rota is managed in the helpdesk. Each week has two named specialists: one covering the Rotterdam hours and one covering the Denver hours. There is no inbox coverage outside those windows; urgent items after hours go through the on-call route. Rota changes are coordinated in #support at least two working days ahead.
