# How students pay their fees

Owner: Student Fees & Accounts (the Bursary) · Last edited by Valentina Demir

**When do tuition fees need to be paid?**
Tuition fees are due at the start of the academic year. Most students pay in three instalments, due at the start of each term. Students can choose to pay the full year upfront if they prefer.

**How do students set up an instalment plan?**
Through the fees pages of the student portal. They pick the number of instalments (usually three, sometimes more for short courses) and their payment method. The portal then generates a schedule showing each amount and its due date.

**What about students who get a tuition fee loan from the {{STUDENT_LOAN_BODY}}?**
The {{STUDENT_LOAN_BODY|code}} pays the loan in three instalments directly to the university, usually around the start of each term. We reconcile these against each student's account in the student record system. If the loan is confirmed but the payment hasn't arrived by the due date, we mark the instalment as "awaiting {{STUDENT_LOAN_BODY|code}}" rather than chasing the student.

**How do sponsor invoices work?**
Where a sponsor (employer, government body, or other organisation) has agreed to pay a student's fees, we invoice them at the start of each term alongside the instalment schedule. Gabriel Graham handles sponsor billing; see the sponsor billing page for the full process. If a sponsor is late paying, we notify the student so they can chase the sponsor — the debt remains the student's if the sponsor defaults.

**Do international students pay a deposit?**
Yes. Offer-holders from outside the UK pay a deposit before they can enrol. The deposit amount is set by Student Fees & Accounts each year and published on the fees pages. It's credited against their first instalment once they enrol.

**What payment methods do we accept?**
Card payments (debit or credit) through the student portal, bank transfer, and direct debit for instalment plans. We don't take cash or cheques at the Bursary counter. Bank transfer details are shown on the fees pages; students should use their student ID as the payment reference.

**What about accommodation fees?**
Accommodation fees for halls of residence are billed through the same fees pages, with instalments aligned to the accommodation contract — usually termly. Students living in Harbour Gate or other residential lettings pay under a separate lettings agreement, not through these pages.

**What if a student misses an instalment?**
We send a reminder through the student portal and by email to their university address. If it's still unpaid after a few working days, we follow up and, where needed, the Credit Control Officer (Lena Young) gets involved. Continued non-payment can lead to restrictions on the student account — they can't re-enrol or get a transcript, and in some cases we refer the debt for collection.

**Can students see what they owe on the portal?**
Yes. The fees pages show their current balance, upcoming instalments, any deposits paid, and a history of payments and charges. If anything looks wrong, they should contact Student Fees & Accounts via the portal rather than the IT service desk.

**Are there late payment charges?**
Late payment interest applies to overdue balances for continuing students, in line with our published terms. We don't apply interest to instalments awaiting {{STUDENT_LOAN_BODY|code}} payment.

**What if a student's fees are paid by a research funder or other internal department?**
Where fees are covered by a research grant or a departmental budget, the relevant office arranges an internal transfer. Students should not be asked to pay out of pocket in these cases. Contact us if the split between the student and the department isn't clear.
