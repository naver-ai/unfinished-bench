# The Student Hub

The Student Hub sits within Admissions, Enrolment & Student Hub and operates desks on both the Hollins Hill and City campuses. We're the first point of contact for most student enquiries, and we handle a set of routine transactions that don't need a specialist team.

**What we handle at the desk:**
- Student ID cards (replacement cards are issued same-day; first cards are produced during enrolment)
- Standard letters (bank letters, council tax letters, confirmation of study) — these are printed from the student record system
- Paper forms handed in at the desk (change of address, module change requests, extenuating circumstances forms) — we scan these into the student casework system and route them to the right team
- General enquiries about fees, timetables, exams, and Student Services — we answer what we can and pass the rest on
- Lost property for the Hollins Hill campus (City campus lost property goes through the City campus reception)

**Queue tickets:**
We use the ticket numbers from the student casework system for anything that needs a response after the student leaves the desk. Walk-up enquiries that are answered immediately don't need a ticket. If we take a paper form, we create a ticket and attach the scan. If we're sending a case to a specialist team, we use the casework system's handover function rather than email — the receiving team sees it in their queue and can track it.

**Passing cases to specialist teams:**
- Student Records (Registry) — module changes, personal details, transcripts, anything about a student's record
- Student Fees — fee enquiries, payment plans, refund requests
- Student Services — support plans, disability funding, student money advice
- Dean of Students & Student Casework — conduct matters, appeals, complaints
- Student Accommodation — halls queries that the hall reception can't sort from their side (Harbour Gate and the other lettings flats go to Residential Lettings)

We don't pass cases to the IT service desk — students contact them directly through the portal, and the Hub only points them there.

**Busy weeks:**
The main surge is enrolment in late September, plus the first two weeks of October when late enrolments and module changes come through. The Hub runs extra desk hours during that period and the team is pulled from across Admissions, Enrolment & Student Hub. January is quieter but picks up around exam timetabling when students want letters to support mitigating circumstances applications. The City campus desk gets busy during exam periods — we see a lot of replacement card requests and lost property.
