**Last edited by:** Dana Okonjo, Student Records Data Coordinator

## Purpose

This note covers the weekly data quality reports, the field ownership split within Student Records & Data, and why the statutory return makes these fields worth chasing.

## Weekly data quality reports

The reports run from the student record system on the first working day of each week and are posted to #student-systems. They cover three groups of issues:

- **Missing fee status** — students with no fee status recorded for the current academic year. Fee status drives the tuition fee charged and is used in the statutory return.
- **Missing nationality** — students with no nationality on their record. Nationality is captured at enrolment; gaps usually come from partner-fed records or late changes.
- **Module marks not entered** — modules where the exam board has met but marks have not been keyed into the student record system. These hold up awards and transcripts.

Each report lists the student IDs, the owning faculty or school, and how long the issue has been open.

## How issues get fixed

The report is shared with faculty registry teams and the relevant faculty office in #science-faculty or #registry. Schools are asked to correct or escalate within five working days. If nothing has changed by the next report, the item is re-flagged and the faculty registry lead is copied in.

Student Records does not edit marks or fee status on behalf of schools. We will correct nationality where we hold the source document; otherwise the school must chase the student.

## Field ownership within Student Records & Data

- **Fee status** — Joel Amari's team (Student Records, Operations). Joel reviews the weekly report for fee status and chases schools that have not responded.
- **Nationality** — Dana Okonjo. Dana reconciles nationality against enrolment records and flags anything that does not match the ID check.
- **Module marks** — Luis Ferreira's team. Luis coordinates with exam officers via #exams-officers and checks the marks coming in from schools before they are sealed.

Connor Sharma and Jasmine Larsen rotate the production of the weekly report. The report is generated from a script that Student Systems maintain; if the script needs changing, raise a ticket with them.

## Why these fields matter

The annual statutory return to the funding body is the main driver. The return includes fee status, nationality and module outcomes for all registered students. Gaps in the data either delay the return or force us to send estimates that are later corrected. Both are worth avoiding.

The return is submitted by Academic Registry; Annika Lindqvist's team gives us the field list and the deadline. We aim to have the data clean two working days before the return is due.
