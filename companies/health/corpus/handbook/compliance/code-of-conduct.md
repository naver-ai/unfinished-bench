# Code of conduct

**Owner:** Compliance & privacy  
**Last edited by:** Curtis Ambrose, Chief Compliance and Privacy Officer  
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, fellows, students, and volunteers at Kellsbrook Health System, Inc., including all sites (South Bend, Valparaiso, Kalamazoo, and Cork) and all lines of business (hospitals, clinics, health plan, research institute).

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets out the standards of conduct expected of everyone who works at or represents Kellsbrook Health. It covers conflicts of interest, gifts and meals, documentation and billing integrity, and how to raise concerns. It does not replace other policies — where another policy is more specific, follow that policy and contact Compliance & privacy if you have questions.

## 1. General standards

1.1. Act in the best interest of patients, members, and the communities we serve. Do not use your position for personal gain.

1.2. Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and Kellsbrook policies. When you are unsure whether something is permitted, ask before acting.

1.3. Follow the principles in the Kellsbrook handbook, including the acceptable use and data classification policies in `handbook/security/`.

1.4. Cooperate fully with internal reviews and audits conducted by Compliance, Internal Audit, Legal, or Quality & patient safety.

## 2. Conflicts of interest

2.1. A conflict of interest exists when your personal, family, financial, or other interests could influence, or appear to influence, your decisions or actions on behalf of Kellsbrook.

2.2. Conflicts may arise in many contexts, including procurement, hiring, referrals, research, and vendor relationships. Common examples include:
- A family member works for or owns a vendor we do business with.
- You have an outside job or board role with an organization that does business with Kellsbrook.
- You own stock in a company whose products we purchase or prescribe.

2.3. Disclose actual or potential conflicts through the annual disclosure in the HR system. If a conflict arises during the year, disclose it to Compliance & privacy as soon as you become aware of it.

2.4. The annual disclosure is required for all employees at manager level and above, all staff in Procurement, Revenue cycle, Health Plan operations, and Research administration, and any other role identified by Compliance. Managers are responsible for making sure their direct reports complete the disclosure.

2.5. Compliance & privacy reviews disclosures and may require you to mitigate the conflict, such as recusing yourself from a decision or divesting an interest. Follow any mitigation plan exactly.

2.6. Do not participate in decisions where you have a conflict, unless Compliance has approved your participation in writing.

## 3. Gifts and meals

3.1. You may not accept gifts, meals, entertainment, travel, or other items of value from vendors, drug or device representatives, suppliers, or anyone who does or seeks to do business with Kellsbrook, except as allowed below.

3.2. Permitted items:
- Modest meals or refreshments (retail value under $50) provided during a legitimate business meeting at which substantive business is discussed.
- Promotional items of nominal value (e.g., pens, notepads, mugs) with a retail value under $25.
- Attendance at a vendor-sponsored educational event, if the event is primarily educational and attendance is approved in advance by your manager and Compliance & privacy.

3.3. The following are never permitted:
- Cash or cash equivalents (gift cards, vouchers, credits).
- Meals or gifts for family members.
- Travel or lodging paid by a vendor, except as part of a written contract or approved educational program.
- Any item offered in connection with a purchasing decision, formulary decision, or clinical protocol decision.

3.4. Vendor representatives, including pharmaceutical and device representatives, must follow the access and badge procedures at each site. If a representative offers you something that is not permitted, decline it and report the offer to Compliance & privacy.

3.5. If you receive an unsolicited gift that you cannot return, report it to Compliance & privacy within five working days and follow their instructions for disposition.

3.6. Kellsbrook employees may not provide gifts or meals to vendors or suppliers except as part of a written agreement or with prior approval from your manager.

## 4. Accurate documentation and billing

4.1. All documentation in the EHR, the claims adjudication system, the CTMS, and other records must be accurate, complete, and timely. Document what you actually did, observed, or reviewed — never more, never less.

4.2. Do not alter or backdate records to misrepresent when care was provided, what was done, or who did it. Follow the amendment and correction procedures in `care-docs/him/chart-corrections.md` for the clinical record.

4.3. Charge capture and coding must reflect the services actually rendered and documented. Follow the charge capture procedures in `care-docs/revcycle/charge-capture.md` and the coding guidance in `care-docs/him/coding-queues.md`.

4.4. Do not submit claims, prior authorization requests, or other documents that you know or suspect are inaccurate. This applies to claims for the health plan, claims to other payers, and any documentation submitted to government programs.

4.5. Research documentation, including protocol records in the CTMS, study data in the EDC, and investigator site files, must be accurate and contemporaneous. Follow the applicable research policies in `research-docs/`.

4.6. Never sign another person's name to a document, and never ask someone to sign for you.

4.7. If you discover an error in documentation or billing, correct it promptly or report it through the appropriate channel. Do not wait for the error to be found by someone else.

## 5. Speaking up and the compliance hotline

5.1. Every employee has a duty to report suspected violations of this policy, other Kellsbrook policies, or the law. Report concerns promptly when you become aware of them.

5.2. You may report in any of the following ways:
- Your manager or any member of the Compliance & privacy team, including Ebony Santos or Rhea Medina.
- The compliance hotline, which is available by phone and through a web form. The hotline is operated by an independent third party and is available 24 hours a day. You may report anonymously where permitted by law.
- The safety event reporting system for patient safety events, or the tickets system for IT-related concerns.

5.3. The hotline is not a substitute for emergency response. If someone is in immediate danger, call the paging system or local emergency services first.

5.4. When you report a concern, provide as much detail as you can — what happened, when, where, and who was involved. You do not need to prove the concern yourself; the Compliance team will review it.

5.5. Compliance & privacy reviews all reports and may coordinate with Legal, Internal Audit, Human Resources, or Quality & patient safety as appropriate. We aim to acknowledge reports within two working days, although the investigation timeline will depend on the nature of the concern.

## 6. Non-retaliation

6.1. Kellsbrook prohibits retaliation against anyone who, in good faith, reports a concern, participates in an investigation, or asks questions about compliance.

6.2. Retaliation includes, but is not limited to: termination, demotion, negative performance reviews, changes in shifts or assignments, exclusion from meetings or communications, harassment, or any other adverse action taken because someone raised a concern.

6.3. Good-faith reports are protected even if the concern turns out to be unfounded. Reporting a concern with reckless disregard for the truth, or making a report for the purpose of harming someone, is not protected and may be subject to discipline.

6.4. If you believe you have experienced retaliation, report it to Compliance & privacy, Human Resources, or the compliance hotline. Reports of retaliation are investigated with the same priority as the underlying concern.

6.5. Managers who observe or learn of retaliation must report it to Compliance & privacy. Managers who retaliate will be subject to discipline, up to and including termination.

## 7. Administration

7.1. Compliance & privacy owns this policy and reviews it at least annually. Questions about this policy should be directed to Compliance & privacy.

7.2. Violations of this policy may result in discipline, up to and including termination of employment or contract. Violations may also be referred to Legal or law enforcement where appropriate.

7.3. The annual disclosure requirement in section 2.3 is administered through the HR system. Compliance & privacy will notify affected staff when the disclosure window opens.
