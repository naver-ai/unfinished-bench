# Pricing and packaging

**Owner:** Pricing and commercial analytics  
**Last edited by:** Omar Haddad

This page covers the standard price list for monitoring subscriptions, tasking orders, API usage, and public-sector sales. For bespoke quotes or anything not listed here, contact Pricing and commercial analytics.

## Monitoring subscription tiers

| Tier | Coverage | Typical customer | Price per site per month |
|------|----------|------------------|--------------------------|
| Standard | 1 sensor, daily satellite revisit, dashboard access | Small agricultural co-ops | €145 |
| Professional | Up to 5 sensors, 2× daily revisit, analytics API included | Water boards, mid-size ports | €390 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited sensors on site, tasking priority, model output feeds | Large ports, national agencies | Custom |

All tiers include the field app, alerting via the customer API console, and email support. Professional and Enterprise include phone support during Rotterdam office hours.

## Tasking orders

Tasking is priced per km², with a minimum order of 100 km² per acquisition. Prices assume a single pass; multi-pass orders are quoted individually.

| Product | Price per km² |
|---------|---------------|
| Optical, ≤ 1 m resolution | €8.50 |
| Optical, ≤ 0.5 m resolution | €14.00 |
| SAR, any resolution | €11.00 |

Tasking orders are placed through the tasking portal. The imagery catalogue is updated within two working days of acquisition. Rush tasking (delivery within 24 hours of acquisition) carries a 40% surcharge.

## API usage bundles

API usage is metered per API call, with a monthly base fee per bundle.

| Bundle | Included calls | Monthly fee | Overage per 1,000 calls |
|--------|----------------|-------------|--------------------------|
| Starter | 100,000 | €250 | €3.50 |
| Growth | 1,000,000 | €1,800 | €2.20 |
| Scale | 5,000,000 | €6,500 | €1.40 |

Unused calls do not roll over. The analytics API runbook in platform-docs covers technical limits.

## Public-sector list prices

Public-sector customers (water boards, provincial authorities, national agencies) receive a 15% discount on the list prices above. For tender responses, see the public-sector tenders page.

## Multi-year terms

Subscription and API contracts can be written for 12, 24, or 36 months. Multi-year agreements receive:

- 24 months: 5% discount on the total contract value
- 36 months: 10% discount on the total contract value

Multi-year terms are billed annually in advance. Tasking orders are always billed per order and are not eligible for multi-year pricing. For billing mechanics, see the billing page in finance-docs.
