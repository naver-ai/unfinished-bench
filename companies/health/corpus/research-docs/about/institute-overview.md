# Kellsbrook Research Institute

The Kellsbrook Research Institute is the research arm of Kellsbrook Health System, based at the Kalamazoo campus. We conduct clinical trials, maintain longitudinal cohort studies, and carry out health services research that informs how care is delivered across our hospitals, clinics, and health plan. Leonard Brandvold, Chief Research Officer, leads the institute and reports to the Executive team.

## Programs

Our metabolic cohort studies are the institute's longest-running effort. These studies follow several thousand participants from northern Indiana and southwest Michigan, with regular collection of clinical measures, laboratory results, and patient-reported outcomes through the patient app and portal. The cohort has supported work on diabetes progression, weight management interventions, and cardiovascular risk prediction in primary care populations.

The cardiology and oncology trial portfolio includes both industry-sponsored and investigator-initiated studies. Cardiology trials typically run through our hospital sites in South Bend, Valparaiso, and Kalamazoo, while oncology trials are concentrated at Kalamazoo and South Bend. Many of these studies use the CTMS for trial tracking and the EDC for case report form data, and the eIRB system handles all ethics review submissions.

Health services research draws on the enterprise data warehouse and the claims adjudication system to study utilization patterns, cost of care, and quality measures across Kellsbrook Health Plan members. This work often involves collaboration with the quality team and the health plan's analytics staff, and findings regularly feed into operational decisions about care pathways and benefit design.

## Cores

The biostatistics core provides study design support, sample size calculations, and analysis for all institute projects. They maintain the analysis datasets derived from the data warehouse and work closely with the research data management team on data cleaning and validation.

The research data management core handles study databases, pseudonymisation of participant data, and preparation of data for sharing with external collaborators. The core also supports the CTMS and EDC environments.

The specimen processing core operates out of the Kalamazoo campus laboratory. They handle sample receipt, aliquoting, storage, and shipping for all institute studies, and coordinate with the lab information system to ensure specimens are tracked from collection through analysis.

## European Trials Office

The Cork office serves as our European trials office and runs the GP-led medical centre in Cork. The trials office supports European sites for our industry-sponsored studies and handles regulatory interactions for those sites. The medical centre also participates in primary care research, mainly through the metabolic cohort program, and refers interested patients to appropriate studies when eligibility criteria are met.

## Funding

Study funding comes from three main sources. Industry sponsors cover most of the interventional trial work, with budgets negotiated through the pre-award process and contracts signed per the delegation of authority in the handbook. Federal grants support our cohort studies and health services research, with applications prepared through the pre-award process in research-docs/grants. Internal funding from Kellsbrook Health supports pilot projects and investigator-initiated work, allocated annually by the institute leadership team.

All funding sources are subject to the same overhead and indirect cost policies, which are documented in the grants pre-award guide. The institute coordinates with Finance on budget management and reporting.
