# Conferences and meetups

Owner: Developer relations · Last edited by Jin Gallagher

## Picking events

- [ ] Confirm the event fits one of our focus areas: developer experience, CI/CD, cloud development, or open source tooling.
- [ ] Check that at least one Gantline engineer or devrel person already has a connection to the organising team, or we have a customer who can introduce us.
- [ ] Review the attendee profile: if it is mostly sales or leadership, it is probably not worth a booth.
- [ ] For paid booths, get budget approval from Marketing before committing. Maya Thornbury's team owns the events budget.
- [ ] For smaller meetups or community days where we are not paying, just confirm with the team in #devrel so we do not double-book people.
- [ ] Add the event to the marketing planning board once confirmed.

## Booth kit

- [ ] The booth kit lives in two places: Lisbon (office storage room, labelled "DevRel booth") and Portland (same, in the closet near the kitchen). Olivier Nunez can help you get into the storage rooms.
- [ ] Kit contents: banner, tablecloth, stickers, demo laptop stand, power strip, HDMI adapter, and a box of Gantline t-shirts (sizes M–XXL).
- [ ] If you take kit items to an event, note what you took in the CRM record for that event so the next person knows what is where.
- [ ] If something breaks or runs out, tell Olivier and he will order replacements.
- [ ] Do not take the kit outside the country without checking with Workplace first — customs paperwork is not worth it for a banner.

## Talk submissions

- [ ] Prefer talks that demo gnt or Gantline Cloud live. Product demos consistently get better feedback than slide-only talks.
- [ ] Use the talk title format "Doing X with gnt" or "Scaling Y on Gantline Cloud" — it is clearer for reviewers.
- [ ] Run the talk abstract past Rukmini Sethi before submitting. She keeps a list of talks we have already done so we do not repeat ourselves.
- [ ] For conference talks, book travel only after the talk is accepted. For meetups, you can book once the date is confirmed.
- [ ] If you are speaking, add a link to the recording on the docs site when it goes live. Rhea Patel manages the talks page.

## Travel

- [ ] Book through the travel desk in the expenses tool, same as any business travel. See handbook/people/travel.md.
- [ ] For Lisbon-based staff travelling to US events, remember the working-across-offices guidance in handbook/people/working-across-offices.md, especially around jet lag and meeting scheduling.
- [ ] If you are travelling with the booth kit, book a car or taxi that can fit the banner tube. It does not fit in a standard sedan.

## After the event

- [ ] Log every meaningful conversation in the CRM within a few working days. Include the person's name, company, what they were interested in, and any follow-up promised.
- [ ] Tag the CRM record with the event name and the Gantline employee who had the conversation, so Customer Success can pick up leads.
- [ ] Add any hot leads to the #customer-success channel with a one-line summary. Do not just leave them in the CRM.
- [ ] If you collected business cards, hand them to Customer Success rather than leaving them on your desk.
- [ ] Update the event record in the CRM with what worked and what did not — booth location, crowd size, talk attendance. This helps us decide whether to go again next year.
- [ ] Return the booth kit to storage within a week of getting back.
