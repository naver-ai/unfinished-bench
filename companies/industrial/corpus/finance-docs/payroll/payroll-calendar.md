# Payroll calendar and cut-offs

**Owner:** Payroll  
**Last edited by:** Gabriel Bishop (Payroll Administrator)

This calendar covers the three payroll cycles we run: UK monthly (in-house at Telford), Germany monthly (via the payroll provider), and US biweekly (via the US provider). It is the reference for cut-offs, pay days, and who to contact when something needs to be corrected.

---

## Cycle overview

| Country | Frequency | Payroll run | Pay day | Cut-off for changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UK | Monthly | In-house, Telford | Last working day of the month | WD–5 before pay day |
| Germany | Monthly | Payroll provider | Last working day of the month | WD–7 before pay day |
| US | Biweekly | US provider | Pay day falls on a fixed weekday | WD–3 before pay day |

---

## Cut-off details

### UK (Telford)

- **Changes** (new starters, leavers, contract changes, salary adjustments) must reach Payroll by **WD–5** before pay day via the HR system.
- **Overtime and shift allowances** from the time-recording terminals are pulled automatically on **WD–3**. Anything clocked after that will land in the following month's run.
- **Manual adjustments** (e.g. corrections from HR or line managers) must be submitted through the HR system by **WD–4**.
- Payroll runs in-house on **WD–2**; the output file goes to the bank on **WD–1**.

### Germany (Reutlingen)

- **Changes** must be in the HR system by **WD–7** before pay day. The provider needs the data file by **WD–5**.
- **Overtime and shift allowances** from the time-recording terminals are exported to the provider on **WD–6**. Late entries are carried to the next month.
- The provider returns the calculated payroll on **WD–2**; we review and approve the same day.

### US (Greenville)

- **Changes** must be in the HR system by **WD–3** before the pay day.
- **Overtime and shift allowances** from the time-recording terminals are pulled on **WD–2**.
- The US provider processes on **WD–1**; the file is sent to the US bank the same day.

## Notes

- The cut-offs are working days before the pay day, not calendar days. If a cut-off falls on a site holiday, the cut-off is the previous working day.
- The UK and German pay runs are aligned to the last working day of the month; the US run always pays on the scheduled pay day regardless of month-end.
- If a pay day falls on a bank holiday in the country concerned, pay day moves to the previous working day. The cut-offs move accordingly.
- Any change requested after the cut-off will be processed in the following run. This includes corrections from managers, HR, and the time-recording terminals.
