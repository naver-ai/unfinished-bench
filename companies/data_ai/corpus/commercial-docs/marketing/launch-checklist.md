# Product launch checklist

**Owner:** Marketing  
**Last edited by:** Naomi Ferrari (Demand Generation Manager)

## Before launch

- [ ] Confirm the target audience and launch window with Product management.
- [ ] Draft the messaging doc and get sign-off from the product lead and Shauna Petrakis.
- [ ] Check the messaging doc against `commercial-docs/comms/brand-voice.md` for tone and terminology.
- [ ] Identify the sales team that will carry the launch and confirm they have time to brief.
- [ ] Book the webinar slot in the company calendar and confirm the platform works from both Rotterdam and Denver.
- [ ] Write the customer email copy; route through the CRM so the audience list is pulled from the right segments.
- [ ] Draft the What's new entry for the customer API console and the imagery catalogue.
- [ ] Write the help article and have a support lead review it for accuracy.
- [ ] Plan the note in #announcements so it does not land on the same day as another launch.
- [ ] Posts for the company social accounts: see `commercial-docs/comms/social.md`.

## During launch week

- [ ] Send the customer email on the agreed day, early enough that support can pick up questions the same day.
- [ ] Publish the help article and the What's new entry.
- [ ] Post the internal announcement in #announcements with a short summary and links to the help article and webinar registration.
- [ ] Share the sales enablement deck with the sales team and the support team; include common questions and how to route them.
- [ ] Run the webinar; record it and make the recording available through the usual channel.
- [ ] Monitor the helpdesk and #support for launch-related questions; escalate anything that looks like a product issue.

## After launch

- [ ] Within two working days of the webinar, share the recording and the attendee list with Sales.
- [ ] Post-launch review with Product, Applied ML and the relevant account teams; cover what worked and what did not.
- [ ] Update the messaging doc with any changes made during the launch.
- [ ] Add any new customer questions to the sales enablement deck and the help article.
- [ ] Close out the launch in the tickets tracker; link the review notes to the original launch ticket.
- [ ] If the launch included a pricing change, confirm the pricing and packaging doc is up to date.
