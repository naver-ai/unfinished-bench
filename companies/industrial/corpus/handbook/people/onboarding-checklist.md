# Onboarding checklist (managers)

Owner: People & HR · Last edited by Santiago Richter (HR Business Partner)

This checklist is for managers who are taking on a new team member (new hire, transfer, or returning from a long absence). Work through it with the employee's HR Business Partner; for Reutlingen that is Luca Petrov, for Telford Eero Ahmadi, and for Greenville Santiago Richter. If you are not sure which HR Business Partner covers your team, ask in #hr-ops.

## Before day one

- [ ] Confirm the start date and site with HR. For Reutlingen, check the works council calendar in the intranet before confirming.
- [ ] Raise the joiners request in the HR system at least five working days before the start date. This triggers the access workflow.
- [ ] Order the laptop and phone through the service desk portal. Standard kit is a notebook with docking station; a second monitor is available on request.
- [ ] Request building access via the service desk portal. For Telford and Greenville, include the plant gate code request if the role needs it.
- [ ] If the role involves entering the shop floor or test lab at any point, book the safety induction with the site EHS team. This applies to office staff too — the site safety basics e-learning does not replace the induction.
- [ ] Request a buddy. For new hires, the buddy should be someone who has been in the team at least six months and is not the manager. Confirm the buddy assignment in chat before day one.
- [ ] Check the team calendar and book the plant tour for the first week. Tours run on fixed days — for Reutlingen, ask in #reutlingen; for Telford, #telford; for Greenville, #greenville.
- [ ] Ensure the mandatory e-learning modules are assigned in the HR system: anti-bribery, data protection, and export control awareness. These must be completed within the first two weeks.
- [ ] For anyone who will handle orders or customer data, request MES and ERP access via the access-requests page in the handbook. Production staff also need the shop-floor PC account.
- [ ] If the role includes driving a company vehicle or operating a forklift, add the licence check to the first-day agenda. The EHS team handles the verification.

## First day

- [ ] Meet the new team member at reception and walk them to their desk. Do not assume they know the layout.
- [ ] Start the buddy introduction in the first hour. The buddy should cover the kitchen, lockers, and how to book a desk if the team hot-desks.
- [ ] Confirm the laptop is set up and they can log in. If the device is not ready, escalate through the service desk portal and tell the team in #it-help.
- [ ] Walk through the site safety basics from the handbook, even for office-only roles. Mention the nearest assembly point and the emergency phone number.
- [ ] Show them the team's chat channels and add them to the relevant ones. For production-adjacent roles, add them to #planning and #quality.
- [ ] Explain the time-recording process. For Reutlingen, this is the terminals on each floor; for Telford and Greenville, the same terminals are used.
- [ ] Book the plant tour if it was not booked before day one. The tour is part of the induction for all roles that will visit the shop floor.
- [ ] Introduce them to the team leads they will work with. A short walk-around is enough; do not schedule a full day of meetings.

## First two weeks

- [ ] Check the e-learning progress in the HR system. Follow up with the employee if any module is not started by the end of the first week.
- [ ] Confirm the safety induction has been completed if the role requires shop-floor or test-lab access. The EHS team records this in their system; ask them if unsure.
- [ ] Review the team's working-time rules from the handbook and confirm the employee's shift pattern, if applicable.
- [ ] Introduce the employee to the relevant process owners: quality (Priya Fujita for Reutlingen), planning, and maintenance as needed.
- [ ] For roles that touch the test lab, book a lab overview session through the lab calendar. The bench booking walk-through is part of that session.
- [ ] For roles that touch the warehouse, arrange a short goods-in and outbound-dock familiarisation with the logistics team.
- [ ] Hold a first feedback conversation. This is informal — the formal check-in happens later, but it is good to catch anything early.
- [ ] Confirm the buddy relationship is working. If the buddy is not responsive, swap them out through HR.
- [ ] Review the access list with the employee and remove anything they do not need. This is the last step before HR closes the joiners request.
