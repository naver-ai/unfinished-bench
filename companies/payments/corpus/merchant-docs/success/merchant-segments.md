# Merchant segments and coverage

Owner: Merchant success (Europe). Last edited by Bilal Yamamoto (Onboarding Specialist).

This page describes how we organise merchants into segments and which team covers them. It is intended for anyone in Merchant success, onboarding, or sales who needs to know who owns a merchant relationship or how a handover should work.

## Segments

| Segment | Typical book size | Primary coverage | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-serve | Up to €50k monthly volume | Merchant support (helpdesk), no named account manager | Onboarded through the KYB onboarding tool without a sales touchpoint. Can use the merchant dashboard, payment links, and invoicing. |
| Growth | €50k–€250k monthly volume, or API/platform partners below enterprise thresholds | Merchant success (Europe) from Dublin; merchant success (US) from Austin | Named account manager. Quarterly reviews via the account-reviews playbook. |
| Enterprise | Above €250k monthly volume, or strategic platform partners | Merchant success (Europe) from Dublin for EEA/UK; merchant success (US) from Austin for US sponsor bank merchants | Dedicated account manager, custom pricing, and a named solutions engineer for integration work. |

Coverage follows the merchant's registered entity location for the primary relationship, not where their traffic originates. A US-registered merchant using the sponsor bank programme is covered from Austin even if their users are in Europe. EEA/UK merchants are covered from Dublin. There are a few legacy exceptions where a merchant was onboarded through the Vilnius office before the segment model was introduced — those are still covered from Dublin but the account manager on record may be based in Vilnius.

## Book sizes

Rough guidance, not a hard rule:

- Self-serve: no per-merchant book size; support handles them in the queue.
- Growth: an account manager typically carries 40–60 merchants. Above that, discuss with Anders Holm before taking on more.
- Enterprise: an account manager typically carries 10–20 merchants, depending on how much custom integration work is involved.

The split between growth and enterprise is reviewed at each quarterly merchant review. If a growth merchant consistently exceeds €250k monthly for two consecutive quarters, the account manager should propose moving them to enterprise and bring that up in the next segment review.

## Handover from sales

Sales owns the merchant until the account is live (first successful transaction on the core ledger). After that, ownership moves to Merchant success.

For self-serve merchants there is no sales handover — onboarding is fully automated through the KYB onboarding tool. If a self-serve merchant later asks for a named contact, the helpdesk can raise a request to move them to growth.

For growth and enterprise:

1. Sales logs a handover ticket in tickets with the merchant's segment, expected monthly volume, and any notes from the sales process.
2. Merchant success assigns an account manager within two working days of the ticket being created.
3. The account manager does a welcome call within five working days of assignment, using the onboarding checklist for managers as a guide.
4. The merchant's plan and fee configuration is already set by sales in the back office before the handover ticket is raised. Merchant success should check that this matches what was sold, but changes after handover go through the normal plan-change billing process.

If the handover ticket is missing expected volume or a clear segment, assign it to the Onboarding Specialist (currently Bilal Yamamoto) to follow up with sales before it goes to an account manager.

## Segment changes

Merchants can move between segments. The account manager proposes the change at the segment review, and Anders Holm confirms the new coverage.

When a merchant moves segments, the old account manager hands over to the new one with a short handover note in tickets covering open items, recent account reviews, and anything the new account manager should follow up on. This is normally done within a week of the segment change being approved.
