# Working across Rotterdam and Denver

**Owner:** People team  
**Last edited by:** Carlos Gashi (People Partner)

## Context

The natural overlap between Rotterdam and Denver is short — roughly Rotterdam late afternoon to Denver morning. With most of the company in Rotterdam and a growing Denver office, recurring meetings that span both sites have to fit into that narrow window, which is inconvenient for one side or the other.

## What we decided

- **Keep recurring cross-office meetings inside the overlap window.** If a meeting cannot fit, it should either alternate between a Rotterdam-friendly and a Denver-friendly time, or be recorded and made async.
- **Record all cross-office meetings.** The default is to record and share the link in the channel. This is not optional for meetings where one site is outside the overlap.
- **Write decisions and discussion in the channel, not in DMs.** Use the relevant team channel (e.g. #data-eng, #ml-platform, #support) so the other office can catch up. DMs are for quick coordination only — if a decision is made there, summarise it in the channel.
- **Rotate the inconvenient slot.** For recurring meetings that have to sit at the edge of the overlap, the side that takes the early or late slot switches each quarter. The People team tracks this; reach out to Carlos if a meeting needs rebalancing.
- **Mind the daylight saving gap.** The US and Europe change clocks on different dates, so the overlap shifts for about a week or two each transition. Check the meeting times for the affected weeks and adjust or cancel if the window disappears.

## Loose ends

- Some teams still have recurring 1:1s outside the overlap that have drifted into async email threads. Those should move to the channel or be recorded.
- The Denver office is still growing its headcount; revisit whether the overlap assumptions hold as team composition changes.
