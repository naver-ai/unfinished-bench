# Social media

**Owner:** Corporate communications  
**Last edited by:** Sabine Kurz

**Who runs the company accounts?**

The main accounts (@Kessendorf, plus the site accounts for Reutlingen, Telford and Greenville) are run by Corporate communications. Eleni Demir schedules posts and answers DMs during working hours. If you see something that needs a fast reply outside those hours, post in #press-desk.

**How do I get something posted on the company channels?**

Send the content to Corporate communications via the intranet form. Include a suggested visual and the date it should go out. We maintain the content calendar in the comms-docs folder (`comms-docs/web/website-updates.md` has the schedule for cross-posting). We aim to confirm placement within two working days. If it's time-sensitive, flag it in the form and also message Eleni directly.

**Can I post about my own work on my personal profile?**

Yes — we encourage it. Use the hashtag we've agreed for product launches and site news, and feel free to share the company account's posts. If you want to write a longer post about a project, check the brand basics first (`comms-docs/brand/brand-basics.md`) for tone and imagery. Do not share internal metrics or customer-specific details.

**What do I do if a customer complains on social media?**

Do not reply to the thread yourself. Capture a screenshot and send it to Corporate communications through the intranet form with the subject "Social complaint." We will route it to the right team and reply. If the complaint is about a shipment or a product fault, the Customer orders or Quality teams may ask for the RMA number so we can reference it in the public reply. Do not delete the comment.

**Are there accounts for the calibration lab or field service?**

The lab and field service teams have their own {{SOCIAL_NETWORK_1}} pages that were set up locally. Those are not managed by Corporate communications. If you manage one of those pages, please follow the brand basics and get in touch with us before posting anything that mentions a customer by name.
