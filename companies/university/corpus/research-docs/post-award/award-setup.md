# Setting up a new award

**Owner:** Research Finance (post-award)  
**Last edited by:** Tomas Herrera (Research Finance Assistant)

This covers what happens once a funder has confirmed they will award us money: accepting the award, setting up the project code and budget lines, the start-up meeting, and recruiting staff onto the grant.

## 1. Confirm the award

1.  The Research & Innovation Services contact for the bid forwards the funder's award notification to Research Finance (post-award) at the shared mailbox.
2.  Check the notification against the costing saved in the research grants system. Flag any difference in value, duration, or budget headings to the PI and the school research administrator.
3.  If the funder requires a signed acceptance form, the PI signs it and returns it through the same mailbox. Log that it has gone back before the funder's stated deadline.

## 2. Set up the project code

1.  Open the research grants system and go to **Awards > New award**.
2.  Enter the funder reference, the internal costing reference, the PI's staff ID, and the school or centre that will hold the budget.
3.  Set the start and end dates exactly as in the award notification. If the funder allows a no-cost extension later, that is a separate change request.
4.  Save the award. The system generates the project code — note it on the award record and send it to the PI and the school research administrator by email.
5.  Assign a research finance officer to the award from the post-award team. This is the person the PI and administrator should contact for the life of the grant.

## 3. Add budget lines

1.  In the award record, open **Budget** and add lines matching the headings in the funder's approved budget: staff, equipment, travel, consumables, and any indirect costs.
2.  Use the funder's own headings where they differ from ours — the ledger needs to reconcile to their payment schedule.
3.  Check the VAT treatment before saving. The VAT notes in the finance docs cover the common cases; if in doubt, ask the finance team before the start-up meeting.
4.  Save and mark the budget as complete. Do not leave the award in draft once the start date has passed.

## 4. Start-up meeting

1.  Book a start-up meeting with the PI, the school or centre research administrator, and the assigned research finance officer. Offer a video call if the PI is at the Malaysia campus.
2.  The research finance officer runs through:
    - the project code and how to find it in the system;
    - the budget lines and which headings are fixed versus flexible;
    - how to raise a purchase request against the award;
    - the reporting calendar for the funder and any internal reporting dates;
    - what happens when the award ends (closedown is covered in the reporting and closedown note).
3.  The PI confirms who will be the named contact for finance queries if they are away.
4.  Send a short summary of the meeting to the PI and the administrator, copying the research finance officer.

## 5. Recruiting staff onto the grant

1.  If the award includes staff costs, the PI recruits through the normal HR route. The research grants system records the posts funded by the award.
2.  When the post is filled, the school administrator raises a request through the research grants system to add the staff member to the award. The research finance officer checks the start date and salary against the budget line before approving.
3.  If the post is part-time or the start date is later than the award start, adjust the budget line to reflect the actual commitment and note it on the award record.

## If it goes wrong

- **Award notification does not match the costing.** Do not set up the project code until the PI and Research & Innovation Services have confirmed which figure is correct. Raise it with Graham if the discrepancy is more than a small rounding difference.
- **Funder delays sending the signed contract.** Set up the award with the dates from the notification, but do not spend against it until the contract is signed. Tell the PI in writing.
- **PI does not respond to meeting invites.** Email the school research administrator and ask them to chase. If it still does not move, tell Paul Brannigan's team so they can take it up with the faculty.
- **Budget line needs to change after setup.** The research finance officer can make minor transfers between lines. Anything that changes the total value, or moves money into equipment from another heading, needs the funder's approval unless the award terms say otherwise.
