# Examiner quality audits

Owner: Claims · Last edited by Keisha Pruitt (Claims Examiner)

## Monthly samples

Each Claims examiner with adjudication or adjustment authority is sampled once per calendar month. Sample size is 25 claims per examiner, drawn by the Claims Operations Lead from claims touched in the prior month. The sample is pulled from the claims adjudication system, spread across claim types (professional, facility, institutional) and across the lines of business Kellsbrook Health Plan serves. Chiara Sorensen assigns the review to a peer examiner who did not work the original claim.

## Accuracy targets

| Measure | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Financial accuracy | 98% | Dollar amount paid or denied is correct against the benefit plan, fee schedule and prior authorization record |
| Procedural accuracy | 95% | Correct claim status, timely filing logic, COB order, denial reason code, and documentation captured in the claim notes |
| Coding accuracy | 96% | CPT, HCPCS, ICD-10 and modifier usage matches the supporting record |

Financial and procedural errors are counted separately. A claim can be financially accurate and still fail procedural accuracy (for example, correct payment but a missing note about a coordination-of-benefits conversation with the member). Coding accuracy is checked on a subset — the first 10 professional claims in each sample that have a procedure code — and combined with the charge capture reviews in the revenue cycle queue when the same claim appears in both reviews.

## Feedback and corrective action

Findings are entered in the adjudication system as an audit note linked to the claim, then the examiner receives a summary through their Claims team lead. Errors are grouped into:

- one-off (no pattern, no further action beyond the note)
- recurring (same examiner, same error type in two of the last four monthly samples) — requires the examiner to redo the relevant section of the claims adjudication training in the learning management system; their next monthly sample is doubled
- systemic (same error pattern across three or more examiners) — raised to Janet Kowalik and the Claims team in #claims-ops

## Calibration sessions

Every other month, Chiara Sorensen runs a calibration session over chat with the senior examiners (Lin and Tessa). The session reviews five claims where reviewers disagreed in the previous month's samples. The goal is to align on how the adjudication rules apply at the edges — partial approvals, bundling, timely filing extensions, and how to document a correction when the original claim was already paid.
