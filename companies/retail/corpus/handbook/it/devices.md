# Laptops and phones

**Owner:** IT & service desk  
**Last edited by:** Anika Torres (IT Manager)

**Q: What laptop will I get?**  
Standard kit is a 14" {{LAPTOP_2}} with 16GB RAM and a 256GB SSD. The US e-commerce team in Columbus gets the same model — no separate hardware pool. If you need more RAM or a bigger screen for design or video work, put a request through the helpdesk and we'll sort it.

**Q: Can I get a company phone?**  
Only if your role needs it — store ops managers, wholesale account leads, and anyone on the on-call rota for payments or platform. Everyone else uses their own phone with the chat and mailer apps installed. If you're on the rota, you'll get a handset when you join the rota and hand it back when you rotate off.

**Q: My laptop is slow / battery dies in an hour — what do I do?**  
Raise it via the helpdesk. We'll run diagnostics remotely first. If it's a battery issue and the laptop is under two years old, we'll replace the battery. Over two years, we'll usually swap the whole machine. Loaner laptops are available from IT in Leeds and Dublin; the Wakefield DC has a small stock too.

**Q: I've lost my laptop or phone — what now?**  
Report it to the helpdesk immediately, then call the IT line (it's on the back of your badge). We'll remotely wipe the device if it's company-owned and revoke its access. For personal phones with Mossgrove apps, use the self-service portal to sign out and wipe work data. The security team will want a note of what was on the device — don't delay this, it's part of the incident reporting process.

**Q: Can I use my personal laptop for work?**  
For reading email and chat, yes, but nothing else. No access to ledgerctl, the CRM, the analytics warehouse, or any customer data from personal devices. The acceptable use policy in the handbook covers this — when in doubt, use your work machine.

**Q: How do I print from my laptop in a store?**  
Store printers are set up for the POS back office only. If you're visiting a store and need a document printed, ask the store manager to print it from their terminal — don't try to connect your laptop to the store network. Wholesale staff covering trade shows should expense printing at the venue rather than asking stores.

**Q: My laptop is getting replaced — what happens to my files?**  
Anything in the shared drives or the analytics warehouse stays put. Local files are your responsibility — copy what you need to the shared drives before you hand the old machine in. IT will wipe the old one as part of the standard decommissioning process.
