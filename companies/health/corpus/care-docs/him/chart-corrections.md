# Amendments, late entries and corrections

**Owner:** Health information management (medical records)  
**Last edited by:** Rahul Pereira (Senior Inpatient Coder)

**Purpose:** This document sets out how entries in the EHR are corrected, how late entries are added, how patients may request amendments to their record, and how misfiled documents found during scanning are handled.

**Scope:** Applies to all clinicians and HIM staff at Kellsbrook Health System, including the Cork medical centre. For scanned paper documents, see chartarc processes; for release of information, see release-of-information.md.

---

1. **Correcting an existing EHR entry**

   1.1 Clinicians must not overwrite or delete an existing entry. Corrections are made by adding a new entry that references the original note and states what is being corrected and why.

   1.2 The corrected entry must be dated and timed automatically by the EHR. Do not alter the original entry's timestamp.

   1.3 If the original entry was signed, the correcting entry must also be signed. Un-signed corrections are not visible to other care teams until signed.

   1.4 For entries that are part of an active hospital stay, the correcting clinician should notify the patient's attending provider through the EHR's normal communication route.

2. **Late entries**

   2.1 A late entry is any entry added after the clinical encounter has ended and the record has been closed for that encounter.

   2.2 Late entries must be clearly marked "LATE ENTRY" at the top, include the date and time of the original encounter, and state when the entry is actually being made.

   2.3 If the late entry is made more than 72 hours after the encounter, the clinician should add a brief note explaining the reason for the delay.

   2.4 HIM does not pre-approve late entries. The clinician's supervisor or department lead may request a review if late entries become frequent for a particular provider.

3. **Patient requests to amend the record**

   3.1 Patients may request an amendment to their record through the patient portal, in writing, or by speaking to any HIM staff member.

   3.2 HIM must acknowledge the request within 5 working days and respond with a decision within 30 working days. If more time is needed, the patient must be told in writing and given a revised date.

   3.3 If the request is approved, the amendment is added to the record as an addendum. The original content remains unchanged.

   3.4 If the request is denied, the patient may submit a statement of disagreement. That statement becomes part of the record and is included in any future release of information.

   3.5 HIM maintains a log of amendment requests in the HIM shared drive. The log is reviewed each quarter by the HIM Manager.

4. **Misfiled documents found during scanning**

   4.1 When a scanned document is found to belong to a different patient or encounter, the scanning technician must immediately correct the index in chartarc. Do not delete the image.

   4.2 If the document was scanned into the wrong patient's chart, HIM must review whether any release of information occurred for that patient after the misfile date. If so, follow the breach process in the privacy office.

   4.3 If the document's correct patient cannot be identified from the content, leave it in a "pending identification" bucket in chartarc and notify the Records Technician team. After 30 working days without identification, escalate to Georgia Marchetti.

   4.4 Document the correction in the chartarc audit trail. No separate form is needed.
