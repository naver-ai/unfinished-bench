# Research contracts

**Owner:** Research Contracts  
**Last edited by:** Laszlo Schmidt (Research Contracts Officer)

This page covers the main types of research agreement we handle, how long they usually take, and how to get one started. If you are unsure which type of agreement you need, contact Research Contracts before submitting the form.

## Types of agreement

| Type | When it's used | Lead negotiator |
|---|---|---|
| Collaboration agreement | Two or more parties working on a project, each contributing resources and sharing results | Naomi Ferrari (Research Contracts Manager) |
| Sub-contract | We are the lead partner and issue a sub-contract to a partner institution delivering part of the work | Laszlo Schmidt (Research Contracts Officer) |
| Research services | A client commissions us to carry out a piece of research and owns the results | Naomi Ferrari |
| Material transfer agreement | Sharing physical materials (samples, equipment, reagents) with an external party | Laszlo Schmidt |
| Non-disclosure agreement | Confidential discussions before a project is set up — usually a mutual NDA, sometimes one-way | Research Contracts team |

Magnus Laurent (Head of Research Contracts) handles agreements that need a more senior eye, including work over the standard thresholds or where the funder's terms need significant deviation from our templates.

## Turnaround

We aim for the following, counted from the day we receive a complete contract request form:

| Agreement type | Standard target |
|---|---|
| NDA | 3 working days |
| Material transfer agreement | 5 working days |
| Research services | 10 working days |
| Collaboration agreement | 15 working days |
| Sub-contract | 15 working days |

These are working days and assume the request form is complete and the other party responds promptly to queries. If the other side's counsel wants heavy changes, add time. We will tell you as soon as we know an agreement will run long, so you can factor it into the project start.

## Contract request form

Every agreement starts with the contract request form on the Research & Innovation Services pages of the staff intranet. The form asks for:

- project title and funder (or proposed funder)
- whether the work is pre-award or post-award
- the named principal investigator and department
- the proposed start date and duration
- which type of agreement is needed
- the other party's full legal name and registered address (the finance system needs this for the supplier record — see `finance-docs/ap/supplier-setup.md`)

Please include the full file from the research grants system, not just the award number, so we can see the costing and any funder terms already applied.

## Who signs

The university's signing authority for research agreements sits with Research & Innovation Services. In practice:

- Magnus Laurent can sign any research agreement up to the institutional limit.
- Naomi Ferrari and Laszlo Schmidt can sign NDAs and material transfer agreements, and collaboration agreements up to the research services threshold.
- Paul Brannigan (Director of Research & Innovation Services) signs agreements above the threshold or where Magnus is unavailable.

Signatories act on behalf of Aldercombe University Higher Education Corporation. Aldercombe Estates Services Ltd and other subsidiary companies need their own arrangements, so route anything that touches a subsidiary through Research Contracts before the form goes anywhere.

## Chasing and updates

We log every request in the research grants system once the form arrives. If you need a status update, reply to the ticket we raise in the IT service desk portal — that keeps the history with the request. We post a weekly summary of what is waiting and what has gone out in #research-office.
