# Who can sign what

**Owner:** Legal  
**Last edited by:** Mikael Gashi (Legal Counsel)  
**Applies to:** All Kilbarra entities and offices (Dublin, Vilnius, Austin)

This matrix covers contracts entered into by Kilbarra itself. It does not cover merchant-facing terms (see the merchant dashboard and onboarding flow), partner programme agreements (see `merchant-docs/partners/partner-programme.md`), or employment documents (see HR). All signatories act in their named capacity; a person cannot delegate their authority under this matrix to someone below the threshold.

## Contract request form

Before any contract is drafted, reviewed, or circulated for signature, the requestor must submit the contract request form through the back office. The form captures the counterparty, the type of agreement, the estimated value, and the proposed signatory. Legal will not review a draft without a form reference. For anything above EUR 25,000, the form must also name the budget owner and the business sponsor.

## Board reserved matters

The following require a board resolution before any signature, regardless of the table above:

- Contracts with a total value above EUR 250,000 or with a term longer than three years
- Any agreement that creates a new line of business or a material new market (for example, a new acquiring scheme)
- Any agreement that indemnifies a counterparty beyond Kilbarra's standard commercial terms
- Any disposal or acquisition of a business or material asset
- Borrowing, guarantees, or security over Kilbarra assets outside the treasury policy

Board approval must be minuted and the resolution attached to the contract record in the back office. Legal will not issue a signature page without the resolution reference.

## Counterparties with their own requirements

Some counterparties require signature by a named officer or a specific title. The contract request form should capture that requirement at submission. If the counterparty insists on a title not listed above, Legal will confirm the nearest equivalent authority and, where the value is above the listed threshold, escalate to the Chief Executive Officer. For US merchants served through the sponsor bank programme, the sponsor bank's own form may name a Kilbarra officer; Legal keeps a current list of those named officers and reviews it at each quarterly access review.

## Execution mechanics

Kilbarra uses electronic signature for most agreements; wet ink is required only where the counterparty or the sponsor bank requires it. The signatory listed in the matrix must personally apply their signature — no shared mailboxes, no pre-signed pages. The executed copy is filed in the contract repository by Legal, and the counterparty is notified through the standard onboarding or procurement flow. If a contract needs to be signed outside business hours, the signatory may do so, but the contract record must be completed on the next working day.
