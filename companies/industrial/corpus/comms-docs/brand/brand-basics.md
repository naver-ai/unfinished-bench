# Brand basics

Owner: Marketing & product management · Last edited by: Paloma Suzuki

## Company name

- Write the full name as **Kessendorf Motion & Messtechnik GmbH** on first mention in external documents. Use **Kessendorf** afterwards.
- Group companies: **Kessendorf UK Ltd** (Telford) and **Kessendorf Inc.** (Greenville, SC). Use these forms in customer-facing text; do not abbreviate to "Kessendorf UK" or "Kessendorf US" in formal material.
- The ampersand in "Motion & Messtechnik" is part of the name; do not replace it with "and".
- In German-language text, the legal form is "GmbH"; in English-language text, write it out once as "GmbH (limited liability company)" only if the audience may not be familiar.

## Logo use

- Use the master logo files from the intranet brand page — never recreate or redraw.
- Clear space: keep a margin equal to the height of the "K" on all sides; nothing may intrude into that space.
- Minimum width for the horizontal logo: 25 mm in print, 90 px on screen. Below that, use the compact mark (K only).
- The logo appears on white, light grey (#F5F5F5) or black backgrounds only. No other background colours.
- Do not rotate, add effects, change proportions, or place the logo on photography without asking the brand owner.
- If a customer insists on a different logo treatment, raise it with Marketing & product management before agreeing.

## Colours

- Primary: Kessendorf blue **#005A9C** ({{COLOR_SYSTEM}} 2945 C). Use for headings, charts, covers, and the logo.
- Secondary: Signal grey **#6B6B6B** for body accents and supporting graphics.
- Accent: Safety orange **#E87722** — use sparingly, e.g. one highlight element per page or slide.
- Text on Kessendorf blue must be white; text on white must be dark grey **#333333**, not black.
- Charts: use the three colours plus two tints of the blue (at 60 % and 30 %). Do not introduce additional palette colours without the brand owner's sign-off.

## Product naming

- Motion controllers: **MC series** — e.g. MC 320, MC 540. Always "MC" in capitals, no periods.
- Direct-drive units: **DU series** — e.g. DU 80, DU 140. "DU" in capitals.
- Torque transducers: **TX series** — e.g. TX 500, TX 2000. The product range is "TX series", not "TX transducers" or "TXs".
- In running text, use the full range name on first mention ("MC series motion controller"), then the short form.
- The calibration lab is "Kessendorf Calibration Lab" (accredited per DIN EN ISO/IEC 17025). Do not call it "the lab" in customer-facing copy without the Kessendorf prefix.

## Documents and templates

- {{WORD_PROCESSOR}}, {{SLIDES_APP}} and PDF templates are on the intranet under Brand → Templates. Use the current revision; the file name ends with the year.
- Slides: title slide uses Kessendorf blue background with white logo; content slides are white with the blue title bar.
- Technical datasheets and manuals follow the engineering template from the PLM — do not restyle them with the marketing template.
- Every external document footer carries the company name, the site (Reutlingen, Telford or Greenville), and the document revision.
- For customer-facing PDFs, set the PDF title and author metadata before exporting; check the file name matches the document title convention.
