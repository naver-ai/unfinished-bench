# Talking to press and posting on social

**Owner:** Press & comms  
**Last edited by:** Ilse Jaramillo (Communications Manager)

**A journalist emailed me asking about our store openings — what do I do?**
Don't reply. Forward it to Press & comms (press@mossgroveoutdoor.co.uk) with the subject "Journalist enquiry" and we'll pick it up. Rosa or Simone will handle the response; if it's about finances, they'll pull in the Finance team via the usual route.

**Can I talk to a journalist at a trade show or industry event if they just ask casual questions?**
No. Even casual chats count as speaking for Mossgrove. If a journalist approaches you at an event, be polite, deflect, and point them to our press contacts. This applies to the wholesale arm too — the independent shops we supply sometimes get approached, and the message is the same.

**What about posting about work on my personal social accounts?**
You're welcome to share public company news (our website posts, campaign links, store openings) as long as you don't add commentary on strategy, sales, or anything not already public. If you're posting from a personal account, don't use the Mossgrove logo as your avatar. Don't tag stores or colleagues in a way that implies you're speaking for us.

**I have a {{SOCIAL_NETWORK_1}} connection in the media who's asked about our supply chain. Can I answer?**
No. Forward to Press & comms. The same rules apply to {{SOCIAL_NETWORK_1}} DMs as to email.

**A customer posted a complaint on {{SOCIAL_NETWORK_3}}/{{SOCIAL_NETWORK_2}} and tagged our store account. Can I reply from my personal account?**
No. Store accounts are managed by the comms team and store social leads only. If you see a customer complaint that looks urgent (safety, legal, or a data issue), message the comms team in chat and we'll route it.

**I've been asked to be photographed for a local news piece about my volunteering. Can I mention where I work?**
You can say you work at Mossgrove, but don't discuss what we sell, our plans, or anything about the business. If the article focuses on your volunteering, that's fine — just don't position yourself as a company spokesperson.

**Who can approve a press release or a statement?**
Only Rachel (CMO) or Rosa can approve external statements. Ilse or Simone can draft them, and the comms team coordinates sign-off. Don't draft your own statement and send it to a journalist — that creates confusion about who speaks for us.

**What if a journalist calls the store and asks for the manager?**
Store managers should take the caller's name and number, explain they can't comment, and pass it to Press & comms. The store manager should not answer questions about stock, pricing, or anything else. Same goes for the Wakefield DC and the Columbus office — forward all media calls to press@mossgroveoutdoor.co.uk.
