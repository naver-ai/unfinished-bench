# IT service desk

The IT Service Desk is the first point of contact for all IT issues and requests from staff and students. We handle tickets raised through the IT service desk portal, calls to the service desk line, and queries that come in via the `#it-help` chat channel.

Our service hours cover the standard working day, with a reduced service during university closure periods. The desk is staffed from both Hollins Hill and City campus; the Malaysia campus is supported by the same queue during UK hours, with local cover arranged through the Malaysia campus IT Manager where needed.

## Priorities and target times

| Priority | Description | First response | Resolution target |
|----------|-------------|----------------|-------------------|
| P1 | Service down affecting a whole campus, faculty or major system; safety-critical issue | Immediate | Within 4 working hours |
| P2 | Service impaired for a large group, or a single user unable to work | Within 1 working hour | Within 1 working day |
| P3 | Standard request or issue affecting an individual | Within 4 working hours | Within 3 working days |
| P4 | Minor issue, cosmetic defect, or a request with no agreed deadline | Within 1 working day | Within 10 working days |

P1 and P2 tickets are visible on the main dashboard in the tickets tool and are reviewed at the morning handover. If a P1 is raised outside service hours, the on-call number in the tickets tool redirect page should be used.

## The tickets tool

All work is logged in the tickets tool. Calls and chat queries must be turned into tickets by the person taking them — if you pick up a query in `#it-help` and resolve it in the channel, still log the ticket afterwards so we have a record. Email to the service desk address is auto-logged.

Every ticket needs a clear summary, the affected person or group, the site (Hollins Hill, City campus, Kuala Lumpur, or Kestrel Vale Academy), and the system or service involved. Use the category list rather than free text where possible — it feeds the reporting we do each quarter.

## Assignment groups

Tickets are assigned to one of the following groups:

| Group | Responsible for |
|-------|-----------------|
| IT Service Desk | First-line triage, password resets, MFA enrolment, standard software installs, access requests |
| IT Infrastructure & Operations | Servers, storage, networking, backups, the estates helpdesk integration, Malaysia campus infrastructure |
| IT Applications | The student record system, VLE, finance system, HR and payroll system, timetabling and room booking, supporter CRM, library system |
| IT Security | Access reviews, suspected compromises, infosec incidents, phishing reports forwarded from `#infosec` |

Most tickets should be resolved at the desk. If you need to escalate, add a note saying what you have tried and what you think the cause might be, then reassign. Do not leave a ticket sitting in the desk queue that belongs to another group — pass it on the same day.

## Escalation

Escalate to IT Infrastructure & Operations via Reza Harper's team for anything involving servers, storage or connectivity. Escalate to IT Applications for issues with the student record system, VLE, finance system, HR and payroll, timetabling, room booking, supporter CRM, expenses tool, accommodation booking system, or the library system. If in doubt, ask in `#it-services` before assigning.

For P1 and P2 issues, tell the IT Service Desk Manager (Alana Haddad) and the Director of IT Services (Priti Desai) as soon as you have confirmed the scope of the incident. They will decide whether a major incident communication is needed.

## Major incident communications

When a major incident is declared, the IT Service Desk is responsible for keeping the communication channels updated. A message goes to `#general` and `#it-help` with a summary of what is affected, what is being done, and when the next update will be posted. Updates should go out at least every 2 hours while the incident is open, or more often if the situation changes.

Students are notified through the Student Hub if the incident affects student-facing systems. The Kestrel Vale Academy office is contacted directly for any issue affecting the school's systems, and the Malaysia campus management team is copied on communications that affect Kuala Lumpur.

The Comms team (Tom Becker) will help draft wording for anything that goes to a wider audience, such as the university website or social media. Do not post to external channels without going through them first.
