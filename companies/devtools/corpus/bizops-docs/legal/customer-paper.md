# Customer paper and redlines

**Owner:** Legal  
**Last edited by:** Filipa Moura

## What is "customer paper"?

Customer paper is any contract, amendment, order form, or other legal document that comes from a customer (or their procurement team) instead of from our standard templates. It includes their MSAs, DPAs, security exhibits, and any attachment they ask us to sign.

## Where do I find our standard terms?

Our standard terms live in the CRM under the account record, attached to the opportunity. The sales team also keeps a copy in the deal desk folder. If you cannot find what you need, ask Legal.

## How are redlines routed?

Sales or customer success starts a ticket in the helpdesk and attaches the customer's paper plus a short summary of what the customer is asking for. The ticket queues to Legal through the standard support flow. If the request is urgent, ping #contracts-help in chat and someone will pick it up. For enterprise deals, the deal desk review happens first; Legal only sees the redline after that.

## What are the typical asks from enterprise customers?

- **Liability caps.** Customers often want a cap that is lower than our standard. The standard cap is in the MSA. Anything below that needs Commercial Counsel approval. We do not do uncapped liability, and we do not cap indemnification for IP or confidentiality breaches.
- **Uptime commitments.** The SLA is published on the status page and in the customer-facing docs. Customers sometimes ask for a higher commitment or a different credit structure. Those asks go to the product team through the normal change process — Legal does not approve uptime numbers.
- **Security questionnaires.** These come through the helpdesk too. We have a standard response pack maintained by the security team. If the questionnaire asks something not covered by the pack, route it to #security. Do not fill in custom answers yourself.
- **Data processing.** If a customer sends their own DPA, send it to Legal. Hollis Brandt reviews DPAs, and the terms we agree are recorded on the account in the CRM.

## What about NDAs?

Customers sometimes send their own NDA instead of signing ours. Standard practice: we sign a mutual NDA on our template. If the customer insists on theirs, Legal reviews it. NDAs with non-standard terms (for example, a unilateral NDA in their favour, or an overly broad definition of confidential information) need Commercial Counsel sign-off. We do not sign NDAs that restrict our ability to serve other customers or that mention specific competitors.

## Where are signed contracts stored?

Signed contracts are uploaded to the CRM against the account and opportunity. The original PDF plus any amendments should be attached to the same record. If you have a signed document that is not in the CRM, send it to Legal and we will file it. We do not keep contracts in chat or in personal drives.

## What if a customer asks for a change to our standard terms that is not covered here?

Ask. The fastest way is to comment on the helpdesk ticket or post in #contracts-help. For anything that changes financial terms, the billing team needs to know — they track plan changes and pricing through the admin console, and a contract change that is not reflected there will cause problems at renewal time.

## Who signs what?

The signing authority table is in the handbook. For customer paper, sales leadership signs order forms within their approval limit; everything else goes through Legal. The CRM record should show who signed and when.
