# Who does what

This page gives a quick orientation to the board functions and the departments under them. It is not a substitute for the HR system — the HR system is the authoritative source for who sits in which role and how teams are structured. If you need to find a person, start there.

Daniel Hollis is the Chief Executive Officer. The board members below each own a set of departments; the Group General Counsel, the Chief People Officer and the Head of Corporate Communications also report to the CEO.

## Board and corporate functions

| Board function | Departments | What they own | Main channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chief Executive Officer | Corporate communications, press office | Company direction; press, analyst relations and internal communications | #general |
| Chief Financial Officer | Group controlling, treasury, accounts payable, group purchasing, Finance (UK), Finance (US), payroll | Reporting and close; cash, banks and FX; suppliers and purchasing; payroll | #finance-ops |
| Chief Operating Officer | The three plants, production planning, quality, logistics & distribution centre, Facilities (UK), HSE | Production at all three sites; quality and calibration; the Telford DC; health and safety | #planning |
