# Expenses

**Owner:** Finance  
**Last edited by:** Tom Whitlock, Financial Controller  

**Purpose.** This page sets out what Mossgrove staff can claim, how to submit expenses, and when reimbursement lands in pay. It applies to all employees and contractors on the Mossgrove payroll unless their contract says otherwise.

**Scope.** Covers business travel, client and supplier meetings, store visits, and incidental costs. It does not cover company card purchases made through the procurement team — those go through the usual approval route in the expenses tool.

## 1. What can be claimed

1.1 Reasonable and necessarily incurred costs for Mossgrove business only. This includes rail and air fares (standard class), hotels, taxis where public transport is not practical, and meals while travelling on business.

1.2 Mileage for using your own car on Mossgrove business is claimable at the {{TAX_AUTHORITY_1|code}} approved mileage rate. Bicycle mileage is also claimable at the {{TAX_AUTHORITY_1|code}} rate. Mileage is paid for journeys between Mossgrove sites, or from home to a non-regular workplace, but not for ordinary commuting.

1.3 Staff in stores who travel between stores, or to Wakefield DC or Leeds HQ, should claim mileage as above. Wholesale staff visiting independent shops or garden centres can claim the same rates.

1.4 Alcohol is not claimable. Evening meals are only claimable when you are away from your home site on Mossgrove business.

1.5 Personal items, upgrades, and anything that extends your trip for non-business reasons are not claimable.

## 2. Receipts

2.1 Receipts are required for every claim line over £5. For amounts under £5, a receipt is not needed but the expense must still be entered in the tool.

2.2 Attach a photo of the paper receipt, or the PDF receipt, to the expense line in the expenses tool. Do not email receipts to Finance — they are not matched that way.

2.3 If a receipt is lost, ask the supplier for a duplicate or a VAT invoice. If you genuinely cannot get one, add a note to the expense line explaining what happened. Finance may ask for more detail before approving.

## 3. Approval

3.1 Your line manager approves your expense report in the expenses tool before it reaches Finance. Reports without manager approval are not paid.

3.2 Managers should approve within a few working days. If a manager is away, their cover should approve — check the org overview in the handbook if you are unsure who that is.

3.3 Finance reviews approved reports and may return lines that are not clearly business-related or that breach this policy.

## 4. Company card vs out-of-pocket

4.1 Where a company card is available for a recurring cost (for example, a monthly train season ticket or a regular supplier lunch), use it rather than paying personally.

4.2 Out-of-pocket spending is reimbursed only after the report is approved and processed. If you are planning a large out-of-pocket cost (over £100), check with your line manager first.

## 5. Submitting claims

5.1 Use the expenses tool (the "SpendWise" portal on the finance intranet page). Enter each cost as a separate line, pick the right category, attach receipts, and submit. The tool is also where you book mileage — it calculates the rate automatically from the postcodes you enter.

5.2 Submit claims within 60 days of the cost being incurred. Claims older than that need a note from your line manager explaining the delay.

## 6. Reimbursement timing

6.1 Reimbursement is paid through the payroll run, not as a separate bank transfer. Claims approved by Finance before the payroll cut-off are paid on the next pay day; claims approved after the cut-off go into the following run.

6.2 The payroll calendar in the finance docs folder shows the cut-off dates for each run. If you need a claim paid urgently, speak to Payroll before the cut-off — they can sometimes add it to the current run if the report is fully approved and the receipt is attached.

## 7. Queries

7.1 Questions about what is claimable go to the Finance team via the helpdesk. Questions about the tool itself go to the same place — the helpdesk will route them.

7.2 Disputes about a rejected line should be raised with your line manager first, then Finance if needed.
