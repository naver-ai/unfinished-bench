# Press and social media

**Owner:** Marketing  
**Last edited by:** Harriet Lindgren (Product Marketing Manager)

**Who do I contact if a journalist calls or emails me?**

Forward it to the Marketing team via #general or by emailing the Marketing alias. Tessa Achebe or Omar Arslan will pick it up — don't reply to the journalist yourself, even to say "no comment." If it's urgent and Marketing is offline, raise it in #incidents so the on-call person can page someone.

**Can I talk to a journalist if I'm just answering a factual question about my own work?**

No. Only the comms people (Marketing) speak for Kilbarra. That covers everything from product questions to anything about merchants, volumes, or our financials. If a journalist asks about something you work on, the answer is "I'll put you in touch with our comms team" and then forward it on.

**What if a merchant's customer tags us on social media with a complaint or question?**

Don't engage directly. Point them to the merchant dashboard or the support contact form, and let the helpdesk handle it. If it looks like a payment problem or something time-sensitive, flag it in #merchant-success so the relevant team sees it. Never ask for or confirm account details over social media.

**What about a merchant who DMs me on {{SOCIAL_NETWORK_1}} or {{SOCIAL_NETWORK_3}}?**

Same as above — direct them to the support channels. If you're in Customer success or a commercial role and you already have a relationship with that merchant, you can acknowledge the message and offer to follow up through normal channels, but don't discuss anything account-specific on social media.

**Can I mention Kilbarra on my personal social media?**

Yes, within the acceptable use policy. You can say where you work and generally what you do (e.g., "I work on the payments platform" or "I'm in the Dublin office"). Don't share internal numbers, roadmaps, merchant names, or anything that isn't already public. If you're unsure whether something is public, check with Marketing.

**Do I need to say "opinions are my own" in my bio?**

It's a good idea if you talk about payments or fintech at all. It doesn't change the policy, but it helps people read your posts as yours. That said, the policy still applies even with a disclaimer — if you post something that reads like an official Kilbarra position, we'll ask you to take it down.

**I got a weird message from someone claiming to be a journalist. What do I do?**

Don't reply. Forward it to Marketing and, if it feels off, also flag it to the security team. Journalists sometimes probe for information through employees, and we'd rather you not answer anything before Marketing has vetted the request.

**Can I share Kilbarra blog posts or press releases?**

Yes, sharing public content is fine. If you're writing something original about Kilbarra (like a {{SOCIAL_NETWORK_1}} post about a project you worked on), keep it to what's already been announced and run it by Marketing first — particularly if it mentions merchants, numbers, or future plans.
