# Brakwater handbook

Owner: People team · Last edited by Carlos Gashi

## What's here

- **Company** — about Brakwater, who does what, glossary.
- **People & office** — leave, expenses, travel, working across Rotterdam and Denver, onboarding checklist for managers, performance conversations.
- **IT & security** — getting access to systems, passwords and MFA, acceptable use, data classification, reporting a security incident.
- **Legal & comms** — contracts and who signs them, what to do if a journalist contacts you.

Department runbooks are not in this repo: they live in `finance-docs`, `platform-docs`, `data-docs`, `ml-docs` and `commercial-docs`, each with its own README.

## Proposing a change

- Open a PR against this repo. If it touches a page you do not own, tag the page owner listed at the top of the file; otherwise the People team reviews.
- Small fixes (typos, broken links, outdated team names) do not need a ticket. Anything that changes policy or process goes through the change tracker and the relevant team lead.
