# Halls of residence

**Owner:** Student Accommodation  
**Last edited by:** Chiara Blanco (Head of Student Accommodation)

## Our halls

Aldercombe has four halls on the Hollins Hill campus and three blocks on the City campus. All halls are managed by Student Accommodation; the residence life team is based across both sites and is the first point of contact for students living in halls.

| Hall | Site | Rooms | Reception |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hartwell Hall | Hollins Hill | En-suite and standard, mostly first years | Hartwell House, ground floor |
| Birch Hall | Hollins Hill | En-suite and studios | Birch Hall lodge, main entrance |
| Lowry Hall | Hollins Hill | Standard and accessible rooms | Lowry Hall common room |
| Quarry Hall | Hollins Hill | Studios and shared flats | Quarry Hall reception desk |
| City Campus Block A | City campus | En-suite, postgraduates and returning students | Block A foyer |
| City Campus Block B | City campus | Studios, some couples | Block B ground floor office |
| City Campus Block C | City campus | Standard and en-suite, first years | Block C, shared with Block B office |

## Room types and price bands

Room prices are set each financial year and published on the accommodation pages of the website. The booking system shows live availability and pricing for each hall; the price bands below are the broad categories used in the system.

| Band | Room type | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| A | Standard room, shared bathroom | All bills, contents insurance, internet |
| B | En-suite room | All bills, contents insurance, internet |
| C | Studio | All bills, contents insurance, internet |
| D | Adapted / accessible room | All bills, contents insurance, internet |

Students on the Kuala Lumpur campus are not allocated through this system; the Malaysia campus property team handles their accommodation separately.

## Residence life team

The residence life team is made up of Residence Life Coordinators and a team of student Residence Assistants. They run social events, support students through the year, and work with Student Services on wellbeing cases. Ada Andersen is the Residence Life Coordinator for Hollins Hill; the City campus coordinator post is currently covered by the same team — contact the Student Accommodation office if you need to reach either.

## Receptions and contact

Each hall has a reception desk during term time; outside term time the Hollins Hill desk at Hartwell House and the City campus desk at Block B are staffed on reduced hours. Lost keys, parcel queries and maintenance reports all go through the relevant reception, or through the estates helpdesk for repair work. The accommodation booking system is the record of all allocations; Ibrahim Reyes manages moves and transfers between rooms, and Shreya Mahlaba handles booking amendments and cancellations.

For anything not covered here, the Student Accommodation office is on the first floor of Hartwell House and the team is usually around during office hours on working days.
