# Acceptable use

**Owner:** Information security  
**Last edited by:** Patricia Lowell

## Purpose

This policy describes acceptable use of Kellsbrook Health's information systems, networks, and accounts. It applies to all staff, contractors, and volunteers who are granted access to Kellsbrook resources, regardless of location (South Bend, Valparaiso, Kalamazoo, or Cork).

## Scope

Kellsbrook systems include, but are not limited to: the EHR, patient and member portals, the claims adjudication system, the credentialing system, the interface engine, the data warehouse, tickets, chat, email, and any device issued by Kellsbrook.

## Policy

1. **Authorized use.** Kellsbrook systems are provided for business purposes. All access must be related to your role and consistent with the data classification policy.

1.1. You may use Kellsbrook systems to communicate with colleagues, patients, members, providers, and research partners as needed for your work.

1.2. Access to patient, member, or study data is limited to what your role requires. You may not browse records, claims, or study data out of curiosity or because you have access.

1.3. Accounts are issued to individuals. Do not share your password, badge, or sign-in credentials with anyone, including colleagues. Do not leave a workstation signed in when you step away.

2. **Personal use.** Incidental personal use of Kellsbrook systems is permitted if it does not interfere with your work, consume excessive resources, or violate any other policy.

2.1. Personal use of chat, email, or internet browsing should be kept to a minimum and must never involve patient, member, or study data.

2.2. Do not use Kellsbrook systems for personal financial transactions, gambling, or any activity that could embarrass Kellsbrook or create a conflict with our business.

2.3. Personal devices may connect to the guest Wi-Fi network only. Do not connect personal devices to the internal network or to clinical workstations.

3. **Monitoring.** Kellsbrook monitors its systems for security, operational, and compliance purposes. Activity on Kellsbrook systems is not private.

3.1. Monitoring includes, but is not limited to, review of login activity, email and chat content, file access, and network traffic.

3.2. By using Kellsbrook systems, you consent to this monitoring. Do not assume that deleted messages or files are gone.

4. **Generative AI tools.** Use of generative AI tools is limited to tools approved by Information security. A list of approved tools is available on the security page of the intranet.

4.1. You may use approved generative AI tools for drafting documents, summarizing text, or generating code, provided the output is reviewed by a person before it is used.

4.2. **Never enter PHI, member data, study participant data, or any confidential Kellsbrook information into a generative AI prompt.** This includes patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, claim details, and study subject IDs. If you are unsure whether something is confidential, treat it as confidential.

4.3. Output from generative AI tools must be checked for accuracy before it is shared. These tools make mistakes.

4.4. Do not use generative AI tools on Kellsbrook devices for personal purposes if doing so would mix personal and business data.

5. **USB drives and removable media.** USB drives and other removable media may only be used on Kellsbrook systems if they are encrypted and issued by Information security.

5.1. Unapproved USB drives found in Kellsbrook facilities should be turned in to Information security. Do not plug them into a Kellsbrook device.

5.2. If you use an approved encrypted USB drive, return it to the issuing team when it is no longer needed. Do not carry patient or member data on removable media outside Kellsbrook sites.

6. **Reporting.** If you believe a system has been misused or you see something that looks wrong, report it through tickets to the Information security team.

7. **Consequences.** Violations of this policy may result in loss of access, disciplinary action up to and including termination, or referral to law enforcement where appropriate.
