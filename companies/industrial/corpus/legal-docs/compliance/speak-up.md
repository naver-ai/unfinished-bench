# Speak-up and whistleblowing

**Owner:** Legal  
**Last edited by:** Delphine Esposito, Contract Manager

## Purpose and scope

This policy covers the Kessendorf speak-up hotline and web form, investigation of reported concerns, confidentiality and protection from retaliation, and site-specific provisions for Germany, the UK and the US. It applies to all employees, contractors and agency staff at all Kessendorf entities.

## Policy

1. **Reporting channels**
   1.1. Concerns may be reported through the speak-up hotline (see the intranet for the current number) or the web form linked from the intranet home page. Reports may be made anonymously where local law permits.
   1.2. Reports may also be made to a line manager, to HR, or directly to Legal. Anyone receiving a report must forward it to Legal within two working days.

2. **Investigation**
   2.1. Legal triages all reports and decides whether an investigation is needed. Investigations are led by Legal, with HR involvement for personnel matters and Group Internal Audit where financial misconduct is alleged.
   2.2. The investigation team may use the QMS to log corrective actions where the concern relates to product quality or safety, and the EHS reporting tool where it concerns workplace safety.

3. **Confidentiality and protection from retaliation**
   3.1. Reports are handled on a need-to-know basis. The identity of the reporting person is not disclosed without their consent, except where disclosure is required by law or necessary for the investigation.
   3.2. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence. This includes retaliation against witnesses and others who participate in an investigation.
   3.3. Anyone who knowingly makes a false report may be subject to disciplinary action.

4. **What happens after a report**
   4.1. The reporting person receives acknowledgement within five working days (if contact details were provided). They are informed of the outcome where possible and appropriate.
   4.2. Legal maintains a speak-up register in the QMS and reports aggregate figures to the Executive Board each quarter.

5. **Site-specific provisions**
   5.1. **Germany (Reutlingen):** The hotline and web form are operated in accordance with the German Whistleblower Protection Act. Reports may be made orally or in writing. The Works Council is informed of the policy and its application in accordance with local arrangements.
   5.2. **UK (Telford):** Reports may be made to Legal or to Owen Hughes in Risk & Compliance. The hotline is available during UK business hours. Concerns may also be raised under the UK's Public Interest Disclosure regime.
   5.3. **US (Greenville):** Reports may be made to Legal or to the Americas service team via the same hotline. Anonymous reporting is available. Retaliation concerns should be reported directly to Legal or to the US General Counsel's office.
