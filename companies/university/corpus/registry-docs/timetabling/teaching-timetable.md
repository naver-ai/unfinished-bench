## Building the next teaching timetable

- Schools submit room and module requests to Timetabling via the timetabling system by the deadline in the academic year calendar. Requests that miss the deadline go into the second pass, which runs after the exam timetable is drafted.
- The timetabling system runs the automated clash check across the Hollins Hill and City campuses. Kuala Lumpur teaching is timetabled locally and imported for reporting only.
- Timetabling publishes the draft timetable to the VLE before enrolment opens. Schools have a short window to raise objections through their Faculty Operations Manager — after that, the draft is frozen and published to students with their enrolment.

## Room requests and ad hoc bookings

- Standard teaching rooms are allocated by the timetabling system. Specialist rooms (labs, studios, the mock courtroom) are allocated by Timetabling in consultation with the faculty that owns them.
- Ad hoc room bookings for one-off sessions go through the room booking system. If a room is free and not a teaching priority, the booking is confirmed automatically; anything in a teaching slot goes to the Timetabling team to approve.
- The Student Accommodation team sometimes ask for rooms for residential events — those go through the lettings system instead, not the room booking system.

## Late changes

- Moving a module after the timetable is frozen requires a written request from the Faculty Operations Manager to Timetabling. We will try, but there is no guarantee the new slot will work for all students.
- Room moves for one-off sessions are fine — just update the room booking system so students see the change in the VLE.
- Any change that affects more than one school goes to the Timetabling Managers' group, which meets monthly during term time.
