# Who does what

A quick map of the teams at Mossgrove and where to find them in chat. For individual contact details, see the people directory in the HR system.

| Team | What they own | Chat channel |
|---|---|---|
| Executive | Company strategy, major decisions, board reporting | #general |
| People | Hiring, onboarding, payroll, benefits, employee relations | #general |
| Finance operations | Ledger, payments, AP/AR, month-end, treasury, tax | #finance-ops |
| Accounts payable | Supplier payments, invoicing, expense processing | #ap-team |
| Credit control | Wholesale collections, overdue accounts, credit limits | #credit-control |
| Payroll | Payroll runs, deductions, adjustments | #payroll |
| Customer support | Customer queries, returns, escalations, account closures | #support-leads |
| Disputes | Chargebacks, payment disputes, fraud queries | #disputes |
| Marketing | Campaigns, brand, email, paid media, social | #marketing |
| CRM | Customer data, segmentation, lifecycle comms | #crm |
| Brand studio | Photography, assets, creative | #brand-studio |
| Checkout | Checkout flow, payment pages, gift cards | #checkout |
| Pricing & promotions | Price lists, offers, wholesale pricing | #pricing |
| Platform & SRE | Infrastructure, deployments, monitoring, backups | #platform |
| Payments platform | billing-api, payment integrations, refunds | #payments-platform |
| Data engineering | Warehouse, pipelines, reporting, catalogue | #data-eng |
| ML recs | Recommendations models, GPU lab | #ml-recs |
| Security | Access reviews, incident response, vulnerability management | #security |
| IT & service desk | Devices, accounts, helpdesk | #it-help |
| Stores operations | Store processes, POS, cash handling | #stores-ops |
| Wholesale | Trade accounts, order booking, credit terms | #wholesale |

The retail stores themselves report through Stores operations; the Dublin office handles most of the tech and data work alongside the Leeds HQ teams. The Wakefield DC manages fulfilment and stock movement.

For anything not listed here, ask in #general or check the handbook index. If you're new, the onboarding checklist in the handbook has the first steps for getting access to the systems you'll need.
