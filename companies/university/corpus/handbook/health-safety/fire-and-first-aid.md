# Fire safety and first aid

**Owner:** Health, Safety & Environment  
**Last edited by:** Eero Silva (Health and Safety Adviser)

## Alarm testing and checks

- Fire alarms are tested weekly, on a rolling day per building — see the estates helpdesk calendar for the schedule at Hollins Hill, City campus, Kestrel Vale Academy and the Kuala Lumpur campus.
- Test results are logged in the estates helpdesk; report any fault or failed device straight away.
- Emergency lighting is checked monthly and serviced under the planned maintenance programme.

## Evacuation and assembly points

- Assembly points are marked on the fire action notices in each building. The main ones: Hollins Hill — the quad and the north car park; City campus — the riverside walk; Kestrel Vale — the lower playground; KL campus — the covered walkway.
- Building evacuations are led by floor marshals; follow their directions and do not re-enter until told.
- Personal emergency evacuation plans (PEEPs) are arranged through Health, Safety & Environment — contact the team to review or update one, including for short-term visitors or contractors.

## First aiders

- First aiders are listed on the noticeboards in each building and on the HSE intranet page. Check the list for your floor — cover varies.
- First aid boxes are kept at reception, in kitchens on each floor, and in the porters' offices at the halls of residence.
- Defibrillators are at main reception (Hollins Hill), the library desk (City campus), Kestrel Vale school office, and the KL campus security desk.

## Reporting

- Report all accidents and near misses through the estates helpdesk (category: HSE). The form takes about two minutes.
- If someone is taken to hospital from a university site, tell Health, Safety & Environment the same day — the team will follow up.
- RIDDOR-reportable incidents are handled by HSE; do not contact the enforcing authority yourself.
