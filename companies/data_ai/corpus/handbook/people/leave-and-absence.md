# Leave and absence

**Owner:** People team  
**Last edited by:** Femke Jansen (Head of People)  
**Applies to:** All Brakwater employees in the Netherlands and the United States (Colorado)

This document describes holiday allowance, how to book leave, sickness reporting, public holidays per office, and parental leave. If something is not covered here, ask the People team in #ask-people.

## 1. Holiday allowance

1.1 Employees on the Dutch payroll accrue 25 holiday days per calendar year on a full-time basis. Accrual is pro-rated for part-time contracts.

1.2 Employees on the US payroll (Colorado) accrue paid time off at 4.615 hours per two-week pay period, which gives approximately 15 days per year on a full-time schedule. Accrual is pro-rated for part-time contracts. Unused US PTO carries over to the next calendar year up to a cap of 240 hours.

1.3 Dutch holiday days must be taken within six months after the calendar year in which they accrued, unless a written agreement with the manager extends the carry-over window. The People team can advise on the statutory minimum if you are close to the cap.

1.4 New joiners accrue holiday from their first working day. The accrual is calculated automatically in the HR system.

1.5 On termination, accrued but unused Dutch holiday days are paid out in the final salary run. US PTO is paid out up to the 240-hour cap.

## 2. Booking leave in the HR system

2.1 All leave must be booked in the HR system before the leave starts. This includes holiday, parental leave, and any other paid absence categories that are set up in the system.

2.2 For planned leave, submit the request at least two working days in advance. Your manager approves or rejects the request in the HR system. If the manager has not acted by the working day before the leave starts, the request is auto-approved.

2.3 For unplanned leave (for example sickness or emergency care), book it in the HR system on the same working day, or the first working day you are back if you could not access the system.

2.4 When booking, pick the correct absence category. If you are unsure which category applies, ask the People team before submitting.

2.5 Managers can view their team's upcoming leave in the HR system. The People team can view all leave records.

## 3. Sickness reporting

### 3.1 Netherlands

3.1.1 Report sickness to your manager by telephone or in the chat before the start of your working day. If you cannot reach your manager, message the People team in #ask-people.

3.1.2 The manager registers the sickness in the HR system on the same working day.

3.1.3 The Dutch occupational health process applies. The People team coordinates with the occupational health service. You may be invited to a consultation with the occupational health physician; participation is mandatory under Dutch law.

3.1.4 During sickness, keep your manager informed about your expected return date. The manager and the People team agree on the reintegration steps. The People team will guide you through the first-year and second-year sickness tracks if your absence extends beyond the usual reintegration periods.

3.1.5 If you are sick on a public holiday or during booked leave, the sick days count as sickness, not as holiday. Your manager should adjust the HR system record accordingly.

### 3.2 United States (Colorado)

3.2.1 Report sickness to your manager before the start of your working day, by telephone or in the chat.

3.2.2 The manager registers the sickness in the HR system on the same working day. Sick time is deducted from your paid sick leave balance. Colorado's paid sick leave rules apply; the HR system tracks your balance and accrual.

3.2.3 For absences longer than three consecutive working days, the manager may ask for a doctor's note. The People team can help if you need to request one.

3.2.4 Sick time can also be used for preventive care or to care for a family member, per the policy in the handbook. The People team can confirm what counts as a qualifying family member.

## 4. Public holidays per office

4.1 Rotterdam office observes the Dutch public holidays. Denver office observes the US federal holidays observed by the State of Colorado.

4.2 Public holidays for the current calendar year are listed in the HR system under the holiday calendar. The People team maintains the calendar and announces any changes in #announcements.

4.3 The HR system shows the replacement day per the applicable rules in each country when a public holiday falls outside the working week. The Rotterdam and Denver calendars can differ; check the HR system for the office that applies to you.

4.4 If you work across offices or support the other office's customers on a day that is a public holiday only in the other country, you can take that day as leave at another time in agreement with your manager. The People team can book the replacement day in the HR system.

4.5 The company closes between the last working day before the end-of-year holidays and the first working day after New Year. The People team announces the exact closure dates each year. Days that fall within the closure are counted against your holiday allowance unless they are public holidays.

## 5. Parental leave

5.1 Dutch payroll: birth and adoption leave follows the Dutch statutory scheme. The partner leave and extended parental leave rules apply. Contact the People team early in your pregnancy or before the expected placement date so they can calculate the leave periods and the benefit percentages for you.

5.2 US payroll (Colorado): Family and medical leave follows the Colorado paid family and medical leave programme. The People team can help you understand the application timeline and how the programme interacts with your PTO balance.

5.3 Parental leave is booked in the HR system under the parental leave category. The People team enters the leave after the statutory or programme paperwork is complete.

5.4 During parental leave, your employment continues and your service date is unaffected. The People team will confirm how benefits (health insurance, pension or retirement contributions) are handled during the leave for your payroll country.

5.5 If you are returning from parental leave and need a phased return, discuss this with your manager and the People team at least a month before your planned return date. We can usually arrange a temporary part-time schedule for the first weeks back.
