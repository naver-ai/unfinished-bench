# Leave and absence

**Owner:** People team  
**Last edited by:** Selin Lindgren (People Partner)  
**Applies to:** All Mossgrove employees, including store colleagues, warehouse colleagues, and remote workers in the UK, Ireland, and the US. Agency workers should follow the terms in their engagement letter.

## Purpose

This policy sets out how holiday and other leave is requested, approved, and recorded, along with the sickness reporting process. It replaces the previous separate UK, Ireland, and US leave pages in the handbook.

## Scope

All permanent and fixed-term employees. For wholesale and store teams, leave should be planned around seasonal peaks — the Retail operations and Wholesale teams publish blackout dates each quarter in chat, and those take precedence over the general booking rules below.

## 1. Holiday allowance

1.1. Full-time employees (37.5 hours per week) receive:

- UK and Ireland: 28 days per leave year, inclusive of public holidays.
- US: 20 days per leave year, plus 5 fixed company holidays. US public holidays are not automatically added; they are taken from the fixed company holiday list published by the People team each year.

1.2. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated allowance based on their contracted hours. The People team can confirm your exact entitlement; the HR system shows it under **My Leave**.

1.3. After 5 complete years of service, UK and Ireland employees receive an additional 2 days per year (30 total). After 10 complete years, a further 2 days (32 total). US employees do not receive additional days for length of service.

1.4. Length of service is calculated from your start date. Periods of unpaid leave (excluding maternity, paternity, shared parental, and adoption leave) do not count towards the 5- and 10-year milestones.

## 2. Booking leave

2.1. All leave is booked through the HR system. Store colleagues can also ask their line manager to book it on their behalf through the POS back office, but the request still needs approval in the HR system.

2.2. Requests must be submitted at least:

- 2 working days for a single day off.
- 5 working days for up to 5 consecutive days.
- 15 working days for longer periods.

2.3. Store and warehouse colleagues should submit requests at least 10 working days ahead, regardless of length, to allow for cover planning. The Fulfilment team uses the same lead time for DC-based roles.

2.4. Managers should respond to leave requests within 3 working days. If a request is not responded to within 5 working days, the employee can escalate to the People Partner for their team.

2.5. Leave can be carried over as follows:

- UK: up to 5 days into the next leave year, with manager approval.
- Ireland: up to 5 days, with manager approval.
- US: no carry-over into the next leave year.

2.6. The leave year runs from 1 February to 31 January for all countries. The HR system aligns everyone to this, so you may see a mid-year change to your remaining balance if you previously ran on a calendar-year basis.

2.7. Annual leave cannot be taken in the first 4 weeks of February for anyone in Finance operations or the payments platform, due to the month-end close and the February audit. This has been in place for a few years and is not changing.

## 3. Sickness reporting

3.1. If you are unwell and cannot work, you must report this before your start time using the **Sickness** option in the HR system. Store colleagues can also call the store phone and speak to the shift lead if they do not have HR system access from home.

3.2. You must speak to your line manager (or the shift lead for store roles) by phone before the end of your first day of absence. A message in chat is not sufficient.

3.3. For absences of 4 or more consecutive days, you must provide a fit note (UK), medical certificate (Ireland), or doctor's note (US) through the HR system. The People team will send a reminder if one is missing.

3.4. Return-to-work conversations are required after any absence of 3 or more days, or any absence related to stress or mental health. Line managers can ask the People team for guidance on running these.

3.5. Pay during sickness:

- UK: company sick pay is paid from the first day of absence, up to 10 weeks in any 12-month period.
- Ireland: company sick pay from day 1, up to 8 weeks in any 12-month period.
- US: short-term disability applies after 7 days of absence. The first 7 days are unpaid unless you use accrued holiday or sick time (US employees receive 5 sick days per year, pro-rated for part-time).

3.6. The payroll team runs sickness reporting monthly; if you spot a discrepancy in your pay, contact them directly.

## 4. Carers leave

4.1. All employees are entitled to 5 days of unpaid carers leave per year in the UK, in line with statutory requirements. Ireland and US employees have the same entitlement under Mossgrove policy, even where not required by law.

4.2. Carers leave is for providing or arranging care for a dependant with a long-term illness, disability, or age-related condition. It cannot be taken in blocks of less than half a day.

4.3. Carers leave is booked through the HR system as **Carers Leave**. It does not need to be taken in one block, but the total cannot exceed 5 days per leave year.

4.4. Unused carers leave does not carry over.

## 5. Bereavement and compassionate leave

5.1. Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of a close family member (partner, parent, child, sibling, grandparent, grandchild).

5.2. For other bereavements or significant personal circumstances, line managers can approve up to 3 days of paid compassionate leave, with the People Partner for the team copied in on the approval.

5.3. Additional unpaid time can be requested through the standard leave process.

## 6. Country-specific notes

6.1. **UK:** Public holidays are included in the 28-day allowance. The People team publishes the list of public holidays each year in the HR system. You do not need to book these as leave; they are pre-marked.

6.2. **Ireland:** Public holidays are included in the 28-day allowance, but the HR system does not pre-mark them. Please check the list from the People team and book the day off if you are not working it. Store colleagues should confirm with their manager whether the store is open on the public holiday — some are.

6.3. **US:** The 5 fixed company holidays are announced by the People team before the start of each leave year. You must book these manually in the HR system if you are not scheduled to work that day. US employees also have 5 sick days per year, which can be used for medical appointments.

## 7. Unpaid leave

7.1. Requests for unpaid leave (beyond the specific types above) should be made through the HR system at least 20 working days in advance, and need approval from both your line manager and the People Partner for your team.

7.2. Unpaid leave is normally limited to 4 weeks in any leave year, unless there are exceptional circumstances (e.g. extended family care).

7.3. Unpaid leave does not count towards continuous service for holiday accrual purposes, but it does not break continuity of employment.

## 8. Recording and queries

8.1. All leave is recorded in the HR system, and balances are visible to you and your line manager at all times.

8.2. If you notice an incorrect balance, raise it with the People team within 30 days of the leave period concerned. Adjustments for older periods are made at the People team's discretion.

8.3. For policy questions, contact the People team via the helpdesk using the **Leave and absence** category. Store colleagues can also ask their line manager or the store systems analyst (Esther Vasquez's team) for help navigating the HR system.
