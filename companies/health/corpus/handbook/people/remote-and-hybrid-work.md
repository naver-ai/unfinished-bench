# Remote and hybrid work

**Owner:** Human resources  
**Last edited by:** Sabine Tanaka

## Purpose

This policy sets out which roles at Kellsbrook Health may work remotely or on a hybrid schedule, what you and your manager need to agree before you start, and the workspace and equipment expectations for working away from a Kellsbrook site. It applies to all regular employees, including those based at our Cork office.

## Scope

This policy covers remote and hybrid work arrangements for employees whose roles are eligible. It does not cover temporary arrangements such as weather-related closures or one-off requests to work from home for a day; those are handled through your manager.

## Policy

1. **Eligibility**
   1.1. Remote and hybrid work is available in the following areas: claims processing, coding and billing, IT (infrastructure, service desk, data engineering, records platform), and select research roles at the Research Institute. Clinical roles that require you to be on site — nursing, lab, pharmacy, imaging, direct patient care — are not eligible.
   1.2. Claims roles based in Valparaiso or South Bend may work remotely if their team has a hybrid rotation in place. Cork IT staff work from the Cork office on their team's rotation unless their manager has approved otherwise.
   1.3. Your manager may decline a remote or hybrid request based on team coverage, training needs, or the specific duties of your role. HR is available to discuss the decision with you.

2. **Agreement with your manager**
   2.1. Before you begin working remotely or on a hybrid schedule, you and your manager must complete the remote work agreement form in the HR system. The form records your expected on-site days (if hybrid), your home work location, and your manager's approval.
   2.2. Hybrid schedules are set by the team. If you need to change your on-site days, discuss it with your manager first. On-site days may need to shift to cover team needs — for example, when the service desk is short-staffed or during claims payment runs.
   2.3. Your agreement is reviewed at your annual review. Either you or your manager can end the arrangement with notice through the HR system.

3. **Home workspace**
   3.1. You must have a private space where you can work without being overheard or overlooked. This is a condition of the arrangement, not a recommendation. If you live with other people, they should not be in the same room during your working hours.
   3.2. **No paper records at home.** You may not take printed charts, claim forms, or any other documents containing patient or member information to your home. If your role requires you to review paper documents, you must do that on site. This includes documents you might print yourself; do not print from the EHR, the claims adjudication system, or any other system while working remotely.
   3.3. Your home workspace should have a door you can close. If you take calls from home and someone else is home, use headphones.

4. **Equipment**
   4.1. IT ships the following to remote and hybrid employees: a Kellsbrook laptop, a second monitor (if your role usually has one on site), a wired keyboard and mouse, and a headset for calls. You should not use a personal computer for Kellsbrook work.
   4.2. You are responsible for your home internet connection. It should be a private broadband connection — not shared with other households — and you should have a way to connect your laptop to it by cable as well as Wi-Fi. You reach Kellsbrook systems from home through the VPN client that comes on your laptop; see the remote access VPN guide in it-docs.
   4.3. If you need a desk or chair for your home workspace, expenses for a basic desk and an ergonomic chair can be claimed once through the expenses tool. Approval from your manager is required before you buy. Kellsbrook does not reimburse ongoing home internet or electricity costs.
   4.4. If you are based in Cork and work with the US teams, IT has a small stock of headsets and webcams at the Cork office. Ask the IT team there if you need one; do not order through the US equipment process.

5. **Working across the time difference with Cork**
   5.1. Some teams work with colleagues in Cork: IT operations and the records platform team, the Research Institute's European trials office, and the Cork medical centre. If your role involves regular contact with Cork, your manager will tell you what overlap is expected.
   5.2. In general, US-based staff are not expected to take calls outside their usual working hours to accommodate Cork. Cork-based staff are not expected to take calls outside their normal working hours to accommodate the US. If a meeting falls outside these windows, the team should find a different slot or record the discussion.
   5.3. If you work remotely and your team has a regular handoff with Cork, you are expected to be available during the agreed overlap. The nursing shift handoff process in care-docs does not apply to non-clinical staff, but the same principle — a clean handoff, no loose ends — applies to claims, IT, and research work.
   5.4. The paging system is for on-call IT and clinical staff only. If you are not on call, you are not expected to respond to pages while working remotely.

6. **General expectations**
   6.1. Remote work is not a substitute for childcare, eldercare, or other dependent care. If you are working, you should be able to give your work your attention. Speak to your manager or HR if you need to explore leave options.
   6.2. You are expected to be reachable during your working hours. For most teams this means being on chat and checking your email. If you work in a role that takes patient or member calls, you must follow the same call-answering standards you would on site.
   6.3. If you work remotely and need to come on site for a meeting or to use a paper-based process, your badge will work at any Kellsbrook site. Parking is free at all sites.
   6.4. The acceptable use policy in the handbook applies to your home workspace exactly as it applies on site. That includes who may use your Kellsbrook laptop and what you may do with it. See handbook/security/acceptable-use.md.

7. **Changes to this policy**
   7.1. This policy is owned by Human resources and is reviewed periodically. Managers will be notified through the usual channels when it changes. If you have questions, ask your HR business partner.
