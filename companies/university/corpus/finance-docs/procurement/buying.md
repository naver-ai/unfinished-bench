# Buying goods and services

**Owner:** Procurement  
**Last edited by:** Simone Klein (Head of Procurement)

This document sets out how Aldercombe University staff buy goods and services, including when quotes or tenders are required, which frameworks to use, how to raise a purchase order, and the rules for purchasing cards.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to all purchases of goods and services for the university, Aldercombe Estates Services Ltd, the Aldercombe Learning Trust (including Kestrel Vale Academy), and Aldercombe University Malaysia, using university funds or funds held on the university's behalf.

1.2 This policy does not cover research grant costs charged to an award after the award is set up; see research-docs/post-award/award-setup.md for how those are handled. It also does not cover expenses claimed by staff; see handbook/people/expenses.md.

## 2. Quotes and tenders

2.1 The following thresholds apply to all purchases, whether funded from core budgets, research grants, or other income. They are based on the current public procurement regulations for a higher education institution in England.

2.2 Purchases up to £10,000 excluding VAT: no formal quote is required, but you must be able to show the purchase represents value for money. A single written quotation or a catalogue price from an approved framework supplier is sufficient.

2.3 Purchases over £10,000 and up to £25,000 excluding VAT: you must obtain at least three written quotations from suppliers, unless you are buying through a framework agreement listed in section 3, in which case one quotation from the framework is sufficient.

2.4 Purchases over £25,000 and up to the UK public procurement threshold (currently around £138,760 for goods and services, excluding VAT): you must run a formal tender. Procurement will run this for you. Contact the Procurement team at the start of the process; do not approach suppliers yourself.

2.5 Purchases above the UK public procurement threshold: the tender must be published on the UK e-notification service and must comply with the full public procurement regime. You must involve Procurement before any supplier is approached.

2.6 If a purchase is urgent because of a genuine emergency (immediate risk to health and safety, or a breakdown that stops teaching or research), contact Procurement immediately. They will advise on the quickest compliant route, which may include a single-source purchase. You must still record the emergency and the approval in the finance system.

2.7 Do not split a purchase into smaller orders to stay below a threshold. If you need to buy the same or similar goods or services from the same supplier more than once in a financial year, add the values together for threshold purposes.

## 3. Framework agreements

3.1 The university has access to the following frameworks. Check these before going to the open market:

- **Crown Commercial Service frameworks** for IT hardware and software, professional services, and facilities management.
- **SUPC (Southern Universities Purchasing Consortium) frameworks** for laboratory supplies, scientific equipment, and many common goods and services.
- **The university's own framework** for construction and refurbishment works, run through Estates. See estates-docs/projects/capital-projects.md.

3.2 If a framework has a named supplier and a published price list, you do not need to seek further quotes. If the framework allows mini-competition between several suppliers, run the mini-competition through the framework's portal or as Procurement advises.

3.3 If you believe no framework covers what you need, contact Procurement before starting a quotation or tender exercise. They may know of a framework you have not considered.

## 4. Raising a purchase order

4.1 All purchases must have a purchase order (PO) raised in the finance system before the goods or services are supplied, except for purchases made with a purchasing card under section 5.

4.2 To raise a PO, go to the finance system and select "Purchase order". You will need:

- the supplier's details — if the supplier is not already set up, see finance-docs/ap/supplier-setup.md
- a description of the goods or services
- the cost centre and activity code to be charged
- the delivery address (include the campus and building)
- confirmation that the relevant quoting or tendering threshold in section 2 has been met

4.3 POs over £10,000 require approval from your budget holder. POs over £25,000 also require approval from your faculty or directorate head. POs over the tender threshold in 2.4 require approval from the Director of Finance (Stephen Mallory) in addition to the Procurement sign-off.

4.4 Do not accept a supplier invoice for goods or services that do not have a matching PO. If a supplier sends an invoice without a PO, contact Procurement; do not forward the invoice to Accounts Payable.

4.5 If you need to amend a PO (change in quantity, price, or delivery), raise a PO amendment in the finance system rather than letting the supplier invoice against the original value.

## 5. Purchasing cards

5.1 Purchasing cards are issued by Procurement on request, and only to staff who need them regularly for low-value purchases. Cards are in the name of the individual member of staff; they are not shared.

5.2 The single-transaction limit is £500 excluding VAT, and the monthly limit is £2,500 excluding VAT, unless a higher limit has been approved in writing by the Head of Procurement.

5.3 Purchasing cards may be used for:

- small consumables and laboratory supplies
- conference fees and event bookings
- books and periodicals
- minor repairs and parts where a PO would be impractical
- approved travel and subsistence, where not claimed through the expenses tool

5.4 Purchasing cards may not be used for:

- any single item over £500 excluding VAT
- goods or services covered by a framework agreement where the framework supplier should be used
- hire of vehicles, plant, or equipment over 24 hours
- cash advances of any kind
- personal purchases, even if you intend to reimburse the university later
- gifts, prizes, or vouchers, except where approved by the Director of Finance

5.5 Cardholders must keep receipts for every transaction and attach them to the monthly statement in the finance system. The statement must be approved by the cardholder's line manager. Unapproved or unreconciled transactions will result in the card being suspended.

5.6 If you lose a purchasing card, report it to Procurement immediately. The card will be cancelled and a replacement issued. You are responsible for transactions made with your card before you report it missing.

## 6. Non-compliance

6.1 Purchases made without a PO, above a purchasing card limit, or without the required quotes or tender may not be paid. The budget holder will be asked to explain the breach, and repeated breaches will be reported to the Director of Finance and the relevant faculty or directorate head.
