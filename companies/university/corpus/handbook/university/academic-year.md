# The academic year

**Owner:** Academic Registry  
**Last edited by:** Annika Lindqvist (Deputy Academic Registrar (Student Lifecycle))

The Aldercombe academic year runs from the start of the autumn term to the end of the summer resit period. The standard pattern is two semesters of twelve teaching weeks, each followed by an assessment period, with a reading week in each semester. Week numbers run consecutively from the first teaching week of the autumn semester (Week 1) through to the end of the summer assessment period.

## Standard year structure (Hollins Hill and City campuses)

| Period | Weeks | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Autumn semester teaching | Weeks 1–6 | |
| Reading week | Week 7 | No scheduled teaching; assessments may still be set |
| Autumn semester teaching | Weeks 8–13 | |
| Autumn assessment period | Weeks 14–16 | Examinations and coursework deadlines |
| Spring semester teaching | Weeks 17–22 | |
| Reading week | Week 23 | |
| Spring semester teaching | Weeks 24–29 | |
| Spring assessment period | Weeks 30–32 | |
| Summer resit period | Weeks 33–36 | Resits for autumn and spring assessments; results published by the end of Week 36 |

Week 1 falls on the first working day of the autumn semester, as published in the academic calendar on the Registry pages of the VLE. The spring semester begins immediately after the autumn assessment period; there is no separate vacation between semesters.

## Winter closure

The university closes for a period over the winter, usually covering the last part of the autumn assessment period. The closure dates are set each year by the University Executive Board and published in the academic calendar. No teaching, assessments or examinations are scheduled during the closure, and the assessment period is extended around it so that the twelve teaching weeks and the assessment weeks remain intact. Read the `handbook/people/leave-and-absence.md` guidance if you are unsure how the closure affects annual leave.

## Kestrel Vale Academy

Kestrel Vale Academy follows the local authority school term dates, which are set by the council and published on the academy's parent pages. The academy year has three terms (autumn, spring, summer), each split into two halves by a half-term break. The academy's term dates do not align with the university semesters, and the academy is not closed during the university's winter closure beyond the normal school Christmas break. The academy's school information system holds the term dates for staff; the Kestrel Vale office team can also confirm them.

## Kuala Lumpur campus

The Malaysia campus follows the same semester pattern as the UK campuses — two semesters of twelve teaching weeks with reading weeks — but the calendar is shifted so that the year starts later in the autumn. The KL campus publishes its own academic calendar with the same week numbering convention, so Week 1 in Kuala Lumpur falls on the first working day of the KL autumn semester and may fall on a different date from Week 1 at Hollins Hill. The KL campus does not observe the UK winter closure, and its assessment periods are scheduled without the break. Public holidays in Malaysia are reflected in the KL academic calendar; where a public holiday falls on a teaching day, the teaching week is adjusted and the week number is retained.

The KL calendar is maintained by the Malaysia campus Registrar's team and published on the KL campus intranet pages.

## Contact

Questions about the academic calendar, week numbering or the timing of assessment periods should go to Academic Registry. The examinations team in Registry schedules the exam periods; see `registry-docs/exams/exam-timetabling.md` for how that works.
