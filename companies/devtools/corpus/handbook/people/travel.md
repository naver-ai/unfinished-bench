# Business travel

Some of this predates the new travel desk process — ask in #people-ops if a step doesn't match what the desk tells you.

Owner: Workplace & facilities. Last edited by Hannah Delgado.

## Booking

- All flights and hotels go through the travel desk. Raise a ticket with the trip dates and the approved budget line; the desk books and emails the itinerary.
- Personal car rental is not covered unless the trip includes a customer site visit. Lisbon office: use rail for anything under 4h door-to-door. Portland: rail only for Portland–Seattle; everything else flies.
- Hotel caps (per night, before tax):
  - Lisbon: EUR 220
  - Porto: EUR 180
  - Madrid: EUR 200
  - Portland: USD 260
  - San Francisco / New York / London: USD 320 / USD 350 / GBP 260
  - Any other city: EUR 250 or USD 280 equivalent
- Over-cap stays need manager approval before booking. The desk will not book above cap without it.

## Lisbon ↔ Portland

- Most cross-office trips are planned around the quarterly planning weeks. Aim to land at least one working day before the first meeting you must attend in person.
- Westbound (Lisbon → Portland): take the direct flight, land in the afternoon, and take the next day as a recovery day. Keep the morning after arrival free of meetings.
- Eastbound (Portland → Lisbon): same rule — recovery day after the crossing, and a free morning after that.
- Bookable through the travel desk only; do not self-book transatlantic legs.

## Expenses

- Follow `handbook/people/expenses.md` for receipts and limits. Meals on travel days are per diem; no receipt needed under the daily cap.
- Recovery-day meals count as travel expenses.

## Notes

- The travel desk does not book airfare for trips shorter than 48h notice — book through the desk anyway, they will find something.
- If the trip includes a stopover longer than 6h, the stopover hotel is covered up to the same cap.
