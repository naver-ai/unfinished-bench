# File shares

**Owner:** Storage & file services  
**Last edited by:** Petra Vasquez (Storage Engineer)

## What is a file share?

File shares are network folders hosted on our central storage. They are the place for team, department and project files that need to be shared by several people. Everything on a file share is backed up nightly.

## How do I request a new share?

Raise a request through the service desk portal. Include the name of the department or project, the site (Reutlingen, Telford or Greenville), the names of the people who need access and the approximate space you need. Storage & file services will create the share and grant access within two working days.

## Is there a naming convention?

Yes. Shares start with a three-letter site code: `reu-`, `telf-` or `gv-`, followed by the department or project name, for example `reu-quality` or `telf-drives-line`. If a share is used by more than one site, use the site of the primary user group.

## What quota applies to my share?

Standard department shares get 100 GB. Project shares get 50 GB unless you say otherwise when you request them. If you need more, ask when you make the request — it is easier to size a share correctly at the start than to move files later. We can raise a quota at any time; just put the request through the service desk.

## How do I get an extra subfolder with different permissions?

We do not create per-person permissions inside a share. If you need a folder that only some people can see, request a separate share for it. This keeps permissions simple to audit and avoids the situation where someone has access to a whole share just because they need one folder.

## Can I restore a previous version of a file?

Yes. Snapshots are taken twice a day and kept for two weeks. To restore a file, right-click the file in the share window and choose **Restore previous version**. If you need a full folder restored, or a version older than two weeks, contact the service desk and we will restore from backup. Restores from backup are done on a best-effort basis and can take up to a day.

## What is pool-b?

Pool-b is our current storage platform. All shares have been migrated to it or are in the process of being migrated. Shares on pool-b are faster and have a higher reliability target than the old filers.

## When is my share moving to pool-b?

Migrations run in waves. We publish the wave plan on the intranet and notify the named contact for each share two weeks before the migration date. If your share is still on an old filer and you have not been contacted, check the wave plan. If your share is not listed, contact Storage & file services.

## What do I need to do before my share migrates?

Nothing. The migration happens without downtime and file paths stay the same. If you have a machine that mounts the share by an old path, check with your local IT support whether it needs a remount. Most machines pick up the new path automatically.

## Why is my share slower than it used to be?

If you are on an old filer, speed will improve after migration. If you are on pool-b and experience slowness, check whether you have large files that were restored from backup recently — restores can take a while to warm up. Otherwise raise a ticket and Storage & file services will investigate.

## Who can see my share's access list?

The share owner and Storage & file services. If you need to know who has access to a share you own, contact us and we will send the list. Access lists are reviewed once a year with the share owner.

## What happens to my share when someone leaves?

When a leaver is processed through the joiners, movers and leavers process, their account is disabled. Share access is removed as part of that. If the leaver was the named owner of a share, the service desk will contact the department head to assign a new owner.

## Can I store personal files on a share?

No. File shares are for company work. Personal files belong in your home directory, which has a separate quota. If you need a bigger home directory, raise a request through the service desk.

## I need to share a file with someone outside the company.

Do not put external parties on a share. Use the company file transfer service (request it through the service desk portal). If you regularly need to exchange files with a customer or supplier, talk to IT infrastructure about a dedicated transfer arrangement.
