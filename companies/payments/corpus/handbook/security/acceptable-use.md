# Acceptable use

**Owner:** Security engineering  
**Last edited by:** Dara Graham (Senior Security Engineer)

## Purpose

This policy sets out how Kilbarra systems, accounts, and data may be used by employees, contractors, and anyone else granted access to Kilbarra resources. It is intended to protect Kilbarra, our merchants, and our partners from harm while allowing everyone to do their jobs effectively.

## Scope

Applies to all Kilbarra systems, including but not limited to the back office, the merchant dashboard, the core ledger, the treasury workstation, the command-line tools teams use to run their services, tickets, chat, the helpdesk, the CRM, the analytics warehouse, the feature flag service, the secrets manager, and any other system or service provided by Kilbarra. Also applies to company-issued laptops, phones, and other devices, and to any personal device used to access Kilbarra resources.

## Policy

1. **Authorised use only.** Access to Kilbarra systems and data is granted for legitimate business purposes only. You may use Kilbarra systems to perform your role, collaborate with colleagues, and serve our merchants and partners. You may not use Kilbarra systems for personal gain, for unauthorised commercial activity, or in violation of any law or regulation.

1.1 **Accounts are personal.** Your accounts, credentials, and API keys are issued to you personally. Do not share them, do not let anyone else use them, and do not use anyone else's. If you believe your credentials have been compromised, report it through the incident reporting process in the handbook.

1.2 **Reasonable personal use.** Incidental personal use of Kilbarra systems (for example, checking personal email or booking travel) is permitted provided it does not interfere with your work, consume excessive resources, or expose Kilbarra data. Personal use of chat channels should be directed to #random where appropriate. Do not store personal files on Kilbarra systems.

1.3 **No unauthorised access.** Do not attempt to access systems, data, or areas outside what your role requires. Do not bypass access controls, security measures, or logging. If you discover a security issue, report it promptly — see the security reporting guidance.

2. **Monitoring notice.** Kilbarra monitors activity on its systems for security, operational, and compliance purposes. This includes but is not limited to log collection in the SIEM, network monitoring, access reviews, and review of chat and email where required. By using Kilbarra systems you consent to this monitoring. Do not have an expectation of privacy in your use of Kilbarra resources.

2.1 **Logs are retained.** System logs, chat history, and other records are retained per the records retention schedule. These may be reviewed by Security, Governance risk & controls testing, or other teams as needed for investigations, audits, or regulatory requirements.

3. **Generative tools.** Use of generative AI tools is permitted only when the tool has been approved by Security engineering for company use. A list of approved tools is maintained by the security team — check before using anything new.

3.1 **No merchant or cardholder data in prompts.** You must never paste, upload, or otherwise provide merchant data, cardholder data, or any data classified as restricted (see the data classification guide) into an unapproved generative tool. Even with approved tools, avoid including personal data wherever possible. If you need to use merchant data in an approved tool, ensure it is properly anonymised first.

3.2 **Outputs are not automatically safe.** Content produced by generative tools may contain errors, biases, or security issues. Review all output before using it in code, communications, or decisions. Do not use generated code without a proper review.

4. **Software and installations.** You may install software on company devices only where needed for your role and where it does not conflict with security policies. If in doubt, ask #it-help. Do not install pirated software, unauthorised remote access tools, or anything that disables security controls.

5. **Devices.** Company-issued laptops, phones, and other devices are subject to this policy. Keep them updated, lock them when unattended, and report loss or theft immediately. See the devices and sign-in guide for details.

6. **Data handling.** You are responsible for handling Kilbarra data in line with its classification. Restricted data (merchant financial details, cardholder data, credentials, etc.) must not be copied to personal devices, USB drives or personal cloud accounts.

6.1 **Personal tools.** Do not use personal email or personal file-sharing services for Kilbarra data, and do not post data in chat channels where it is not needed.

7. **Incident response.** If you suspect a security incident, a data breach, or suspicious activity involving Kilbarra systems, report it immediately. See the incident reporting page in the handbook for the right channel and what to include. Do not attempt to investigate on your own beyond what is needed to protect data.

8. **Third-party access.** Access granted to contractors, partners, or vendors is subject to the same rules as employee access. If you sponsor or request access for a third party, you are responsible for ensuring they understand and follow this policy.

9. **Policy violations.** Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract, and may be reported to regulators or law enforcement where required. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask the security team in #security before doing it.

10. **Questions.** For questions about this policy, contact Security engineering in #security. For questions about access or account issues, contact #it-help.
