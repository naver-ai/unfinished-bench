# Clinic opening and closing checklist

**Owner:** Clinics (primary and specialty care)  
**Last edited by:** Rania Adler, Director of Clinic Operations

## Opening

- [ ] Confirm the clinic has power and the alarm was disarmed by the first person in.
- [ ] Fridge temperatures logged in the EHR — anything outside 2–8°C is flagged to the clinic manager before seeing patients.
- [ ] Crash cart checked against the sealed contents list; seal number recorded. If the seal is broken or anything is missing, call the nursing leads channel and do not use the cart until restocked.
- [ ] Medication cabinets unlocked only after the morning count matches the previous evening's count. Discrepancies go to the pharmacy system ticket queue.
- [ ] Review today's schedule in the scheduling and registration system — note the first appointment, any add-ons from the prior day, and whether the rooms assigned are correct.
- [ ] Check the interface engine for any feed failures since the last close; if something hasn't arrived, raise a ticket to the HIS platform team.
- [ ] Rooms stocked: exam table paper, gloves, gowns, and the standard supply cart per the clinic supply list. Replenish before the first slot.

## Closing

- [ ] Specimens collected after the last courier pickup are logged in the lab information system and placed in the secure holding fridge; the courier sheet is left in the pickup bin.
- [ ] Any specimens that were rejected or recollected are noted in the lab system and the clinic manager is copied on the result.
- [ ] Rooms wiped down and exam tables re-papered for the next day. Used linens and sharps containers are sealed and placed in the pickup area.
- [ ] Controlled substances and all medications back in the locked cabinet; the cabinet is locked and the count is recorded.
- [ ] All patient charts (paper or scanned in chartarc) are filed; no loose records left at a workstation.
- [ ] Doors checked and locked; the alarm is armed by the last person leaving.
- [ ] The closing checklist is signed off in the EHR by the last clinical staff member on site. If the clinic is closing early, the clinic manager is notified through the chat channel for that site.

If any step cannot be completed, note it in the EHR and message the clinic manager before leaving.
