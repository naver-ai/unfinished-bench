## ERP entities

Three ledgers in the ERP, one per legal entity:

- **DE10** — Kessendorf Motion & Messtechnik GmbH, Reutlingen
- **UK20** — Kessendorf UK Ltd, Telford
- **US30** — Kessendorf Inc., Greenville

Intercompany accounts are mapped in the ERP item master; see `intercompany.md` for the settlement schedule. Currency revaluation runs on the last working day of the month through the consolidation tool.
