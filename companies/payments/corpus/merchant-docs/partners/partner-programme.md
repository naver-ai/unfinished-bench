# Partner programme

**Owner:** Partnerships  
**Last edited by:** Oskar Chandra

The partner programme covers three types of relationships: referral partners, resellers, and software-platform partners. Referral partners introduce merchants to Kilbarra and earn a one-time fee on qualifying business. Resellers contract directly with merchants and handle their own pricing, invoicing, and support; Kilbarra supplies the underlying accounts and processing. Software-platform partners embed payments into their product using our APIs and platform accounts, and are the largest and most operationally involved group.

Partners are assigned to a tier — Standard, Select, or Strategic — based on annual processed volume, number of active merchants, and integration maturity. Tiers determine revenue share percentages, access to partner managers, and eligibility for joint marketing funds. Revenue share is paid monthly through the treasury workstation, using the same payout rails as merchant settlements. Maren Holt's team handles the marketing fund side; Oskar Chandra and the Partner Managers own tier reviews and commercial terms.

Deal registration happens in the CRM. Partners submit a deal before the merchant first applies, and the registration is valid for 90 days. If the merchant is already known to Kilbarra — meaning they have an existing merchant account, a previous application, or a lead logged in the CRM within the last six months — the deal is not eligible. The Partnerships team reviews registrations within two working days and notes the outcome on the CRM record. Disputes between partners on the same merchant are rare but are resolved by the Partnerships team on a first-registered basis.

The partner portal gives partners access to their merchants' basic transaction summaries, payout history, and fee reports. It is a separate surface from the merchant dashboard and shows aggregates rather than individual transactions. Portal access is set up by Revenue operations, and API keys for portal integrations are issued by Integrations & partners. Partners who want raw data feeds instead of portal access should follow the process in [partner-feeds.md](./partner-feeds.md).

Quarterly partner reviews are run by the Partnerships team for Select and Strategic partners, and on request for Standard partners. Reviews cover volume trends, merchant activation rates, support ticket volume, and any changes to the partner's integration. Felix Oyelaran attends reviews for Strategic partners, and Marcus Delaney joins when commercial terms are on the table. Action items from reviews are tracked in the CRM and followed up by the assigned Partner Manager.

New partners go through KYB as part of onboarding, using the same merchant onboarding tooling but with a partner-specific workflow. The Risk controls team assesses the partner's own compliance programme, and the Partnerships team confirms commercial terms before the partner is activated. A partner can be downgraded or exited if they stop meeting their tier's volume expectations or if their merchant portfolio's risk profile changes materially; the decision sits with Partnerships in consultation with Risk controls.

Questions about the programme, tiering, or deal registration should go to the Partnerships team in #partnerships. Anything related to portal access or partner API keys should be raised in #partner-integrations.
