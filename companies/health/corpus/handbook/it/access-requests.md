# Getting access to systems

**Owner:** IT service desk  
**Last edited by:** Luca Estrada

This covers how to request access to Kellsbrook systems, who approves requests, and what happens when someone leaves.

## Requesting access

1. Open a ticket in tickets using the **Access request** form. Include:
   - Employee name and Kellsbrook ID (or contractor/agency ID if applicable)
   - Job role and department
   - Site (South Bend, Valparaiso, Kalamazoo, Cork, or remote)
   - Systems needed (EHR, claims adjudication system, member portal, etc.)
   - Start date for the access
   - For clinical staff: which clinic or hospital location they work at

2. The employee's manager approves the request in tickets. The system owner also approves — for example, the claims adjudication system is owned by the claims operations team, the EHR by clinical applications (HIS), and so on. The ticket will route to the right approvers automatically based on the systems selected.

3. Once both approvals are in, the service desk creates the accounts and assigns permissions. Most access is provisioned within one working day of approval. If something needs extra review (for example, access to the data warehouse or research systems), it may take another working day or two.

## Role-based EHR access templates

Most EHR access is granted through role templates rather than individual permissions. When you request EHR access, pick the template that matches the role:

- **Physician** — full chart access, ordering, documentation
- **Nurse** — documentation, medication administration, view-only for some orders
- **Medical assistant** — vitals, rooming, view-only chart access
- **Front desk/registration** — scheduling, registration, insurance verification
- **Billing/coding** — charge capture, coding queues, release of information (as applicable)
- **Research coordinator** — chart access limited to study participants at the research institute

If a role doesn't fit an existing template, put that in the request notes. The clinical applications team will review and either add permissions to the template or create a custom set.

## Temporary access

Students, agency staff, and locums get temporary access with an expiration date. The expiration is set when the account is created — usually matching the placement end date. The service desk sends a reminder to the manager and the temp worker before it lapses.

If someone needs extended temporary access, the manager opens a follow-up ticket referencing the original request. The extension must be approved before the existing access expires.

## Removing access for leavers

When someone leaves Kellsbrook, their manager submits the **offboarding** form in tickets. The service desk deactivates all system accounts on the effective date. This includes:

- EHR and patient portal access
- Claims and member systems
- Email, chat, VPN
- Badge access to buildings (coordinated with facilities)

If a manager doesn't submit the offboarding form, access stays active — so please submit it as early as possible. Termination letters from HR also trigger offboarding, but the manager-initiated form is the fastest path.

For contractors or agency staff, the agency's account manager is copied on the offboarding ticket so they know their person's access has been removed.

## If it goes wrong

- **Access not working after approval** — reply to the original ticket. Don't open a new one; the history helps us find the issue faster.
- **Wrong template applied** — contact the service desk immediately. If it's an EHR permission issue, we'll route it to clinical applications. Do not try to work around it.
- **Manager not seeing the approval task** — check that the request named the correct manager. For employees at the Cork medical centre, the clinical lead (Aoife Brennan) is the approver for clinical roles.
- **Leaver still has access** — if you know someone has left and their accounts appear active, tell the service desk right away. We'll deactivate on the spot and work out the paperwork after.

For anything urgent outside business hours, page the IT on-call via the paging system.
