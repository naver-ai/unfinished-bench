# About Mossgrove

**Owner:** Executive team  
**Last edited by:** Oliver Brandt (Chief Financial Officer)

Mossgrove started as a single camping shop in Otley in the 1970s, run by the founder and two part-time staff. The business stayed local for a long time — the Otley shop is still trading — and grew steadily through the 1990s and 2000s, opening stores across the North of England before expanding into Scotland, Wales and Ireland. The wholesale arm came later, originally as a way to sell excess stock to independent shops, and is now a proper business unit in its own right with its own account managers and credit terms.

Today Mossgrove has three main parts:

**Retail (stores and online).** We operate 61 stores across the UK and Ireland, from large destination stores in places like York and Bristol to smaller high-street shops. The stores team handles everything from merchandising to the POS back office, and the online shop serves customers in the UK, Ireland and the US. Online orders for the UK and Ireland are fulfilled from the Wakefield DC; US orders go through the Columbus fulfilment centre.

**Wholesale.** We supply independent outdoor shops, garden centres and a few smaller chains with our own-brand clothing, tents, sleeping bags and homeware. Wholesale operates on its own credit terms and has a separate pricing structure from retail. The team manages trade accounts through the CRM and uses the ledger for invoicing and payment tracking.

**Home and garden.** This is the newer part of the business, added when we started selling homeware alongside camping kit. It covers things like outdoor furniture, lanterns, cookware and textiles, and it runs through the same online platform and stores as the rest of the retail business, but with its own buying team and product ranges.

The support functions sit across all three: finance (the ledger, AP, credit control, payroll), technology (Leeds and Dublin), marketing and brand, HR, and the shared services teams that handle customer support and returns.

Our offices are in Leeds (HQ, where the exec team, finance, marketing, HR and most of the retail operations sit), Dublin (our tech hub, home to most of the engineering teams including payments, checkout and data), Columbus, Ohio (US e-commerce and fulfilment), and Wakefield (the main distribution centre, which also has a small office for the fulfilment systems team). The Otley store still has a small back-office space that the retail operations team uses when they're out visiting stores in the area.

We have about 2,400 staff across all four locations, plus the store teams. The financial year starts in February, which matters for budgeting and for the month-end close calendar.
