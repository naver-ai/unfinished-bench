# Month-end and year-end

**Owner:** Financial accounting & reporting  
**Last edited by:** Chloe Madsen (Financial Controller)

This note covers the month-end timetable for the management accounts, and the timetable for the July year end and external audit. It is written for Finance staff and for colleagues in other teams who need to know when their figures or approvals are due.

## Month-end timetable

Month-end runs from WD-2 to WD+6 on the working-day calendar published by Finance at the start of each month. Day-to-day postings carry on as normal throughout; anything posted after WD-1 is picked up in the following month's accounts.

| Working day | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| WD-2 | Cut-off for expense claims and purchase invoices to be entered in the Finance system | All staff; AP team |
| WD-1 | Departmental accruals and prepayment forms due to Financial accounting & reporting | Faculty and professional service budget holders |
| WD-1 | Cut-off for journals from non-Finance teams (e.g. Research Finance recharges, Estates recharges) | All teams |
| WD+1 | Financial accounting & reporting posts prepayments and accruals; payroll journals posted | Financial accounting & reporting |
| WD+2 | Fixed asset additions and disposals for the month entered | Financial accounting & reporting |
| WD+2 | Inter-site recharges (Hollins Hill, City campus, Kuala Lumpur) posted | Financial accounting & reporting |
| WD+3 | Bank and control account reconciliations completed | Financial accounting & reporting |
| WD+4 | Draft management accounts pack circulated to the Director of Finance | Financial accounting & reporting |
| WD+5 | Management accounts pack signed off by the Financial Controller | Financial accounting & reporting |
| WD+6 | Management accounts pack issued to Council and the Finance Committee | Financial Controller |

The management accounts pack covers the consolidated university, Aldercombe Estates Services Ltd, and the Aldercombe Learning Trust (including Kestrel Vale Academy). It includes the income and expenditure account, balance sheet, cash flow, and a short narrative on significant variances. Capital projects are reported separately through the estates capital projects process; the pack shows the overall capital spend against budget but not project-by-project detail.

Items that miss a month-end cut-off go into the following month. If you think something needs to be in the current month's accounts after WD-2, email Financial accounting & reporting and we will tell you whether it can still be included.

## Year-end timetable (July)

The financial year runs from August to July. The July year-end follows the same WD pattern as a normal month-end, with additional steps for the external audit and the financial statements. The audit is carried out by our external auditors, who usually have a team on site at Hollins Hill for the fieldwork period.

| Working day | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| WD-2 | Year-end cut-offs for expense claims, purchase invoices, accruals and prepayments | All staff; AP team |
| WD-1 | Final journals from non-Finance teams | All teams |
| WD+1 | Payroll accrual for July salaries posted | Financial accounting & reporting |
| WD+2 | Fixed asset year-end adjustments | Financial accounting & reporting |
| WD+3 | All balance sheet reconciliations completed and signed off | Financial accounting & reporting |
| WD+4 | Draft financial statements issued to the external auditors | Financial Controller |
| WD+5 | Audit fieldwork starts | External auditors; Financial accounting & reporting |
| WD+12 | Audit fieldwork complete; schedule of audit points issued | External auditors |
| WD+15 | Financial statements updated for audit adjustments | Financial accounting & reporting |
| WD+18 | Financial statements and audit findings report to the Audit and Risk Committee | Financial Controller |
| WD+20 | Financial statements to Council for approval | Director of Finance |

The external auditors also review the Aldercombe Estates Services Ltd accounts and the Aldercombe Learning Trust accounts. Those are consolidated into the university's financial statements. The Kuala Lumpur campus is consolidated through Aldercombe University Malaysia; the auditors review the local entity accounts as part of the group audit.

The July payroll is processed in the HR and payroll system on the normal payroll calendar. The year-end does not change the payroll cut-off.

## Non-finance teams: what you need to do

If you hold a budget, the main thing that matters to you is the WD-1 deadline for accruals and prepayments. If you know goods or services were received before the month-end but the invoice has not arrived, submit an accrual form. If you have paid for something in advance that covers a period beyond the month-end, submit a prepayment form. Forms are on the Finance section of the staff intranet.

Research grants are handled slightly differently: Research Finance post their own accruals and prepayments for active awards, so you do not need to submit those through the standard form. If you are unsure whether something is a research cost or a departmental cost, ask Research Finance before the WD-1 cut-off.

The Management Accountants (in Financial accounting & reporting) are happy to talk through a draft accrual before you submit it, but the form needs to be in by WD-1 regardless.
