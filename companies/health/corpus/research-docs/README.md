# research-docs

Owner: Kellsbrook Research Institute · Last edited by Leonard Brandvold

Guides for the research side of Kellsbrook — IRB office, trials office, data management, cohort studies. Kalamazoo campus and Cork trials office both use these.

## What's here

- **Institute overview** — `about/institute-overview.md` — who we are, lines of reporting, how we sit alongside the Health Plan and clinical divisions.
- **IRB** — `irb/submitting-a-study.md` — new study submissions through the eIRB system, review timelines, resubmission after deferral.
- **Trials** — `trials/` — site activation, monitoring visits, protocol changes, CTMS and EDC basics, investigational product handling.
- **Data** — `data/` — sharing study data via labshare, pseudonymisation and study IDs.
- **Cohort** — `cohort/visit-windows.md` — visit windows and scheduling for longitudinal studies.
- **Grants** — `grants/pre-award.md` — grant prep and internal routing before submission.
- **Training** — `training/gcp-training.md` — GCP and human subjects training requirements.

## Adding a page

Drop a markdown file in the relevant folder, link it from the folder's README if one exists, and add a one-line entry above. Keep titles lowercase-hyphenated. If it spans more than one team (e.g. IRB plus data management), put it where the primary owner sits and cross-link.

## Notes

- Cork trials office keeps its own local copies of the trials guides; flag in #clinical-trials when you update anything in `trials/` so they can sync.
- The old study tracker spreadsheet in the shared drive is retired — anything that references it should point to the CTMS instead.
- Access to the EDC and CTMS is requested through tickets; Research data management approves it.
