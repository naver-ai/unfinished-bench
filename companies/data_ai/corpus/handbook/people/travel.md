# Business travel

Needs updating after the move — ping #rotterdam-office if something here looks wrong.

- **Booking**: all travel through the travel desk. Don't book direct unless the desk is closed and it's an emergency. Expense tool won't reimburse unapproved bookings.
- **Rail in Europe**: take the train for legs under about six hours door-to-door. Rotterdam–Paris, Rotterdam–Hamburg, that range. Fly for anything longer. First class only on night trains.
- **Rotterdam–Denver**: book economy plus for the long-haul leg. The desk applies this automatically; if the fare class isn't available they may ask you to pick a different flight. Domestic US legs are standard economy.
- **Hotels**: nightly cap is €180 in Rotterdam, $220 in Denver, $250 in London and Zurich. Caps include taxes. If the cap doesn't work for a conference or a field site, ask the travel desk to raise it with budget holder approval.
- **Field work**: travel insurance is automatic for sensor site visits and imagery ground-truth trips. The field app records the visit; insurance applies from when you check in there. For anything else (conferences, customer sites) insurance is the standard corporate policy.
- **Expenses**: submit within two weeks of returning. The travel desk can extend this for multi-stop trips. Receipts under €25 don't need to be attached but keep them for audit.
- **Contact**: #it-help for travel desk access issues, #finance for expense questions. The desk itself is in the HR system under Travel.
