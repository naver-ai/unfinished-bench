# Leave and absence

**Owner:** People & Culture  
**Last edited by:** Freya Bauer (HR Adviser)

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how leave and absence are managed at Aldercombe University, including annual leave entitlement, bank holidays and closure days, booking leave, sickness reporting, and family leave. It applies to all staff except where specific arrangements for Kestrel Vale Academy or Aldercombe University Malaysia are noted.

## 1. Annual leave entitlement

1.1 Annual leave entitlement is calculated on a full-time equivalent basis and is determined by grade and length of continuous service.

1.2 Staff in Grades 1–5 are entitled to 25 working days per annum, plus bank holidays and closure days.

1.3 Staff in Grades 6–8 are entitled to 28 working days per annum, plus bank holidays and closure days.

1.4 Staff in Grades 9 and above are entitled to 30 working days per annum, plus bank holidays and closure days.

1.5 After five years of continuous service, staff in Grades 1–5 receive an additional two working days per annum. After ten years of continuous service, all staff receive one additional working day per annum.

1.6 Entitlement is pro-rated for part-time staff and for staff joining or leaving part-way through the leave year.

1.7 The leave year runs from August to July, in line with the university's financial year.

1.8 Staff may carry forward up to five working days of untaken leave into the next leave year, subject to line manager approval. Any leave not taken or approved for carry-forward by the end of the leave year is forfeited.

## 2. Bank holidays and closure days

2.1 The university observes the standard bank holidays for England and Wales.

2.2 The university also observes a number of closure days, typically between the Christmas and New Year period, when the university is closed and no work is expected. These closure days are counted as part of annual leave entitlement.

2.3 Closure days are confirmed by People & Culture at least one month in advance and published on the HR intranet page.

2.4 Staff who are required to work on a bank holiday or closure day (for example, staff at Kestrel Vale Academy or staff supporting critical services) will receive equivalent time off in lieu, to be taken at a mutually agreed time with their line manager.

## 3. Booking leave

3.1 All leave must be booked in the HR and payroll system in advance, using the leave request function.

3.2 Staff should submit leave requests to their line manager for approval. Requests should be submitted at least twice the length of the leave period in advance, except for emergency leave.

3.3 Line managers should respond to leave requests within three working days.

3.4 Leave should not be taken until the request has been approved in the system.

3.5 Staff on the Hollins Hill and City campuses should book leave using the same process. Staff at Aldercombe University Malaysia book leave in the same system, but their entitlement is aligned to local employment law and the local public holiday calendar.

3.6 Staff at Kestrel Vale Academy should follow the academy's own leave request process, which is managed through the school office and the academy's school information system.

## 4. Sickness reporting and fit notes

4.1 Staff who are unable to attend work due to sickness must report their absence to their line manager by telephone before the start of their working day, or as soon as reasonably practicable.

4.2 Staff must also record their absence in the HR and payroll system on the first day of sickness.

4.3 For absences of up to seven calendar days, staff may self-certify. The HR and payroll system includes a self-certification form that must be completed.

4.4 For absences of more than seven calendar days, staff must provide a fit note from their GP or other medical practitioner. Fit notes should be uploaded to the HR and payroll system or given to their line manager.

4.5 Staff should keep their line manager informed of their expected return date and any adjustments that may be needed on their return.

4.6 Return-to-work conversations are expected after any period of sickness absence. Line managers can contact People & Culture (Freya Bauer or Astrid Fujita) for guidance on supporting staff returning from sickness.

4.7 Absence triggers and the management of long-term or frequent short-term absence are covered in the absence management process, which is available from People & Culture.

## 5. Family leave

5.1 Staff are entitled to statutory maternity, paternity, adoption, shared parental and parental bereavement leave. Statutory pay rates are applied as set out in the relevant legislation.

5.2 Staff should notify their line manager and People & Culture as early as possible when they intend to take family leave. The HR and payroll system has specific request forms for each type of family leave.

5.3 Staff taking maternity, adoption or shared parental leave will have their annual leave entitlement preserved for the period of leave, and may take accrued leave before or after their family leave period, subject to line manager approval.

5.4 Staff on family leave continue to accrue annual leave during their period of leave.

5.5 People & Culture (Olga Bose) is the first point of contact for questions about family leave, including pay and the interaction with annual leave.

## 6. Academy staff

6.1 Staff employed at Kestrel Vale Academy have their annual leave aligned to the school calendar. Entitlement is set out in their contract of employment.

6.2 Academy staff are expected to take their leave during school holiday periods, except where the Head Teacher has agreed otherwise for operational reasons.

6.3 Bank holidays are observed in line with the school calendar. Closure days do not apply in the same way as for university staff; the academy has its own arrangements.

## 7. Malaysia campus staff

7.1 Staff employed at Aldercombe University Malaysia have their annual leave entitlement set out in their contract of employment and aligned to local employment law.

7.2 Malaysia campus staff observe the local public holiday calendar rather than the England and Wales bank holidays.

7.3 Leave is booked in the HR and payroll system in the same way as for UK staff. Where local practice differs, the Malaysia campus HR team will advise.

## 8. Other absence

8.1 Staff should request time off for appointments (for example, medical or dental appointments) through the HR and payroll system as a leave request, selecting the appropriate absence type.

8.2 Staff may take reasonable time off for dependants in line with the statutory provisions. This should be recorded in the HR and payroll system as dependant leave.

8.3 Unpaid leave may be granted at the discretion of the line manager, in consultation with People & Culture. Requests should be submitted in writing at least one month in advance, except in exceptional circumstances.

8.4 Staff who are absent from work for reasons not covered by this policy should speak to their line manager or People & Culture in the first instance.
