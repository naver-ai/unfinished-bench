# Planned maintenance and compliance

Owner: Facilities Management (Aldercombe Estates Services)  
Last edited by: Greta Saito

This note covers the statutory and planned maintenance schedule for buildings we manage on the Hollins Hill and City campuses, plus the Malaysia campus properties and Kestrel Vale Academy. It sits alongside the permits-to-work guidance and the maintenance request process — this page is about the calendar, not about raising a job.

## Compliance tracker

The compliance tracker lives on the estates shared drive (`estates/compliance/compliance tracker.xlsx` — the file moved from the old `AES folder` a while ago). Ava Lindgren updates the tracker as certificates and test results come in. If you complete a test or inspection, email the certificate to the helpdesk rather than editing the tracker yourself — Ava reconciles the two.

## Notes on specific sites

**Kestrel Vale Academy** — the school has its own asbestos log and fire risk assessment, held by the academy office. Our team covers the gas safety and PAT there, but the weekly alarm test is done by the school caretaker. Martin Oduro's team will flag any access issues via the helpdesk.

**Kuala Lumpur campus** — statutory requirements follow Malaysian regulations, which differ in places from the UK schedule. The regional facilities coordinator maintains a separate tracker for that site and reports into Sofia Doyle each month. The UK schedule above applies only to the two UK campuses, Kestrel Vale, and the Hollins Hill residential blocks.

**Harbour Gate** — as a residential letting, the routine checks above apply, but the resident notice process for entry to flats sits with Residential Lettings, not with FM. If access is needed for a statutory test, raise it through the lettings team so they can give notice.

## Annual and longer-cycle work

- Fire risk assessments: reviewed annually per building by Neil Ashcroft's team, with actions logged in the tracker. Major findings go to the Health & Safety Committee.
- Asbestos re-inspection: every 12 months for all buildings with an asbestos register, alternating between the two campuses each quarter so nothing lapses.
- Water hygiene risk assessment: every 2 years, or after any significant change to the water system.
- Damp and mould inspections: carried out as part of the autumn pre-winter walkaround of all residential blocks, with follow-up works planned through the maintenance request queue.

## How to find what's due

Ava produces a monthly list of items due in the next 60 days and sends it to the Building Services team. If you're covering for Ava, the list is generated from the `next due` column in the tracker — don't rely on the sticker dates on equipment, as the tracker is the authoritative source. Any item that falls more than 30 days past its due date is escalated to Sofia Doyle in the weekly FM team meeting.
