# Laptops, phones and workstations

*Needs updating after the move — ping #it-help if something here looks wrong.*

**Which laptop do I get?**
Standard issue is a {{LAPTOP_2}} for most roles. Clinicians with badge tap on their workstations keep those as-is; if you need a laptop for rounds, request one through tickets and pick it up from the South Bend or Kalamazoo IT office. Cork staff go through the Cork IT desk.

**Can I get a Mac?**
Only for design work in the digital team, and that's approved case by case. Otherwise we don't support Macs on the network.

**What about clinical workstations on wheels?**
The COWs in the hospitals are managed by the IT service desk, not nursing. If a COW is missing a scanner or the badge reader isn't working, put in a ticket with the unit name and the COW number. Don't swap parts between COWs — the serial numbers are tracked.

**How do I get a shared unit phone?**
Each nursing unit and clinic has a shared phone for on-call and patient communications. Request one through tickets with your unit or clinic name. The phone stays on the unit; it doesn't go home with anyone. If you're the one carrying it for a shift, sign it out on the clipboard in the charge nurse office.

**My laptop is old and slow. Can I get a new one?**
We replace laptops on a rolling cycle — roughly every three to four years. If yours is in the replacement window, the service desk will reach out. If it's not but it's genuinely causing problems, open a ticket and we'll take a look. Sometimes it's just a full disk and we can clean that up.

**I dropped my phone and the screen cracked. What do I do?**
Open a ticket with the device serial number. For loaner phones, we have a small pool at each site — South Bend, Valparaiso, Kalamazoo and Cork. Bring the damaged phone to the IT desk and we'll swap it. If it's under warranty, we handle the return.

**How do I report a lost or stolen device?**
Call the service desk right away — don't wait to put in a ticket. We'll disable the device and start the replacement process. You'll also need to file a report with campus security (or the local {{POLICE_SERVICE}} station in Cork) if it was stolen. The service desk can help you with what to say.

**Can I use my personal phone for work email?**
Yes, for email and calendar only. You'll need to set up the work profile on your device and agree to the acceptable use policy. We can wipe the work profile remotely if the phone is lost, but we can't wipe the whole phone. Don't put patient information in any app that isn't the approved patient app — and that includes text messages.
