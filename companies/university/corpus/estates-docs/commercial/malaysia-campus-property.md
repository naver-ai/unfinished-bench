# Malaysia campus property

The Kuala Lumpur campus includes a mix of academic space and commercial property that the university lets to third parties. This covers the campus village retail units on the ground floor of the main teaching block, office floors let to partner companies in the innovation centre, staff and student residences, car parking bays, and a small number of storage rooms in the basement of Block C. The Campus Property & Leasing (Malaysia) team manages these tenancies day to day, with oversight from the Director of Campus Operations, Malaysia, and financial reporting through the Financial Controller.

All tenancies are recorded in the lease administration system, which is part of the estates systems we use across the university. Each tenancy gets a record with the tenant name, the unit or space identifier, the term dates, the rent and any service charge, and the notice period. The team in Malaysia keeps these records up to date, and the lease administration notes in `estates-docs/commercial/lease-administration.md` cover the general approach. For the KL campus, Kenneth Tan handles the day-to-day updates, Sienna Okafor maintains the tenant contact details and billing addresses, and Nurul Hassan files the signed copies of agreements, licences and side letters. Aoife Brennan reviews the records before each quarterly reporting cycle.

Rents are invoiced through the finance system, and the team raises the invoices from the tenancy records at the start of each month. The campus finance team (Siew Lan Chong) handles the receipts and any chasing, but the Malaysia team deals with tenant queries about the property itself, such as access, cleaning or maintenance. Service charges are reviewed once a year and tenants are given notice of any change in line with their agreement. Repairs and maintenance on the KL campus are handled by the local facilities team, and the Malaysia team raises those requests on behalf of tenants where needed.

Car park spaces and storage rooms are let on shorter terms than the retail and office units, often monthly or termly, and the records for these are kept in the same lease administration system with a flag that marks them as short-term lets. The team sends renewal reminders to these tenants before the current term ends, and if a space is not renewed it is offered to the next person on the waiting list. There is a waiting list for car park spaces, which Nurul Hassan maintains.

The quarterly report to the campus board covers occupancy, rent collected, arrears, and any tenancies that ended or were renewed in the quarter. Chloe Madsen provides the finance figures, and Marcus Lindahl approves the report before it goes to the board. The report is prepared from the lease administration system and the finance system, and it is stored in the estates document repository under `estates-docs/commercial/` alongside the other commercial property papers.

Tenancy data requests from other teams within the university go through the leasing coordinator, who checks the request against the records and provides what is needed. Requests from outside the university are handled separately, and the notes in `estates-docs/commercial/tenancy-data-requests.md` set out the process.

The KL campus property team works closely with the wider estates team on the Hollins Hill and City campuses, and the lettings overview in `estates-docs/lettings/lettings-overview.md` is a useful reference for how the university approaches residential lettings generally. For the KL campus residences, the accommodation booking system is used for student rooms, while staff residences and the commercial units sit in the lease administration system.
