# Mail and calendars

**Owner:** Messaging & collaboration  
**Last edited by:** Leah Dubois

## How do I get a shared mailbox or distribution list?

Open a ticket in the service desk portal and pick "Mailbox / DL request" from the category list. Include the purpose, the expected number of users, and the name of the person who approves it for your team. Most shared mailboxes and DLs are provisioned within two working days. If you need it sooner, say so in the ticket and we will see what we can do.

## Can I just create a distribution list myself?

No. DLs are created by Messaging & collaboration only. This keeps the address list tidy and makes sure we do not end up with duplicate names. If you already have a DL and need to change its members, you can do that yourself in {{MAIL_CLIENT}} as long as you are listed as an owner.

## How big can my mailbox get?

The default quota is 50 GB. You will get a warning at 45 GB. If you regularly sit above 45 GB, archive old mail to your personal archive folder or move large attachments to a file share and delete the copies. If you genuinely need more, raise a ticket and tell us why. We have approved a few exceptions for people who must keep years of machine-specific correspondence.

## What happens when I hit the limit?

You stop receiving new mail. The sender gets a bounce message. That is it. No forwarding, no auto-archive. So keep an eye on the warning and clear things out before you get there.

## How do I book a meeting room?

Use {{MAIL_CLIENT}} room finder when creating a meeting in Reutlingen, Telford, or Greenville. The rooms appear as "K-RT-101", "K-TF-201", "K-GV-301" and so on. If the room shows as free but the door is locked when you get there, call the site reception or raise a ticket with the site facilities team (the CMMS covers the locks).

## Can I book a room for someone else?

Yes, add the room as an attendee and invite the person as well. The room will accept if it is free. If you need a room for a recurring meeting, book the whole series and then let the room resource handle it.

## Do the plants' shared terminals have mail access?

The terminals on the shop floor (the ones used for MES work instructions and shift handover) do not have {{MAIL_CLIENT}}. They have a read-only web view of shared mailboxes for the shift teams. If you need something sent from a terminal, send it from your own workstation or ask the shift lead to do it from theirs. The Telford drives line and the Reutlingen test lab are the same way.

## I need a room booked at short notice for a customer visit. Who do I ask?

Use the room finder first. If nothing is free, ask the site coordinator in #reutlingen, #telford, or #greenville. They know which rooms are actually available even if the calendar says otherwise. Do not book a room that is marked as reserved for the test lab — those are kept for measurement setup and calibration work and cannot be used for meetings.

## Can I share my calendar with someone outside Kessendorf?

Only with a Kessendorf account. External calendar sharing is disabled. If you work with a supplier or customer who needs to see availability, send them a few suggested times by mail instead. This has been asked a few times and the answer is still no.

## Where do archived mailboxes go?

When you leave, your mailbox is put into the mail archive. The retention is tied to the HR leaver process — see the mail archive page in tech-docs for details. If you are a manager and need access to a former team member's mailbox, raise a ticket and the team will set it up once HR confirms the leaver date.

## Fix the room list on my calendar — it still shows the old building.

The room list is maintained by Messaging & collaboration. If a room name is wrong or the capacity has changed, raise a ticket and we will update it. The Telford extension and the Greenville mezzanine rooms were updated a while back; if you still see the old labels, clear your {{MAIL_CLIENT}} cache once and the new list should come down.

## Who do I contact about mail flow problems?

Start with a ticket in the service desk portal. If it is urgent, post in #it-help and mention the sender and recipient. Cecilia and Aurelio usually see that quickly. For anything that looks like a security issue (phishing, strange forwards from a known address), use the reporting incident page instead.
