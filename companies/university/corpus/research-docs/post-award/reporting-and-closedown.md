# Financial reporting and closedown

**Owner:** Research Finance (post-award)  
**Last edited by:** Gaurav Obi (Research Finance Officer (post-award))

## Funder financial reports

Interim funder reports are prepared by the Research Finance Officer assigned to the award, using the finance system's grant ledger. Most funders expect a narrative alongside the figures; the Principal Investigator writes the narrative and the Research Finance Officer checks it against the ledger before submission. The Research Development (pre-award) team should be copied where the funder requires the institutional signature.

Standard deadlines:
- Quarterly reports are due no later than two working weeks after the funder's stated period end.
- Annual reports follow the same rule, counted in working weeks from the funder's year end.
- Where a funder asks for a report on a date that falls in the university's closedown, agree a revised date with the funder's grants team in writing before the original deadline.

## Final expenditure statements

When an award ends, the Research Finance Officer runs a final expenditure report and reconciles it to the grant ledger. The Principal Investigator must confirm that all commitments have been invoiced or will be transferred to the departmental cost centre. Once confirmed, the final statement is submitted to the funder.

Costs that are still pending at the point of final statement preparation (for example, a supplier invoice that has not yet been processed) should be accrued if they relate to the award period. If the amount is material, discuss with Graham Osei before submitting.

## Audit certificates

Awards above the threshold in the funder's terms require an audit certificate. Research Finance (post-award) arranges this through the university's internal audit team. The certificate is sent to the funder with the final statement, and a copy is filed in the project file.

The internal audit team needs at least ten working days' notice. If a funder asks for a certificate at short notice, tell them what the lead time is rather than promising a date we cannot meet.

## Overspends and underspends at the end

At closedown, any overspend against the funder contribution is transferred to the faculty cost centre shown on the award setup form. The Research Finance Officer writes to the faculty finance contact before making the transfer, setting out the amount and the reason. The faculty can challenge within five working days; after that the transfer proceeds.

Underspends are returned to the funder in line with the award terms. Where the funder allows virement into a related award or into a bridging fund, the Principal Investigator should request this in writing before the final statement is submitted. We do not hold underspends on the grant ledger after the final statement has gone out.

## Archiving the project file

The project file in the research grants system is closed when the final statement has been submitted and any audit certificate issued. The Research Finance Officer marks the award as closed and adds the final statement reference. Paper records are sent to central storage with the award reference on the box.

Access to a closed award remains with Research Finance (post-award) and the Principal Investigator. Anyone else needing to view the file should contact the team.
