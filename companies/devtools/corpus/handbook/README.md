# Gantline handbook

Owner: People operations · Last edited by Ebony Muller

## What's in this repo

- `company/` — about Gantline, office info, org overview
- `people/` — benefits, leave, expenses, travel, onboarding, working across offices
- `it/` — laptop setup, getting access to systems
- `security/` — data classification, incident reporting
- `legal/` — contracts and signing, open source
- `comms/` — talking to press, posting on social

## Proposing changes

- Open a PR against the page you want to change. If the page has an owner listed at the top, tag them for review; otherwise tag the People ops team.
- Small factual fixes (typos, outdated links) can go straight in — no need to ask first.
- Anything touching policy or benefits should be reviewed by the relevant team lead before merging.
- If you're not sure who owns a page, ask in #engineering or #general.
