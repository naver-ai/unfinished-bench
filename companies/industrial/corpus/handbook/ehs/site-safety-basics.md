# Site safety for office staff

**Owner:** Health, safety & environment  
**Last edited by:** Dalia Alvarez (HSE Manager)

Applies to everyone whose normal workplace is an office — including visitors, contractors and colleagues from other Kessendorf sites — when walking through production or the test lab. If you work on the floor full-time, your area induction covers the details; this is the quick reference for the rest of us.

## Before you go onto the floor

- [ ] PPE zones are signed at every entrance. Hard hat and safety glasses are mandatory in all marked zones; steel-toe boots are required in the Reutlingen assembly hall, the Telford drives line and the Greenville plant floor.
- [ ] Visitors must be escorted by a Kessendorf employee at all times. If you bring a visitor, you are the escort until they leave the building.
- [ ] Long hair tied back, no loose jewellery, no headphones. If you wear prescription glasses, the safety glasses go over them.
- [ ] If you are only walking through, use the marked walkways. Forklifts have priority; make eye contact with the driver before crossing.

## In the test lab

- [ ] The lab at Reutlingen has its own PPE rules at the door — laser encoder testing uses class 2 lasers and some rigs run open rotating shafts. Read the sign before entering.
- [ ] The rig control room is off-limits unless you are with someone from the test lab team; do not touch cables or power switches on the measurement racks.
- [ ] If a rig is running, keep clear of the marked exclusion zone. The NVH chamber door must stay closed while a measurement is in progress.

## First aid and emergencies

- [ ] First aiders are listed on the green boards at every staircase and at the main reception on each site. The list is also on the intranet under EHS.
- [ ] Fire wardens are on the same boards. Know who the warden is for your floor before you need them.
- [ ] On hearing the alarm: leave by the nearest marked exit, go to the assembly point shown on your floor plan, and wait for the roll call. Do not use the lifts.
- [ ] If you discover a fire or a person injured, raise the alarm first, then call the internal emergency number (printed on every phone and on the boards).

## Reporting a near miss

- [ ] Report any near miss — a forklift that passed too close, a wet patch on a walkway, a guard left off a machine — through the EHS reporting tool. It takes two minutes and it is how we fix things before someone gets hurt.
- [ ] If you are not sure whether something counts, report it anyway. The HSE team (Dalia, Ilse or Malik) triages everything and will come back to you if they need more detail.
- [ ] You can report anonymously if you prefer, but leaving your name helps the team follow up with you about what changed.

## Around the sites

- [ ] Telford: the drives line has a dedicated pedestrian route — do not shortcut across the pick faces. The distribution centre loading bays are for dock staff only.
- [ ] Greenville: the US plant layout differs from Reutlingen — check the site-specific map in the plant office before your first visit. Curtis Obeng's team runs the floor induction there.
- [ ] Reutlingen: the calibration lab and the test lab are both in Building C but are separate areas with separate PPE requirements — read both signs.

Questions or unclear signage: contact the HSE team via the intranet or catch one of us in the office. We would rather answer the same question ten times than have someone walk into a hazard.
