# Payroll

**Owner:** Accounting & payroll  
**Last edited by:** Arjun Meyer (Accountant)  
**Applies to:** All Gantline employees  

## Overview

Payroll runs on separate schedules for Portugal and the US. The Portuguese payroll is processed monthly in-house, and the US payroll is processed semi-monthly through our payroll provider. Expense reimbursements are included in the next applicable payroll run after approval, provided the expenses clear the cut-off below.

## Pay periods and pay days

| Region | Pay frequency | Pay period | Pay day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Portugal | Monthly | 1st through last day of the month | Last working day of the month |
| US | Semi-monthly | 1st–15th and 16th–last day | 15th and last working day of the month (or the preceding working day if the date falls on a non-working day) |

## Cut-offs

All payroll changes — new hires, terminations, salary adjustments, one-off payments, and expense reimbursements — must be approved and entered in the HR system before the cut-off to appear in the upcoming run.

| Run | Cut-off (working days before pay day) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portugal monthly | WD–5 | Includes 13th and 14th month accruals |
| US semi-monthly (first half) | WD–4 | Cut-off for the 15th pay day |
| US semi-monthly (second half) | WD–4 | Cut-off for the last working day pay day |

Changes received after the cut-off are processed in the following run. The Accounting & payroll team publishes the exact cut-off dates in #finance at the start of each quarter.

## Portugal: 13th and 14th month

The 13th month payment is made with the June payroll and the 14th month payment with the November payroll. Each payment is one month of base salary, prorated for joiners and leavers per the Portuguese labor code. The proration is calculated automatically by the HR system based on the employee's start or end date; Accounting & payroll reviews the output before each run.

## Expenses in payroll

Expense reimbursements approved in the expenses tool are paid through payroll in the region where the employee is employed. The approval must be recorded before the cut-off. There is no minimum amount; any approved expense is included. Expenses approved after the cut-off are paid in the next run.

## US payroll specifics

The US payroll is run through the payroll provider, and Gantline Inc. is the employing entity. All US employees must have their bank details and tax withholding forms current in the HR system. The provider imports the payroll file from the HR system on the cut-off date; the Accounting & payroll team reviews the import report and confirms the totals before submission.
