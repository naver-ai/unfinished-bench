# Returns and exchanges

**Owner:** Customer support  
**Last edited by:** Renata Vogel  
**Applies to:** Support team, stores, finance ops, wholesale

---

**How long does a customer have to return an item?**

30 days from delivery for change of mind, and 12 months for faults. The 30-day window runs from the date the carrier marks the parcel delivered, not from despatch. For store purchases it runs from the date on the receipt.

**What counts as faulty?**

Anything that stops the product working as it should or makes it unsafe — split seams on tents, leaking water bottles, zips that separate, cooking stoves that won't light. Normal wear and tear is not a fault. If a customer has used a waterproof jacket for two seasons and the DWR has stopped beading, that is wear and tear, not a fault.

**Do we need the customer to return the item before we send a replacement?**

For faulty items under £50, no. Offer a replacement or refund straight away and ask them to return the faulty item within 14 days using the prepaid label. For faulty items over £50, we need the item back first unless the fault is obvious from the photos. If the customer sends photos and the fault is clear (torn seam, broken buckle), we can still refund without waiting for the return.

**How do store returns work for online orders?**

Any UK or Ireland store can take an online return. The store processes it through the POS back office and the refund goes back to the original payment method. Stores don't need to check the original order against the customer's ID — the order number or the email confirmation is enough. The store should scan the returns label if the customer still has it, but it's fine if they don't.

**Can a customer return a store purchase online?**

Yes. They can post it back using the returns portal — the portal accepts store purchases as long as the customer has the receipt or the store can find the transaction. The refund goes back to the card used in store. If the customer paid cash in store, they need to go back to a store for the refund; we can't post cash.

**What's the exchange policy?**

Exchanges are treated as a return plus a new order. We don't hold stock for exchanges. If a customer wants a different size or colour, refund the original and advise them to place a new order. For faulty items where a replacement is needed and the item is still in stock, we can raise a replacement order through the support desk — the orders team handles those, not the store.

**Do we refund delivery costs?**

For faulty items, yes — refund the original delivery charge and any return postage if the customer used their own courier. For change of mind, no. If the customer paid for premium delivery and the fault was on our side, refund the difference between standard and premium as well.

**What about gift cards bought with a credit card?**

Refunds for gift cards go back to a new gift card. We can't refund a gift card purchase to the original card once the gift card has been activated. If the gift card was never activated, the refund can go back to the original payment method.

**How are refunds posted?**

Finance ops post refunds through ledgerctl after the return is scanned in at the warehouse or the store completes the return in the POS back office. The refund lands on the customer's card within 3-5 working days, though most show up sooner. For {{PAYMENT_WALLET}}, it's usually the same day.

**What do we tell a customer who says the refund hasn't arrived?**

Check the return status first. If the return is showing as received but the refund hasn't been posted, raise it to finance ops through the #finance-ops channel with the order number and the return ID. If the return hasn't been scanned yet, explain it can take up to 2 working days from receipt at the warehouse to show as scanned. Don't promise a specific refund date.

**Can we refund to a different card than the one used to pay?**

No. Refunds always go back to the original payment method. If the original card has expired, the card issuer still routes the refund — the customer doesn't need to do anything. If the card was a {{CARD_SCHEME_1}} or {{CARD_SCHEME_2}} debit card and the account is closed, the card issuer will reject the refund and we'll need to send a bank transfer, but that goes through finance ops and takes longer.

**Do we offer returns for wholesale customers?**

Wholesale returns are handled by the wholesale team, not customer support. If a wholesale customer calls or emails about a return, pass the enquiry to the wholesale team via the #wholesale channel. Don't promise anything about credit notes or collection.

**What about items bought in Ireland stores?**

Same 30-day window and same fault policy. Refunds for Irish store purchases go back to the original payment method. If the customer paid in euros, the refund is in euros. Online refunds for Irish customers are processed in the currency they paid in.

**Can a customer return an item they bought in the US?**

US orders are handled by the Columbus team. If a US customer contacts the UK support line, take the details and send them to the Columbus support inbox — don't process the return yourself. The US team has its own returns process and the window is slightly different, so it's safer to hand over.

**Do we need to see proof of purchase?**

It helps, but it's not essential. We can find the order by email address, order number, or the card used. If we can't find the order at all and the customer has no receipt, we can still accept the return at the store manager's discretion for items that look like our current stock. This is rare — most of our products are findable in the system.

**What's the condition for a change of mind return?**

The item must be unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging where applicable. Sleeping bags must be returned in the stuff sack, tents with all pegs and poles. If an item shows signs of use (mud on a tent footprint, food marks on a stove), it's not returnable as change of mind. If the item is faulty as well as used, treat it as faulty.

**Are there any items we don't take back?**

Underwear, socks, and swimwear can only be returned if the packaging is sealed and the item is unworn. This is for hygiene reasons. Gas canisters and fuel cannot be returned by post — if a customer bought these online and wants to return them, they need to take them to a store, and the store will dispose of them safely. We don't refund gas canisters once they've been opened.

**How do we handle a return where the customer says the item was damaged in transit?**

Ask for photos of the damage and the packaging before they send it back. If the damage is clear, offer a refund or replacement without waiting for the return. The carrier claim is our problem, not the customer's — don't ask them to keep the packaging for a claim. If the outer box is damaged but the product is fine, it's not a return.

**What if the customer has lost the returns label?**

They can generate a new one from the returns portal using their order number. If they can't access the portal, we can email them a new label through mailer. The label is prepaid — customers shouldn't pay for return postage for faulty items, and for change of mind we deduct £2.99 from the refund.

**Do we deduct anything for change of mind returns?**

We deduct the return postage cost of £2.99 from the refund. There's no restocking fee. For faulty returns, there's no deduction and we refund the original delivery charge as well.

**How long does a store have to process a return?**

Stores should process returns on the day the customer brings the item in. If the store is busy, it can be done before the end of the shift. Delayed returns cause the customer to chase us, so it's important to process them promptly. The POS back office sends the return to the warehouse system automatically.

**What happens after the store processes a return?**

The refund is queued for finance ops to post. If the store processed it correctly, finance ops usually post it within a day. If the item needs to go back to the warehouse from the store, the store ships it in the next weekly stock transfer — the refund is not delayed by this.

**Can a support advisor authorise a refund without the item being returned?**

For faulty items under £50, yes. For anything above that, the advisor should check with a support lead first. The lead can authorise a refund without return if the photos are clear and the fault is obviously ours. This is at the lead's discretion — don't escalate to Renata unless the lead has declined and you think it's still the right call.
