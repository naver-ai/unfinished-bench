# Plan products at a glance

**Owner:** Kellsbrook Health Plan  
**Last edited by:** Colleen Haines (President, Kellsbrook Health Plan)

This page summarizes the Kellsbrook Health Plan product lines, the networks they use, and who typically buys them. For details on how claims move through adjudication or payment, see the claims pages in this repository.

## Employer group and individual products

| Product | Network | Deductible range (individual) | Out-of-pocket max range (individual) | Primary purchasers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gold PPO | Kellsbrook Choice PPO | $1,000 – $2,500 | $3,500 – $6,000 | Large employer groups, self-funded admin services only |
| Silver HMO | Kellsbrook Care HMO | $1,500 – $3,500 | $4,500 – $7,500 | Small and mid-size employer groups, individual exchange |
| Bronze EPO | Kellsbrook Select EPO | $3,000 – $6,000 | $6,500 – $9,000 | Individual exchange, cost-conscious employer groups |
| Platinum PPO | Kellsbrook Choice PPO | $0 – $500 | $1,500 – $3,000 | Employer groups with richer benefit designs |

The Gold PPO remains our most widely held employer product, particularly among manufacturers and logistics employers in the South Bend and Valparaiso corridors. Silver HMO is the most common plan sold through the individual exchange in both Indiana and Michigan. Bronze EPO is available on the exchange only; we do not offer it to employer groups outside of a few grandfathered arrangements.

## Network notes

- Kellsbrook Choice PPO includes all three Kellsbrook hospitals, most employed and affiliated clinics in the region, and contracted out-of-network coverage at a reduced level.
- Kellsbrook Care HMO is narrower: it covers the Kellsbrook hospitals, employed primary and specialty clinics, and a contracted set of community providers in each county. Referrals are required for specialty care.
- Kellsbrook Select EPO uses the same provider network as the HMO but does not require referrals. There is no out-of-network coverage except for emergency care.

## Enrollment and administration

Employer groups enroll through the benefits administration portal. Individual and {{PUBLIC_HEALTH_INSURANCE_1}} members enroll through the member portal or by phone with member services. The Valparaiso office handles group enrollment and billing; the South Bend office manages individual and {{PUBLIC_HEALTH_INSURANCE_1}} enrollment. The Cork medical centre does not offer Kellsbrook Health Plan products — it operates on a separate arrangement for employer-group members at US companies' Cork sites and for the public.

For questions about which product a member is on, check the member portal or contact member services. For network questions, contact provider services.
