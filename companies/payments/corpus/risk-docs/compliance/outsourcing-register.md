# Outsourcing register

**Owner:** Compliance  
**Last edited by:** Jada Garza (Compliance Monitoring Officer)

This register records critical and important outsourcing arrangements plus other material vendors that Kilbarra relies on to run its business. It supports the annual review required under our outsourcing framework and feeds into the compliance monitoring programme.

The register is maintained in the Compliance shared drive. The Compliance Monitoring Officer keeps it up to date; changes are logged in the file history.

## What goes on the register

An arrangement belongs on the register if it meets any of the following:

- It is an outsourcing arrangement for a function that is critical or important to our e-money or payment services.
- The vendor provides a service that, if disrupted, would materially affect our ability to meet regulatory obligations or serve merchants.
- The vendor processes personal data on our behalf and the engagement is not already covered by another register (for example, the processor inventory in the data protection records).

Intra-group arrangements (for example, services provided between Kilbarra and any affiliate) are recorded in a separate section of the register and are reviewed on the same cycle.

## Fields kept for each entry

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Vendor name | Legal entity name and, where relevant, the group parent |
| Service description | What the vendor does for Kilbarra |
| Critical or important | Yes / No, with a short rationale |
| Location(s) | Where the service is provided from, including any sub-processor locations |
| Contract start / renewal | Key dates; renewal date is confirmed with Procurement |
| Sub-processors | Listed where the vendor engages sub-processors for our services |
| Data involved | Categories of data shared with the vendor |
| DPO / risk sign-off | Date of the most recent sign-off |
| Exit plan | Link to the exit plan document in the vendor folder |
| Last review date | Date of the most recent annual review |

## Annual review

Each entry is reviewed at least once a year. The review covers whether the service is still critical or important, whether the vendor's risk profile has changed, whether sub-processors have changed, and whether the exit plan is still workable. Reviews are scheduled by the Compliance Monitoring Officer and completed with input from the business owner for the service.

The annual review is also the point where new arrangements are confirmed as in scope or out of scope. Findings from the review are summarised for the Risk & Compliance committee.

## Exit plans

Every critical or important arrangement has an exit plan. The plan covers:

- The steps to exit the service, including data retrieval and deletion.
- The internal team responsible for running the exit.
- The alternative supplier or internal capability, where one exists.
- The expected lead time for an orderly exit.

Exit plans are stored in the vendor folder alongside the contract. They are refreshed as part of the annual review and whenever the service changes materially.
