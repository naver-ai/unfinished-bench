# Kilbarra handbook

Repo owner: People team. Last edited by Ruth Demir.

## What lives here

- Company, people and IT policy (handbook/company, handbook/people, handbook/it, handbook/legal, handbook/security, handbook/comms)
- Anything that applies to everyone, regardless of role or team
- Pointers to where team-specific process lives

## What lives elsewhere

- **eng-docs/** — engineering, SRE, platform, payments bus, edge, incidents, on-call
- **security-docs/** — SOC, PKI, keys, secrets, access reviews, appsec, compliance evidence
- **finance-docs/** — close, payroll, treasury, regulatory reporting, tax, supplier payments
- **risk-docs/** — AML/KYB, transaction monitoring, merchant risk, complaints, control testing, privacy
- **merchant-docs/** — merchant onboarding, billing, support, partner programme, playbooks, comms

## Proposing a change

- PR against the page you want to change; add the page owner as reviewer (if unclear, ping #general or the relevant team channel)
- For handbook-wide changes, loop in the People team
- Small wording fixes don't need an owner lookup — just submit and mention it in #it-help if it's urgent

## Old intranet

- The intranet was decommissioned; content moved here or to the relevant team repo
- Anything you can't find should be raised in #it-help — do not recreate pages until we've confirmed they aren't already maintained elsewhere
