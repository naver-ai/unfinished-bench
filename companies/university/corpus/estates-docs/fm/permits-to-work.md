# Permits to work

**Owner:** Health, Safety & Environment  
**Last edited by:** Eero Silva (Health and Safety Adviser)

Applies to all contractors and university staff carrying out the activities listed below on Hollins Hill, City campus, Kestrel Vale Academy, or Aldercombe Estates Services managed properties. Permits are issued from the estates office at Hollins Hill during normal office hours; out-of-hours requests go through the estates helpdesk.

## Before any permit is issued

- [ ] Contractor induction completed at the estates office — includes site orientation, emergency procedures, and the location of first aid points. Book via the estates helpdesk, not by turning up.
- [ ] Asbestos register checked for the specific building and area of work. If the register is unclear or the area isn't covered, stop and ask Health, Safety & Environment before proceeding. Henrik Pham's team keeps the register; do not rely on the contractor's own records.
- [ ] Contractor has provided a method statement and risk assessment for the task. These stay with the permit at the worksite.
- [ ] Permit form completed in the estates helpdesk system, with the correct location (building, floor, room or plant identifier). Vague descriptions are returned without review.
- [ ] Permits are valid for the dates and times stated on the form. Extensions require a new form, not a handwritten note on the old one.

## Hot works

- [ ] Hot works permit required for any task involving flame, spark, or heat — welding, grinding, soldering, bitumen work, hot air tools, and similar.
- [ ] Fire extinguisher and fire blanket on site before works begin.
- [ ] 30-minute fire watch after completion of the works, and again 60 minutes later. The fire watch log goes back to the estates office with the permit.
- [ ] Gas cylinders stored in the designated cage outside the plant room at Hollins Hill; never taken inside outside working hours.

## Working at height

- [ ] Permit required for any work at height above 2 metres, including access equipment setup and dismantling.
- [ ] Stepladders and podium steps are not covered by this permit if the task is under 30 minutes and the surface is level. Anything longer or on stairs, slopes, or uneven ground needs a full permit.
- [ ] Edge protection and anchor points to be inspected by the contractor's own competent person before use; the inspection record is attached to the permit.

## Electrical and water isolations

- [ ] Isolation permit required for work on live or de-energised electrical circuits, and for any work that disturbs water supply, drainage, or heating pipework serving more than one room.
- [ ] Isolation point confirmed with the estates team and, for City campus, with the building management office. Do not assume the labelled isolator is the correct one — verify it.
- [ ] Lockout/tagout kit issued from the estates stores; contractor's own kits are acceptable if they meet the standard Greta Saito uses for her own work.
- [ ] For water isolations affecting Halls of residence, notify Student Accommodation before closing the supply. Chiara Blanco's team needs a minimum of two working days' notice for non-urgent work.

## On completion

- [ ] Permit signed off by the contractor and countersigned by the estates team.
- [ ] Any damage to fabric, finishes, or existing services reported through the estates helpdesk, not left for the next team to discover.
- [ ] Keys, fobs, and access passes returned to the estates office on the same day the permit closes.
- [ ] Health, Safety & Environment carry out spot checks on permits and worksites; permits found to be inactive or with incomplete paperwork are closed out and the contractor's supervisor is contacted.

## Notes

- Permits are specific to the task, location, and contractor named on the form. A permit cannot be transferred between contractors, and subcontractors must be listed on the original application.
- University staff carrying out the activities above under their own job role — for example the electrical team, or Neil Ashcroft's plumbing contractors — follow the same process. Greta's team applies for electrical isolations the same way as external contractors.
- For planned maintenance work that is already in the estates-docs/fm/planned-maintenance.md schedule, the permit application should reference the maintenance reference number so the estates team can check for clashes.
- Queries about whether a particular activity needs a permit go to Health, Safety & Environment via the estates helpdesk, not to the contractor's own safety officer.
