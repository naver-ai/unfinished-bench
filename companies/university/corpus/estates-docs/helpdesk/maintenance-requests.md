# Maintenance requests

**Owner:** Facilities Management (Aldercombe Estates Services)  
**Last edited by:** Greta Saito (Electrician)

All reactive maintenance and fault reporting for university buildings, halls of residence, lettings flats, and the Kestrel Vale Academy site goes through the estates helpdesk. The helpdesk is staffed during working hours on the Hollins Hill campus; requests can be raised via the estates helpdesk portal, by phone, or in person at the FM office.

## Raising a request

Include the building and room number, a description of the fault, and whether it affects safety, access, or a teaching or residential space. Photos help. If you are reporting the same fault that has already been raised, quote the existing ticket number rather than opening a new one.

For faults in halls of residence, use the accommodation booking system to link the request to the correct building and flat. For lettings flats at Harbour Gate and elsewhere, raise through the lettings system and mention the tenancy reference.

## Priority levels

| Priority | Response target | Examples |
|----------|----------------|----------|
| P1 – Emergency | Within 2 hours, 24/7 | Gas leak, flooding, fire damage, total power failure, blocked sole means of escape |
| P2 – Urgent | Within 4 working hours | No heating in a residential block, lift stuck, water leak contained but ongoing, no hot water in a teaching building |
| P3 – Standard | By the next working day | Faulty light fittings, dripping tap, door closer not working, minor plaster damage |
| P4 – Planned | Within 5 working days | Cosmetic damage, furniture repairs, non-urgent decoration |

P1 faults should also be reported to campus security on the emergency number so they can coordinate with the FM duty officer. The helpdesk will confirm who is attending and give you an estimated arrival window.

Out-of-hours means after the FM office closes, at weekends, and on university closure days. Campus security holds the FM on-call list and will contact the relevant trade where needed. Do not attempt to make safe anything you are not trained for — for electrical or gas issues, security will contact the on-call electrician or the duty building services engineer.

## Reactive vs planned work

Anything reported through the helpdesk is reactive maintenance and is logged as such. Planned and compliance work — statutory inspections, servicing, condition surveys — runs through the planned maintenance programme and is not raised via the helpdesk. If you see a contractor on site carrying out planned work, that is expected; if you have concerns about what they are doing, contact Sofia Doyle's team rather than raising a duplicate fault.

P2 and above requests in teaching spaces during term time are prioritised so that rooms are available for the next timetabled session. The helpdesk coordinator, Ava Lindgren, will liaise with the timetabling team if a room needs to be taken out of use.

## Updates and closure

The engineer assigned to the job will update the ticket when they arrive and again when the work is complete. If you raised the request, you will get a notification when the ticket is closed. If the fault is still present after closure, reopen the ticket and add a note — do not raise a new one.

For faults that need parts ordering, the ticket stays open and the helpdesk will add a note with the expected delivery time. Luis Ortega keeps a small stock of common parts at Hollins Hill; Greta Saito and Emma Amsel carry standard electrical spares on the van.

## Sites and routes

| Site | How to report |
|------|---------------|
| Hollins Hill campus | Estates helpdesk portal or phone |
| City campus | Estates helpdesk portal or phone |
| Kestrel Vale Academy | Via the academy office, who forward to the helpdesk |
| Kuala Lumpur campus | Local FM team; not through the UK helpdesk |
| Lettings flats | Lettings system, then helpdesk |
| Halls of residence | Accommodation booking system, then helpdesk |

The helpdesk does not handle security faults, cleaning requests, or IT issues. Security faults go to campus security; cleaning goes to the cleaning supervisor for the building; IT faults go to the IT service desk portal.
