# Donations and Gift Aid

Owner: Income & gift accounting  
Last edited by: Theo Ikeda (Income Manager)

## Purpose

Notes on how gift income from Development & Alumni Relations reaches the finance system, how Gift Aid claims are prepared, and what happens on legacy notifications. Intended for Income & gift accounting staff and anyone in Development who needs to trace a gift.

## Coding of gifts

Donations are coded when they arrive in the finance system from the supporter CRM. The Development team records the fund destination on the donor record; we map that to the corresponding fund code in the finance system at month-end.

- Unrestricted gifts go to the general fund.
- Restricted gifts go to the specific fund code held for that purpose (e.g. a named scholarship, a faculty appeal, a capital project).
- If a gift arrives without a clear destination, it sits in a holding code until Development confirms. Nadia Kelmendi chases these with the Development Operations team on the first working day after month-end.

The mapping table lives in the finance system and is maintained by Income & gift accounting. Changes to the mapping are agreed with Felipe Sato before they are applied.

## Gift Aid

Gift Aid is claimed on eligible gifts where the donor has completed a Gift Aid declaration. The declaration is captured in the supporter CRM at the point of giving; Ebony Adler checks declarations against the donor record before each claim.

Claims are submitted to {{TAX_AUTHORITY_1|code}} quarterly. The cutoff for including a gift in a claim is the end of the month before the claim is due to be submitted — gifts received after that go into the following quarter's claim.

The claim file is generated from the supporter CRM, then reviewed in the finance system against the gifts posted in the relevant period. Ebony Adler prepares the claim and Theo Ikeda approves it before submission.

Where a donor's declaration has lapsed or been withdrawn, the CRM flags this and the gift is excluded from the claim. If a gift has already been claimed and the declaration is later found to be invalid, we notify {{TAX_AUTHORITY_1|code}} on the next claim and adjust the amount claimed.

## Legacy income

Notifications of legacy gifts come through from the executors or from Development & Alumni Relations. When we receive a notification:

- The Development team logs the legacy in the supporter CRM.
- Income & gift accounting sets up a receivable in the finance system once the value is confirmed.
- Payments received are matched to the receivable. Where the final amount differs from the notification, the difference is coded to the same fund as the legacy.

Legacy income is always treated as restricted unless the will or the donor's instructions say otherwise. Where there is any doubt, we ask Development to confirm before coding.

## Monthly reconciliation

On the second working day after month-end, Income & gift accounting reconciles gift income between the finance system and the supporter CRM.

- Total gifts posted in the finance system for the month are compared with the CRM's gift report for the same period.
- Differences are investigated line by line. Common causes are timing (a gift recorded in the CRM before it reaches the bank) and coding mismatches.
- The reconciliation is saved in the income folder on the shared drive, and any unresolved items are raised with Development at the monthly income meeting.
