## Daily takings

1. At close, run **End of Day** in the POS back office. This prints the till summary and posts the day's sales to the ledger under the store's cost centre.
2. Count the cash drawer against the till summary. Notes go in the cash box, coin in the clear plastic bags provided. Keep the counted amount on the **Takings Declaration** sheet in the safe.
3. Leave the till drawer open and empty when the store is closed. The float stays in the locked drawer box, not the safe.
4. If the store uses the drop chute (stores with the newer safes only), bag the takings with the declaration slip inside the bag, not taped to the outside.

## Safe limits

- Each store has a safe limit set by Retail ops — check the safe logbook for yours (it's written on the inside of the lid).
- If the day's takings take the safe over limit, do a cash collection the next working day. Do not leave cash sitting over limit for more than one working day.

## Cash collections

- Collections are arranged through the **cash collection portal** on the POS back office (under *Banking → Collections*). Book at least one working day ahead.
- The collector arrives with an ID badge and a collection bag. Confirm the badge matches the name on the booking confirmation.
- Hand over the cash in the sealed deposit bag with the collection slip. Get the collector's signature on the store copy of the slip and file it in the safe.
- Wholesale showroom takings are collected on the same schedule as the retail store where the showroom is attached. Standalone wholesale offices bank directly — see #wholesale for the arrangement.

## Till reconciliation

1. In the POS back office, open *Banking → Till Reconciliation*.
2. Select the till and the date. The screen shows the expected cash total from the till summary.
3. Enter the counted amount per denomination. The system flags any difference over £2.
4. If the difference is within tolerance, save and finalise. The reconciliation posts to the ledger automatically.
5. If the difference is over £2, enter the amount as counted, add a note explaining the likely cause (e.g. "short change given on a £20", "float miscounted at open"), and save. This escalates to the Regional Operations Manager.

## If it goes wrong

- **Till doesn't balance** — don't adjust the float to make it balance. Record the difference as above and let the ROPs team review. Repeated small variances on one till get picked up in the weekly review.
- **Cash collection doesn't arrive** — call the collection company's store line (number on the safe logbook). If it's after 4pm UK time, leave the cash in the safe and rebook for the next working day. Flag it in #stores-ops so the Regional Operations Manager knows.
- **Banking discrepancy** — if the banked amount comes back different from the collection slip, keep the slip and the banking receipt and raise it with #finance-ops. They reconcile against the ledger and will chase the collection company.
- **Safe is over limit and no collection is available** — contact your Regional Operations Manager. They may authorise a transfer to a nearby store with a larger safe, or an ad-hoc collection.
- **Cash goes missing** — do not discuss it with anyone except your Regional Operations Manager or the store manager. Log the discrepancy in the POS back office and call #security right away.
