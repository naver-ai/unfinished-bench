# Student complaints

**Owner:** Dean of Students & Student Casework  
**Last edited by:** Enzo Kaplan, Head of Student Conduct

## Purpose and scope

1. This procedure sets out how a student may raise a complaint about the university, and how the university will handle it. It covers all students on taught and research programmes at the Hollins Hill and City campuses and at Aldercombe University Malaysia.
1.1 The procedure does not cover academic judgement of assessment outcomes, appeals against examination results, or allegations of student misconduct. Those have their own routes.

## Early resolution

2. A student who wishes to complain should first raise the matter informally with the member of staff or the school/service concerned. Most complaints are resolved this way, and we encourage staff to respond pragmatically and quickly.
2.1 The member of staff who receives the complaint should try to resolve it within 5 working days. If they cannot resolve it, they should put the student in touch with the relevant head of school or service manager.
2.2 If the complaint is about a member of staff and the student is uncomfortable approaching them directly, the student may instead contact the member of staff's line manager or the Dean of Students & Student Casework.

## Formal stage

3. If early resolution has not been possible, the student may submit a formal complaint. Formal complaints should be submitted through the student casework system, which feeds into the Dean of Students & Student Casework team. Students who are unable to use the online form may submit a written complaint by email to the Student Casework team.
3.1 The formal complaint should be submitted within 20 working days of the matter arising or of the early resolution attempt concluding. We may extend this where the student has been unable to submit sooner for good reason.
3.2 The Dean of Students & Student Casework team will acknowledge the complaint within 3 working days of receiving it.
3.3 The team will allocate the complaint to an investigating officer who is independent of the matter. The investigating officer will normally be from a different school or service to the one complained about.
3.4 The investigating officer will review the matter, speak to the student and to any relevant staff, and produce a written outcome. The outcome will set out findings and any action to be taken.
3.5 The investigating officer should complete the investigation within 20 working days of the complaint being allocated. If the investigation is likely to take longer, the team will write to the student with a revised timescale.

## Review

4. If the student is dissatisfied with the outcome of the formal stage, they may request a review.
4.1 The review request must be made within 10 working days of the outcome being sent to the student, and should state why the student considers the outcome to be wrong or the process to have been flawed.
4.2 The review will be carried out by a senior member of staff who has not been involved in the complaint previously. For complaints about academic or student-facing services, this will normally be a Dean of Faculty or a Director of Professional Services. Where the complaint is about a service, the review will be conducted by the relevant Director or their nominee.
4.3 The reviewer may uphold the original outcome, vary it, or quash it and direct a fresh investigation. The review outcome is final at university level.
4.4 The review should be completed within 20 working days of the request being received.

## Completion of procedures letter

5. Once the review is complete, the Dean of Students & Student Casework team will issue a completion of procedures letter to the student.
5.1 The letter will set out the outcome of the review, confirm that the university's internal procedures are exhausted, and explain the student's right to refer the matter to the Higher Education Complaints Review Service (HECRS).
5.2 Students at Aldercombe University Malaysia should note that the HECRS route is not available to them; the letter for those students will instead confirm the position under Malaysian law.

## Independent adjudicator

6. A student who remains dissatisfied after the completion of procedures letter may refer the complaint to the Higher Education Complaints Review Service within 12 months of the completion of procedures letter being issued.
6.1 The university will cooperate with the HECRS's enquiries and will implement any remedy the HECRS directs.

## General

7. A student may be accompanied at any meeting held under this procedure by a friend or a representative from the Students' Union Advice Service.
7.2 The student casework system records all formal complaints and their outcomes. The Dean of Students & Student Casework team reports anonymised complaint data to the University Executive Board each quarter.
7.3 Staff who receive a complaint should not discuss it with colleagues who are not involved in the matter, and should not share the student's details outside the investigating team.
