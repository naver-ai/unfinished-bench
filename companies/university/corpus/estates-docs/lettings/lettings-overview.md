# Residential lettings

The Residential Lettings team manages the university's housing portfolio for staff and postgraduate families. Our main site is Harbour Gate on the City campus, which comprises 64 flats let on assured shorthold tenancies. We also manage the visiting academic flats in the two converted townhouses on Hollins Hill, which are let on shorter licences to visiting scholars and their families. The team sits within Estates & Facilities and works closely with the Income team in Finance, the front desk staff at Harbour Gate, and the Estates helpdesk for maintenance matters.

Tenancies are set up and managed in the lettings system, which sits alongside the wider estates systems. When a new tenancy is agreed, the Property Administrator creates the record, generates the tenancy agreement and associated paperwork, and arranges for the keys to be handed over at the Harbour Gate front desk. The front desk team, led by Joanne Pruitt, handles key handovers, visitor access, and day-to-day queries from residents. The lettings system holds the tenancy schedule, rent account history, and correspondence with tenants. The team also keeps the room booking system updated for the visiting academic flats so that the conference office and faculties can see availability.

Rent is collected monthly by direct debit, with the Income team in Finance managing the collection runs and posting receipts to the finance system. The lettings team is responsible for setting up the direct debit mandates at the start of a tenancy, issuing rent statements when a tenant asks for one, and chasing arrears in the first instance. If arrears cannot be resolved within a few weeks, the matter passes to the Income team for formal action. Rents for each flat type are set at the start of the financial year, and the lettings team updates the lettings system and the advertising pages on the university website accordingly.

The Harbour Gate front desk is staffed during office hours on weekdays, with an on-call arrangement for emergencies outside those hours. The front desk log is the record of deliveries, visitor access, and any incidents. Maintenance requests from residents go through the Estates helpdesk, and the front desk can raise these on a resident's behalf if they are unable to use the online form. The lettings team reviews the maintenance request list each week to identify any flats where recurring issues might suggest a bigger problem.

The visiting academic flats on Hollins Hill are let through the lettings system on a separate licence agreement. Bookings are taken by the lettings team directly from faculties and from the international office, usually for periods of a few weeks to a few months. The licence fee is set each year with Finance and is charged to the department or faculty making the booking rather than to the individual. Key handover for these flats is arranged with the Hollins Hill campus services team.

Property inspections are carried out twice a year at Harbour Gate and after each stay in the visiting academic flats. The lettings team arranges the inspections and sends the inspection report to the tenant or to the department for the visiting flats. End-of-tenancy inspections are carried out with the tenant present where possible, and any disputes about dilapidations are handled by the Property Manager in the first instance.

The lettings system also produces the resident notices that are issued from time to time about works in or around the buildings. For Harbour Gate these are usually agreed with the front desk and posted in the communal areas as well as emailed to tenants. The team uses the lettings system's notice function for this, and copies of all notices are kept in the lettings system so there is a record of what was issued and when.

For anything relating to leases, service charges, or the freehold arrangements at Harbour Gate, see the lease administration page in the estates commercial docs. The team's contact details are on the Estates & Facilities intranet page, and the lettings inbox is monitored during office hours.
