# Laptops and devices

**Owner:** IT & workplace tech  
**Last edited by:** Alana Rahman

---

**What laptop will I get when I join?**

Standard kit for engineering roles is a 16" {{LAPTOP_1}} Pro with 32 GB RAM and a 1 TB SSD. Non-engineering roles get a 14" {{LAPTOP_1}} Pro with 16 GB RAM and a 512 GB SSD. If you're in a role that needs something different — video editing, heavy local VMs, {{DESKTOP_OS_2}}-only tooling — raise it with IT during onboarding and we'll sort it. {{DESKTOP_OS_3}} workstations are available on request for people who need them.

**How often can I get a new one?**

The replacement cycle is three years. You'll get a reminder through the helpdesk when you're due. If your machine dies or becomes unusable before that, open a ticket and we'll get you a replacement from the spares pool — usually within a day or two. Hardware failures aren't charged to your team's budget; losses are.

**My laptop was stolen. What do I do?**

Open a ticket in the helpdesk right away and mark it as high priority. Also file a report with the local police if you can — we'll need the reference for the insurance claim. For a company-issued device, we'll wipe it remotely. If you have a personal phone enrolled for chat and email, we can wipe that too — but only the work profile, not your personal data. If the device had disk encryption enabled (it should have been by default), you're covered.

**Can I use my personal phone for chat and email?**

Yes. We use the mobile device management profile for chat and email on personal phones. It creates a separate work container — we can wipe that container remotely if the phone is lost, but we don't have visibility into your personal apps or photos. If you'd rather not enroll, you can use the web versions of chat and email instead. That's fine; just be aware you won't get push notifications for chat.

**I'm leaving Gantline. What do I need to return?**

All company hardware: laptop and charger, any external monitors or docks, phones, YubiKeys, and the lab badge if you have one. Bring them to the Lisbon or Portland office — or arrange a courier through IT if you're remote. Your access to chat, email and the admin console is cut off on your last working day; we'll also revoke your VPN certificate and remove your laptop from the device management profile. If you have a personal phone with the work container, it gets wiped automatically when your account is deactivated.
