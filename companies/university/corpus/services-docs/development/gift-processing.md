# Gift processing

**Owner:** Development & Alumni Relations  
**Last edited by:** Kalani Fonseca (Major Gifts Manager)

This covers how gifts are recorded in the supporter CRM, from the first cheque or card payment through to the thank-you letter and the Income team's reconciliation. It sits alongside the Income & gift accounting team's own notes on Gift Aid.

## Gift entry

1. Open the supporter record in the CRM and check the contact details are current. If anything looks out of date, update it before recording the gift.
2. Go to the **Gifts** tab and select **Add gift**.
3. Choose the gift type:
   - **One-off** for single payments
   - **Pledge** where a donor has committed to a future payment
   - **Direct debit** for regular giving (set up through the direct debit instruction form)
4. Enter the amount, the fund code (ask Development if you are unsure which fund a gift should be coded to), and the payment method.
5. For cheques, write the cheque number in the reference field. For card payments, the reference field should show the card transaction ID from the payment terminal.
6. Save the gift. The CRM will assign it a gift reference number starting **G-**.

## Thank-you letters

1. The CRM generates a thank-you letter automatically when a gift is saved, using the template for that fund code.
2. Check the letter before it is sent — particularly the salutation, the amount and the fund description.
3. Letters for gifts above £500 go through the Major Gifts Manager for a personal note. Otherwise, send the letter once it has been checked.
4. Email the letter where we have an email address; otherwise print and post it.

## Gift Aid

1. Check the supporter record for a valid Gift Aid declaration. The declaration must have been made within the last four years and the donor must have paid enough UK tax to cover the claim.
2. If there is no declaration, send one with the thank-you letter. Do not claim Gift Aid on the gift until the signed declaration is back.
3. If a declaration is already on file, tick **Gift Aid eligible** on the gift record. The Income & gift accounting team picks these up from the CRM each month.
4. If a donor cancels a Gift Aid declaration, Ebony's team should be told the date it was cancelled so they can stop claiming on that donor's gifts.

## Pledges and direct debits

1. When a pledge is recorded, set the expected payment date. The CRM will flag pledges that are past their expected date in the **Pledge reminders** view.
2. Marisol's team works through that view and chases pledges by email or phone.
3. Direct debit instructions are collected on paper forms or through the online giving page. Send completed forms to Income & gift accounting within two working days.
4. Direct debit collections run on the first working day of the month. If a collection fails, the CRM updates the gift record with the failure reason and Marisol's team contacts the donor.
5. Do not record a direct debit gift as received until the collection has gone through. Until then it stays as a pending instruction.

## Matching with the Income team

1. The Income & gift accounting team posts bank receipts to the CRM each working day. They match gifts to the payment reference or to the donor's name on the bank statement.
2. If a gift has not been matched within five working days, check the gift record — has it been saved with the correct payment method and reference?
3. Sometimes a donor pays by bank transfer without telling us. If you spot an unmatched receipt that looks like it is for a known pledge or appeal, send it to Income & gift accounting with the donor's name and the fund code.

## If it goes wrong

- **Wrong amount on a gift record.** Do not edit the amount once the gift has been reconciled. Tell Income & gift accounting and they will post a correction.
- **Duplicate gift records.** Merge the records in the CRM using **Merge records** under the supporter's actions menu, keeping the earlier record as primary.
- **Gift Aid claimed in error.** Flag the gift to Income & gift accounting. They will handle the correction with {{TAX_AUTHORITY_1|code}}.
- **Cheque bounces.** Notify Income & gift accounting and reverse the gift in the CRM. A member of the Development team will contact the donor.
- **Thank-you letter goes out with a typo or wrong amount.** Send a corrected letter with a short apology. Do not draw attention to it beyond that.
