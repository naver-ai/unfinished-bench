# Expenses

**Owner:** Finance  
**Last edited by:** Stephen Mallory, Director of Finance

**Purpose.** To set out what staff may claim as business expenses, how to claim, and the limits that apply. This policy applies to all employees of Aldercombe University and its subsidiaries (Aldercombe Estates Services Ltd and the Aldercombe Learning Trust), regardless of campus or location.

**Scope.** Covers travel, subsistence, and other reasonable out-of-pocket costs incurred on university business. It does not cover relocation costs, staff benefits, or costs covered directly by a research grant's own budget — those are handled separately by the relevant team.

---

1. **What can be claimed**

   1.1. Staff may claim expenses that are necessarily and wholly incurred in carrying out university business, and that are not otherwise provided by the university.

   1.2. This includes: rail and bus fares, air fares (economy only, unless the budget holder agrees otherwise in advance), taxi fares where public transport is not practicable, car mileage, parking, tolls and congestion charges, and subsistence (meals and refreshments) while away from your normal place of work on university business.

   1.3. It also includes: conference and training fees, visas and vaccinations required for university travel, and other costs that the budget holder agrees in advance are necessary.

   1.4. Personal items, fines, and costs arising from poor timekeeping or poor planning (for example, a taxi because you missed the train) are not claimable.

2. **Receipts**

   2.1. Receipts are required for all expenses over £5. For expenses of £5 or under, a receipt is not required, but the expense must still be listed in the claim.

   2.2. A receipt must show the supplier, the date, the items purchased, and the amount. A bank statement or card statement alone is not sufficient.

   2.3. If a receipt is lost, the line item may still be claimed if the budget holder is satisfied the cost was incurred. The claim must note that the receipt is missing.

3. **Mileage**

   3.1. Mileage is payable at the {{TAX_AUTHORITY_1|code}} approved rate, which is currently 45p per mile for cars and vans, and 24p per mile for motorcycles. The rate is reviewed when {{TAX_AUTHORITY_1|code}} publishes a change.

   3.2. Mileage is claimable between your normal place of work and a temporary workplace, or between two university sites (Hollins Hill, City campus, Kestrel Vale Academy, or the Kuala Lumpur campus where applicable).

   3.3. Commuting between home and your normal place of work is not claimable.

   3.4. Staff who use a university hire car or a pool vehicle may claim fuel costs with receipts, but not mileage.

4. **Subsistence**

   4.1. Subsistence is claimable at actual cost up to £10 for breakfast, £10 for lunch, and £25 for an evening meal when you are away from your normal place of work on university business and the cost is necessarily incurred.

   4.2. Where subsistence is provided as part of a conference or event (for example, a lunch included in the registration fee), you may not claim for that meal.

   4.3. Alcohol is not claimable.

5. **Approval by the budget holder**

   5.1. All claims must be approved by the budget holder for the cost centre to which the expense is charged. The budget holder is the person named as such in the finance system for that cost centre.

   5.2. For expenses over £250, the budget holder's approval is required before the expense is incurred, not just before it is claimed. This includes travel bookings and conference fees.

   5.3. The budget holder may delegate approval to another member of their team in writing, but the delegation must be recorded in the expenses tool.

6. **University card versus out-of-pocket**

   6.1. Where a university card is held, it should be used for university purchases rather than paying out of pocket. This applies particularly to travel bookings and conference fees.

   6.2. If you use your own money because a university card was not available, you should still make the claim promptly and attach the receipt.

   6.3. Personal use of a university card is not permitted and will be treated under the university's disciplinary policy.

7. **The expenses tool**

   7.1. Claims are submitted through the expenses tool, which is reached from the Finance page on the staff intranet.

   7.2. Claims should be submitted within 60 days of the expense being incurred. Claims older than this require the Director of Finance's approval.

   7.3. The tool routes the claim to the budget holder for approval, and then to Accounts Payable for checking. Once checked, the claim is passed to payroll for payment in the next available payroll run.

   7.4. The expenses tool replaced the older paper form (the green form from Finance) several years ago. Paper forms are no longer accepted.

8. **Reimbursement**

   8.1. Reimbursement is made through the payroll run, usually on the last working day of the month.

   8.2. Reimbursement is subject to tax and National Insurance where required by {{TAX_AUTHORITY_1|code}}. Most expenses that meet the conditions of this policy are paid tax-free, but if the conditions are not met (for example, a receipt is missing and the budget holder has not agreed to it), the amount may be taxed.

   8.3. If you leave the university, any outstanding expense claims must be submitted before your last working day.

9. **Kuala Lumpur campus**

   9.1. Staff based at the Kuala Lumpur campus claim expenses in Ringgit through the same expenses tool. The exchange rate used is the university's monthly rate, set by Finance and published on the Finance page.

   9.2. Travel between the Kuala Lumpur campus and the UK campuses is covered by this policy, but the budget holder's approval must be obtained before any booking is made.

10. **Queries**

    10.1. Queries about this policy should be sent to the Finance team via the IT service desk portal, choosing "Finance" as the category.
