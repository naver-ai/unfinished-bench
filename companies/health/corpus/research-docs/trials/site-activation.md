# Activating a trial site

**Owner:** Clinical trials office  
**Last edited by:** Fabio Rossi (Site Coordinator, European sites)

## Feasibility

- [ ] Confirm the site has the patient population and referral base to support enrolment. For US sites, check the catchment of the nearest Kellsbrook hospital or clinic network — South Bend, Valparaiso, or Kalamazoo. For European sites, confirm coverage through the Cork medical centre's GP list and employer-group panels.
- [ ] Verify the principal investigator's credentials, current licensure, and GCP training status in the credentialing system.
- [ ] Check the site's staffing — who will coordinate, who will consent, who will handle investigational product. For European sites, confirm the coordinator role with the Cork office before proposing a timeline.
- [ ] Review the protocol's visit schedule and procedure burden against the site's clinic calendar. Flag any visit-window conflicts early; see `research-docs/cohort/visit-windows.md`.
- [ ] Confirm the site has the equipment the protocol requires (centrifuge, freezer, exam room, secure storage). The Cork medical centre shares space with the GP practice, so storage and consult room availability must be confirmed in writing with the practice manager.
- [ ] Ask the research institute's data management team to confirm the EDC and IWRS can support the site's language and time zone if it is a European site.

## Clinical trial agreement and budget

- [ ] Draft the clinical trial agreement (CTA) using the current template from the research institute's legal folder. Do not start site work before the CTA is fully executed.
- [ ] Budget: use the current fair-market-value rate card. For European sites, the Cork office prepares the budget in EUR and checks it against local cost comparisons before it goes to the sponsor.
- [ ] Route the CTA and budget through the Clinical Trials Office (Ben Bauer's team). The Chief Research Officer signs off on any budget above the standard threshold.
- [ ] Confirm the sponsor has provided the certificate of insurance and any local coverage requirements are met. For Ireland, confirm the medical centre's clinical negligence cover extends to trial activity.
- [ ] If the site is a hospital or clinic inside Kellsbrook Health, confirm the site's own finance team has the budget line set up. The research institute's grants team handles setup; see `research-docs/grants/pre-award.md`.
- [ ] File the executed CTA, budget, and any amendments in the study's regulatory binder and in the CTMS.

## Regulatory binder

- [ ] Create the regulatory binder in the CTMS with the current version of the protocol, the investigator's brochure, and the approved consent form.
- [ ] Confirm the ethics committee approval is on file. For US sites, that is the Kellsbrook Research Institute IRB; for European sites, the ethics committee the Cork office submits to. See `research-docs/irb/submitting-a-study.md`.
- [ ] Collect and file the investigator's current CV, medical licence, and GCP certificate (training records are also in the learning management system).
- [ ] File the signed Form FDA 1572 for US sites. For European sites, file the clinical trial regulation declaration and any required national notifications.
- [ ] Confirm the delegation of authority log is current. Each person listed must have GCP training and be named in the delegation log before they do any study procedure.
- [ ] For European sites, Oskar Eklund reviews the binder before the site initiation visit to confirm all local regulatory documents are in place.

## Site initiation visit

- [ ] The Clinical Trials Office schedules the site initiation visit (SIV) once the CTA is executed, the binder is complete, and the EDC and IWRS accounts are ready.
- [ ] Send the SIV agenda to the site at least five working days before the visit. Include the sponsor's representative if the sponsor plans to attend.
- [ ] At the SIV, cover: protocol overview, consent process, visit scheduling, adverse event reporting, investigational product handling, EDC entry, and query resolution.
- [ ] Confirm the site team can log in to the EDC and IWRS during the SIV. Do not close the visit until every named team member has a working account.
- [ ] For European sites, Fabio Rossi or Petra Delgado runs the SIV. The Cork office arranges the room, the projector, and any videoconference link for the sponsor.
- [ ] Document the SIV in the CTMS. Note who attended, what was covered, and any follow-up items with owners and target dates.
- [ ] Follow-up items from the SIV must be resolved and confirmed in the CTMS before the site can enrol the first participant.

## IWRS and EDC accounts

- [ ] Submit the account request through the CTMS to the data management team. Include the site's staff list and roles.
- [ ] EDC accounts are provisioned by the research institute's data management team. IWRS accounts are provisioned by the sponsor or the IWRS vendor; the Clinical Trials Office sends the request.
- [ ] Confirm each account uses the site's official email address. For Cork, use the kellsbrookhealth.org address, not the medical centre's public address.
- [ ] Confirm the EDC training was completed before the SIV. Zeynep Blanco tracks EDC training completion in the CTMS.
- [ ] If a team member leaves the site, the Site Coordinator must notify the Clinical Trials Office the same working day so the accounts can be disabled.

## First participant in

- [ ] The site is ready for the first participant when: the SIV is closed, all follow-up items are resolved, the regulatory binder is current, and all team members have working EDC and IWRS accounts.
- [ ] For European sites, the Cork office confirms readiness to the Clinical Trials Office. Fabio Rossi is the point of contact for the readiness confirmation.
- [ ] The first participant's screening visit is scheduled through the normal clinic scheduling system for US sites. For European sites, the Cork medical centre's own scheduling is used; the coordinator logs the visit in the CTMS.
- [ ] Notify the CTMS when the first participant signs consent. The Clinical Trials Office uses this date for the site's activation milestone.
- [ ] After the first participant is enrolled, the site enters the monitoring schedule described in `research-docs/trials/monitoring-visits.md`.

## European site activation through the Cork office

- [ ] European sites are activated through the Cork office. The Cork office confirms feasibility, prepares the budget in EUR, coordinates the CTA with the sponsor, and arranges the SIV locally.
- [ ] The Cork office submits the regulatory documents to the ethics committee and handles any national authority requirements.
- [ ] The Cork office also confirms the medical centre's GP practice has the capacity for any extra visits the trial will generate.
- [ ] Site payments for European sites are handled in EUR through the Cork office's finance process. The Clinical Trials Office tracks payment milestones in the CTMS.
- [ ] The Cork office sends the Clinical Trials Office a monthly summary of active European sites, enrolment status, and any open follow-up items.
