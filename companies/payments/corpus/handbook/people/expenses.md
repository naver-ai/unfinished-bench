# Expenses

**Owner:** Finance operations  
**Last edited by:** Laszlo Brennan (Revenue Accountant)

**Purpose.** This policy sets out what employees can claim as business expenses, how to submit claims, and how reimbursement works. It applies to all Kilbarra employees and contractors who incur expenses on behalf of the company.

**Scope.** Covers travel, accommodation, meals, and other business-related purchases made using personal funds or company cards. Per diem rates apply to trips between Dublin, Vilnius, and Austin.

---

1. **Eligible expenses**
   1.1. Reasonable and necessary costs incurred for Kilbarra business are claimable. This includes transport, accommodation, client entertaining, and work-related supplies.
   1.2. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims older than 90 days will not be reimbursed unless approved by the Head of Finance Operations.
   1.3. Alcohol is claimable only when entertaining a client or partner and must be itemised separately.

2. **Receipts**
   2.1. Receipts are required for all expenses over €25 (or equivalent). For amounts below this, a line-item entry in the expenses tool is sufficient.
   2.2. Receipts must show the merchant name, date, and total. Itemised receipts are required for hotel bills and restaurant bills over €100.
   2.3. Lost receipts: if a receipt is lost, the claimant must provide a signed declaration explaining the purchase. The line manager must approve the declaration in the expenses tool.
   2.4. Digital scans or photos of receipts are accepted. Upload them to the expenses tool at the time of submission.

3. **Per diems for inter-office travel**
   3.1. When travelling to another Kilbarra office, employees may claim a fixed per diem instead of itemised meal receipts.
   3.2. Rates per full day: Dublin — €50; Vilnius — €40; Austin — $70. Half-day rates apply when travel takes up more than half the working day.
   3.3. Per diems cover meals and incidental costs only. Accommodation and transport are claimed separately with receipts.
   3.4. No per diem is payable for days spent working from the employee's home office location.

4. **Company cards**
   4.1. Company cards are issued through the expenses tool for employees who travel regularly or have recurring business purchases. Requests go through the line manager and Finance operations.
   4.2. Cardholders must reconcile transactions within 10 working days of the statement date. Unreconciled items may result in the card being suspended.
   4.3. Company cards are for business use only. Personal purchases on a company card must be reimbursed to Kilbarra within 30 days.

5. **Approval**
   5.1. All claims require line manager approval in the expenses tool before payment.
   5.2. Claims over €2,500 require additional approval from Finance operations.
   5.3. Claims from directors and the executive team are approved by the Chief Financial Officer.

6. **Reimbursement**
   6.1. Reimbursement runs with the payroll cycle. Claims approved by the payroll cut-off are paid in that cycle; claims approved after the cut-off go into the following cycle.
   6.2. Reimbursements are paid into the employee's nominated bank account via the payroll provider. Contractors are paid through the accounts payable run.
   6.3. Currency: expenses incurred in EUR are reimbursed in EUR; expenses in USD are reimbursed in USD for Austin-based staff and converted at the company rate for staff based elsewhere.

7. **Exceptions**
   7.1. Any deviation from this policy requires written approval from Finance operations before the expense is incurred. After-the-fact exceptions are granted only in extraordinary circumstances.

8. **Changes to this policy**
   8.1. This policy is reviewed annually by Finance operations. Suggestions for changes should be raised with the team in #finance-eng.
