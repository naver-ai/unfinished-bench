# Payroll calendar and cut-offs

Owner: Payroll & pensions · Last edited by Erin Hartmann

Monthly pay runs on the last working day of the month. The cut-off for all changes to that month's pay is **eight working days before pay day**. Anything received after that goes into the following month's run.

| Cut-off | What is due |
|---|---|
| WD-8 | All HR and payroll change forms, new starter paperwork, leaver notifications, contractual changes, overtime, expense claims via the expenses tool, and casual or hourly-paid claim sheets |
| WD-6 | Payroll & pensions sends the pre-pay report to Faculty and Directorate administrators for review |
| WD-4 | Final pay data is exported to the finance system for the {{PAYMENT_SCHEME_3|code}} file |
| WD-1 | Bank file submitted to the bank; payslips released on the HR and payroll system portal |
| Last working day of month | Pay day |

If a cut-off falls on a non-working day, it moves to the previous working day. The full calendar for the payroll year is published on the Payroll & pensions page of the staff intranet, and the Finance office also circulates it at the start of each payroll year.

## Casual and hourly-paid claims

Casual workers and hourly-paid staff submit claims through the HR and payroll system. Claims must be authorised by the budget holder in the system before the WD-8 cut-off. Paper claim sheets are no longer accepted except for Kestrel Vale Academy support staff, who still use the paper form — those need to reach Payroll & pensions by the same cut-off.

Hourly-paid teaching claims from the timetabling system are imported automatically each month, but any manual adjustments need to be logged as a change on the HR and payroll system before the cut-off.

## Academy staff

Kestrel Vale Academy teaching and support staff are employed by the Aldercombe Learning Trust, not by the university. Their pay is calculated by Payroll & pensions but processed through the trust's own payroll arrangement. Academy payroll runs on the same monthly cycle and the same cut-offs apply, but academy staff are paid on the trust's own pay date, a few working days before the university's pay day. The academy's school information system feeds attendance and supply data into the HR and payroll system for academy staff.

## Malaysia campus

Aldercombe University Malaysia runs its own local payroll for staff employed on Malaysian contracts. University Payroll & pensions provides the pay scales and pension contribution rates, and the KL campus HR team administers the monthly run locally. The KL payroll calendar is set by the campus office and is not aligned to the UK cut-offs; any queries on Malaysia payroll dates go to the KL campus HR team.
