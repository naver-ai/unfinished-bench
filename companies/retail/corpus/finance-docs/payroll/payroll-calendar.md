# Payroll calendar and cut-offs

**Owner:** Payroll  
**Last edited by:** Aiko Weber (Payroll Administrator)

This page covers the monthly payroll cycle for office staff and the four-weekly cycle for store colleagues, plus the cut-offs for submitting changes, overtime and expenses.

## Pay days and cut-offs

Pay day is always the last working day of the month for monthly colleagues and the last working day of the four-week period for store colleagues. Cut-offs are measured in working days before pay day (WD = working day).

| Deadline (working days before pay day) | What must be in by then |
| --- | --- |
| WD-12 | Payroll receives confirmed new starters, leavers, and contract changes from the HR system. |
| WD-10 | Store managers submit overtime sheets via the POS back office. |
| WD-8 | Expense claims for the period must be approved in the expenses tool. Anything submitted after this goes into the following pay run. |
| WD-6 | Payroll team finishes checking the draft pay file. |
| WD-5 | Draft pay file is shared with Oliver Brandt's team for review in the ledger. |
| WD-3 | Final pay file is locked in the ledger. No further changes after this point. |
| WD-1 | Pay files are sent to the bank before the bank file cut-off. |

## Monthly payroll (office staff)

Office-based colleagues across Leeds, Dublin and Columbus are paid on the monthly cycle. The Columbus payroll follows the same calendar but is run in USD and uses the US bank file format. The Dublin office is paid in EUR; the rest of the UK and Ireland office staff are in GBP.

The monthly cycle runs from the first working day after the previous pay day to the last working day of the month. Overtime for office staff is not normally paid through payroll — it is taken as time off in lieu and recorded in the HR system. If overtime pay is needed for a specific role, the line manager raises it through the expenses tool as a one-off claim before the WD-8 cut-off.

## Four-weekly payroll (stores)

Store colleagues across the 61 UK and Ireland stores, plus the Wakefield DC, are paid on a four-weekly cycle. The periods are fixed in the HR system and the pay days are published in the stores-ops channel at the start of each financial year.

| Period | Pay day |
| --- | --- |
| Period 1 | Last working day of the first four-week block of the financial year |
| Period 2 | Last working day of the second four-week block |
| ... and so on through period 13 | |

Store managers submit overtime through the POS back office by WD-10. The store cash and banking page in finance-docs has details on how till discrepancies are handled — those are not part of the payroll run.

## Expenses in payroll

Expense claims approved in the expenses tool by WD-8 are paid with the next pay run. Claims approved after WD-8 go into the following run. This applies to both monthly and four-weekly colleagues.

For store colleagues, travel between stores and uniform purchases are the most common claims. Wholesale staff who visit independent shops and garden centres claim mileage the same way.

## Wholesale commission

The wholesale team's commission is calculated by the CRM and imported into payroll by WD-6. The import is checked against the CRM's own reports — any discrepancy is raised with the data-eng team before the draft pay file is shared.

## Contact

For questions about a specific cut-off, contact the Payroll team in #payroll. If a deadline was missed, raise it in #payroll as early as possible — we can sometimes accommodate late changes before WD-3, but it depends on the nature of the change and whether the pay file has been locked.
