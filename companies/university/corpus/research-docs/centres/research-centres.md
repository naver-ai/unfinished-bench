# Research centres and institutes

Research & Innovation Services keeps this list of the university's research centres and groups. For enquiries about a centre, contact the named administrator or school office in the first instance. For new centre proposals or changes to an existing centre's status, contact Research & Innovation Services.

The Centre for Coastal Systems' survey boat is available to other centres and to teaching by arrangement — Farid Mansour (laboratory and field operations manager) keeps the boat diary. Research in schools, including at Kestrel Vale Academy, goes through faculty-level ethics review; see `research-docs/ethics/ethics-review.md`.
