# Investigational product handling

**Owner:** Clinical trials office  
**Last edited by:** Noemi Brunner, Clinical Trial Manager

---

## Receipt

- [ ] Confirm the shipment against the CTMS receiving record and the EDC study setup before signing the courier manifest.
- [ ] Verify the product name, strength, lot number, and quantity match the packing list and the study-specific ID labels.
- [ ] Inspect packaging for damage, leaks, or broken seals. Photograph any damage and notify the sponsor contact the same working day.
- [ ] All product for Kellsbrook sites arrives at the Kalamazoo research pharmacy unless the study setup in the CTMS says otherwise. Cork site product goes through the Cork medical centre and is logged there.
- [ ] If the shipment is late or the temperature excursion is unclear, quarantine the carton and contact the Clinical trials office before opening.

## Storage and temperature logs

- [ ] Store product only in the designated, locked research pharmacy refrigerator or the locked investigational product cabinet in Cork. No other storage areas are approved.
- [ ] Temperature logs are recorded in the EDC or on the paper log provided by the sponsor. The research pharmacy records the Kalamazoo unit readings; the Cork site coordinator records the Cork unit.
- [ ] Logs are reviewed at least twice a week by the site coordinator. If a reading is outside range, treat it as an excursion immediately, do not wait for the next scheduled review.
- [ ] Keep continuous temperature data for the duration of storage plus retention. Trim the probes per the manufacturer instructions and keep the calibration certificate with the study file.
- [ ] If the research pharmacy refrigerator is full, do not move product to another unit without asking the Clinical trials office first. The backup unit in the pharmacy is for vaccine storage and is not to be used for investigational product.

## Temperature excursions

- [ ] When an excursion is identified, quarantine the affected product and label it "Do not use — excursion." Move it to a separate container if possible.
- [ ] Record the excursion in the EDC and in the CTMS. The Clinical trials office decides whether the sponsor needs to be notified by phone or by the EDC alert.
- [ ] Do not return product to active stock until the sponsor or the Clinical trials office confirms in writing that it is acceptable. Keep that confirmation with the accountability records.
- [ ] For the Cork site, Fabio Rossi coordinates the written confirmation with the Clinical trials office and enters the excursion details into the EDC.

## Accountability

- [ ] The accountability log lives in the EDC for each study. The research pharmacy maintains the log for Kalamazoo dispensing; the Cork site coordinator maintains the Cork log.
- [ ] Each entry records the subject ID, visit, date, lot number, quantity dispensed, and the initials of the person dispensing.
- [ ] Log entries are made on the working day of dispensing. Do not batch entries.
- [ ] The Clinical trials office reconciles the log against the EDC visit data at each monitoring visit and after study close.
- [ ] If a subject misses a dose or the dose is partially used, note it in the log and in the EDC per the study-specific instructions.

## Dispensing through research pharmacy

- [ ] Dispensing is done only by the research pharmacy staff or, in Cork, by the medical centre nurses who have completed the study-specific delegation log.
- [ ] The prescribing clinician must have the subject's eligibility confirmed in the EDC before the first dose is released.
- [ ] The pharmacy checks the subject ID against the visit schedule in the EDC and confirms no protocol hold or safety hold is active. If a hold is active, the pharmacy does not dispense and pages the Clinical trials office.
- [ ] Dispense the exact quantity for the visit or the window specified in the study setup. Do not give additional units unless the protocol says so.
- [ ] For home dosing, include the patient-facing instructions printed from the EDC and confirm the subject or caregiver understands the storage requirement at home.

## Returns and destruction at study close

- [ ] At study close, the site coordinator prepares a return or destruction manifest in the EDC. The manifest lists lot numbers, quantities, and subject IDs for any partial containers.
- [ ] The sponsor's return instructions are followed for product that is returned. If the sponsor authorises destruction on site, the research pharmacy performs the destruction with a second witness.
- [ ] For Cork, destruction is witnessed by the practice secretary and the site coordinator. The destruction record is signed by both and filed with the study records.
- [ ] All product that is not returned or destroyed is accounted for in the final reconciliation. The Clinical trials office sends the closing reconciliation to the sponsor within the timeline set in the study agreement.
- [ ] The Clinical trials office keeps the final accountability and destruction records in the research files for the retention period required by the study.
