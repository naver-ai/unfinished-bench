# Expenses and travel

**Owner:** Accounts payable  
**Last edited by:** Laszlo Yamamoto, Accounts Payable Clerk

**Purpose.** This policy explains what business expenses Kellsbrook Health reimburses, how to claim them in the expenses tool, and how reimbursement is paid. It applies to all employees and contractors who incur expenses on behalf of Kellsbrook Health.

**Scope.** Covers travel, mileage, meals, conference attendance, and other business purchases. It does not cover relocation, entertainment of non-employees, or gifts — those need separate approval from the Executive team.

## 1. What can be claimed

1.1 Reasonable and necessary business expenses incurred while carrying out your role.

1.2 Transportation — flights, train, taxi, rideshare, and parking — when travelling between Kellsbrook sites or to an external meeting or conference.

1.3 Mileage for personal vehicles used for business travel. The rate is set each financial year and published by Accounts payable. Mileage between campuses is claimable at the full business rate; mileage within your usual campus is not.

1.4 Meals while travelling away from your home campus on business. Meal claims do not require itemised receipts up to the daily cap; above the cap, receipts are required.

1.5 Lodging when business travel needs a hotel stay. Claims must be accompanied by the hotel invoice.

1.6 Conference registration fees and reasonable conference-related costs, where the attendance has been approved in advance (see section 4).

1.7 Work-related books, subscriptions, and software tools that cost less than the approval threshold in the expenses tool. Anything above the threshold needs your manager's approval before purchase.

## 2. Receipts

2.1 Receipts are required for any single expense over $25. For claims under $25, a receipt is not required but the expense must still be entered in the expenses tool.

2.2 Attach the receipt to the expense line in the expenses tool at the time you submit. Paper receipts can be photographed or scanned and attached as a PDF or image file.

2.3 If a receipt is lost, attach a signed statement explaining the purchase and why the receipt is missing. Accounts payable may ask for more information before approving.

2.4 Do not split an expense into multiple lines to stay under the receipt threshold.

## 3. Submitting and approving

3.1 Expenses are submitted in the expenses tool. Choose the correct cost centre: your department, the research institute for study-related travel, or the health plan for member-facing activity.

3.2 Your manager approves expenses in the tool. Approval is required before Accounts payable will process the claim.

3.3 Submit expenses within 30 days of incurring them. Late submissions may be returned to you with a request to explain the delay.

3.4 Accounts payable reviews submitted claims twice a week. If a claim is incomplete or a receipt is missing, the claim is returned to you through the tool with a note.

## 4. Conference travel

4.1 Conference attendance — including registration, travel, and accommodation — needs approval from your manager before you register. Use the expenses tool to create a conference request; your manager approves it and the request becomes a pre-approved claim.

4.2 If you are presenting at a conference, include the session title and date in the conference request notes.

4.3 Conference travel for research institute staff should be discussed with the Clinical trials office first if it is related to a study.

## 5. Reimbursement

5.1 Approved expenses are paid through the payroll run. Reimbursement appears as a separate line on your payslip, not as part of your salary.

5.2 Payments are made on the next scheduled payroll date after approval. If you are on leave, the payment is made on the following payroll date.

5.3 If payroll has already closed for the cycle when your claim is approved, the reimbursement moves to the following cycle.

## 6. Other

6.1 The expenses tool is the only way to claim reimbursement. Do not email receipts to Accounts payable expecting payment.

6.2 Accounts payable may ask for original receipts for any claim, regardless of amount.

6.3 If you are unsure whether an expense is claimable, ask Accounts payable before you spend the money.
