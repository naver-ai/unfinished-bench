# Kellsbrook handbook

Owner: Human resources. Last edited by Kofi Klein (Benefits Specialist).

This repo holds system-wide policies and how-tos for all staff. If you work in a hospital, clinic, health plan, research, or IT, start here for the cross-cutting stuff; your department repo has the deep dives.

## What's in here

- **about/** — who we are and who does what
- **compliance/** — code of conduct, HIPAA basics
- **it/** — access requests, devices, passwords and sign-in
- **legal/** — signing authority for contracts
- **people/** — onboarding, benefits, PTO, expenses, performance
- **security/** — acceptable use, data classification

## Proposing a change

Open a pull request against the page you want to change and tag the page owner listed at the top of the file. If there's no owner listed, tag Human resources. Small copy fixes don't need a review beyond the owner; anything that changes policy needs a lead from the affected team to sign off in the PR.

## Other repos

- **plan-docs/** — health plan operations: claims, members, providers, utilization management
- **care-docs/** — clinical care: registration, HIM, labs, nursing, patient experience, revenue cycle
- **research-docs/** — research institute: trials, IRB, data, grants, cohort
- **it-docs/** — IT: infrastructure, clinical systems, records platform, security, service desk, on-call

## Old intranet

The old intranet pages were migrated here. If you bookmarked something on the intranet, it now lives in one of the repos above — search for the page title. The intranet itself is read-only until it's decommissioned, so don't update anything there.
