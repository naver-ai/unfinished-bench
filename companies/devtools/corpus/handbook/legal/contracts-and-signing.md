# Who can sign what

**Owner:** Legal  
**Last edited by:** Ines Carvalho (Contracts Manager)

This page covers signing authority for contracts and agreements at Gantline. If you need a signature on something, check the table first, then raise a contract request via the tickets system (type: signing) so Legal can track it. All signed originals land in the Legal drive folder `contracts/` — that is the single source of truth.

## Contract request form

Before any contract goes out for signature, raise a signing request in the tickets system. Include:

- Counterparty legal entity and contact
- Link to the current draft (or the redline if terms deviate from our paper)
- Value and term
- Which signatory applies per the tables above
- Any deadlines — note that Legal needs at least two working days to review non-standard terms

If you are not sure whether terms are standard, ask Legal before sending anything to the counterparty. Do not route around the request form; signatures obtained outside this process are not recorded and will need to be re-run.

## Signed copies

Signed PDFs land in the Legal drive folder `contracts/` with subfolders by year and counterparty. Filing is done by Legal staff; if you receive a signed copy directly, forward it to Legal within one working day. The CRM holds a pointer to the signed copy for customer contracts, but the Legal drive folder remains the authoritative location.

For questions about who can sign something not covered here, ask in #contracts-help or raise a ticket.
