# Benefits in Portugal

## Health insurance

**How do I add my spouse or children to the Gantline health plan?**
Open a ticket in the tickets system with the subject "Health plan family update" and attach the family member's tax identification number and birth certificate (for children). People operations will action it within a few working days. Coverage starts on the first day of the month after we process the request.

**Can I add my parents?**
Yes, if they are tax dependents in Portugal. Same process as above.

**Do I need to do anything when my child is born?**
No — the hospital or the registry office will notify the relevant systems, but please also open a ticket so we can update the plan promptly. Backdated coverage applies from birth.

## Meal allowance card

**When do I get the card?**
On your first working day, we order it through the expenses tool. It usually arrives within a week. If it doesn't, ping #it-help with your ticket number.

**What happens if I lose the card?**
Open a ticket in the expenses tool and select "Meal card – lost or stolen". A replacement is issued and the old one is blocked the same day.

**Can I use it outside Portugal?**
Yes, anywhere that accepts the card network. The daily limit is set by the carrier and resets at midnight.

## Training budget

**What can I use the training budget for?**
Courses, certifications, conference tickets, and books that are relevant to your role. The budget resets each January and does not roll over.

**How do I request reimbursement?**
Submit the receipt through the expenses tool and select "Training". Approval is automatic up to the annual cap; above that, it goes to the Head of People Operations.

## Gym partnership

**How do I get the gym discount?**
We have a partnership with a network of gyms across Lisbon and Porto. Bring your Gantline badge to reception at participating locations and they will register you. The discount applies to monthly and annual memberships.

**Is the partnership available in other cities?**
Not yet. If you are in Portland, the office has a separate wellness benefit — see the Portland office guide in the handbook.

## 13th and 14th month payments

**When are these paid?**
The 13th month is paid with the June payroll and the 14th month with the November payroll. Each is one month of base salary; the payroll page in bizops-docs has the cut-offs.

**Do I get them if I joined mid-year?**
Yes, prorated from your start date. If you leave, the unused portion is settled in your final payroll run.

## Pension top-up

**How does the top-up work?**
Gantline contributes an additional percentage of your base salary into your pension plan on top of the legal minimum. The percentage depends on your tenure with the company.

**Can I change my pension provider?**
Yes — open a ticket with People operations and we will provide the forms. Transfers usually complete within a month.

**Is the top-up applied to bonuses?**
No, only to base salary.
