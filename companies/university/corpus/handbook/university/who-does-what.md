# Who does what

This page gives a quick overview of the directorates, faculties and schools at Aldercombe, what each one looks after, and where they tend to hang out on chat. For individual names, check the staff directory on the intranet.

A few notes that don't fit neatly in the tables:

- The **Student Hub** sits inside Admissions, Enrolment & Student Hub but works closely with Registry and Student Services. It's the first place students go for most enquiries, and it handles a lot of the routine student correspondence.
- **Estates & Facilities** runs the estates helpdesk through the IT service desk portal (category "Estates"). Maintenance requests, cleaning, room bookings and the permit-to-work process all start there.
- **Aldercombe Estates Services Ltd** is our facilities management subsidiary. It's part of the Estates & Facilities directorate and employs many of the people you see around the campuses.
- **Development & Alumni Relations** uses the supporter CRM for donations and alumni contact. Their runbooks are in services-docs.
- **Research & Innovation Services** covers both pre-award (costing, approvals) and post-award (award set-up, financial reporting, closedown). They're a good first port of call for anything research-related.
- **Registry** looks after the student record, enrolment, exams and the exam boards. Their pages live in registry-docs.
- **People & Culture** keeps the handbook, including this page. If you're not sure who does what, ask on #people-and-culture or look up the team on the intranet directory.

For anything that isn't on this page, the IT service desk portal is a good starting point — they'll route you to the right team.
