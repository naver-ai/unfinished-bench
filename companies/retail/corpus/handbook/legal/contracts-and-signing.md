# Who can sign what

Owner: Legal · Last edited by Georgia Gordon (Paralegal)

This page sets out who can sign contracts on behalf of Mossgrove, and what needs to go through the contract request form. If you are unsure whether something needs signing, or who should sign it, ask in #legal or raise a request through the contract request form in tickets.

## Signing authority matrix

| Contract type | Value threshold | Who can sign | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer returns / refunds settlement | Any | Store manager or above | Only for amounts already processed through the POS back office; no new liabilities |
| Supplier purchase orders | Up to £10,000 | Department head or above | Must match an approved PO in the ledger |
| Supplier purchase orders | £10,000–£50,000 | Director or above | |
| Supplier purchase orders | Over £50,000 | CEO or General Counsel | CFO cover for CEO; Legal cover for GC |
| Wholesale credit terms | Standard terms only | Credit Control team | Any deviation requires Legal sign-off |
| Wholesale credit terms | Custom terms | Head of Wholesale + Legal | Both signatures required |
| NDAs (inbound, i.e. they sign ours) | n/a | Any manager | Standard form only, from the contract request form |
| NDAs (outbound, i.e. we sign theirs) | n/a | Legal only | Their form must go to Legal for review first |
| Marketing / brand partnerships | Up to £20,000 | Head of Marketing | |
| Marketing / brand partnerships | Over £20,000 | CEO | |
| Property leases / store licences | Any | CEO + General Counsel | Both signatures required |
| IT / software subscriptions | Up to £15,000 | Head of Platform | Only for services on the approved supplier list |
| IT / software subscriptions | Over £15,000 | CFO | |
| Data sharing agreements | Any | General Counsel | See data-docs/sharing.md |
| Employment contracts / settlement agreements | Any | HR Director + Legal for settlements | Standard offer letters are HR-only |

## NDAs

- Always use the standard NDA from the contract request form unless Legal has agreed otherwise.
- Inbound NDAs (another party signs ours) can be issued by any manager. The signed copy goes to Legal for the records.
- Outbound NDAs (we sign theirs) must go through Legal. Allow extra time — their form may need redlines before we can sign.
- NDAs never need a PO or a value on them.

## Contract request form

The contract request form lives in tickets under the "Contracts" heading. It asks for:

- The other party's full legal name and registered address
- The name of the Mossgrove person who will own the contract
- The value and duration
- The type of contract (pick from the list, or describe it)
- Any redlines you have already discussed with the other side
- The date you need it signed

Legal aims to pick up requests within two working days. If your deadline is tighter than that, say so in the request and message the legal team in chat.

## Supplier terms

- New suppliers need to complete supplier onboarding before we sign anything (see finance-docs/ap/supplier-onboarding.md).
- If a supplier sends their own terms and won't use ours, that is a Legal review — do not sign their terms directly even if the value is under your threshold.
- The standard terms are the ones stored in the contract request form; do not edit them locally and send a modified copy without Legal seeing it first.
- For wholesale customers, the credit terms in finance-docs/wholesale/credit-terms.md are the starting point. Anything outside those terms needs Head of Wholesale and Legal sign-off as shown in the matrix.

## General notes

- Signatures can be electronic via the contract request form. If a paper copy is needed, scan it into the same ticket once signed.
- Delegation is not allowed for the signatories listed above — if the named person is away, the request waits for them or goes up a level, not sideways.
- Contracts with a value over £100,000 or a term over three years always need CEO and General Counsel, regardless of what the matrix says.
- If two parts of Mossgrove disagree on who signs, Legal decides.
