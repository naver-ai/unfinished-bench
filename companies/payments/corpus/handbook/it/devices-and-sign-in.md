# Laptops, phones and sign-in

**Owner:** IT & workplace  
**Last edited by:** Nasser Young

## What kit do I get?

Standard kit is a company-issued laptop ({{LAPTOP_1}} Pro or {{LAPTOP_2}}, depending on your role) and a company phone if your role needs one. Most people in engineering, sales and merchant success get a phone. If you're not sure whether your role qualifies, ask #it-help.

## My laptop is slow / the battery is shot / the screen is cracked. What do I do?

Open a ticket in the tickets system under "IT & workplace" and pick the device category. For a cracked screen or a battery that swells, stop using the device and bring it to the Dublin or Vilnius office IT desk, or the Austin office if you're there. For anything else, we'll try remote fixes first. If we can't fix it, we'll issue a replacement.

## How long does a replacement take?

Usually a few working days. If you're travelling and it's urgent, we can ship one to a Kilbarra office near you. We keep a small stock of each model in Dublin, Vilnius and Austin.

## My laptop was stolen. What do I do?

Tell IT immediately — call the IT desk if it's during working hours, otherwise raise an urgent ticket and page the on-call IT person through the paging tool. We'll revoke the device's certificates and keys, and wipe it if it phones home. You should also report it to local police if you're travelling and get a crime reference number. Your manager needs to know too.

## I lost my phone. Same thing?

Yes, same process. If it's a company phone, we can wipe it remotely. If it's your personal phone and you have the Kilbarra mail app or the back office app on it, you should also change your Kilbarra password from another device and check your MFA methods — more on that below.

## Do I have to use the password manager?

Yes. Every Kilbarra account that has a password uses the password manager. We don't accept "I remember it" as a reason not to. The password manager is also where you store any shared credentials for team accounts. If you find a service that doesn't integrate with it, tell #it-help.

## What about MFA?

MFA is required on everything. Email, the back office, the merchant dashboard, the core ledger, the treasury workstation, the CRM, the analytics warehouse, the chat, the tickets system — if it's a Kilbarra system, it has MFA. Use the authenticator app, not SMS, wherever the system allows it.

## I got an MFA prompt that I didn't ask for. What do I do?

Don't approve it. If you get a push notification or a code you didn't request, that could be someone trying to get into your account. Report it to the security team as described in `handbook/security/reporting-an-incident.md` — even if you think it was a mistake. Change your password and rotate your MFA tokens after you report.

## Can I use my personal phone for work?

For email and chat, yes — that's fine. For the merchant back office, no. The back office has a higher level of data classification and we need the device controls we have on company phones. If you need to check something in the back office while you're away from your desk, use your company laptop.

## I'm leaving Kilbarra. What happens to my devices?

Return them to IT before your last day. If you're remote and can't get to an office, we'll send you a prepaid return label. Your access is revoked on your last working day, and we'll wipe the devices once they're back with us.
