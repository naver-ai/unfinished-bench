# Who can sign what

**Owner:** Legal  
**Last edited by:** Gregory Tillery (General Counsel)

This page covers signing authority for contracts and agreements across Kellsbrook Health. If you are not sure whether you have authority to sign something, or the document does not fit the categories below, route it through Legal before anyone signs.

## Contract request form

All new contracts, renewals, and amendments start with the contract request form in the Legal workspace. The form captures the counterparty, term, value, and the business owner. Legal reviews the request and assigns a reviewer; the reviewer will tell you who signs based on the tables below. Do not circulate a signed copy of anything until Legal confirms the signature path.

## Signing authority by contract type and value

Values are per contract term (not annualized). "Executive team" means any member of the Executive team with responsibility for the relevant line of business.

| Contract type | Up to $50,000 | $50,000–$250,000 | Over $250,000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vendor / IT / facilities | Business owner's VP or Director | Chief Information Officer (IT) or Chief Financial Officer (non-IT) | President and Chief Executive Officer |
| Physician services (employment, call coverage, professional services agreements) | Chief Medical Officer or Chief Nursing Officer (nursing roles only) | Chief Medical Officer | President and Chief Executive Officer |
| Payer agreements (Kellsbrook Health Plan network, employer group, individual, {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}}) | Plan President | Plan President | President and Chief Executive Officer |
| Clinical trial agreements (Kellsbrook Research Institute) | Director, Clinical Trials Office | Chief Research Officer | President and Chief Executive Officer |
| Leases (real estate, equipment) | Facilities Director | Chief Financial Officer | President and Chief Executive Officer |
| Business associate agreements (with vendors) | Privacy Officer | Privacy Officer | Privacy Officer |

Notes:

- Business associate agreements (BAAs) always require the Privacy Officer's signature regardless of value, even when the underlying vendor contract is signed higher up.
- Any contract with a term longer than three years goes to the President and Chief Executive Officer regardless of value.
- Amendments that change the value or term use the same table as the original contract. Amendments that only change dates or contact details can be signed by the original signer's successor in the same role.
- The Chief Research Officer may sign clinical trial agreements up to $250,000 without further review only if the study has a current IRB approval and the budget matches the approved grant or sponsor budget. Anything outside that goes to the President and Chief Executive Officer.

## Where signed contracts are kept

Signed contracts are scanned into the contract archive in the Legal workspace within three working days of signature. The Legal team manages the archive. If you need a copy of a signed contract, ask Legal rather than keeping your own local copies — the archived version is the authoritative one.

## Things Legal will not sign

Legal will not sign any contract that has blanks, handwritten changes, or a counterparty's signature missing. The contract request form must be complete before Legal reviews. If a vendor sends its own paper version of a BAA or contract, route it through Legal — do not sign it even if it looks identical to our standard form.
