# Registration and insurance verification

**Owner:** Patient access & scheduling  
**Last edited by:** Gaurav Wojcik, Scheduling Coordinator

---

## Front desk registration

- [ ] Confirm identity with a government-issued photo ID. Acceptable: driver's license, state ID, passport, military ID.
- [ ] Scan the photo ID into the registration system. If the scanner is down, note the ID type and number in the comment field and flag the record for a scan at the next visit.
- [ ] Scan the insurance card (front and back) into the registration system. For Kellsbrook Health Plan members, the member ID on the card should match what the eligibility check returns.
- [ ] Verify the patient's address, phone number, and emergency contact at every visit. Ask "Is any of this information different since your last visit?" rather than reading everything back.
- [ ] Collect the patient's preferred name and pronouns if they offer them; do not ask unprompted.
- [ ] For minors, record the guarantor and confirm who may receive protected health information about the visit.
- [ ] For self-pay patients, complete the self-pay estimate in the registration system and collect the deposit amount shown. Refer to the financial assistance screening step below before collecting payment.

## Online registration (patient portal and patient app)

- [ ] Portal and app registrations flow into the same registration queue as front desk visits. The queue is monitored by the South Bend and Valparaiso patient access teams during business hours; Cork reviews its own queue on Irish working days.
- [ ] Registration is not considered complete until the patient has uploaded or presented a photo ID and insurance card. If the patient registered online and arrives without their insurance card, complete registration with the ID and mark the insurance card as "pending — patient to provide within 5 working days."
- [ ] If the online registration includes a photo of the insurance card, check that the image is legible and that the member name matches the patient. Unreadable images are rejected and the patient is asked to re-upload or bring the card in person.
- [ ] Online registrations that cannot be matched to an existing patient record are reviewed by Patient Access Representatives (Aiko Silva and Bianca Iyer alternate coverage) before the appointment is confirmed.

## Eligibility checks

- [ ] Run an eligibility check at registration for every visit where the patient has insurance. Use the insurance eligibility screen in the registration system; do not rely on the patient's printed benefits summary.
- [ ] Commercial plans, {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}} (including Kellsbrook Health Plan {{MANAGED_PLAN_PROGRAM}}), and {{PUBLIC_HEALTH_INSURANCE_2}} all have different eligibility response formats — read the response carefully, especially for effective dates and copay/coinsurance amounts.
- [ ] If the eligibility check returns "inactive" or "not found," do not turn the patient away. Register the visit, note the result, and flag the account for the revenue cycle team through the registration system's follow-up queue.
- [ ] For Kellsbrook Health Plan members, verify that the member ID on the card matches the eligibility response. Mismatches are usually due to a plan change or a card that has not been reissued — contact the member services line if the mismatch is not obvious.
- [ ] Coordination of benefits: if the patient has more than one insurance, record the primary payer exactly as the eligibility response lists it. Do not accept the patient's word for which plan is primary.
- [ ] Eligibility checks are not a guarantee of payment. The claim will still be adjudicated by the claims system based on the member's benefits at the time of service.

## Financial assistance screening

- [ ] Screen every self-pay patient for financial assistance before collecting more than the co-pay amount. The screening questions are in the registration system under "Financial assistance."
- [ ] Patients who appear eligible are given the application and a pre-addressed envelope, or are pointed to the patient portal where the application can be submitted electronically.
- [ ] Financial assistance determinations are made by the revenue cycle team, not by patient access. Do not promise a discount or a write-off.
- [ ] If the patient is uninsured and does not qualify for assistance, offer the self-pay estimate and the payment plan options available through the revenue cycle team.

## Duplicate records

- [ ] Before creating a new patient record, search by last name and date of birth, then by last name and first name, then by the insurance member ID. Use the search tool in the registration system; do not skip straight to "new patient."
- [ ] If a possible duplicate is found, open the existing record and confirm the address, phone, or photo ID before deciding. When in doubt, use the existing record and flag it for the HIM office to review.
- [ ] Do not merge records yourself. Merging is done by the HIM office (Georgia Marchetti's team) through the patient index tool. Flag suspected duplicates with the "possible duplicate" flag and the reason.
- [ ] Newborns and babies under 1 year: search by the guarantor's last name and the baby's date of birth. Babies are frequently registered under slightly different spellings of the surname.
- [ ] If a patient says they have never been seen at Kellsbrook but the search returns a close match, register them as a new patient and flag the possible duplicate. It is better to have two records temporarily than to attach a visit to the wrong record.

## Notes on sites

- [ ] Cork medical centre registers patients in its own practice system, not this one. Photo ID is still checked for all adult patients; the Irish {{PUBLIC_HEALTH_SERVICE_2}} card is not a substitute for photo ID but can be recorded as a secondary identifier.
- [ ] Kalamazoo Research Institute trials office does not register research participants through this process. Trial participants are registered by the clinical trials office using the study-specific scheduling workflow.
- [ ] Valparaiso health plan offices do not register patients. Walk-in members needing insurance help are directed to the member services lobby or the member portal.
