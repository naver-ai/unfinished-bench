# Employee benefits

**Owner:** Human resources  
**Last edited by:** Sabine Tanaka

## Medical plans

**I’m new — how do I get enrolled in health coverage?**
You’ll get a benefits packet during onboarding and the HR portal link in your welcome email. Elections are made in the HR system within 30 days of your start date. If you don’t pick anything, you’re enrolled in the standard individual plan automatically.

**Which medical plans are offered?**
All employee medical coverage is through Kellsbrook Health Plan. There are two plan options — the PPO and the HDHP with a health savings account. Both cover the same network of Kellsbrook providers and the broader regional network.

**Do I pay less if I see a Kellsbrook doctor?**
Yes. Copays and coinsurance are lower at Kellsbrook hospitals, clinics and the Cork medical centre. You’ll see the difference at the point of service — the patient app shows you which rate applies before you book.

**My spouse works for a company in Chicago and has coverage there. Do I have to take Kellsbrook coverage?**
No. If you have other group coverage, you can waive the medical plan and get a monthly credit added to your paycheck. You’ll need to provide proof of coverage each year during open enrollment.

## Dental and vision

**Is dental separate?**
Yes, dental is a separate plan through the same carrier we’ve used for years. It covers two cleanings per year, basic and major services at 70% and 50% after the deductible. Orthodontia is covered for dependents up to age 19.

**What about vision?**
Vision coverage includes an annual eye exam and a lens allowance every 12 months. Frames are covered up to the allowance once per year; contact lens fittings are covered in full when you use a network provider.

## 403(b)

**When can I start contributing?**
You’re eligible from your first day. The match kicks in after you’ve completed one year of service. The match is 50% of your contributions up to 6% of pay.

**Can I contribute to both the 403(b) and the HSA?**
Yes. If you’re on the HDHP, you can contribute to the HSA and the 403(b) — they’re separate limits.

## Tuition assistance

**Does tuition assistance cover any degree?**
It covers job-related coursework at an accredited institution. That’s typically a clinical degree, an IT certification, or a business or healthcare administration program. Prior approval from your manager and Human resources is required before the term starts. Reimbursement is 75% of tuition, up to the annual cap, paid after you pass the course with a C or better.

## Employee assistance program

**Is the EAP just for mental health?**
No. The EAP covers counseling, but also legal and financial consultations, child and elder care referrals, and wellness coaching. It’s free for employees and household members, up to six sessions per issue per year. The phone number is on the HR portal — you don’t go through your manager or the timekeeping system for it.

## Cork staff

**I’m based in Cork. Which of the above applies to me?**
Cork staff are employed through the Irish entity and have separate arrangements. Medical coverage through Kellsbrook Health Plan is not available to Cork-based employees. You’ll receive the Irish health insurance allowance and pension arrangements as described in your contract. Dental and vision are not offered as separate benefits in Cork. The EAP and tuition assistance are available to Cork staff — check the HR portal for the Irish EAP contact number.
