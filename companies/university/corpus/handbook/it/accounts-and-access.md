# Getting access to systems

**Owner:** IT Service Desk  
**Last edited by:** Alana Haddad (IT Service Desk Manager)

This page covers how you get an IT account, how to request access to business systems, guest accounts for visitors, and what happens when someone leaves. If you are a manager setting up a new starter, also read *New starters (for managers)* in the staff handbook.

## 1. Your IT account

Your IT account is created automatically when your HR record is completed. You will receive an email to your personal address with a temporary password. On first sign-in you must:

1. Go to [https://password.aldercombe.ac.uk](https://password.aldercombe.ac.uk).
2. Enter the temporary password.
3. Set a new password and register for multi-factor authentication (see *Passwords and sign-in*).

If you do not receive the email within two working days of your start date, contact the IT Service Desk.

## 2. Requesting access to business systems

Most business systems are requested through the IT Service Desk portal. Log in at [https://servicedesk.aldercombe.ac.uk](https://servicedesk.aldercombe.ac.uk) and raise a request under “Access”.

Requests need two approvals:

- Your **line manager** approves that you need the access in your role.
- The **system owner** approves the specific permission level.

| System | System owner |

| VLE | Student Systems |
| Finance system | Financial Accounting & Reporting |
| Research grants system | Research Finance |
| HR and payroll | People & Culture |
| Timetabling system | Registry |
| Room booking system | Estates |
| Estates helpdesk | Estates |
| Lettings system | Residential Lettings |
| Supporter CRM | Development & Alumni Relations |
| Library system | Library |
| Expenses tool | Financial Accounting & Reporting |
| IT service desk portal | IT Service Desk |
| Accommodation booking system | Student Accommodation |
| Student casework system | Student Services |
| Academy school information system | Kestrel Vale Academy |

Approvals are recorded in the portal and normally take up to two working days. If you already have access and need a change (for example, a different role in the finance system), raise the same request type and state what you need changed.

For access to shared drives or chat channels, state the share name or channel in the request. Your line manager’s approval is sufficient; no system owner is needed.

## 3. Guest accounts

Visitors, contractors, and visiting researchers who need access to university systems for more than one day require a guest account.

1. The host raises a guest access request through the portal, naming the visitor and the systems they need.
2. The host’s head of department approves the request.
3. The IT Service Desk creates the account and sends the credentials to the host, who gives them to the visitor.

Guest accounts expire after 90 days. If a visitor needs longer, the host must raise a renewal before the expiry date.

## 4. When someone leaves

When a member of staff leaves, the IT Service Desk runs the offboarding routine once HR confirms the leaving date. This is normally on the final working day.

- The IT account is disabled and the mailbox is redirected to the line manager.
- Access to business systems is removed where the system owner has set up automated deprovisioning. For systems without this, the line manager must raise a request to remove access.
- Shared drives and chat channels are reviewed by the line manager, who decides what to archive or delete.

Line managers should raise any access removal they want done on the leaving date itself at least three working days in advance.

## If it goes wrong

- If your account is locked or you cannot sign in, call the IT Service Desk or raise a “Sign-in problems” ticket through the portal.
- If an access request has been waiting more than five working days, reply to the ticket to chase it. The service desk can see where it is stuck in the approval chain.
- If you believe you should be able to approve a request but it is not appearing in your queue, contact the IT Service Desk — the system owner may not have set your approval role up correctly.
