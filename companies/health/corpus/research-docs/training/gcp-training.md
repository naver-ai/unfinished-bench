# GCP and human subjects training

**Owner:** Research ethics & IRB office  
**Last edited by:** Ingrid Aasen (Ethics Committee Liaison)

**Who needs GCP training?**

Anyone listed on an IRB-approved study as site staff — that includes study coordinators, sub-investigators, and the principal investigator. If your name is on the personnel page of a submission, you need to have completed GCP before the study is activated. Lab staff who only process de-identified samples do not need it; the lab quality coordinator can confirm if you are unsure.

**I am new to the Research Institute. What do I take first?**

The {{RESEARCH_TRAINING_PROVIDER|code}} GCP course for clinical research (the one with the Kellsbrook Research Institute affiliation) and the human subjects protection module. If you are at the Cork office, the same two courses apply — the European trials office uses the same {{RESEARCH_TRAINING_PROVIDER|code}} affiliation, so do not enrol under a different organisation or your certificate will not route to us.

**How often do I renew?**

Every three years from the date you completed the course, not from your hire date. The IRB office sends a reminder to your manager and to the clinical trials office about two weeks before your certificate lapses. If it lapses, you are removed from the personnel page of active studies until the renewal is on file. We do not backdate renewals.

**Where do I put my certificate?**

Upload the PDF to the learning management system under the GCP training tile. The IRB office pulls certificates from there when we review a submission. Do not email certificates to Nico or Dalia — they will ask you to upload it. If you completed the course years ago and the certificate is only in your email, upload it anyway; we mainly need the completion date and the {{RESEARCH_TRAINING_PROVIDER|code}} affiliation.

**I work on the clinical trials side in Cork but I am not on any study staff list yet. Should I take the courses?**

Yes. If your role will involve monitoring visits, handling the EDC, or working with study datasets, take the courses before you are added to a study. It is easier than holding up an activation because we are chasing a certificate. The European trials office keeps a local list of who has completed training; they check it against the IRB file before a site activation.

**Does the medical centre GP staff need GCP?**

Only if they are listed on a study. Routine care at the medical centre does not require it. Zeynep or Clara can tell you whether a particular project is a study or not before you spend the time.

**I took GCP at my previous employer. Does that count?**

If it was {{RESEARCH_TRAINING_PROVIDER|code}} and the course content matches the current version, we can accept a certificate from within the last three years. Send the PDF to the IRB office and we will review it. If it was not {{RESEARCH_TRAINING_PROVIDER|code}}, you will need to take our course.

**Do people outside the Research Institute need this training?**

Yes — if you are at a clinic or hospital and you are named on a study submission, the same rules apply. The IRB office sees quite a few nursing leaders and practice administrators on studies, and they all need the certificate on file before the study moves forward.
