# Working with the outsourced desk

**Owner:** Customer support  
**Last edited by:** Renata Vogel (Support Operations Manager)

## Context

We extended our evening and bank-holiday coverage with an outsourced desk in early spring. They handle first-line queries for two queues in the support tool. This covers what was agreed at the handover meeting, plus the standing notes that have built up since.

## Queues the partner desk covers

- **Evening overflow** – everything that comes in after the UK/Ireland phone lines close and web chat drops to the out-of-hours message. They triage, answer knowledge-base articles, and log anything that needs a callback.
- **Bank-holiday overflow** – the same queues on bank holidays, including the Republic of Ireland holidays when the Dublin office is shut. They do not cover US hours; the Columbus team picks that up as usual.

They only touch the two overflow queues in the support tool. They do not pick up tickets from stores, wholesale, or the POS back office — those stay with the store support rotas.

## Handover notes

- Before the desk goes live each evening, the last UK shift leaves a handover note in the shared channel (`#support-leads`). This includes any open escalations, known issues with the shop or checkout, and anything the desk should watch for.
- The desk posts a summary back into the same channel before they finish. Support leads review it the next working day.
- If something needs immediate attention outside of our core hours, the desk escalates to the on-call person for the platform team via the incident channel. They should not page anyone for non-urgent items — those wait for the next working day.

## Quality reviews

The partner desk's tickets are sampled on a rolling basis. The support quality leads rotate the sampling each month, and the results go into the usual quality tracker. We aim for a ticket to be reviewed within a couple of working days of it being closed.

- Any ticket that gets a low score is flagged back to the desk lead directly, with a note on what to fix.
- Common issues have been: missing customer context in the ticket notes, and not attaching the right article to the reply. These have been fed back already.
- If the same issue shows up three times in a month, it gets raised at the monthly review call with the desk.

## What they cannot access

- No access to the CRM, the analytics warehouse, or any of the internal finance tools. They work entirely from the support tool and the public-facing help centre.
- They cannot issue refunds, adjust orders, or change anything in the POS back office. Any of those requests get logged for the day team.
- They do not have access to the internal chat, other than the `#support-leads` channel they use for handovers. They are not in any other channel.
- They can see the same refunds and returns policy pages that are public, but they are not allowed to make exceptions or promise anything to a customer that is not written down in those policies.

## Loose ends

- The handover template is still a shared doc that gets copied into chat each evening. It was supposed to move into the support tool's saved replies but that never happened.
- A couple of the quality leads from the original pilot have moved on to other teams, so the sampling rota in the tracker still lists one of the old names. It gets updated when someone picks up the rotation.
- The original link to the desk's onboarding pack is in the support team wiki, but it points to an old folder that was archived. The current pack lives under the shared drive, and the wiki page has not been updated.
