# New starters (for managers)

Owner: People & Culture · Last edited by Morag Dunlop

## Before day one

- [ ] Send the new starter's contract and offer letter through the HR and payroll system. The team aims to have this done at least a week before the start date.
- [ ] Request the staff card through the IT service desk portal. Cards are printed on the Hollins Hill campus; for City campus or Kuala Lumpur staff, say which site the card should be sent to. Kestrel Vale Academy cards come through the same route.
- [ ] Check the new starter's IT account request has been submitted. Access follows from the HR and payroll system record, but if the person needs additional equipment or software, put that on the request.
- [ ] Arrange a buddy. The buddy should be someone in the same team or a closely related one, not the line manager. Let the buddy know what they have agreed to — it is a light-touch role, mainly showing the person around and being someone to ask.
- [ ] Book the desk and any equipment through the estates helpdesk if the team does not have a spare setup. Malaysia campus desks go through the campus management office.
- [ ] Tell the new starter where to go on the first day and who to ask for. If they are based at Kestrel Vale Academy, the office team coordinates this.

## First day

- [ ] Meet them at reception (Hollins Hill or City campus). For Kuala Lumpur, the campus management team will bring them up.
- [ ] Make sure they have their staff card, a building map, and their desk. The card opens the buildings they need; if it does not, raise it with the IT service desk the same day.
- [ ] Their IT account should already work. If not, contact the IT service desk — do not share someone else's login, and do not use a shared account.
- [ ] Walk them through the mandatory training modules. These are in the VLE:
  - Information security (all staff)
  - Equality and diversity (all staff)
  - Safeguarding (anyone who works with under-18s — this includes Kestrel Vale Academy staff, anyone in Student Services, and anyone who might have unsupervised contact with children on campus)
- [ ] Point them to the handbook and the who-does-what pages. The comms team has a short tour of the university they can join within their first week.
- [ ] Show them the relevant chat channels for their team and site. The university chat is the main way people keep in touch day to day.

## First month

- [ ] Hold the first probation meeting. This should happen within the first two weeks. The probation period is three months for most roles, six for senior appointments — check the contract.
- [ ] Use the probation meeting to go through the job description, the team's current work, and how the annual review cycle works. This is also the time to agree any training needs.
- [ ] Make sure they have done all three mandatory modules. The HR and payroll system shows completion; if something is missing, agree a date for it to be done.
- [ ] Book a second probation meeting before the end of the first month. The purpose is to catch any problems early, not to make a formal assessment — that comes later in the probation period.
- [ ] Introduce them to the people they will work with, not just their own team. For example, someone in a faculty office should meet the registry team they will deal with; someone in Development should meet the finance team.
- [ ] If they are based at Kestrel Vale Academy, make sure they know the school's routines — the office team has a short briefing they run for new starters.
- [ ] Check the buddy has been in touch. If not, nudge them.

## Notes

- People & Culture sends a reminder about the staff card and IT account two working days before the start date. If you have not heard anything, chase through the IT service desk portal.
- Probation meetings do not need to be long. Fifteen minutes is fine. The important thing is that they happen.
- The training modules take about an hour each. They can be done in chunks.
- If the new starter is joining from another UK university, their vetting and right-to-work checks may already be held by us — People & Culture will confirm. If not, the new starter should bring their documents on the first day.
