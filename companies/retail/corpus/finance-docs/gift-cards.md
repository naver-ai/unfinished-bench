# Gift cards

**Owner:** Finance operations  
**Last edited by:** Anders Fonseca (Finance operations)

## Gift cards

**Who issues gift cards and where do they come from?**

Gift cards are issued through the POS back office in stores and through the online shop. The plastic cards come from our supplier via the Wakefield DC — stores order them through the usual stock process. The e-gift card option is generated by the online shop and emailed by mailer.

**When does the money hit our bank account?**

For store sales, the gift card value is captured at the till and included in the store banking process — see `finance-docs/cash/store-banking.md`. For online sales, the payment is processed through our normal checkout flow and lands in the same settlement batch as any other online order.

**How long are gift cards valid for?**

Gift cards are valid for 24 months from the date of purchase. The expiry date is printed on the back of the card for store-issued cards and shown in the confirmation email for e-gift cards. The POS back office and the online shop both enforce this at redemption.

**What happens when a gift card expires?**

The remaining balance is written off at the end of the month in which the card expires. For accounting purposes this is treated as breakage — we recognise the unredeemed balance as revenue at that point. We track issued vs redeemed values in the ledger under the gift card liability account. If you need a breakdown of the breakage entries, the month-end close pack in `finance-docs/month-end-close.md` has the summary.

**Can a customer check their balance?**

Yes — balance checks are available in the online shop under "Gift cards", and store staff can check a balance from the POS back office. For phone enquiries, customer support uses the same POS back office lookup. There is no separate balance-check API yet; it is on the payments platform team's roadmap.

**A customer wants a refund for an item paid for with a gift card. Do we refund to the same gift card?**

If the original purchase was made with a gift card, the refund goes back to a gift card. We issue a new gift card for the refund amount rather than re-loading the original card, unless the original card is still in date and the customer has it with them — in that case the POS back office can load the balance back onto it. This applies in stores and for online returns processed through the returns flow in `support-docs/returns-and-exchanges.md`.

**What about a refund where part was paid by gift card and part by debit/credit card?**

The refund is split proportionally to the original payment split. The gift card portion goes back to a gift card and the card portion goes back to the original payment method. If the original card payment was made more than 6 months ago and the payment provider will no longer accept the refund, the card portion is also issued as a gift card — the customer is told this at the point of return.

**Can a gift card be used in the wholesale arm or on the US site?**

Gift cards purchased on mossgroveoutdoor.co.uk (UK/IE) can be redeemed in UK and Ireland stores and on the UK/IE site. They cannot be used on the US site or in the Columbus operation. US gift cards are a separate product and are only redeemable on the US site. Wholesale accounts cannot pay invoices with gift cards.

**How do we handle a lost or stolen gift card?**

We do not replace lost or stolen gift cards. The card is treated like cash — the balance is gone once it is redeemed. The only exception is when the card was purchased online and the customer can show the unused e-gift card code from the original email; in that case we can transfer the remaining balance to a new e-gift card. Store staff should direct customers to customer support for this.

**Is there a limit on gift card purchases?**

Not for customers buying them. For store cash transactions, the normal cash handling limits in `finance-docs/cash/store-banking.md` apply. Wholesale customers cannot buy gift cards on account — it has to be paid at the point of purchase.

**Do gift cards count as revenue when sold?**

No. The sale creates a liability on the balance sheet. Revenue is recognised when the card is redeemed, or at expiry as breakage. There's a note on this in `finance-docs/tax/vat-notes.md` — VAT is due at the point of sale for the card purchase itself, not at redemption.

**Where do gift card queries go in Finance operations?**

Balance and redemption queries from stores go to the finance ops inbox. Anything about the liability or breakage numbers goes to the Finance operations team. Chargebacks on gift card purchases are handled under the normal chargeback process in `finance-docs/chargebacks.md` — note that the card itself is still valid even if the original purchase is charged back.
