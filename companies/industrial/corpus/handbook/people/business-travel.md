# Business travel

*Needs updating after the move — ping #hr-ops if something here looks wrong.*

**Owner:** People & HR · **Last edited by:** Claudia Rehm

This policy covers booking travel through the travel desk, rail travel within Germany, travel to our Telford and Greenville sites, flight class rules by trip length, hotel caps by city, visas for customer visits abroad, travel insurance, and daily allowances by country. It applies to all employees travelling on company business.

1. **Booking through the travel desk**
   1. All business travel must be booked through the travel desk using the intranet booking form. The travel desk confirms bookings by email within one working day.
   2. The travel desk operates during Reutlingen office hours. Urgent same-day bookings should be raised via chat in #hr-ops so the team can pick them up.
   3. If you book directly with a carrier or hotel because the travel desk is unavailable, you must log the booking in the expenses app within three working days and attach the confirmation.

2. **Rail travel within Germany**
   1. Rail is the default for domestic journeys between Reutlingen and any German city. First class is permitted for journeys over four hours each way.
   2. For shorter journeys, standard class is the default. Upgrades are at the traveller's discretion if paying the difference personally.
   3. The {{RAILCARD}} Business is held by frequent travellers in the firm — check with #finance-ops before ordering a new one, as the card stock is managed centrally.

3. **Travel to Telford and Greenville**
   1. For Telford, fly into Birmingham or Manchester and take the train or a hire car from there. The drives plant is a short taxi ride from Telford Central.
   2. For Greenville, fly into GSP (Greenville-Spartanburg) where possible. If connecting through Atlanta or Charlotte, allow at least three hours between flights.
   3. Rail within the UK between London and Telford is acceptable for trips originating in the London area; book via the travel desk so they can apply the corporate discount.

4. **Flight class by trip length**
   1. Economy class for flights under four hours.
   2. Premium economy for flights of four hours or more but less than eight hours.
   3. Business class for flights of eight hours or more.
   4. The travel desk books upgrades only with the head of department's go-ahead.

5. **Hotel caps by city**
   1. The following nightly caps apply (room only, excluding breakfast where breakfast is charged separately):
      - Reutlingen: €130
      - Telford: £110
      - Greenville: $150
      - Munich, Frankfurt, Berlin: €160
      - London: £180
      - New York, San Francisco: $280
   2. If the cap is exceeded because no room is available within it, the travel desk will flag this and seek approval from the traveller's manager before booking.
   3. Where breakfast is charged separately, it comes out of the daily allowance in clause 8.

6. **Visas for customer visits abroad**
   1. Employees travelling to countries requiring a visa for German, UK or US passport holders must apply through the travel desk at least four working weeks before departure.
   2. The travel desk will coordinate with the local HR Business Partner for any letters of introduction needed.
   3. Visa fees are paid by the company and are coded to the cost centre of the travelling employee's department.

7. **Travel insurance**
   1. All employees are covered by the corporate travel insurance policy for business trips, including medical cover, trip cancellation and baggage loss.
   2. The policy number is held by the travel desk and is shown on every booking confirmation.
   3. Personal travel added to the start or end of a business trip is covered for the business portion only. The personal portion must be insured separately by the employee.

8. **Daily allowances by country**
   1. Daily allowances cover meals and incidental expenses and are paid per the rates in the expenses app when the trip is logged.
   2. Current rates (per full day):
      - Germany: €49
      - UK: £55
      - USA: $75
      - Other EU member states: €55
      - Switzerland: CHF 80
      - Asia (all countries): €60
   3. Half-day rates apply for departure or return days as configured in the expenses app. The system calculates this automatically from the travel desk booking times.
   4. If a meal is provided by the customer or is included in the hotel rate, the allowance for that meal is reduced per the rates in the expenses app.
