# Talking to press and posting on social

**Owner:** Communications  
**Last edited by:** Magnus Gupta

## What happens if a journalist or analyst contacts me?

Send their email or call notes to comms@gantline.dev as soon as you can, and don't reply to them yourself. If they've asked you something directly, let them know the Communications team will be in touch — that's all. If they're on a deadline, say so in your message to us so we can prioritise.

## Can I talk about my work at conferences or meetups?

Only if you've cleared it with Communications first. That includes panel discussions, podcast appearances, and community meetups where you're representing Gantline. If you're attending as yourself and not speaking about work, you're fine. Jin in Developer Relations can help you figure out which is which.

## Can I post about Gantline on my personal social accounts?

Yes, and we'd love you to share things you're proud of. Please don't post about unreleased features, customer contracts, internal metrics, or anything marked confidential. When in doubt, ask Communications or check the data classification guide in the handbook. If you mention where you work in your bio, that's fine — just don't imply you speak for the company.

## A reporter follows me or DMs me on social — what do I do?

You don't need to block them, but you also don't need to answer. If they're asking about Gantline, point them to press@gantline.dev and let us know. If they're asking about your personal projects, that's your call.

## I saw something inaccurate in an article about Gantline. Should I correct it?

Leave that to Communications — we have relationships with the outlets and know how to handle corrections. If you have context that would help us respond accurately, send it to comms@gantline.dev with the article link.

## What about internal channels?

Chat and our internal docs are internal — don't screenshot them or share them outside the company. This includes posting about internal discussions in private communities or group chats with people outside Gantline.

## Who do I contact if I'm not sure?

Post in #general and tag Communications, or email comms@gantline.dev. For anything time-sensitive, ping Magnus or Ines directly in chat.
