# Leave and absence

**Owner:** People operations  
**Last edited by:** Solveig Arnesen, Head of People Operations

## Purpose

This document sets out how leave and absence work at Gantline across both our Lisbon and Portland offices. It covers holiday allowance, public holidays, booking leave, sickness reporting, and parental and carers leave. Where rules differ between Gantline Software, Lda. (the Portuguese entity) and Gantline Inc. (the US entity), those differences are called out explicitly.

## Scope

Applies to all employees of Gantline Software, Lda. and Gantline Inc. Contractors and temporary staff should refer to their engagement agreement; the HR system will only accept leave requests from employees on the permanent payroll.

## 1. Holiday allowance

1.1 Employees employed by Gantline Software, Lda. (all Lisbon-based staff) receive 22 working days of paid holiday per leave year. The leave year runs from the first working day of January to the last working day of December.

1.2 Employees employed by Gantline Inc. (all Portland-based staff) receive 20 working days of paid holiday per calendar year, plus the office closure days described in section 3.2.

1.3 Holiday allowance accrues proportionally for part-time employees and for anyone joining or leaving partway through the leave year. The HR system calculates accrued entitlement automatically on the first working day of each month.

1.4 Unused holiday may be carried over into the next leave year up to a maximum of five working days. Any carryover must be requested in the HR system before the last working day of the leave year and approved by the employee's manager. Carryover beyond five working days is not permitted.

1.5 Employees may take holiday before it has accrued, up to a maximum of five working days in advance, subject to manager approval. If the employee leaves Gantline before the advanced days have accrued, the overpayment is deducted from their final salary.

## 2. Booking leave

2.1 All holiday must be booked in the HR system before the leave is taken. Requests should be submitted at least two working days in advance for single days, and at least five working days in advance for any period of two or more consecutive days.

2.2 Managers approve or reject leave requests in the HR system. Where two or more people from the same team request overlapping leave, the manager should balance team coverage. There is no seniority rule; the first request in the HR system wins only if the manager does not intervene.

2.3 Employees working across the Lisbon and Portland offices should be mindful of the other office's public holidays when planning leave that spans both sites. The shared team calendar shows each office's holidays; when in doubt, check with your manager before booking.

## 3. Public holidays

3.1 Lisbon office: employees observe the Portuguese national public holidays. The full list is published in the HR system at the start of each leave year. When a public holiday falls outside the working week, no replacement day off is given; the allowance in 1.1 already accounts for this.

3.2 Portland office: employees observe US federal holidays plus the state of Oregon holidays. In addition, the Portland office closes for the period between the last working day before the US Christmas holiday and the first working day after the US New Year holiday. These closure days do not count against the holiday allowance in 1.2. The exact closure dates are announced in the HR system and on #general each quarter.

3.3 If a public holiday falls while an employee is on holiday, the day is not deducted from their allowance. The HR system handles this automatically for employees whose work location is set to the office where the holiday applies.

3.4 Employees who work from the other office temporarily (for example, a Portland employee spending a month in Lisbon) should follow the holiday calendar of their home office, not the office they are visiting.

## 4. Sickness reporting

4.1 Employees who are unwell and unable to work must report their absence in the HR system before the start of their working day, or as soon as reasonably possible after that. They should also send a message to their manager and to their team's chat channel so that work can be covered.

4.2 Self-certified sickness: for absences of up to three consecutive working days, no medical certificate is required. Employees self-certify in the HR system when they report the absence.

4.3 Certified sickness: for absences of four or more consecutive working days, a medical certificate is required. Lisbon employees must submit the Portuguese medical certificate (declaração de incapacidade para o trabalho) through the HR system. Portland employees must submit a doctor's note within five working days of returning to work.

4.4 Sick pay: Lisbon employees receive full pay for the first 30 days of sickness in any rolling 12-month period, after which Portuguese social security provisions apply. Portland employees receive full pay for up to 12 weeks of sickness in any rolling 12-month period, after which state and federal provisions apply. The HR system tracks the rolling periods automatically.

4.5 Employees returning from a period of certified sickness of more than two weeks should arrange a check-in with their manager and, if they wish, with a People Partner. This is to agree any adjustments to their return, not to question the absence.

## 5. Parental leave

5.1 Gantline Software, Lda. employees are entitled to parental leave in line with Portuguese law. The HR system contains the current statutory durations and pay rates. Employees should notify their manager and the People operations team at least 30 days before the expected start date, or as soon as practicable for early or unexpected arrivals.

5.2 Gantline Inc. employees are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the birth, adoption, or fostering placement of a child. This applies to all parents regardless of gender or how the child joins the family. The leave must be taken within the first 12 months of the child's arrival and can be taken in one continuous block or split into up to two blocks, subject to manager approval.

5.3 Employees on parental leave keep their holiday allowance and continue to accrue it for the duration of the leave. For Gantline Software, Lda. employees, the statutory accrual rules apply. For Gantline Inc. employees, the full allowance accrues as normal.

5.4 Anyone returning from parental leave of more than two months should arrange a return-to-work conversation with their manager and a People Partner at least two weeks before their return date. The purpose is to agree a phased return if needed and to confirm any changes to team structure or responsibilities that happened while they were away.

## 6. Carers leave

6.1 Employees who need to take time off to care for a dependent (a child, partner, parent, or other relative who relies on them for care) may take up to five working days of paid carers leave per leave year. This is separate from holiday allowance and from any emergency leave described in 6.3.

6.2 Carers leave should be requested in the HR system using the dedicated request type. If the need is not foreseeable, employees should report it as soon as they can and provide details to their manager. No medical evidence is required for individual days, but a manager may ask for a brief explanation to confirm the request qualifies.

6.3 Emergency leave for urgent family or household matters (for example, a burst pipe or a child falling ill at school) is not deducted from holiday or carers leave. Employees should report the absence in the HR system and tell their manager. Up to two days of emergency leave per incident, and up to five days per leave year, are paid.

6.4 Where an employee needs longer than the carers leave allowance to care for a dependent, they should speak to their manager and a People Partner. Options may include unpaid leave, flexible working, or using holiday allowance. Each case is handled individually; there is no automatic entitlement beyond what is set out here.

## 7. Differences between Gantline Software and Gantline Inc.

7.1 The main differences between the two entities are the holiday allowance (section 1), the public holiday calendars and the Portland office closure (section 3), and the parental leave provisions (section 5). Sickness reporting and carers leave operate on the same principles in both entities, with the statutory variations noted in sections 4 and 6.

7.2 Employees moving between entities (for example, a transfer from Lisbon to Portland) should confirm their leave balances with People operations before the move. The HR system converts accrued but unused holiday at the point of transfer; the conversion rate is the ratio between the two allowances.

7.3 Where this document refers to "the HR system", it means the same system for both entities. Leave requests, sickness reports, and carers leave are all handled there, regardless of the employing entity.

## 8. Other absence

8.1 Unpaid leave is available at the manager's discretion and with People operations approval. Requests should be made in the HR system at least ten working days in advance, and the total unpaid leave in any leave year may not exceed 30 working days without Chief People Officer approval.

8.2 Jury duty, civic duty, and bereavement leave are handled separately from this policy. Employees should contact People operations for the current provisions.

8.3 Any absence not reported in the HR system is treated as unauthorised. Persistent unauthorised absence is addressed through the performance and conduct process, which is managed by People operations in line with local law.
