# Leave and absence

**Owner:** People team  
**Last edited by:** Elias Mishra (People Operations Coordinator)  
**Applies to:** All Kilbarra employees in Ireland, Lithuania and the US

## Purpose

This document sets out how annual leave, public holidays, sickness absence, parental leave and carer's leave work at Kilbarra. It covers the statutory minimums and the company's enhancements, and where the three countries differ. If a policy clause conflicts with local law, local law wins — ask People Operations if you are unsure.

## Scope

All employees of Kilbarra Payments DAC and its Irish, Lithuanian and US offices. Contractors and consultants should follow the terms of their engagement, not this document.

## 1. Annual leave entitlement

1.1 Annual leave is calculated on the basis of a full-time working year. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.

1.2 Ireland: the statutory entitlement is 20 working days. Kilbarra adds 5 company days, giving a total of 25 working days per leave year. The leave year runs January to December.

1.3 Lithuania: the statutory entitlement is 20 working days. Kilbarra adds 5 company days, giving a total of 25 working days per calendar year. Employees with more than 10 years of continuous service at Kilbarra receive 1 additional day; the extra day applies from the first day of the month after the anniversary.

1.4 US: there is no federal statutory annual leave requirement. Kilbarra grants 20 working days per calendar year for full-time employees, increasing to 25 after 5 years of continuous service. The increase takes effect on the first day of the month after the anniversary.

1.5 Entitlement is capped at 1.5 times the annual allowance. There is no separate "sick leave bank" — sickness absence is covered separately in section 4.

## 2. Public holidays

2.1 Ireland: the 10 public holidays are observed on the dates set out in Irish law. Where a public holiday falls on a non-working day, the next working day is taken instead.

2.2 Lithuania: the 13 public holidays are observed on the dates set out in Lithuanian law. Where a public holiday falls on a non-working day, the next working day is taken instead.

2.3 US: the company observes the 11 federal holidays. The Austin office follows the federal calendar; when a holiday falls on a non-working day, the preceding working day is observed, and when it falls on a non-working day at the end of the week, the following working day is observed.

2.4 Employees working from a different country than their home office on a public holiday should follow the calendar of their home office, not the country they happen to be in. If both calendars have a holiday on the same day, only one day is taken.

2.5 Public holidays do not come out of the annual leave allowance. Part-time employees who would not normally work on a public holiday are not given a substitute day.

## 3. Booking annual leave

3.1 All annual leave is booked in the HR system. Requests are approved by the employee's manager. There is no automatic approval — the manager should consider business coverage, particularly during the financial close windows and the mid-year and end-of-year release trains.

3.2 The HR system shows the remaining balance for the current year and the projected balance for the next year. It is the employee's responsibility to track their own usage; managers are expected to raise it when someone is at risk of losing leave at the end of the year.

3.3 Employees should give as much notice as possible. For a single day, 5 working days' notice is the norm. For a week or more, at least 15 working days' notice is expected. The manager may approve shorter notice where the team can cover.

3.4 Unused leave carries over as follows:
- Ireland: up to 5 days may be carried into the next leave year, to be taken by the end of the first quarter of the new leave year.
- Lithuania: up to 5 days may be carried, to be taken by the end of the first quarter of the new year.
- US: no carry-over. Leave not taken by the end of the year is forfeited.

3.5 The HR system will flag any booking that would take the employee over their allowance or past the carry-over deadline. The People Operations team is copied on those flags and will follow up with the employee and manager.

## 4. Sickness absence

4.1 If you are too unwell to work, notify your manager before the start of your working day, and record the absence in the HR system on the same day. If you cannot access the HR system, your manager can record it on your behalf.

4.2 Ireland: the company pays full salary for the first 13 weeks of sickness absence in any 12-month period. After that, statutory illness benefit applies where the employee qualifies. A medical certificate is required from the first day of absence for any period longer than 3 working days.

4.3 Lithuania: the company pays full salary for the first 5 working days of sickness absence. From the 6th day, the state sickness benefit applies, and Kilbarra tops it up to 80% of base salary for up to 60 calendar days in any 12-month period. A medical certificate is required from the first day.

4.4 US: the company provides 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Unused sick days do not carry over. For absences longer than 3 working days, a return-to-work note from a healthcare provider is required. Colorado and New York City-specific requirements are handled by People Operations on a case-by-case basis.

4.5 If an employee is absent for more than 5 consecutive working days, the manager should contact the People team. People Operations will reach out to the employee to confirm support and, where relevant, discuss a phased return or reasonable adjustments.

## 5. Parental leave

5.1 Ireland: employees are entitled to maternity leave of 26 weeks, with an option to take an additional 16 weeks of unpaid maternity leave. Paternity leave is 2 weeks. Parental leave of up to 26 weeks per child is available for children under 12, unpaid. Parents' leave of 7 weeks is paid at the statutory rate and topped up by Kilbarra to full base salary.

5.2 Lithuania: maternity leave is 18 weeks, paid at 100% of the employee's average salary, with the state reimbursing the company. Paternity leave is 30 calendar days, paid at 100%. Childcare leave is available until the child turns 3, with the state providing a benefit; Kilbarra tops this up to full base salary for the first 6 months.

5.3 US: the company provides 12 weeks of paid parental leave for the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child, available to all parents regardless of gender. The leave must be taken within the first 12 months of the child's arrival. Short-term disability for birth parents is handled separately by the insurer.

5.4 Employees should notify their manager and People Operations at least 30 days before the intended start of parental leave, or as soon as practicable where the timing is not known in advance. The HR system has a specific parental leave request type.

## 6. Carer's leave

6.1 Ireland: employees are entitled to up to 5 working days of unpaid carer's leave per year to provide urgent care for a dependent. The company allows these days to be taken as paid leave, counting against the annual leave allowance if the employee prefers.

6.2 Lithuania: employees are entitled to up to 5 working days per year of paid carer's leave for a dependent. The company matches the statutory entitlement and does not deduct from annual leave.

6.3 US: there is no separate statutory carer's leave. Employees may use sick days for carer responsibilities, and the Family and Medical Leave Act provisions apply for longer-term care situations. Employees should speak to People Operations for guidance on unpaid leave options.

## 7. Differences between countries

7.1 The main practical differences are:
- Annual leave totals: 25 days in Ireland and Lithuania; 20 days in the US, rising to 25 after 5 years.
- Public holiday counts: 10 in Ireland, 13 in Lithuania, 11 in the US.
- Sick pay: full salary for 13 weeks in Ireland, 5 days in Lithuania, 10 days in the US.
- Parental leave: the statutory structures differ significantly; the company enhancements bring the paid element closer together but the mechanics of state benefits and top-ups vary.
- Carer's leave: paid in Lithuania, optionally paid in Ireland, no separate entitlement in the US.

7.2 Where an employee transfers between offices, the leave balance is converted on a pro-rata basis at the point of transfer. People Operations makes the conversion and confirms the new balance in writing.

7.3 Employees on secondment keep the entitlements of their home office unless the secondment agreement says otherwise.

## 8. General

8.1 The HR system is the source of truth for leave balances and absence records. If there is a discrepancy between this document and the HR system, raise it with People Operations.

8.2 Managers are responsible for ensuring their team's leave plans are visible in the HR system and that coverage is maintained for operational roles, including on-call rotation and the monthly reconciliation close.

8.3 People Operations reviews leave and absence data quarterly for patterns that may indicate wellbeing concerns or workload issues, and will reach out to managers where appropriate.

8.4 Questions about this policy should go to the People team in the first instance via #it-help or the People Operations inbox.
