# Business travel

Needs a tidy-up after the restructure — ping #people-and-culture if something here looks wrong.

## Booking

- All travel booked through the travel management company (TMC) portal. Contact #finance for the account code if you don't have it.
- Rail is the default for domestic trips. Flights only where the journey is over 400 miles or there's a same-day return requirement that rail can't meet.
- {{RAIL_OPERATOR}} for international rail where practical. Fly from Bristol or Exeter unless the connection doesn't work — then London airports are fine.
- Advance purchase fares wherever possible. First class is not permitted except on sleeper services.

## Insurance

- University travel insurance covers all staff on university business. No need to arrange personal cover.
- The policy is annual and auto-renewed. If you're travelling outside the UK for more than 30 days, email #finance to confirm the extension is on your record.

## Overseas travel risk assessments

- Required for any destination on the Foreign Office's advisory list, and for all travel to Kuala Lumpur.
- Submit via the HR and payroll system (the travel assessment form is under "Me" → "Travel"). Allow at least five working days before departure.
- The People & Culture team reviews and confirms. If the destination is high-risk, the Director of People & Culture signs off.

## Kuala Lumpur campus

- Flights booked through the TMC — no self-booking for KL trips.
- Accommodation at the KL campus guest flats is arranged by the KL operations team, not through the TMC. Email the KL campus office directly.
- Per diem rates for Malaysia are in the expenses tool. Meals are paid as a flat rate, no receipts needed.
- Visa letters for Malaysia issued by Legal Services & Governance — request at least two weeks ahead.

## Other

- Personal car use for business travel is claimable at the standard rate via expenses.
- Taxis only for journeys under 10 miles or after public transport stops running. Receipts required.
