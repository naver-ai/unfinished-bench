# Personal details and module changes

**Owner:** Student Records & Data  
**Last edited by:** Joel Amari (Senior Student Records Officer)

This covers changes to a student's name, contact details, personal tutor and optional modules on the student record. It is aimed at Student Hub staff and School Administrators who handle these at the desk, and at Student Records colleagues who process what comes through the queue.

## What the Student Hub can change at the desk

1. Open the student's record in the student record system.
2. Use the **Personal Details** screen for:
   - Preferred first name (not legal name — see below)
   - Phone number
   - Emergency contact details
   - Address (term-time and permanent)
   - Personal email address (the one we hold alongside the university address)
3. Save and confirm the change on screen. No further approval is needed for these.
4. For a change of personal tutor, use the **Tutor Assignment** screen and select the new tutor from the list for that School. If the tutor does not appear, contact the School Administrator rather than typing a name in manually.

## What goes to Student Records

Send a ticket to Student Records (via the IT service desk portal, category **Student Records – Amendment**) for:

- Legal name change (see evidence below)
- Change of date of birth
- Change of nationality or fee status
- Any correction to a name that appears on an official transcript or certificate
- Module registration changes after the module addition deadline for the teaching block

Include the student's ID, the old value and the new value, and why the change is needed. If the change comes from a School (for example a timetable clash that means a module swap), the School Administrator should note that on the ticket.

## Legal name change

A legal name change means a change of the name on the student's birth certificate, passport or other official identity document. We do not accept a change to the legal name on the strength of a preferred name request alone.

1. Ask the student to bring the original document (passport, birth certificate, deed poll, or court order if applicable) to the Student Hub.
2. The Hub staff photocopy the document and note "original seen" with their initials and the date on the copy.
3. Attach the copy to the ticket to Student Records.
4. Student Records updates the legal name fields and re-issues the student ID card if needed. The card is printed at the Hollins Hill campus and can be collected from the Hub after two working days.

If the student cannot bring the original in person, they can send a certified copy from a solicitor, notary or the university's own Student Services team. We do not accept scans of the original sent by email.

## Optional module changes

For optional module changes within the published change window:

1. The School Administrator checks the module is open in the timetabling system and there is space.
2. The change is made on the student record using the **Module Registration** screen.
3. If the change is after the deadline, the School must approve it first. The School Administrator sends the approval to Student Records, and Student Records makes the change. Schools cannot make post-deadline changes directly.

## If it goes wrong

- If a change does not save, check the student is not in a "locked" state (for example, a pending exam board or a hold from Student Services). If the record is locked, the screen will show a message — send a ticket to Student Records with the wording of the message.
- If the student's name appears differently in different places (e.g. the VLE shows one version and the student record another), do not try to fix the VLE directly. Report it on the ticket and Student Records will check the feed.
- If a module change causes a timetable clash, the timetabling system will flag it. Do not override the flag — contact the timetabling team through the usual channel.
