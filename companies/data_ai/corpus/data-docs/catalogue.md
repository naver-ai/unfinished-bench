# Data catalogue (short)

Owner: Data governance and open data · Last edited by Isla Penrose

This catalogue lists the main datasets we hold, who is responsible for them, how they are classified, and how often they refresh. It is meant as a quick reference — for access requests follow `data-docs/governance/data-access.md`, for open data releases see `data-docs/governance/open-data.md`.

## Core datasets

| Dataset | Owner | Classification | Refresh |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imagery archive (scenes and COGs) | Geo operations (Joost Hendriks' team) | Internal | Continuous; new scenes within hours of downlink |
| AOI orders | Geo operations / Imagery acquisition | Confidential | On order; status updates via the tasking portal |
| Sensor readings | Geo operations / Sensor network | Internal | Streaming; readings every 15 minutes from river and rain gauges, hourly from soil probes |
| Flood extents | Applied ML | Internal | Per event; updated while an event is active |
| Crop indices | Applied ML | Internal | Weekly during growing season |
| Customer usage events | Data (Sofia Rossi's team) | Confidential | Daily batch into the warehouse |
| Billing tables | Data / Finance | Confidential | Daily; reconciled at period close |
| Sensor site registry | Geo operations / Sensor network | Internal | When sites are commissioned, moved or closed |

## Imagery archive

The imagery catalogue holds both raw scenes and processed COGs. Raw scenes are the master copy; COGs are derived and stored in the object store with a STAC catalogue entry. Retention follows the imagery ingest pipeline (`data-docs/pipelines/imagery-ingest.md`). Refresh is driven by satellite passes and tasking orders, so it is continuous in practice but not on a fixed schedule.

## Sensor readings

Readings from our ground sensor network arrive through the field app and the network gateway. The raw stream is kept in the warehouse as parquet tables, with a rolling window of high-resolution data and aggregated history beyond that. See `data-docs/sensors/sensor-network.md` for the table layout and retention details.

## Derived products

Flood extents and crop indices are produced by the applied ML teams from imagery and sensor inputs. They are published to the analytics API and to customer dashboards. Refresh cadence depends on the product: flood extents are event-driven, crop indices follow the weekly pipeline.

## Usage and billing

Customer usage events are generated by the analytics API and the tasking portal. They land in the warehouse in the `usage` schema and feed the billing tables. The billing tables themselves are owned jointly by Data and Finance; changes to their structure go through the normal warehouse change process (`data-docs/warehouse/dbt-conventions.md`).

## Notes

- Classification levels follow `handbook/security/data-classification.md`. Most operational data is Internal; anything that identifies a customer or their sites is Confidential.
- For questions about a dataset's contents, contact the owning team directly. For access requests, use the process in `data-docs/governance/data-access.md`.
- The data catalogue in the BI tool mirrors this document but may lag by a day or two; this file is the source of truth for ownership.
