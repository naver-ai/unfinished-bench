# Laptops, phones and printing

**Owner:** Service desk  
**Last edited by:** Bruno Bakker

**What kit do I get?**

Standard issue is a Kessendorf 15" laptop with dock and a company phone. Engineering, firmware and test-lab staff get a 17" model with more RAM; shop-floor team leads get a rugged tablet instead of a laptop unless their role needs both. Sales and field service get the 15" plus a spare battery and a mobile hotspot. If you think your role needs something different, raise it with the Service desk and we'll look at it with your manager.

**My laptop is slow / broken / won't start. What do I do?**

Open a ticket via the service desk portal. If it's hardware, we'll lend you a pool unit while yours is repaired. If it's still under the manufacturer's warranty, we swap it. If it's older, we repair unless the repair cost is more than half a replacement — then we replace and you send the old one back through the internal post.

**I lost my phone / it was stolen. What now?**

Tell the Service desk right away. We block the SIM and, if you have the device tracking app enabled, we can try to locate it. You'll get a replacement from the pool. Lost laptops: report it the same way. The desk will check whether the disk was encrypted and, if not, walk you through what that means for any files on it — most of your work should be in the ERP or the MES anyway, so local files are usually just drafts.

**Can I use my own laptop or phone for work?**

For email and calendar, yes — see the acceptable-use page in the handbook for what's allowed. You won't get full access to the ERP, PLM or MES from a personal device. If you need those, use the remote desktop option. Personal phones can be set up for company email and chat; the Service desk does the setup, and we'll remove the profile when you leave.

**How does printing work?**

Follow-me printing: you send a job to the printer, then tap your badge at any of the multifunction devices to release it. Jobs sit on the print server for four hours, then drop off. Colour printing is available but the lab and engineering drawings go to the plotters in Reutlingen and Telford — talk to the test lab if you need a large-format output. The Greenville site has one plotter in the plant office.

**The shop-floor tablets keep dying on us.**

We know. The rugged tablets in the plants are an older generation and the batteries don't hold as long as they used to. If yours is one of the ones with the yellowing case, it's on the replacement list — put a note in the ticket so we can check the serial. Anything that won't charge or shuts off mid-shift, send it back and we'll swap it from the pool. The MES runs fine on them; it's the battery you'll notice first.
