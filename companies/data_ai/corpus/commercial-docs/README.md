# commercial-docs

Owner: Commercial &mdash; last edited by Marcus Ellery (Chief Commercial Officer).

Index of the commercial document tree. Product and pricing detail lives in the sales and marketing folders; customer-facing process notes live under customer-success and support. If you work in Commercial, make sure the file you need is listed here before creating a new one.

## What is here

- `commercial-docs/sales/` &mdash; pricing and packaging, public-sector tenders. Pricing decisions stay in `pricing-and-packaging.md`; anything quoted outside that file needs Commercial sign-off.
- `commercial-docs/customer-success/` &mdash; onboarding flow for new monitoring subscriptions and imagery orders. References the onboarding checklist in the handbook.
- `commercial-docs/support/` &mdash; coverage across Rotterdam and Denver, and the escalation path into the helpdesk and incidents channel.
- `commercial-docs/marketing/` &mdash; launch checklist for new products and packaged offerings. Read this before telling the tasking portal or customer API console customers about anything new.
- `commercial-docs/comms/` &mdash; brand voice (`brand-voice.md`) and the company social accounts (`social.md`).

## Where the CRM playbooks live

CRM playbooks are kept in the CRM itself, not here. Look under the account and opportunity records for the playbook attached to each stage. If a playbook is missing or stale, flag it in #sales and the Commercial team will update it in the CRM.

## Related trees

Operational detail for the products we sell lives in other document roots: `data-docs/` for catalogue and delivery notes, `platform-docs/` for the analytics API and support systems, `ml-docs/` for model behaviour. Point customers at those only via the customer success team.
