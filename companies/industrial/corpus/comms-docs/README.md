# comms-docs

Owner: Corporate communications. Last edited by Eleni Demir.

This repo holds the working documents for communications and marketing. Everything here is current; if a page is stale, tell Eleni or Sabine.

## What's here

- `brand/brand-basics.md` — brand basics, tone, naming. The brand asset library (logos, fonts, templates) lives in the asset library, not in this repo.
- `customer-stories.md` — customer story pipeline, approvals, and where the published versions go.
- `events/trade-fairs.md` — trade fair calendar, booth bookings, stand logistics for Reutlingen, Telford and Greenville.
- `press-desk/handbook.md` — how the press desk works.
- `social/social-media.md` — channel ownership, posting cadence, escalation path.
- `web/website-updates.md` — website change requests, CMS access, publishing checklist.

## Who owns what

- Sabine Kurz — corporate communications overall and executive messaging; Grainne Walsh runs the press office and the press desk.
- Eleni Demir — internal comms, this repo, social channels, trade fair coordination with the sales team.
- Trade fair booth and event staffing: coordinate with the CSO office.
- Website updates: routed through the web team via the service desk portal; see `web/website-updates.md` for the request form.

## Conventions

- Files are markdown, one topic per file. No images in this repo — link to the asset library.
- Commit straight to main with a short message saying what changed, and tell the page owner if you changed their page.
- Customer stories need a signed release from the customer before they go to the web team.
