# Writing for Aldercombe

**Owner:** Communications & Marketing  
**Last edited by:** Tom Becker (Communications Lead (Development & Alumni))

This page covers house style, brand essentials and the checks to run before anything goes out under the Aldercombe name.

## House style

- Write in plain English. Short sentences. Active voice. If a sentence needs re-reading, rewrite it.
- Use "we" for the university and "you" for the reader. Do not refer to "the University" as a third party.
- Spell out acronyms on first use, even for internal audiences. Always spell out "VLE" and "CRM" the first time they appear.
- Use sentence case for headings and titles — not Title Case.
- Write dates as the day then the month in words, without "th" or "st", and avoid all-number dates.
- Use "Aldercombe" on its own after the first mention. Do not write "Aldercombe University Higher Education Corporation" more than once per document.
- Numbers under ten are written out. Use figures for everything else.
- Keep paragraphs short. Use bullet lists where a reader might scan.

## Logo and colour palette

- Use the master logo files from the shared drive. Do not redraw, stretch or add effects.
- Clear space around the logo: at least the height of the "A" on all sides.
- Do not place the logo on a photo or coloured background unless the white or black version is supplied for that purpose.
- Palette: Aldercombe blue (#1F3A5F) for headings and primary elements, Aldercombe gold (#C9A227) for accents only. Do not use gold for body text.
- When in doubt, use black text on white. That is always acceptable.

## Accessibility checks

- Run the built-in checker in {{WORD_PROCESSOR}} and the web accessibility tool in the CMS before publishing.
- Headings must be real headings (H2, H3), not bold text.
- Every image needs alt text that says what it shows. Decorative images get empty alt text.
- Links must say where they go. "Click here" is not acceptable.
- Check colour contrast: body text must pass AA on white. Do not use grey for small text.
- PDFs: export from {{WORD_PROCESSOR}} using the "Tagged PDF" option, then check the reading order in {{PDF_APP}}.

## Photos and permissions

- Staff photos: use the portraits from the intranet profile pages. Do not crop heads or apply filters.
- Student photos: written consent is held by Communications & Marketing. If a photo is not in the approved library, do not use it.
- Event photography: the photographer's brief includes the consent form. If you are organising an event, confirm the photographer has enough forms before the event starts.
- Buildings and campus shots: no permission needed, but check the Estates team's calendar in case scaffolding or temporary works are visible.

## Before you publish

- Run the readability check in {{WORD_PROCESSOR}} — aim for a reading age of 14 or below.
- Read it aloud. If it does not sound like a person, it needs editing.
- Check the page or document against the current template from the comms team. If you need the template, ask in #comms.
- Send the final version to the comms team for a brand check if it is going to an external audience or the homepage.
- Internal documents for one team do not need a brand check, but they still follow the house style above.
